# Citizens of Reval (1343)

Status: implemented as content (task **R-CITIZENS-001**); runtime wiring is not built, see [`docs/SYSTEMS/CITIZENS.md`](../SYSTEMS/CITIZENS.md).

The living population of the seamless city: a census derived from the 641 buildings of the city plan, a ledger entry for every one of its residents, deep cards for a stratified sample of them, and faction pages that explain who these people are to the powers fighting over them. It exists so that a player running through Lower Town meets *someone*, never a "generic NPC": each resident has an age, a trade, a household, a faith, a face, and a reason to be on that street at that hour.

## Four tiers of people

| Tier | What | Where | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cast | Quest-bearing and faction-face characters, with arcs and outcomes | [`docs/CHARACTERS/`](../CHARACTERS/README.md) | 17 |
| Citizen card | A deep, linked portrait of an ambient resident: face for modelling, routine, motive, network | [`people/`](./people/README.md) | see [`census_tables.md`](./census_tables.md) |
| Census seed | One ledger row per resident: age, trade, household, faction, appearance numbers | [`ledger/`](./ledger/README.md), [`city_census.json`](../data/city_census.json) | 4,247 |
| Crowd | Unnamed Tier 2 bodies generated at runtime from the ledger's distributions | `scripts/world/` (not wired) | variable |

A resident is promoted upward by writing a card (seed to Citizen card) or a brief in `docs/CHARACTERS/` (Citizen card to Cast, through the P7-009 promotion plan in [`docs/cast_faction_promotion.md`](../cast_faction_promotion.md)). Quest content must not treat a Citizen card as active cast until it is promoted.

## What is here

| Page | Use |
|---|---|
| [`CENSUS.md`](./CENSUS.md) | Method, numbers, brackets, coverage of the city's needs, how to extend, verification |
| [`census_tables.md`](./census_tables.md) | Generated statistics: district, sex, age, ethnicity, trade, faction, buildings |
| [`ledger/`](./ledger/README.md) | Every household and resident, one page per street |
| [`people/`](./people/README.md) | Deep cards, one file per person, grouped by district |
| [`factions/`](./factions/README.md) | Ten faction pages with generated census rosters |
| [`TEMPLATE.md`](./TEMPLATE.md), [`WRITING_CARDS.md`](./WRITING_CARDS.md) | Card structure and the writer brief (seed rules, canon sheet, quality rules) |

## Commands

```bash
python3 tools/city/build_city_census.py            # census.json (deterministic; do not re-roll once cards exist)
python3 tools/city/plan_citizen_cards.py           # who gets a card, and the social edges between cards
python3 tools/city/build_citizen_ledger.py         # ledger pages and census_tables.md
python3 tools/city/build_faction_rosters.py        # generated roster blocks inside the faction pages
python3 tools/validate_citizen_cards.py --require-all
```

Data files: [`docs/data/city_census.json`](../data/city_census.json) (residents), [`docs/data/citizen_cards_plan.json`](../data/citizen_cards_plan.json) (card selection, edges, and the agreed facts that keep both ends of a relationship consistent).
