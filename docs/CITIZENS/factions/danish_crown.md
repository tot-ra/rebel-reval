# Danish Crown

Status: implemented as content (task **R-CITIZENS-001**). Confidence: `plausible composite`. Ledger id: `danish_crown`. Scope: the 202 residents of the census who lean toward Toompea, plus an estimate of the hinterland (outside the census). No April 1343 muster of the crown's friends survives; every figure is a composite.

## What they want and what they cost

The launch table gives the crown one want and one shadow: it wants to keep its distant, indebted rule of Estonia, and it taxes its subjects into ruin to fund a fading claim. On the hill that reads as a household trying not to admit it is poor. Viceroy Konrad Preen ([konrad_preen](../../CHARACTERS/konrad_preen.md)) keeps seals, ledgers and audience hours going because the seals are most of what is left. Copenhagen is weeks away by sea, the garrison is thin, and the council of Lower Town pays its dues with the manner of a man paying a debtor he plans to outlive.

To an ordinary person the crown is the tax collector, the court that hears a boundary quarrel, and the roof over a hundred wages. A castle cook eats because the crown still stocks the kitchen; a Harju peasant is bound to a manor because the crown's vassals will it so. Both are true at once, and the page should never let one swallow the other. The cost is felt as levies of grain and labour, fines at the Rahukohtu court, and the sense that every favour from the hill arrives with a clerk who writes it down.

## Why people join

The shared root is dependence: the hill pays wages, owns debts, holds the keys to a future, or simply raised you. Nobody here is moved by a slogan about kings. The reasons differ.

- **Fear.** [Marquard Rotermund](../people/toompea/marquard_rotermund.md), a vassal knight of 21, holds his fief at the viceroy's pleasure and cannot afford to be seen wavering. [Winand van Kolne](../people/toompea_foot/winand_van_kolne.md), an armourer on the Western castle road, makes castle weapons on credit and cannot refuse an order.
- **Love and gratitude.** [Beke van Deventer](../people/lower_town/beke_van_deventer.md), a maid in a Lower Town tap-room, was given a trade by a quartermaster's wife and believes Toompea keeps its word. She will carry a message but not name a neighbour.
- **Debt and money.** [Reynold van Wismar](../people/lower_town/reynold_van_wismar.md), nineteen and a merchant's son, reports gate traffic to the castle to pay off his grandfather's shame. [Torben Brok](../people/toompea/torben_brok.md), a crown clerk, has made informing a clerk's routine.
- **Belonging.** [Eler van Dortmund](../people/lower_town/eler_van_dortmund.md), a shop boy of 14 born in the viceroy's kitchen, counts men at Vespers for the falconer's lad because the hill once fed him.
- **Loyalty inherited.** [Katharina](../people/lower_town/katharina.md), a maid on Lai, carries her mother's laundress loyalty; she wants a place on Toompea one day.
- **Trade and fear of rebels.** [Ermelin van Bremen](../people/lower_town/ermelin_van_bremen.md), a spinner on Lai whose father's wine reached the viceroy's table, wants a Reval that stays under a tolerable peace.
- **Office and duty.** [Meinhard Snelle](../people/lower_town/meinhard_snelle.md), a watch sergeant at the town hall, is a core member who sits inside the council's own guard. [Elisabet Jaagu tütar](../people/toompea/elisabet_jaagu_tutar.md), a castle servant of 55, is core because she has served the household for decades.
- **Craft and rank.** [Kristoffer Bjelke](../people/toompea/kristoffer_bjelke.md), the chamberlain, and the falconers [Reynold Gude](../people/toompea/reynold_gude.md) and [Helmich Gude](../people/toompea/helmich_gude.md) belong to the viceroy's household, where service is a family inheritance.
- **Ambition.** [Absalon Skjalm](../people/toompea/absalon_skjalm.md), a squire of 21, and [Arend van Minden](../people/toompea/arend_van_minden.md), a clerk of 20, hope the hill will advance them if it survives.

## Who belongs

The roster below counts 202 residents, 4.8% of the census, which fits a faction that is a garrison, a court and a household, not a movement. Of these, 150 live on Toompea, 36 in Lower Town and 14 in the vassal yards below the castle; only two live along the cattle and Harju roads. The census puts the castle household at about 100, so the rest are dependants: wives and children of men-at-arms, retired craftsmen, washerwomen, spinners and the servants of vassal knights.

By sex it is 124 men to 78 women, a male tilt from the garrison. By age it is a working-age faction: 147 of 202 are between 15 and 44, 13 are over 60 and only four are children, because soldiers and clerks in service are mostly unmarried or live with families elsewhere. By ethnicity, Germans (90) and Danes (72) dominate, with 38 Estonians who cook, clean and carry, and one Finn and one Swede. The trades read like a castle: 31 men-at-arms, 18 clerks, 16 servants, 11 vassal knights, 6 cooks, 5 squires, with spinners and washerwomen (20 and 6) as the dependant women's economy.

Roles: 28 core, 42 active, 95 sympathisers, 19 coerced or dependent, and 18 informers. Eighteen informers is a large number for 202 people, and that is the point. The crown cannot afford soldiers in the town, so it buys ears.

For gameplay, the mix means the crown is mostly *soft*: a sympathiser answers a friendly question, but only the 28 core and 42 active know anything worth hiding. A balanced city has this faction spread thin through Lower Town households, with the hill concentrated and legible. A map with too few crown informers makes the hill blind; too many makes every maid a spy and the player paranoid for no reason.

## Where they are

Lossi plats holds 71 members, the castle square and its barracks, kitchens and chambers. The Western castle road (17), Toom-Kooli (14), Kiriku põik (12), Toom-Rüütli (10), Kohtu (10), Pikk jalg (8), Rahukohtu (7) and Rutu (6) are the dwellings of clerks, falconers, retired craftsmen and vassal knights. Kiriku plats, beside St Mary's cathedral, holds the cooks of the chapter kitchens, who are Estonian and cross freely between chapter and castle.

In Lower Town the crown's members are scattered: Pikk (6) and Lai for maids and merchants' sons, Rataskaevu for [Beke van Deventer](../people/lower_town/beke_van_deventer.md), and Raekoja, where a watch sergeant and a council servant ([Peter van Campen](../people/lower_town/peter_van_campen.md)) sit inside the town hall. Messages travel along Pikk jalg, the steep road between hill and town, and out of the hill gate on market days. A basket of bread carried up on Friday is the commonest cover.

## Organisation and secrecy

The crown's friends are not a conspiracy; they are an administration with a few conspirators in it. The core is a chain of command (viceroy, chamberlain, steward, clerks, captain of the garrison) that keeps ordinary records. Around it sits an informal network of informers who never meet.

- **Cells.** Informers work in twos and threes, usually joined by a clerk. A cell may be a maid, a falconer's boy and a clerk who never learn each other's trade.
- **Signs.** A red thread on a cellar door, a pebble from the castle chapel, a sprig of rowan: small domestic marks, not signals, which the hill's friends read as friendship.
- **Messages.** Notes go in Latin or Low German with a burned-after-reading habit; word goes by basket, laundry, falconer's lads and a Friday errand to the hill. Money goes in small coin or in kind: a cloak lent, a debt forgiven, a place found for a nephew.
- **Who knows whom.** [Eler van Dortmund](../people/lower_town/eler_van_dortmund.md) tells a falconer's lad; [Katharina](../people/lower_town/katharina.md) tells the same boy; [Reynold van Wismar](../people/lower_town/reynold_van_wismar.md) and [Mogens Skjalm](../people/toompea/mogens_skjalm.md) each notice the other's silence without knowing why.

A Kalev commission could reveal this in pieces: a sealed order for armourer Winand, a basket carrying more than bread, a key forged for a clerk. Nothing shows the whole web, and a player who pulls one thread learns mostly how frightened its owner is.

## Resources and economy

The crown's money is tolls, fines, rents of crown land, and a fading share of the Hanseatic customs. It pays in silver by weight and in kind: a place in the castle kitchen, grain from the crown's barns, a vassal's fee. Informers earn a few pfennigs or a cloak, not fortunes. Arms are few: the castle holds spears, crossbows and a small armoury kept by men like Winand van Kolne, but not enough for a second night like the first. The crown needs the city's food, the Rat's silence, the harbour's tolls and labour from the Estonians who cook and carry. It takes through fines, labour services, and the right to quarter men.

## Relations with other factions

- **Hanseatic guilds** ([hanseatic](./hanseatic.md)): grudging partners. The Rat pays tolls and resents the hill's claims.
- **Livonian Order** ([livonian_order](./livonian_order.md)): the great threat, offering help that swallows. The crown fears a loan more than a lance.
- **Black Cloaks** ([black_cloaks](./black_cloaks.md)): open enmity once writs are ignored. The crown has informers among those who meet them.
- **Harju Kings** ([harju_kings](./harju_kings.md)): rebels against crown vassals; enmity, with the crown too weak to answer.
- **Cult of Metsik** ([cult_metsik](./cult_metsik.md)): tolerated as long as it stays quiet; Estonian castle servants keep rowan and red thread.
- **Pskov / Novgorod** ([pskov_novgorod](./pskov_novgorod.md)): trade guests whose tolls matter.
- **Vitalienbrüder** ([vitalienbruder](./vitalienbruder.md)): an irritation at the harbour the crown cannot police.
- **Church** ([church](./church.md)): the chapter on Toompea is the crown's friend; the town parishes are not.

## Tensions inside

1. **Garrison against clerks.** Men-at-arms such as [Niels Bjelke](../people/toompea/niels_bjelke.md) and [Tyge Lunge](../people/toompea/tyge_lunge.md) think the clerks bleed the hill; clerks such as [Niels Skjalm](../people/toompea/niels_skjalm.md) think soldiers cost more than they earn.
2. **Loyalty against fear.** [Torben Rud](../people/toompea/torben_rud.md), a retired craftsman who is active on principle, scorns [Marquard Rotermund](../people/toompea/marquard_rotermund.md), who stays only because he cannot leave.
3. **Danes against the rest.** Old Danish families such as the Lunges and Skjalms regard the hill as theirs; German servants and clerks like [Gyse Hovesche](../people/toompea/gyse_hovesche.md) are useful and unwelcome.
4. **Informers against each other.** [Ove Lunge](../people/toompea/ove_lunge.md), a glazier, and [Ricbod van Gripeswold](../people/toompea/ricbod_van_gripeswold.md) live on the same lane and neither knows the other sells news.

## Spring 1343 timeline

- **Before 23 April.** Easter was 13 April; the hill hears rumours of gatherings in Harju. Informers are busier: notes on gate traffic, on who buys iron, on which councillor is angry. The viceroy counts spears.
- **The night.** On the night of 23 April the hill closes its gates. Core and active members hold the castle; sympathisers in Lower Town stay home or hide a neighbour. Some informers send their last note and go quiet.
- **The siege weeks.** Garrison and servants live on short rations. The crown's authority shrinks to the hill. Members who lived in Lower Town are suspected by neighbours; some return up the hill, some burn their notes.

## Outside the walls

Estimates here are `plausible composite` and outside the census. Perhaps 200 to 350 people in the hinterland lean toward the crown: vassal households on the manors near the hill, tenant farmers who fear the rebels more than they like the lord, bailiffs, and the families of garrison soldiers. They are weakest where manor lords have bound peasants, and strongest near the castle road. They cannot be mobilised as a force.

## Game hooks

**Ledger events that raise standing:** delivering a sealed order, carrying a crown writ, proving the thin garrison, sheltering a crown clerk, keeping quiet about a note. **That lower it:** hiding a Black Cloak, mocking a seal, exposing an informer, refusing a commission from the hill.

**Barks:** "The hill still pays." / "Mind the red thread." / "A cloak is nothing between friends." / "I saw no one."

**Quest seeds:**
1. *The Friday basket.* A baker's basket going up Pikk jalg is too heavy; Kalev may look, or look away.
2. *A key for a clerk.* [Torben Brok](../people/toompea/torben_brok.md) wants a copy of a chest key, no reason given.
3. *Burned notes.* [Reynold van Wismar](../people/lower_town/reynold_van_wismar.md) asks for a place to burn paper after curfew.
4. *Armourer's debt.* [Winand van Kolne](../people/toompea_foot/winand_van_kolne.md) asks Kalev to finish an order he cannot.

**Reputation reactions:** at high standing, castle servants lower their eyes less and the Western castle road offers credit. At low standing, the hill's gate guards ask questions and Lower Town informers stop talking in front of Kalev.

**At standing -3,** a faction-tied resident says: "Not here, smith. Walk on. The hill has a long memory and a short temper." **At standing +3:** "You have done the hill a kindness. If anyone asks, you were never here, and neither was I."
