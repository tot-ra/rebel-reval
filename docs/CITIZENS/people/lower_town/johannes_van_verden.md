## Biography
Born on Pikk in 1308 to a rope-maker turned merchant. At sixteen he sailed to Visby as a ship's boy, nicked an ear in a gale, and came back with a taste for cargo and gossip. He married Sibbe Blyde in 1328 and has eight children. A debt to a Lübeck factor in 1338 left him exposed, and the unmarried merchants' set offered to ease it in return for what he heard. He has been their ears since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear his debts and apprentice his sons in good houses.
- **Fear:** His informing coming to the council's ears.
- **Contradiction:** He is a family man and a spy, and loves both parts.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** His debt is not cleared; he still owes 40 marks.

## Work and money
He ships salt, herring and rope; he earns about 80 marks a year. He owes 40 marks to a Lübeck factor, paid at quarter-days, and is owed 15. The shed rent brings 4 marks. A bad month is a late ship.

## Faction and belief
Informer for the unmarried merchants, from debt, not love. A small favour: news of a ship; a large one: a signature; to inform, he does. He gives to St Nicholas and carries a lead sailor's medal.

## Voice

- **Delivery:** Husky and hearty, dropping to a murmur when he sells.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who owes what at the quay and which clerks drink. He trades it for debt relief. He believes the false rumour that the Order has bought Wesenberg.
