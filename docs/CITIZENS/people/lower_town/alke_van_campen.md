## Biography
Born in 1330 in Kampen on the Ijssel, the youngest of five sons of a barge-hand, Alke was sent to Reval with a cousin who died of fever on the road. The master took him in without asking questions, and he has worked off the passage ever since. He cannot read and does not mind. The boys call him "Ox" and he lets them. He knows the weight of every bale in the yard by lifting it once. He sleeps in the apprentices' loft.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A full belly and, one day, a barge of his own.
- **Fear:** Deep water, and being sent back.
- **Contradiction:** He is the strongest in the yard and the most afraid of the dog's growl.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps the crusts for the dog and has named it Pieter, after his dead cousin.
