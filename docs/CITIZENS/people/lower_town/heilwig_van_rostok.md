## Biography
Heilwig was born in 1322, the first of Lutke and Hille's girls, in the room above the shop. She learned to gut a herring at seven and to bargain at ten. She picked up Estonian from the porters and the fish-wives and speaks it better than her mother. In the bad winter of 1341 the shop nearly closed; she walked to the harbour every dawn through ice and brought back whatever was left. That winter also gave her a hoarse voice that never recovered. A betrothal fell through when the dowry did not come. She has since stopped waiting and works.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A shop of her own, with an Estonian cook she can trust.
- **Fear:** Being married off to an old widower for the debt's sake.
- **Contradiction:** She jokes at everything and notices every unkindness.
