- Lean, tonsured and husky, with a faint pock-scarred forehead.

- Rivalry of lamp-oil and candle-ends with the priests of Vene and Niguliste.
- Carries a private grief he does not name.

## Biography
Born in 1304 in Reval, the son of a German cooper. He was schooled at St Nicholas and took minor orders at nineteen. A young woman in his father's street bore him a daughter in 1330 and the child was sickly; in the priest's absence a neighbour baptised her in haste, and the daughter died within a month. He took full orders in 1332, and has served the Holy Spirit house since 1337. His sister's funeral in 1340 was led by the same neighbour, because he was at the deathbed of an inmate. He carries these things silently and gives alms in their name.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To make the hospital's winter bread last through a dear spring.
- **Fear:** That his own sins unfit him to absolve others.
- **Contradiction:** A man who keeps count of others' lamp-oil yet gives away his own candles.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He prays each year for a daughter whose grave is unmarked.

## Work and money
He receives a stipend of 12 marks a year from the Rat's fund, plus offerings, and passes most to the almsfolk. The hospital owns a few houses and a mill; a bad month means a lean pot. He owes a book-seller 2 marks.

## Faction and belief
Core of the Church faction, from faith and habit. For a small favour he would carry a blessing; for a large one he would shelter someone in the chapel; to inform he would break no seal. He keeps the fasts and prays for the dead.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Latin in the office; Middle Low German with parishioners.

- **Sample lines:** "Come, sit. What troubles you?" "God sees, and so do I, but I keep silent." "Dat is nicht so licht, it is not so easy."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the confessions of half the street, and keeps them. He would trade only a blessing. Believes falsely that the Dominicans plan to take the hospital.
