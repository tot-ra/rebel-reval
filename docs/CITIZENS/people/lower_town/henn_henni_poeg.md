## Biography
Henn was born in 1322 in a Harju village and sent to town at ten after his father's death. A Dominican friar taught him letters in exchange for carrying water. He was hired by Tideman in 1340, to keep the Estonian debts. His mother is still in Harju.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be a scribe at the Rathaus.
- **Fear:** Dismissal because he is Estonian.
- **Contradiction:** Educated yet loyal to his village.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He writes for illiterate villagers on Sundays.
