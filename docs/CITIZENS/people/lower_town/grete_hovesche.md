## At a glance
- A stocky woman with a limp and a high, quick voice, who is everywhere in the lane before noon.
- A German by birth, raised partly by an Estonian wet-nurse, so she speaks both street languages.
- Gives small kindnesses like seeds and expects a harvest.
- Oddity: a hired maid who has more people owing her than her master does.

## Biography
Born 1316 in the harbour district, the daughter of a German cooper who died when she was three. Her mother put her to an Estonian wet-nurse in Kopli and she grew up in both tongues. At fourteen she fell from a barrel-stack and broke her shin, set badly by a quack; she has limped since. She entered the Zierenberg house in 1335. A year ago she gave a girl shoes and a message to pass; the girl is [Mett Toomase tütar](mett_toomase_tutar.md), and she is only one of several.

## Motivation

## Work and money
Pay is 8 schillings a year, board, and two gowns. She saves 6 pfennig a month toward a stall. A bad month is the limp acting up.

## Faction and belief
Her Hanseatic leaning is practical: the council order feeds the great houses, and a maid in one is safer than a maid outside. She works as a carrier of news. A small favour she asks first; a large one she trades; informing she does when it pays. She keeps Estonian charms for her leg.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German with household; Estonian with Kopli folk.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, running words together.
- **Sample lines:** "Do you owe me? No, no, I never said." / "Kuule, walk with me." / "It was nothing, truly."
- **Verbal tic:** "Little thing," for any favour.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who owes whom among the servants of Lai. She would trade it for a stall. She falsely believes her shin could be set right by a pilgrim's relic.
