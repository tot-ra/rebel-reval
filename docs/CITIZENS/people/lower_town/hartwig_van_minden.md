## Biography
Born 1293 in Minden on the Weser, third son of a cloth merchant, he came to Reval at nineteen as a factor's assistant and married the widow of a wax-dealer, Adelheid, in 1319. By 1330 he owned the great house on Lai and by 1336 sat on the council. Adelheid died in 1338. In 1341 his elder daughter married into a Wismar house, and to find the dowry he borrowed nine marks from Reynold Gude, a Danish falconer on Toompea who lends to burghers at a polite rate. The marriage went ahead and the debt remains; every Michaelmas a servant in the king's livery arrives on Lai to remind him. His voice, which carries, has made him a good speaker in council and a poor liar.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To pay Gude before Michaelmas and sit on the bench owing the hill nothing.
- **Fear:** That the debt gives Toompea a claim on his vote, and that someone will say so aloud.
- **Contradiction:** Votes with the council majority each week and meets the circle each month.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He leans toward the circle that gathers around Reimar Fatbinder and has never put his name to anything.

## Work and money
He trades broadcloth from Ypres and wax from the east. His turnover runs to several hundred marks a year, most of it on credit. Household of eleven eats a quarter of his profit. The nine marks to Gude are small beside his stock but large beside his ready silver. A bad month is a ship lost to pirates or a debt called early.

## Faction and belief
Officially Hanseatic core: his money is in the Hanse's cloth, and the council is his bench. His own reason for the circle is a creditor on the hill. He would sign a small favour, lend a cart, but not stand in a street; asked to inform, he would lie loudly.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German in council and trade; Latin at mass and in contracts; a murmur in the circle.
- **Delivery:** Booming, slow, every sentence a statement; he lowers it to nothing when it matters.
- **Sample lines:** "The council has spoken, and I with it." "Wine at my table, gentlemen, wine." "Wat is, dat is."
- **Verbal tic:** "I say plainly."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the council's grain figures. Would trade them for Gude's note. Believes falsely that Konrad Preen means to garrison the Lower Town.
