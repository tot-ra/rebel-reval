## At a glance
- A wart on the right cheek that he touches when a figure displeases him.
- A flat monotone with which he delivers verdicts and condolences alike.
- Known for paying for a funeral candle and remembering it for years.
- Oddity: a councillor whose power rests on kindnesses small enough to be called courtesy.

## Biography
Born in Reval in 1290 to a Deventer cloth-merchant; he was a clerk at nineteen and a factor at thirty. He joined the Rat in 1332 and has sat on grain and harbour committees. His first wife died in childbirth; he married [Beke van Luneborch](./beke_van_luneborch.md) in 1325. He now holds a modest fleet share and a network of small debts.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A quiet city, a seat on the Rat for his sons-in-law.
- **Fear:** Disorder and a Rat divided.
- **Contradiction:** Genial in public, a bookkeeper of favours in private.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He quietly maintains a cell of informants among servants and clerks; he stood surety for a tanner's fine and now watches who watches.

## Faction and belief
He is the Hansa's man because the Rat is. He wants merchants to keep their purses and the town to keep its peace. He would ask for a small favour, call in a large one, and inform on anyone who threatened the quay. Devout, formal, scornful of charms.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German in council, Latin in oaths.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, measured, never loud.
- **Sample lines:** "I remember what you owe, friend." "A kindness is a loan." "Let us be reasonable."
- **Verbal tic:** Touches the wart.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who owes whom. He would trade it for a vote. He believes the false rumour that the viceroy will tax the Rat's wine.
