## Biography
Born in 1320 on a ship's deck in the roads off Visby, to a mother who died of it. She grew up on the Gotland wharves, learned to walk on a rocking boat and never learned another way. She came to Reval at twelve with a pedlar who left her on Gyse Hardekop's step.

She sleeps beside Gyla and shares her bread.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry a man with a steady roof.
- **Fear:** Open water and silence.
- **Contradiction:** She talks without stopping and keeps the one secret that matters.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows who lifted the boundary stake and is trying to decide whether to tell.
