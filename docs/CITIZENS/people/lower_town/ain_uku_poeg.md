## Biography
Born in 1329 to Uku and his first wife Maret, who died of a winter fever when he was two. He remembers a smell of milk and nothing else. Wendla raised him from three and taught him his letters of thread: counting skeins, colours, twists. At twelve he was set to the barrels as his father's apprentice and has been carrying since. He is on the brink of being useful, and knows it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To stand in the market stall with his own voice and have a buyer stop for him, not his father.
- **Fear:** That his voice never settles and the street laughs.
- **Contradiction:** Obeys his father and mocks him behind his back.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has twice measured short on purpose for a hungry woman and put the difference on his own tally.

## Work and money
He receives food, a bed and a belt knife; no wage. His father pays Ain's fraternity dues, three pfennig a quarter. He carries two lispfund of salt at a time. A bad month is one when his father is short-tempered over Tyde's pledge.

## Faction and belief
No faction; he is fourteen and wants his breakfast. He would run an errand for anyone who asked nicely. He kisses the Holy Spirit door-post and spits over his shoulder at the sight of a black cat.

## Voice

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every dog on the street. He would trade the information for a spare crust. He believes that a ship of the Order lies beyond Nargen, which is false.
