## At a glance
- A slight man with a high, narrow forehead, a once-broken nose and a flour-white shirt.
- He fires the oven before dawn, drives the delivery rounds, and bargains hard with the miller.
- Odd for a journeyman: he lodges with a Russian widow and speaks German like a Swede and Estonian like a baker.

## Biography
Born in 1316 at a coastal town near Kalmar. His father was a miller who died in 1334; Gunnar came to Reval as a deckhand in 1335 and was taken in by a baker. He hired on with Marfa in 1340 after Grigory died. He broke his nose in a quarrel over a loaf in 1338. He sleeps in the loft above the oven.

## Motivation
- **Want:** His own oven by the Harju road.
- **Fear:** Marfa's debts closing the shop.
- **Contradiction:** Prickly with the miller and tender with the children.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has saved eleven marks and has not told Marfa.

## Work and money
He earns a mark and a half a year and keep, plus a pfennig on each delivery. He saves eleven marks. He owes nothing. A bad month is one without rye.

## Faction and belief
None; he is a stranger and wants his oven. He would carry a loaf anywhere and say nothing. He keeps St Olaf's day and a Swedish lullaby for the girls.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish with himself and at Mass; clipped German at the oven.
- **Delivery:** Nasal and quick, with a Swedish lilt.
- **Sample lines:** "Hot, take it now." "Siim, I wait, you wait." "Det är bra."
- **Verbal tic:** Pinches his crooked nose before bargaining.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which mills are short of rye. He would trade it for an oven lease. He believes the Danish king will tax bread, which is false.
