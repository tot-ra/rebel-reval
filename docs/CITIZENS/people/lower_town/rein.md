## Biography
Rein was born in a hamlet near Reval, apprenticed to an Estonian furrier on Hobusepea at ten, and took over the shop in 1320. He married Gertrud in 1322. He bought the house on Hobusepea with a loan from a Westphalian merchant, repaid it in 1331, and has been borrowing ever since. Last year the Amt fined him twelve marks for selling coney as squirrel; he borrowed it from Heyno and has not repaid.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry Ell and Magdalena well and keep the shop in the family.
- **Fear:** A bad winter with unsold pelts and a pledge called in.
- **Contradiction:** A creditor who is himself a debtor.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He stood surety for Wolter's fine and is quietly afraid Wolter will flee.

## Work and money
Squirrel pelts come from Novgorod via Pskov traders at a few pfennigs apiece; a good cap fetches a schilling. Rein carries two debts: twelve marks to Heyno, and fifteen marks as surety for Wolter. He is owed six marks of grain by Jaak.

## Faction and belief
He has no faction: pelts sell to everyone. A small favour he gives on credit; a large one he counts; informing he thinks dangerous and unprofitable. He nails a hare's foot over the loft door.
