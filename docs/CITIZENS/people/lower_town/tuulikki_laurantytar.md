## Biography
Born 1316 near Turku, daughter of a boatwright, Lauri, who died when a ship slipped from its cradle. She crossed the Gulf at nineteen with a cousin who sold fish and settled in Reval to earn a dowry. The cousin married and left; she stayed. The van Campens took her in 1337 to mind Lysbeth, then the rest. She has nursed all five of the youngest and counts them as hers, which is not hers to count.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To go home to the Finnish coast with a dowry of ten marks.
- **Fear:** Sickness in the house, and being blamed for it.
- **Contradiction:** Speaks little yet is the one the children confide in.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sends a mark each year to a sister in Turku via a fish carter.

## Work and money
She earns 3 marks a year with board and a pair of shoes. Linen from the mistress's chest, soap from the soap-boiler, thread from the shop below. Savings: four marks. A bad month is a fever in the nursery.

## Faction and belief
No faction; she is a foreigner and knows how fast a foreigner is blamed. Asked for a small favour she would give it; a large one, she would refuse; informing she would not do. Folk practice: a knife under the child's cot; church on Sundays.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish to herself and in prayer; Low German to the house, in short sentences.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses between words.
- **Sample lines:** "Yes... the baby has slept." "I will see... after the washing." "Hyvä, hyvä."
- **Verbal tic:** A long "mm" before any answer.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the children's fears and the household's quarrels. She would trade it for a letter written for her. False belief: that the Gulf freezes late because of the Order.
