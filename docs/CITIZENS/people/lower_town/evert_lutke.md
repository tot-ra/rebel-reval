## At a glance
- Sits at a slanted desk in the yard office with ink to the wrist.
- Smudges every page because he writes left-handed.
- Odd: he knows more of the town's debts than the Kämmerer and tells nothing.

## Biography
Born 1313 in Lübeck, a notary's third son, taught Latin by a cathedral priest. He came to Reval in 1335 to a Dunkri merchant and moved to Wolter's yard in 1340 because the pay arrived. He was dismissed once for a disputed ledger and has not forgotten it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A burgher right and his own desk.
- **Fear:** Being blamed for Wolter's debts.
- **Contradiction:** Meticulous with figures, careless with his own coin.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Made a second copy of Wolter's accounts for his own protection.
