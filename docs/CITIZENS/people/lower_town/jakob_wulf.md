## At a glance
- A tall, slight man with a sandy moustache, ink at the cuffs and a gravelly voice that surprises people who expect a thin one.
- He keeps the Rode ledger, copies the master's letters, and lunches on bread at his desk.
- Odd for a clerk: he thinks of himself as a craftsman of the pen, and says so.

## Biography
Born in 1314 in Reval to a cooper, Wulf, who died when he was eleven. A priest at St Nicholas taught him letters in exchange for sweeping, and Berend took him into the counting-room at nineteen. He has copied ten thousand letters. He eats at the back of the hall and sleeps in a closet behind the counting-room.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A clerk's post at the town hall, with a salary.
- **Fear:** Dismissal after a mistake in the books.
- **Contradiction:** Prides himself on his neat hand and his sloppy accounts of his own purse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has been asked by three different men not to mention the others.

## Work and money
He earns six marks a year and keep, with a gift at Christmas. He saves two marks a year. He owes a mark to a friend. His quills come from a Lübeck dealer. A bad month is when the letters run short and the master is idle.

## Faction and belief
He believes in the council and the guilds as the order of the town; he does not know why he has been asked to watch. He would copy a letter for a small favour, hide a document for a large one, and inform if his place were safe. He lights a candle at St Nicholas on Fridays.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German at the table; Latin phrases in letters.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly and quiet, with a pause before names.
- **Sample lines:** "I copied it as written, master." "That is not my hand." "Dat steit in dem boke."
- **Verbal tic:** Blows on his fingertips as if the ink were wet.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every bale's destination. He would trade it for a post at the town hall. He believes the Danish viceroy will sell the province, which is false.
