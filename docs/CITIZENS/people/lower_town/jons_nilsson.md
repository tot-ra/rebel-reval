## Biography
Born in 1313 at Stockholm to a Swedish notary, he was taught Latin by a parish priest and sent to Reval at nineteen to clerk for a Hanseatic factor. He lost that place when the factor left in 1337 and has lodged with Siim Jaagu poeg since, keeping his accounts for board. He lost a brother in the Flemish wars.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A place as secretary to a councillor.
- **Fear:** Dismissal and a return to Stockholm.
- **Contradiction:** A Swede who serves German merchants and calls it a career.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has copied letters from the tavern's guests for the guild, and never told Siim.

## Faction and belief
Active on the Hanseatic side, because the Hansa is the only world that pays him. A small favour is a copied letter; a large one a hidden one; he would inform before he would starve. He keeps Saint Erik's day and Latin prayers.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish at home, Latin in writing, careful German in town.
- **Delivery:** Low, unhurried, precise.
- **Sample lines:** "I will write it down." "Not in the open." "Det är så, it is so."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which letters carry bad news. He would trade it for a secretary's place. He believes, wrongly, that the Order will land troops before Midsummer.
