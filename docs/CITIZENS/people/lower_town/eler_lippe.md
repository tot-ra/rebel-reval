## At a glance
- Heavy, bearded, loud; he fills the porch of his house on Pikk.
- Left-handed by birth, taught to write with the right; the left still reaches first for knife, cup and coin.
- Sits on the council and runs a quiet circle of clerks and servants.
- Keeps his wife's name in the ledger beside his own, which the Rat finds odd.

## Biography
Born on Pikk in 1302, the second son of a Lübeck-trained merchant. His left hand was tied behind him at school in St Nicholas; he learned to write right-handed and never forgave the master. He sailed to Visby and Novgorod at twenty, took his father's house when his elder brother drowned off Åland in 1331, and married Berta van Soest in 1325 (before his brother's death) for her dowry and her silence. Elected to the council in 1338, he now sits among the twenty and dines at the burgomaster's.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see his eldest son in the council and his daughters well married.
- **Fear:** A siege that cuts the Novgorod route and the dowry money with it.
- **Contradiction:** He praises Hanseatic law and breaks it quietly on the weight of wax.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He pays Ropert Vrie, a council servant, to read him the sealed minutes.

## Work and money
He ships cloth, wax and furs; about 400 marks pass through his hands each year. Credit is settled at quarter-days. He owes 120 marks to Lübeck correspondents and is owed 200 by the Novgorod trade. A bad month is a closed road and two ships late.

## Faction and belief
Core Hanseatic. His reason is simple: the roads are his fortune. A small favour: a word at council; a large one: silver and a locked cellar; to inform: he already pays for it. He fasts in Lent, gives to the Holy Spirit hospital, and touches the lintel for luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German; Latin for contracts.
- **Delivery:** Booming, slow, a council voice that fills a room.
- **Sample lines:** "A price is a promise, sir." / "I have said what the Rat says; I have not said what I think." / "Dat is een ding, dat is gewogen."
- **Verbal tic:** "Weighed and found so."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the council's minutes before the clerks. He would trade it for a safe shipping date. He believes the false rumour that the Order has already moved a force to Wesenberg.
