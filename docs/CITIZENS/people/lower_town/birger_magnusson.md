## Biography
Born in 1263 in Visby on Gotland, he was a cutler's boy during the town and countryside war of 1288 and burnt his hand on a forge fire when the countrymen raided. He worked on in Visby until his wife died, then followed his son [Magnus Birgersson](magnus_birgersson.md) to Reval in 1325. He sits by the hearth and whispers advice. A year ago [Borchard van Hamelen](borchard_van_hamelen.md) lent him a loaf and a favour, and Birger returned it by finding another recruit.

## Faction and belief
Active on the Hanseatic side because it fed the Gotland trade in his youth. For a small favour he would carry a word; for a large one he would shame a debtor; to inform he would hesitate. He prays and keeps a candle for the dead.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish.
- **Delivery:** Soft, almost whispering.
- **Sample lines:** "Kindness is a loan." "Lyssna, listen." "I remember Visby."
- **Verbal tic:** Closes his eyes between sentences.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows old Gotland debts and who is kin to whom. He would trade it for a warm seat. Believes falsely that the Hanse will move its Reval counting-house to Visby.
