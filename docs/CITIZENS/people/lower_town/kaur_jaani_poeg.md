## Biography
Born in 1330 on Olevimägi in the room above the net-loft. He has sailed twice as far as Naissaar and once to Gotland, and was seasick on all three. His father has apprenticed him in the household to keep the wage, and he learns the knots, the lead-line and the tides.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** His father's silence.
- **Contradiction:** He loves the ship and is sick on it.
