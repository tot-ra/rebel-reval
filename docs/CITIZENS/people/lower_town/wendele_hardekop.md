## Biography
Born 1312, daughter of a Hardekop net-maker of the harbour; she began knotting at eight in a damp cellar, which gave her the fingers. She learned Estonian from the fishers who bought her nets. She married Wolter in 1337. She has borne two sons and has paid his clerk's wages from her own net-money more than once.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Her sons apprenticed to something dry.
- **Fear:** Wolter's debt becoming public.
- **Contradiction:** Scorns his bragging yet never contradicts it in public.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Keeps four marks hidden from the household purse.

## Work and money
A net of forty fathoms brings 8 schillings and takes a month. She buys flax thread from Estonians and sells to the shore.
