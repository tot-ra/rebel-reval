## Biography
Evert was born in 1330 in a Harju village to Joosepi, a miller. The mill burned in 1339 and his elder brother Ants, who now serves as stable hand in the same house, brought him to Reval. Mikk took him as apprentice in 1341.

He lost the tooth when a barrel lid sprang loose. He sings the miller's songs.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be big enough to carry two sacks.
- **Fear:** The dark cellar and its rats.
- **Contradiction:** Loud and brave in the yard, silent in the dark.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He hides a mouse in his cap and feeds it crumbs.
