## Biography
Born near Kopli in 1323; she followed her sister [Ann Antsu tütar](./ann_antsu_tutar.md) into the Minden house at fourteen. She learned numerals at the market, where a fish-wife taught her counting by pebbles. She sleeps in the garret beside Ann.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A bread oven of her own.
- **Fear:** Fire in the kitchen.
- **Contradiction:** Sharp-tongued, soft with animals.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She feeds a stray cat in the yard against orders.
