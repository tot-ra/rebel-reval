## At a glance
- A broad, slow-moving boy who carries two casks where others carry one and sniffs as he works.
- Left-handed, which got him cuffed in two houses before this one.
- Talks through his nose and has lost his back teeth, so his cheeks look sunken at fifteen.
- Is being paid to remember who says what.

## Biography
Johan was born in 1328 in Riga, where his father, a boatman of the Kniphof family, drowned in 1334. His mother took service in the house of a wine-importer, and at eleven Johan was sent to Reval to a cooper on Lühike jalg, the same master who later took Hartwig Stolte. At thirteen he passed to Nicolaus van Wismar. The wine-importer, who deals with the Order's stewards, covers his mother's rent in Riga, and in return a grey-coated agent meets Johan after Mass and asks what the tap-room said. Johan has been doing it for a year.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep his mother in her room and finish his term without a beating.
- **Fear:** Being found out in the tap-room, and his mother being turned out.
- **Contradiction:** He eats Nicolaus's bread and repeats Nicolaus's customers' jokes to a stranger.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has never reported anything about the van Wismar family itself, and tells himself that makes it clean.

## Work and money
He earns no wage, only bed, bread, two shirts a year and a term-end suit worth perhaps 2 marks. The stranger's money does not reach him; it goes to his mother's landlord in Riga. A bad month is when the master hears he has been slow with a delivery.

## Faction and belief
Livonian Order, a coerced dependent. Not belief but rent: the Order's man holds his mother's roof. He would repeat a name for a small favour, hesitate over a large one, and refuse to name the van Wismars. He prays his Paternoster fast and leaves a crust under the cellar stair.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German only; nasal, careful with elders.
- **Delivery:** Nasal and slow; repeats the last word of a question before answering.
- **Sample lines:** "Aye, master." "I only carry it." "Ik weet nicht." (I do not know.)
- **Verbal tic:** Sniffs before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who drinks too much, who curses the council and who owes the master. He would trade it for news of his mother. He believes the false rumour that the Order's knights will arrive by Easter week.
