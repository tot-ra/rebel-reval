## Biography
Born 1297 in a village near Rae, the daughter of a miller named Jaan; she came to Reval at sixteen to sell wool and met Andres at the market in 1320. They married at St Olaf's in 1321. She bore four children; Ursula and Susanna lived, and two died in infancy in the hard winters of 1331 and 1335.

The bad winter of 1341 nearly emptied their store. She spun for pay at night, and the thread bought flour; she has not forgotten the price.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A year without hunger and a good marriage for Ursula.
- **Fear:** A cold winter, and seeing Susanna cough.
- **Contradiction:** She corrects everybody and cannot bear to be corrected.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She spins for two merchants at half rate to repay a debt she has not told her husband.

## Work and money
Her thread brings about eight marks a year. She buys flax at the market by the lispfund and wool from a Harju carter. She owes two marks to a wool-seller. A bad month is a shortage of flax.

## Faction and belief
No faction. For a small favour she gives thread; for a large one a night's spinning; she would not inform. She leaves a bit of wool on the windowsill on St John's Eve and hears mass at the friary.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who pays on time and who pays in promises. She would trade it for cheaper flax. She believes the false rumour that thread spun on a Friday will not bleach.
