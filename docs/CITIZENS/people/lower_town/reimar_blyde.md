## Biography
Born in 1326, eldest of the Blyde children. He studied Latin with the Dominicans and went to Lübeck for a season in 1341. He now keeps his father's grain book and runs errands to the quay.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To prove he deserves the seat he will inherit.
- **Fear:** Failing before his father.
- **Contradiction:** Poised in public, anxious in private.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows his father's debts and has said nothing.

## Faction and belief
Sympathy for the Hansa is inherited, not reasoned. He would pass a note, hold a package, inform when asked by his father. Devout, tidy.
