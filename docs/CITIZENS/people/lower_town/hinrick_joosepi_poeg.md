## Biography
Hinrick was born in 1295 at Nõva on the west coast, the son of a Joosep who hired out as a boatman. At fifteen he took a berth at Reval, learned Low German from the crew and has been on the Baltic since. In 1322 a galley fire on a Gotland cog burned his hand to the bone while he saved the skipper's strongbox; the skipper gave him a mark and a shrug. The ship that carried him in 1333 sank in a storm off Hiiumaa and he swam to the shore. He has no family; he has never been asked to name one.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A winter berth under a roof that does not leak, and to die dry.

- **Contradiction:** Quiet and uncomplaining, yet fond of an old quarrel about wages.

## Work and money

## Faction and belief
No faction. He has seen enough burning to wish no one a rising. He would carry water for a fire and would not inform. He prays to St Nicholas.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the galley stoves of ten ships and which are unsafe. He would trade it for a dry berth. He believes the false rumour that salt thrown on a galley fire puts it out.
