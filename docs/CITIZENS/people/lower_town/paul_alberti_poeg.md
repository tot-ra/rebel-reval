## Biography
Born in a cold spring, Paul was small and stayed small. His mother fed him extra curds. He learned to shout on the quay, where gulls steal herring, and his hoarse voice is the proof. He has never been inside the Rathaus and does not wish to.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To beat the gander, and to eat the egg that Ursula hides.
- **Fear:** The bell of St Olaf's when it tolls for the dead.
- **Contradiction:** Smallest but loudest.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has fed the gander a sweet turnip so it will like him.
