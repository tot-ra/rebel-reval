## Biography
Born in 1313 in a Harju village near the coast, daughter of a shepherd named Jaakob; the wind there left her face permanently red. A cousin brought her to town at fifteen to scrub in a brewery, then she was taken into the Viru house by Gesa Siimu tütar after a fever swept through the earlier maids. She has had a child, a girl, stillborn in 1336, which she never mentions. For fifteen years she has done the linen, the washing and the upper rooms, and she believes the house depends on her and says so to nobody.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A tub, a yard and a line of her own, as a laundress for hire.
- **Fear:** Being turned out when she is forty and the master is poor.
- **Contradiction:** She hates waste and secretly takes the spoiled end-pieces of linen to sell.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has been learning the shape of the letters of her name from the carved board on the church door.

## Work and money
She earns a mark and a half a year, board, and a pair of shoes. Her sale of linen scraps brings about six pfennigs a quarter. She owns a tin spoon, the slicker, a prayer-bead string and a fur-lined mitten.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A rising would burn the linen she has counted for fifteen years, so she wants the town quiet. A small favour she does; a large one she does only for the mistress; she would not inform. She hangs a rowan twig above the linen chest and says her Paternoster at the church door while tracing the board with a finger.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with the household; a few dry Low German phrases for guests.
- **Delivery:** High and quick; orders in threes, then silence.
- **Sample lines:** "Sheet, pillow, napkin: go!" "Ära puutu, do not touch." "One missing; I will find it."
- **Verbal tic:** Counts on her fingers, aloud, in Estonian.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the state of every shirt in the house, and so who has been ill or in love. She would trade it for a place of her own. She believes, wrongly, that the master has a hidden chest of gold behind the cellar wall.
