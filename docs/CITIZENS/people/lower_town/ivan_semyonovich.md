## Biography
Born 1318 in Novgorod, a fuller's son. He came to Reval with a Novgorod convoy in 1337 and stayed because the pay was steady. He has been journeyman to Stepan since 1340. His tremor began after he nearly drowned in a felt-tub accident.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry and open his own shop on Vene.
- **Fear:** Losing his steadiness, and his trade.
- **Contradiction:** Placid at work, quick to fight in drink.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Carries messages to Marfa Ontsiforovna in the lining of a cap.
