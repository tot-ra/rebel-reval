## Biography
Born in 1327 in Hervorde in Westphalia, the fourth son of a miller, Hermann was sent to Reval at twelve in a cloth-bale to learn the Hanse trade. Two masters passed him on: one for his left hand, one for his mouth. He joined the Westfal household at Martinmas 1340. He learned the town on his feet: which smiths pay boys, which bakers give a heel of bread for a message, and which lanes to avoid after curfew. He has not seen Hervorde since, and sometimes forgets the sound of the water-mill. He sleeps in the shared apprentices' loft.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be taken on as a journeyman before he turns eighteen and shaves daily.
- **Fear:** Being returned to the mill.
- **Contradiction:** He is loud and cheeky to everyone but the person whose opinion he most cares for, Kersten Corte, whom he watches in silence.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has been trading small stolen nails for sweet buns with a smith's boy.

## Work and money
He earns board, bed and a pair of shoes at Michaelmas; a master's silver fund for his first year as a journeyman waits, 4 marks, in the master's chest. His own purse holds 3 pfennigs. Bad months bring beatings for spilled ink.

## Faction and belief
He has no faction; a boy of his rank has no say. A small favour for coin; a large one he would run from. He prays to St Nicholas for a journeyman's belt and spits for luck at the Dunkri pump.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German, loud and rough, with a Westphalian drawl in the vowels.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, fast and slightly too loud; he cannot whisper.
- **Sample lines:** "Master says six, I say seven nails." "I never touched it." "Dat is nix för mi." (That's nothing to do with me.)

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every short cut and every shouting smith on Dunkri and Voorimehe. He would trade this for a bun and a warm corner. He believes the false rumour that smiths forge a spear a day for the Order.
