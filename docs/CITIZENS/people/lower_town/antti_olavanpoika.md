## At a glance
- A voice that rattles the stable rafters in a frame that looks too slight for it.
- Understands about forty words of German and uses them all with confidence.
- Known for carrying double loads and humming over them.
- Oddity: speaks only Finnish; the house communicates with him by gesture and by Telseke.

## Biography
Born on the Finnish coast near Porvoo in 1319; he shipped as a deck-hand at sixteen and was left in Reval by a captain in 1339 over a debt. [Gottschalk van Deventer](./gottschalk_van_deventer.md) paid it and took him on. He sleeps over the stable and sings in Finnish at night.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To work off the debt, then ship home.
- **Fear:** Being mocked for his tongue, and being beaten for what he misunderstands.
- **Contradiction:** A gentle man with a loud voice.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He understands far more German than he shows.

## Faction and belief
No faction; he cannot follow the talk. Small favours yes, large no, informing impossible. He prays in Finnish and leaves the first spoon of porridge for a forest spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish only; a handful of German nouns.
- **Delivery:** Booming, slow, cheerful.
- **Sample lines:** "Hyvä, hyvä!" "Horse good. Master good." "Ei hätää."
- **Verbal tic:** Booms "Ja!" at everything.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the horses' habits. He believes the false rumour that Finns will be barred from the quay.
