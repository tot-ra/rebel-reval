## Biography
Born in 1303 in a Harju village and brought to Reval at eight by his widowed mother Kaie, who brewed and sold ale under a council licence, Toomas learned the trade at her side. When she died in 1331 he kept the licence and the word "alewife". He married Lutsia Everti tütar in 1322 and has eight living children to feed. The roof fell in during the January gale of 1342, and he borrowed twenty-four marks from a German shoemaker to replace it. He is generous with other people's quarrels and tight with his own.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the roof debt by Michaelmas and keep the house.
- **Fear:** Seizure of the brewing-vats, and being thrown back into labour at the quay.
- **Contradiction:** He is steady in a crisis and cannot bring himself to ask Hildebrand van Campen for money owed.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has already watered the small beer twice this spring and fears the lane has noticed.

## Work and money
He boils three batches a week and sells small beer at a penny the cup. Malt comes from a Harju miller; hops, such as can be had, from a German chandler. Takings are about a mark and a half a week, of which half goes to malt. He owes twenty-four marks to the shoemaker Albert van Paderborne and has paid about twelve; the bond falls due at Michaelmas. He stood surety for Hildebrand van Campen's twelve-mark fine and has seen none of it back. A bad month is a spoiled batch.

## Faction and belief
He belongs to no party; he sells to all and remembers nothing. A small favour he gives in ale; a large one he refuses; he would inform on a thief and on no one else. He keeps both Mass and the old way, pouring a splash of the first brew to the hearth-spirit before every batch.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who drinks too much and who is late with rent in every lane. He would trade it for time on the debt. He believes, falsely, that the council means to tax every brew-copper from midsummer.
