- Her German carries an Estonian lilt that masters find charming and mistresses do not.

## Biography
Born 1316 in Reval, an Estonian porter's daughter in the lane behind St Olaf. She went into service at fourteen and has served here since 1337. She has a quick tongue and a steady eye for a bargain. The market is where she is herself; the house is where she is useful. She sings in Estonian under her breath and masters pretend not to hear.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hold her own stall at the Sunday market.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to her father's lane.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet in the hall, ferocious at a stall.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has saved a half-mark in a stocking beneath the loft.

## Faction and belief
None; she has a stall to win. A small favour she gives; a large one she trades; informing she shrugs off. She keeps St Anne's day.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at heart; Middle Low German with a sing-song Estonian cadence under the words.
- **Delivery:** Quick, melodic, rising at the end of sentences.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the price of every egg on Pikk. She would trade it for a stall. She believes a left-handed girl is lucky at the market, which is half true.
