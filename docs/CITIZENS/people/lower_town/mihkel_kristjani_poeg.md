## Biography
Born 1312 near Rae to a farmer, Kristjan, who sent him into Reval in 1329 when the manor took the second son for labour; he walked in with a pack and found work as a porter at the harbour. He was hired by the Stenhus house in 1334 to carry wax and rye, and he has been there since.

In 1340 a sack of wax fell from a loft and broke his left ring fingernail; he blames the cleft chin for an unlucky face. He lives in the warehouse loft and eats in the kitchen.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To eat well and to be asked to sing at the Easter feast.
- **Fear:** That his strength will go and he will be sent back to a manor.
- **Contradiction:** He is a loud man who hates a quarrel and will carry any load to avoid one.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Some nights he carries sacks from the cellar to a covered cart and believes it is a charity for the friary; he has never asked.

## Work and money
Wage: board and twelve öre a quarter. He spends it on candle ends, onions and a new belt. He owes a harbour tavern one örtug. A bad month is a stomach-ache from bad herring.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He has never had a quarrel with the Rat or the crown and does not want one. For a small favour he lifts a load; for a large one he asks the master; he would not inform, being sure he knows nothing. He prays at St Olaf's and leaves bread on the wall for the house snake, as his mother did.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian in the yard and at church; German to the family.
- **Delivery:** Booming, even in a whisper; people shush him and he cannot help it.
- **Sample lines:** "A load is a load!" / "I did not hear anything, I swear it!" / "Mis sa ütled? Räägi valjemini." (What do you say? Speak louder.)
- **Verbal tic:** Laughs before the end of his own sentence.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows what is in every sack and which carter is late. He would trade it for a pair of boots. He believes the false rumour that the Dominicans have a ghost in their cellar who counts coins.
