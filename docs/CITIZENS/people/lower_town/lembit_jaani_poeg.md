## Biography
Born in 1333, the second son of Jaan and Triin. He sang in the lane at six and a passing priest told his mother he had a choirboy's gift, which made her proud and anxious. He runs errands to the smith and the tavern and has learned every back lane between Börsi käik and the harbour.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To sing at St Olaf's.
- **Fear:** Geese and the dark loft.
- **Contradiction:** Easy and brave in song, trembling at a goose.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once heard two men on the quay planning a night raid and told no one.
