## Biography
Born 1298 in Revel to a tanner of the Lowergerver house; apprenticed to master Dethard in Laboratooriumi with Gunnar Larsson, who later trained under the same master after him. He married Gerburg van Soest in 1327 and took her father's yard on Vaimu. He has six living children and a seventh in the cradle. His best journeyman, an Estonian, will never be a master under the guild's rules; Tyde thinks this foolish and keeps it to himself.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Ludolf apprenticed to a good master and Ermelin well married.
- **Fear:** A bad hide year, and his pledge-debts being called in by the Rat.
- **Contradiction:** He is a hard creditor and a soft father.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He pays his Estonian journeyman a mark above the guild rate in secret.

## Work and money
He buys hides from butchers, bark from the hinterland and lime from burners, and sells to shoemakers and saddlers. Income about 12 marks a month in good weather. He holds Uku Laurentsi poeg's tools for 24 marks and owes Gerdt Pauli poeg 6 marks. A bad month is a drowned bark shipment.

## Faction and belief
Core Hanseatic: his trade, his credit and his seat in the St Canute fellowship depend on the Rat. He is no rebel; his sympathy is for reform of the guild rules. Small favour: a hide; large: he asks the Rat's leave; informing: he would, with regret. St Nicholas's parish, and a bark-charm.

## Voice

- **Delivery:** Clear, carrying, genial.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which shoemakers cheat on hides and which councillors owe bark. He would trade it for a place for his journeyman. He believes the false rumour that the Rat will ban tanneries within the walls.
