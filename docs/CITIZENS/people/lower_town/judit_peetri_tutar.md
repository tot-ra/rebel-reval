## Biography
Born in 1326 in a fishing village near Viimsi, the daughter of a cooper named Peeter, Judit was sent to Reval at fourteen when her mother died. The nick in her ear came from a gull's beak at twelve; she says a goose. The cold sore came the winter she arrived, and left its scar. Rein took her in at Michaelmas 1340 because Marga needed a laugh in the house. She sleeps in the bath-house loft with Mari.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband with a boat, and a laugh at the end of every day.
- **Fear:** The loft in winter and a scar that will not fade.
- **Contradiction:** She is the loudest in the house and the least able to keep a secret that matters.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sweet-talks the sexton Olev for stub-ends and has told no one how much she likes him.

## Work and money
Wage is 1 mark a year with board; she has 6 pfennigs. She buys candle ends at a halfpenny and resells at one pfennig. A bad month is a missing bag.
