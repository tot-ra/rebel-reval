## Biography
Born in 1317 to a Harju tenant, she came to Reval at twelve. She has carried water for Gerdt's house for fourteen years, and her shoulders have gone crooked. She saved for a new yoke of her own and now carries it with pride. Last Easter week she began walking home from the church door with Rein Laurentsi poeg, the bathkeeper on Harju, a widower of fifty-three, and they talk about water.

## Motivation
