## Biography
Born 1318 in Lübeck, the son of a poor notary, and sent to Reval at sixteen with a recommendation to the Kindergilde. He served two masters before Borchard, learning that a merchant's clerk hears more than a councillor. A friend's funeral in 1341 drew him into the whisper. He believes order is cheaper than chaos, and he counts his loyalty in those terms.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A clerk's seat that leads to partnership.
- **Fear:** Exposure as a go-between.
- **Contradiction:** Fond of order, yet quietly pleased by secrecy.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He carries a sealed note to a Müürivahe craftsman each month.

## Faction and belief
Active Hanseatic: he sees the guilds as the only structure that pays clerks. A small favour he does, a large one he weighs, and informing he does as a matter of habit. Church-going, with a rosary he does not trust.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German; Latin in company.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, quick, tidy.
- **Sample lines:** "I saw nothing, naturally." / "Silence is a coin, too." / "Gode nacht, friend."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who attends which funerals. He would trade it for a promotion. He believes the Order will not come to Reval, which is false.
