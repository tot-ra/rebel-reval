## Biography
Born 1322 in the house on Kuninga. Smallpox at six left her scars. She was taught the old charms by her grandmother's friend Ell of the Harju road, who recruited her a year ago with a small kindness.

- **Fear:** The priest finding the charms.
- **Contradiction:** Devout at mass, devout at the grove.
