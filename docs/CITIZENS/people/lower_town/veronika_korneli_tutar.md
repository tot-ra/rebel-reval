## At a glance
- Runs the household purse, the maids and the larder; Lauri builds, she decides.
- Spins at every idle moment, even walking, with a distaff tucked in her belt.
- A rough, low voice that startles visitors who expect something finer from a Pikk housewife.
- Stays out of politics deliberately, because she suspects what her husband does at night and would rather not be told.

## Biography
Born 1310 in Reval, daughter of the cooper Kornelius (Korneli), who sold barrels to the harbour and died of a fever in the autumn of 1328. She spun for a Lai draper to feed her mother, and married Lauri in 1329 with a dowry of a bed, a loom frame and nine marks. She has borne him five children and buried two, and counts them all. She taught herself numerals from her father's tally boards and keeps the household accounts better than the man who frames roofs. Of late the house has grown to ten people and she has learned to be firm; at night she lies awake and listens to Lauri come in at odd hours and asks nothing.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A full pantry through May and a good apprenticeship for Laurents.
- **Fear:** Hunger in the lean weeks, and the knock at the door she cannot name.
- **Contradiction:** Sharp with maids over a wasted crust, yet slips an old man at the church door a pat of butter on fast days.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has found marks of a stranger's cut in Lauri's cloak lining and has said nothing.

## Work and money
She spins for a draper, about 14 pfennig a day in work and perhaps 3 marks a month together, and holds the household's purse. A bad month is a missing wage: with ten at table, rye at a rising price eats the margin. She owes the grocer 2 marks and will not say so to Lauri.

## Faction and belief
None. She wants no part of any side, and has reasoned that anyone who changes the town raises the price of rye. For a small favour she shares bread; for a large one she says she must ask Lauri, and does not; informing she will not do. Devout in a practical way, with a rowan sprig over the cradle against the evil eye.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian in the house, careful Middle Low German to the draper.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, low, brisk; bites her thumbnail between sentences.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the price of every ware on Pikk and which household is behind on its rent. She would trade it for a better bargain at the fish stalls. She believes the Dominicans bury the poor in lime to hide the plague, which is false.
