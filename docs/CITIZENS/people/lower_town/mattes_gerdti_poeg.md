## Biography
Born in 1331 in the house on Müürivahe, Mattes learned his numerals from his mother's tally-sticks. His father has put a plane into his hand this winter. His great-aunt Alit taught him to plait a bast rope. His younger brother Siim, eight, follows him everywhere. The goose in the yard has bitten him twice.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed on the roof frame and to hear the choir sing in the loft.
- **Fear:** The night watch at the gate and the dark of the cellar.
- **Contradiction:** He is brave with the goose and a coward at night.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He hides his lead pencil in a knot-hole in the yard fence.
