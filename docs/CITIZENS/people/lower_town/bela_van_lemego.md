## Biography
Bela was born in Lemgo in 1278 and came to Reval with her father, a cheese-trader, at fifteen. She married Tideman in 1304 and bore six children, of whom none live in the house. She keeps two cows on a rented meadow outside the Viru gate and sells butter and cheese at the market. In the winter of 1341 she and a young maid from Lai kept each other alive on a pot of whey.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see her cheese ring in the Dominican refectory.

- **Contradiction:** Booming and bossy, but tender with sick animals.
