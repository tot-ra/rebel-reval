## Biography
Born in 1306 at Rannamõisa on the coast to Albert, a boatman. Came to town at sixteen, was taken into Siim's kitchen at twenty-eight, and has been there since. The scar is from a flung tankard in a brawl in 1336. She brought her younger sister Marga into the house in 1338.
