## At a glance
- A gaunt, six-foot clerk with a full dark beard and a pale scar on the upper lip.
- Known for his accuracy and for a voice thin as a reed pipe.
- Oddity: he writes the warehouse's second ledger from memory and burns the working notes.

## Biography
Born 1317 in Reval to an Estonian cooper called Kauri, who paid for four winters at the cathedral school. He learned Latin and counting from a Danish deacon and was taken on by Lambrecht Stenhus in 1337 after a trial in which he found a four-mark error in the warehouse tally. He has been there since, sleeping in a closet off the strongroom.

He is quiet and slow to trust. His father died in 1340 of a lung sickness and left him a set of cooper's tools he cannot use and cannot sell.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A house of his own with a desk that does not belong to someone else.
- **Fear:** A fever, and the night the warehouse is searched.
- **Contradiction:** He hates secrets and keeps a second ledger that no one is supposed to see.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has quietly noted which sacks go out at night, and by whose order, and has told nobody.

## Work and money
Wage: board, closet and twenty öre a quarter. He earns a few pfennig on the side copying letters. He owes nothing and saves what he can. A bad month is a lost page.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He believes in a clean account and fears a dirty one. For a small favour he lends a quill; for a large one he would hesitate; he would not inform without being certain of the facts. He prays at St Olaf's and keeps his father's cooper's mallet over the closet door.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at church and with servants; German with the master; Latin with the friars.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy, careful; each sentence finishes on a rising note.
- **Sample lines:** "Forty-one sacks, not forty." / "I wrote what I saw." / "Ma ei räägi, ma kirjutan." (I do not speak, I write.)
- **Verbal tic:** Touches the scar on his lip before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows what comes in and goes out of the warehouse by night. He would trade it for a place as clerk to the Rat. He believes the false rumour that the warehouse is cursed by a dead mason.
