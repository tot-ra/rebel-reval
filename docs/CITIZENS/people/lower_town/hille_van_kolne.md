## Biography
Hille was born in Cologne in 1295, the daughter of a spinner, and came to Reval in 1317 with her widowed mother. The crescent scar is from a falling door at six; the shoulders from carrying water as a girl. She married Lutke in 1322 and put her savings into the shop. She had three children who lived; Heilwig, Gyse and Yda. A fourth, a boy, died in the winter of 1331. She learned numerals from her father's tally-sticks and keeps the accounts in a counting rhyme because Lutke cannot read and she will not be cheated. Now she spins linen thread for sale in the shop doorway, minds the girls, and watches who comes in.

## Faction and belief
She sympathises with the Hanseatic guilds because her family eats at their tables. She would give a nod, not a vow. She keeps the Church and lights a candle at St Nicholas for the girls.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who owes the shop and who spins well. She would trade it for a guarantee of Heilwig's dowry. She believes the council will lower the bread price, which is false.
