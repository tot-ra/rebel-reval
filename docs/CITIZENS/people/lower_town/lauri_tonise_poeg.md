## Biography
Born 1329, the only child to live past two. He has hauled kegs since nine. His father calls him heir; his mother calls him lazy. He knows the taproom talk and does not repeat it, because his mother once cuffed him for it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To run the cellar and add a second room.
- **Fear:** His father's friends at night.
- **Contradiction:** Proud of the taproom, ashamed of the debt.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Has seen what sits under the third barrel.
