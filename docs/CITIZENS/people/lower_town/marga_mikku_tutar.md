## Biography
Marga was born in 1309 in a Harju village and came to town at fifteen after her father died. She served three houses and has been with the Schomakers since 1337. A dog took the rim of her ear when she was seven. She has saved a few marks in a knot in her mattress.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband who is not a drunk, or a mark saved.
- **Fear:** Dismissal in old age.
- **Contradiction:** Thrifty but cannot resist a cheap ribbon.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Sends part of her wage to a sister in Harju.
