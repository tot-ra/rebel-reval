## Biography
Born in 1303 on the Swedish coast near Kalmar, Bengt shipped on a Gotland cog at fifteen. A fall from the yard in 1330 broke his hip and ended the sea; the van Campen house took him on as a carrier and gate-keeper. He is not related to the weaver Bengt Magnusson up the street and the two are always confused at the church door. He has no wife and no letters. He keeps a mussel-shell on a cord that his mother gave him.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A steady berth in one house until he is too old, and burial at St Olaf's.
- **Fear:** Deep water at night, and being turned off without a good word from the master.
- **Contradiction:** He loathes the sea and sleeps better with the wind in the rigging than in a bed.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has twice put the master's strongbox to rights after a dismissed servant tried it, and has never told.

## Work and money
He has board, bed and four marks a year. He keeps one mark in a sock. He has no debts, no property but a chest and a knife. A bad month is a letter lost in the rain.

## Faction and belief
He follows no party; a ship's company taught him to hold his tongue. He would carry a letter without asking; he would refuse to inform for fear of the master. He prays to St Olaf and spits over his left shoulder at a black cat.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish with Margareta and sailors; slow Middle Low German with the house.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses; the household finishes his sentences wrongly.
- **Sample lines:** "The wind... will turn." "I carry it. That is all." "Det blir väl bra."
- **Verbal tic:** "Aye... well."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which cogs are late and which captains drink. He would trade it for a quiet corner. He believes, falsely, that a seagull crying inland means a death in the house.
