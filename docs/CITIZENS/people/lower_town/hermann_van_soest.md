## Biography
Born in 1328 in Soest in Westphalia, the son of a salt-factor's carter, Hermann came to Reval at eleven as a ship's boy on a Lübeck cog, jumped ship at the quay and was taken in by Heyno at Michaelmas 1339. He knows the harbour from the water side: which cogs ride low, which captains are drunk. He caught the cold sore the winter his master's cog was iced in, and the scar stayed. His voice cracked early and never recovered. He sleeps in the apprentices' loft.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To sail on a cog as a clerk's boy, not a carrier.
- **Fear:** The ice-bound quay, and the cold-sore returning in front of customers.
- **Contradiction:** He looks idle and keeps the exact count of every cask unloaded.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a tally of which crews leave the harbour at night, and has not told anyone why.
