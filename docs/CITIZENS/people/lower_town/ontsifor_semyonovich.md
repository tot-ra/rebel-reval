## Biography
Born 1286 in Novgorod, son of Semyon, a candle-maker, and Irina Dmitrievna. His mother moved the family to Reval in 1290 when his father took a post as the Novgorod guests' agent. Ontsifor learned weights from the Hanseatic factors, and has kept the Tolli shop since 1316. He married Olena Timofeyevna in 1328 and has two sons from her. He thinks of himself as a Reval man; the Rat thinks of him as a guest. He is patient to a fault: in 1340 a skipper lost his share in a voyage, and he has not pressed the debt.

## Motivation
- **Want:** For Semyon and Fedor to inherit a shop the Rat cannot close.
- **Fear:** A tax or edict aimed at the Russian guests.
- **Contradiction:** Prides himself on exact weights and forgives debts without a word.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He prays at night to a household icon hidden in the loft, behind the wax racks, because the neighbours mock it.

## Work and money
He buys wax and flax from Novgorod carts, sells to churches, brewers and housewives, and clears about 6 marks a month. He is owed 5 marks by Tilman van Lubeke. A bad month is a closed road to Novgorod.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the Novgorod road's prices and the weights of the Hanseatic factors. He would trade it for a clear title to the shop. He believes the false rumour that the viceroy will expel the Russian guests.
