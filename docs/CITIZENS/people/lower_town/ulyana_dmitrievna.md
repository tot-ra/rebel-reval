## Biography
Born 1325 in the Lai cellar, the second child of Dmitri and Irina. She has spun since seven and sells her yarn to a weaver. Her sister was married in Pskov, and the dowry debt sits in her mind like a stone. Her grievance is that her family pays interest to Germans for a marriage in her own country.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry in Pskov and see its gates.
- **Fear:** Being married off to a German's clerk to clear the debt.
- **Contradiction:** Hates the German street and spins for it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has promised to keep an old woman's messages.
