## Biography
Born in 1336 in a hard winter, the second son, and named for his father by Elisabet. He sang before he spoke, and the voice that came was a baritone that startled the priest. The neighbours say it comes from his grandmother's side. He is not expected to bake; he is expected to sing in the choir school one day, if the fees can be found. For now, he minds the infant Henn and stirs the honey pot.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** Being forgotten when Henn cries.
