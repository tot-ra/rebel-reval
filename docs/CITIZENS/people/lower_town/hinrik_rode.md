## Biography
Born in Reval in 1330, the son of a poor German cooper who died of fever. Placed with Gavril by the St Nicholas almoner at twelve. He sleeps in the loft and eats Russian cabbage soup.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To learn silver.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to the cooperage.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to a master who is a foreigner.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has seen the master leave and told no one.
