## Biography
Born 1306 in a farm near Rebala, daughter of Gerdt, a freeman. At sixteen she came to Reval and learned cooking in a merchant's kitchen. The burn came in 1325 from an overturned pot of fat. She joined Hartwig's household in 1333 and runs its kitchen. A year ago Dietrich van Wismar, a retired craftsman on Rataskaevu, gave her a salve for the hand; Eylard van Goslar wrote a petition that freed her brother from a fine. Since then both have asked small things of her.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the kitchen quiet and the household fed.
- **Fear:** Fire in the thatch, and the town burning.
- **Contradiction:** An Estonian who prefers German order and distrusts German men.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She writes nothing, but remembers every guest's order.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic sympathiser because the house feeds her and the town's calm keeps her kitchen. She would carry a message, taste a poison, but not name a name. She keeps the old rites: a bread-crust on the hearth for the house spirit.
