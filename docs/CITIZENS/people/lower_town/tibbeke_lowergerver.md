## Biography
Born 1331 on Vaimu. She learned numerals from her father at six and found them easier than thread. She is the one who notices when the tally is short. She is not yet sure what she will be, and neither are her parents.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the tannery's accounts herself.
- **Fear:** Being told that girls do not.
- **Contradiction:** She is exact with sums and loses her gloves.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She found a mistake in her father's tally and has not told him, to see if he finds it.
