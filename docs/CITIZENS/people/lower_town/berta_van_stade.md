- A tall, sturdy woman of fifty-six with sandy-grey hair and a clear, carrying voice.
- Second wife of Dietrich Zierenberg and stepmother in all but name to three wards.

- Oddity: the loudest woman on Lai is also the one who never gossips.

## Biography
Born 1287 in Stade on the Elbe, a shipwright's daughter, brought to Reval in 1329 as second wife to the widower [Dietrich Zierenberg](dietrich_zierenberg.md). She had lost a husband and two infants before; she took the three wards and ten servants as a charge. She spins for the church, bargains for the household, and has never borrowed a penny.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see the three wards well settled before she dies.
- **Fear:** Her husband's unspoken worries and the winter of 1342 coming again.
- **Contradiction:** A woman of strict order who feeds half the lane.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sells a little of her own thread to pay for the Hospital's bread.

## Work and money
She runs a household budget near 120 marks a year. Grain comes by the last from the Harju mills, wax by the pfund. A bad month is a late ship.

## Faction and belief
None; the house is her cause. She would do a small favour, make a large one a matter for her husband, and never inform. She keeps St Nicholas's feast with a loaf at the door.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German with servants and neighbours.
- **Delivery:** Clear and carrying; each word pronounced.
- **Sample lines:** "Grete, the pot!" / "Eat, you are thin." / "It is not my business."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who in the household is fed or hungry. She would trade it for her wards' marriages. She falsely believes the harbour water brings fever.
