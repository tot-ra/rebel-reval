## Biography
Drude was born in 1329, the second child of [Wolter van Hamelen](../../people/lower_town/wolter_van_hamelen.md) and [Gyse van Goslar](../../people/lower_town/gyse_van_goslar.md), and nursed by an Estonian girl called Liis, who sang to her and taught her Estonian words; Liis died of fever in 1340. Drude kept the cadence and has never lost it. She learned her letters from the parish clerk but only the numerals stayed. Her mother has set her in the kitchen, the linen-room and the spinning chamber so that she will not marry ignorant. Last Michaelmas she began to wear her cap low.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be sent to a convent school at St Michael's, where there is peace.
- **Fear:** A match arranged by her father's debts.
- **Contradiction:** She wants to leave the house yet loves the scullery best.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows Estonian songs and sings them to the baby when no one listens.

## Work and money
She has no income but a penny at Easter and her keep. She helps with the thread-house and is learning to count outwork by the pfund. A bad month is a scolding.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A girl of fourteen has no say and few plans. For a small favour she would carry a message; for a large one she would tell her mother; to inform she would cry. She says her prayers to St Catherine and keeps a lock of Liis's hair in a locket.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German with Estonian music; Estonian words when she sings.
- **Delivery:** Sing-song, rising and falling like a lullaby.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the songs the Estonian maids sing, and which servants meet in the yard. She would trade it for a place in the convent. She believes, wrongly, that the nuns of St Michael never eat meat.
