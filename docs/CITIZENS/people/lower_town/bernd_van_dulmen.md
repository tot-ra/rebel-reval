## At a glance
- Wears his forked beard like a badge, trimmed every Saturday with borrowed shears.
- Reads prices in the street like other men read faces.
- Known for a nasal drawl that makes every opinion sound like a complaint.
- Oddity: a resident without burgher rights, so no faction because he cannot yet afford to be anyone's man.

## Biography
Born in Dülmen in Westphalia in 1321, son of a cloth-seller who died of fever. At sixteen he travelled to Lübeck, learned figures there, and shipped to Reval on a salt hulk in 1340. He lodges with [Meinhard Wise](./meinhard_wise.md) as journeyman, under a contract for three more years. He hopes to marry a burgher's daughter and buy the rights; at present he has nothing but his hand and his tongue.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Burgher status and a house with his own name over the door.
- **Fear:** Poverty like his father's, and the shame of a sale gone wrong.
- **Contradiction:** Scornful of Estonians and servants, yet envies [Uku Mihkli poeg](./uku_mihkli_poeg.md)'s better memory.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a private list of prices in a little wax tablet and sells advance notice of them to a Pikk grocer.

## Work and money
Wage 4 marks a year plus board and clothing. He sells price news for a few pfennigs a week. He owes his Lübeck tailor 2 marks. A bad month is a master who demands his silver for a damaged bale.

## Faction and belief
No faction; a journeyman who backs the wrong side loses his contract. Small favour yes, large no, informing for a fee he might consider. He keeps the Church's fasts and prays to Saint Nicholas for good sales.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German; Latin for contracts.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, drawn out, with a sigh before every price.
- **Sample lines:** "Nay, the price is the price." "I could have told you, had you asked." "Three schillings, not a pfennig less."
- **Verbal tic:** "As I said."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the day's prices to the pfennig. He would trade them for a good word with a burgher's daughter. He believes the false rumour that salt will soon be forbidden at the gate.
