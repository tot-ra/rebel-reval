## Biography
Born 1286 in Pskov, son of a cap-maker; he came to Reval in 1318 on a merchant's convoy and stayed after his first wife died. The scar is from a Pskov fight over felt in 1306. He remarried Marfa in 1324. He has taught caps to three journeymen and lent grain to half the street after the harvest failed in 1341. Pskov men pass through his house, and he feeds them without asking.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep his house and trade independent of the guilds.
- **Fear:** Hanseatic restrictions on Russian guests.
- **Contradiction:** Cautious in speech, generous with debt.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Carries messages from Pskov in the lining of caps.

Caps sell at 3 örtug; fur-trimmed ones at 2 marks. Felt comes from Novgorod traders. He has 6 marks out to Jaak and stands surety for 12 marks for Nicolaus.

## Faction and belief
A sympathiser of Pskov, not a spy. His own reason is that his sons' graves are in Pskov. He would hold a letter, never inform on a neighbour. Orthodox, with a lamp before the icon.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Russian at home, careful Low German in trade.
- **Delivery:** Slow, long pauses.
- **Sample lines:** "I do not name interest. I name what is owed." "Wait. Think. Then speak." "Slava Bogu."
- **Verbal tic:** Strokes his beard before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who is behind with which debt and which houses take Pskov guests. Would trade it for the Rat's tolerance. Believes the Danes will expel Russian guests at midsummer.
