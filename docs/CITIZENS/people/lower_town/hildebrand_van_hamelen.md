## Biography
Born 1333 in the house on Lai. His father's tally taught him numbers before his mother taught him prayers; Hildebrand learned the notches. His grandfather Berend carved him a toy cart, and the boy wears the wheel round his neck on a string. Last autumn he began carrying water, and that is where the cat began.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to stand in the gate arch beside his father.
- **Fear:** The cat, then the dark cellar, then the day his father is not home.
- **Contradiction:** Hates being sent to the well, yet lingers there to listen.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows his father cried once at the gate and told no one.
