## Biography
Born in 1261 in a fishing hamlet on the shore east of the harbour, the daughter of a fisherman called Kauri, Lutsia saw the Danes come back in 1285 and the first stone houses go up. She married Laurentsi, a fisher, and bore Rein in 1290; he drowned in 1305. She combed wool for the Harju weavers for fifty years. She was baptised young, and keeps her old prayers beside the new. She came to live with Rein and Marga after her knees gave way, and now lives in the kitchen, in the warm.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Hele grown tall enough to carry the water pail.

- **Contradiction:** She forgets the day of the week and remembers every storm since 1280.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sings a drowning-charm for fishers at the shore each spring, and does not call it a prayer.

## Work and money
She has no income; Rein supports her, and she sells a combed fleece to a weaver for 1 schilling each month. She owns a comb, a shawl and horn spoons. A bad month is a cold stove.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction; she remembers what the last ones cost. A small favour, yes; a large one, no. She puts a pinch of salt on the hearth and crosses herself in the same motion.
