## Biography
Born 1289 in Pskov, daughter of Timofei, a flax-dealer. Married Ontsifor Semyonovich in 1328 after the death of her first suitor, who drowned in the Velikaya. Came to Reval with a dowry of linen and a loom, and has two sons. Her brother was fined in Narva in 1339 for trading outside the Hanse quay, and she has not forgotten. She spins for Hanseatic mercers and carries news both ways; nobody has asked her to, she simply listens.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see her sons safe in a Reval that does not treat them as guests.
- **Fear:** The shop closed by decree.
- **Contradiction:** She dislikes the Hanse and spins for their wives.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She passes price and levy news to a Novgorod clerk; she is not sure why she began.

## Work and money
Flax from Novgorod carts, thread sold to mercers' wives at 2 öre the skein. About 3 marks a month. A bad month is a bad flax year.

## Faction and belief
Active for Pskov and Novgorod: not for gain but because her brother was fined. Small favour: news; large: she would carry a letter; informing: she would deny knowing Russian. Orthodox, with thread knotted against the evil eye.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who is buying flax and who is selling letters. She would trade for her brother's fine. She believes the false rumour that Novgorod will soon send an army.
