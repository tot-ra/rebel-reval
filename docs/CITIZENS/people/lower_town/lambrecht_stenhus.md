## Biography
Born 1298 in Reval, the only son of a wax trader from Lübeck who died of a fever in 1319. He took over a warehouse and a debt and paid it off by 1325. He entered the Rat in 1331. In 1339 the Danish bailiff on Toompea seized a cargo of his wax for a disputed toll; he appealed to the Rat, which refused to back him against the crown. He paid the loss in silence and has never forgotten which councillors voted for the Rat's quiet.

Since that spring he has been dealing off the ledger, buying rye from carters who come in at the Smiths' Gate to avoid the staple. A year ago he began sending a small part of it to a lane where he does not ask what is done with it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To leave his sons a house with no debts and a name nobody can seize.
- **Fear:** That Mikk Simoni poeg's suspicions will become a Rat inquiry.
- **Contradiction:** He is a Hanseatic councillor who profits from the staple and quietly works to see it broken.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** About a lispfund of rye in every five, and some cord and iron, leaves his cellar for the circle, and he has told only Mett Toomase tütar, whom he has never met in daylight.

## Work and money
His house trades wax, rye, cloth and herring. Wax goes to Lübeck, rye to Reval's bakers and cloth from Flanders by Lübeck. Annual turnover about 900 marks; profit roughly 150. He owes Gavril Gavrilovich the moneychanger 40 marks at six pfennig the mark per month. A bad month is a lost cargo or a shuttered gate.

## Faction and belief
He is Hanseatic by trade and loyalty to his sons' future, and a rebel by grievance in a single locked room. His reason is neither love nor faith but a ledger: the Rat refused him in 1339 and he has never forgiven it. For a small favour he lends cord; for a large one he opens the cellar; he would inform on nobody, since he cannot be certain who is who. He hears mass and burns a candle for his father each year.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the staple prices, which ships carry what, and who needs credit. He would trade it for a quiet word with the Kuninga gatekeeper. He believes the false rumour that the Danish viceroy intends to sell Reval to the Order.
