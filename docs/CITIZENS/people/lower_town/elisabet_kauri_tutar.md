## Biography
Born 1320 on a farm near Maardu. Her father, another Kauri, drowned in a ditch in 1334 and her mother sent her to town at fourteen to take any work. She began as a scullery girl for a baker, then moved to the van Hervorde house in 1338 as nursery maid, and stayed. She picked up German in a year. The master trusts her with the pantry keys, which she treats as a vow.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry a carter and have a house of her own with a window.
- **Fear:** Losing the place if the master falls from favour.
- **Contradiction:** Hears everything and repeats nothing, yet is the lane's best gossip.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She slips leftover bread to a family in the lane every Friday.

## Work and money
Pay is 6 schillings a year, board, and two gowns. She sends a schilling to her mother. A bad month is a sick child in the house: she works through the night.

## Faction and belief
None. Servants are used by every faction and thanked by none. A small favour she would do; informing she would not. She keeps Estonian customs at Midsummer, and tells her beads at St Olaf's.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who visits the master after dark. She would trade it for a husband's introduction. She falsely believes the master's insomnia is an Estonian curse.
