## Biography
Born in 1320 in a hamlet near Kose, the daughter of a woodcutter called Matti, Mari came to Reval at fifteen when a landlord took her father's strip. She served at a Pikk alehouse for two years until her mistress sold her indenture to Rein at Candlemas 1338. She learned to fire a stove, to judge steam by ear and to look away when a guest weeps. She sends home 2 pfennigs a month. She lives in the bath-house loft with Judit.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hear a priest's voice read to her in her own tongue.
- **Fear:** A scalding, and being the person the Rat questions.
- **Contradiction:** She is the most discreet in the house and the one with the most to say.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She counts the coins of every dowry-bag left on a bench and has never touched one.

## Work and money

## Faction and belief
She has no faction. A small favour, a word to the chaplain; a large one, no. She says the Pater Noster in Latin she does not understand and in Estonian she does.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with the family; careful German with the chaplain.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, even and slow.
- **Sample lines:** "The stones are ready." "I will not say." "Ma ei ütle." (I will not say.)
- **Verbal tic:** "Even so."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who weeps in the steam. She would trade for a private mass. She believes the false rumour that the chaplain can read hearts.
