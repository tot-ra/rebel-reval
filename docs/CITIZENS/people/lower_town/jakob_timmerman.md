## Biography
Born about 1275 in Reval, the son of a ship's carpenter. He raised half the roofs on Pikk after the fire of 1314, and framed the cellar vaults of three merchants. He married Sibbe Olde in 1298. His elder daughter Gertrud married a Visby skipper at Michaelmas 1341, and the dowry cost him eight marks borrowed from the joiner Jaan Jaani poeg; the marriage went ahead and the debt remains. He is not as fast on a ladder as he was.

## Work and money
He earns about 4 marks a month in season, from roofs, floors and cellar frames. He buys timber from Harju foresters by the stack, sells to burghers on contract, and owes Jaan 8 marks. A bad month is a wet one: no roofs, no pay.

## Faction and belief
None; his customers are on every side. A small favour: a beam. A large one: no. He crosses himself on every roof.
