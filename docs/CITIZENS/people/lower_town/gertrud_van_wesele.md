## Biography
Born in 1282 in Wesel on the Rhine, the daughter of a linen merchant. She married [Tilman van Lubeke](tilman_van_lubeke.md) in 1306 and brought two chests of linen and a stubborn thrift. Of her children, a daughter lives in Stralsund and two sons died in infancy. When the 1330s pushed the house into debt she began to do the washing herself and joined the washerwomen's fraternity at St Nicholas, where she is respected and a little envied. Now she supervises the great wash twice a month.

## Motivation

## Work and money
The wash is done within the house for the clerks, the maids and the cook; she sells surplus tallow soap at the market for pfennigs. The house's debts worry her more than the loss of the wash trade. A bad month means no new thread.

## Faction and belief
None; she stays out of politics because the Rat is her husband's business and her own is the linen. She would not inform. She prays to St Nicholas and leaves lavender in the press.
