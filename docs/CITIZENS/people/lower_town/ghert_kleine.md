## Biography
Born in Reval in 1294, the son of a baker, Ghert married Geseke van Lemego in 1320 and bought the lease of the Dunkri taproom in 1324. He sold ale from his own cellar, watered nobody's drink, and so earned a name for reckoning. The barley field he rented outside Karja gate failed in 1341, and he took grain from Borchard van Groninghe to keep the house fed. Since then he has paid interest he cannot see written. In autumn 1342 he lent Paul Tõlli poeg 5 marks for a roof. He lives over the taproom with his wife, three children, an aunt and a maid.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A cellar full of his own ale and no one's grain in his larder.
- **Fear:** A bond he cannot read being called in front of a witness.
- **Contradiction:** He is stingy with measures and generous with credit, and cannot tell which is which when pressed.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has not paid interest to Borchard for two quarters and has told no one in his house.

## Work and money
He brews with malt and hops from the harbour and sells at about 2 pfennigs a stoop; a good week brings 5 marks. He owes Borchard 3 marks' worth of grain and is owed 5 marks by Paul Tõlli poeg, half of it paid. His tally-board is the only ledger he owns. A bad month is a seized cask.

## Faction and belief
He is a Hanseatic dependent: the Hansa buys the beer. A small favour, a message in a keg; a large one he would refuse. He keeps St Nicholas's feast and rubs the hearth-stone before he lights the fire.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German only.
- **Delivery:** Clipped and precise, with each word set down like a coin.
- **Sample lines:** "Two pfennigs, no more, no less." "I do not forget." "Dat steit in't Holt." (It is cut in the wood.)
- **Verbal tic:** "Exact."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who drinks, who owes and who slips out. He would trade this for time. He believes the false rumour that the Rat will tax ale by the cask.
