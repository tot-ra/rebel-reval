## Biography
Born 1302 on Kuninga, eldest son of Tõnu, a master carpenter who left him the house and the plot. He was gatekeeper at the Long Hill Gate from 1332. He married Ell in 1321 and has ten children, of whom eight live. In 1340 he bought a share in a Visby voyage with the shoemaker Sander van Deventer, and it failed.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To feed a house of ten and see Kornel apprenticed to a master.
- **Fear:** The post lost; his father's stake exposed.
- **Contradiction:** A town servant who keeps a list of the town's grievances.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** His late father moved the boundary stake with the neighbours' yard.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic only as a dependant; his circle is his own. A small favour: yes. A large: after thought. Informing: never. He makes the sign over the gate each dawn.
