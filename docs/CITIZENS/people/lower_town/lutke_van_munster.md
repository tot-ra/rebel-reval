## Biography
Lutke was born in Münster in 1281 but was sent to Reval as an infant with a merchant uncle and nursed by an Estonian woman, from whom he took his accent. He was ordained in 1308 and served in the Danish chapel before moving to St Nicholas in 1320. He lived through the troubles of 1313 to 1325 and buried more than he baptised. His one rare possession is a brass pair of dividers he uses to reckon the date of Easter.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To finish the Easter tables and die in his own bed.
- **Fear:** That he has confessed to the wrong man.
- **Contradiction:** He demands restitution and forgives debts of his own.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Sick at Lent, he confessed to a layman for want of a priest.
