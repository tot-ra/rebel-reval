## Biography
Born in 1331 to the mason Hinrick, who fell from the Viru Gate scaffold in 1339. He remembers the funeral and the plumb-bob. Since then he has worked beside his mother, mixing mortar and carrying hods. The ear was nicked by the goose; the lip is an old fever sore.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To lay a whole course alone.
- **Fear:** Heights, and the goose.
- **Contradiction:** Afraid of high scaffolds, yet he asks for them.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He hid a piece of his father's chisel in the wall of the well.
