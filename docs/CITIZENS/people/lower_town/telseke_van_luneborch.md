## Biography
Born in Lüneburg in 1313, the daughter of [Beke van Luneborch](./beke_van_luneborch.md)'s dead sister. Orphaned at twelve, she came to Reval at eighteen to be employed in her aunt's house. She learned Estonian from the kitchen girls.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A dowry and a respectable match.
- **Fear:** Being a charge on her aunt forever.
- **Contradiction:** Proud of her blood, working like a servant.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She is saving to buy her way into a convent.
