## Biography
Born 1303 at Stockholm to a Swedish father and raised after his father's death among Estonian tanners at Reval, hence the lilt. He trained under the master who later trained Tyde Lowergerver. Last year the council fined him 18 marks for fouling the ditch with lime-liquor; Gottschalk van Deventer stood surety and Gunnar has not repaid. His late wife's mother Karin lives with him.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the 18 marks.
- **Fear:** The surety called in.
- **Contradiction:** Proud of his clean leather, ashamed of the stench.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He still pours the liquor at night.
