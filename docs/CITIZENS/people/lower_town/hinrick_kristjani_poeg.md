## Biography
Born in 1304 in Reval to a carter, Hinrick was bound at eleven to a carpenter on Müürivahe, the same master who later trained Gerdt Jaani poeg. He learned beams, scarf joints and the rafter-compass, and learned to do it all with the left hand while the master tried to beat it out of him. He became a master in 1335 and took over the plot on Müürivahe when his master died childless. In 1342 a Pikk maid named Elin Birgersdotter got him a hospital roof contract at a time when work was thin, and a month later asked a small favour in return. He has carried messages ever since.

## Work and money
He charges 3 to 4 pfennigs a day per journeyman and 1 mark for a small roof, about 10 marks a month in a good season. Oak comes from Harju carters, pine from Finland by sea. He owes the timber dealer 4 marks and is owed 7 by two Pikk households. A bad month is when a client delays and Wendla's laundry earns the bread.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic courier, by arrangement rather than loyalty: the guild houses give him work and he would rather work than starve. He would carry a message as a small favour, hide a man for a night as a large one, and refuse to inform on a neighbour. He prays at St Olaf and pegs a small oak cross into every new roof-tree.

## Voice

- **Verbal tic:** Squints and taps the beam twice before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the state of every roof in the Lower Town. He would trade it for a safe road for the next message. He believes a roof raised on a Friday will burn, which is folk belief.
