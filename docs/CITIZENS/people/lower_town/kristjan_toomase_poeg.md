## Biography
Born in 1311 in Reval, Kristjan went to sea at sixteen on a coaster to Visby and Lübeck, where a deck-fight in 1331 broke his nose and a rope-end opened his chin. He came ashore to marry Kristiina in 1335, bought a tallow-pan from a failing master and joined the lane's trade. His mother Anna Madise tütar lives with him; his father Toomas is dead. He rents a shed from the merchant Johannes van Verden, and his chimney has smoked into the neighbour's loft for two winters.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy his own shed and the next pan, and keep the family fed through the lean spring.
- **Fear:** A fire in the tallow, and a German landlord's rent rise.
- **Contradiction:** He fears the sea he misses, and has never told his wife.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has lent a merchant three marks and has never asked for them back.

## Work and money
He buys mutton and beef suet from the butchers, boils it down and sells tallow dips at a pfennig for two. A good week brings two schillings; the shed rent takes four pfennigs of it. He lent three marks to the merchant Tilman van Lubeke for a dowry and holds the debt unredeemed. A bad month is a shed fire.

## Faction and belief
He has no faction and little use for one. For a small favour he gives candle-ends; for a large one he wants payment; he would not inform. He hears Mass at St Olaf, and drops a pinch of salt in the first pan of the season.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; flat Middle Low German with landlords.
- **Delivery:** Level and monotone, never raised.
- **Sample lines:** "Two for a pfennig." "The chimney draws, in the main." "Ega meri tule pärast."
- **Verbal tic:** "As it is."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which butcher mixes bad fat. He would trade it for a longer lease. He believes, falsely, that tallow made on St Martin's Day burns twice as long.
