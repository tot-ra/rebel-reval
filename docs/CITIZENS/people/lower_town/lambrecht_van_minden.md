## Biography
Born in Reval in 1312, son of a Minden family that came north in the 1280s. At twenty he sailed to Visby as supercargo; a fall on the Gotland wharf broke his shin in 1334 and it healed crooked. He inherited the Lai house and a share in two cogs in 1338. He married [Sibbe Hovesche](./sibbe_hovesche.md), and they have Berta, Ropert and the infant Egbert. He met knights of the Order at Riga in 1340 and has dreamed of their iron discipline since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To make the Lai house the richest in the street and to see Reval ordered and quiet.
- **Fear:** Mobs, and being seen by his peers as a cripple.
- **Contradiction:** A host who weighs everything against his guests; a patrician who hates the Hanseatic guilds' small rules.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has twice dined with a Riga agent at an inn on Pikk and has promised nothing; the agent still writes.

## Work and money
Income about 900 marks in a good year, spread over cloth, herring and grain. He owes the Kindergilde for a share and carries Priidik's fish on credit until quarter-day. A bad month is an unpaid cargo and a stuck ship.

## Faction and belief
His admiration for the Order is of discipline and cleanliness; he does not want a war. A small favour (a letter) he would do; a large one he would weigh against his house; informing he would refuse on the town. Piety: a regular at mass, sceptical of hearth charms.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German, Latin for contracts.
- **Delivery:** Hoarse and loud; softens when lying.
- **Sample lines:** "Sit, sit, eat." "I will pay at quarter-day, as ever." "An ordered town is a rich town."
- **Verbal tic:** Rubs the knee.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the Riga agent's name and what the Order wants from Reval. He would trade it for a monopoly on grain freight. He believes the false rumour that the Order has already sent knights disguised as merchants.
