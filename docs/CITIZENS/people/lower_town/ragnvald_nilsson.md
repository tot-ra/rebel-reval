## Biography
Ragnvald was born in Kalmar in 1323, the son of a Swedish ship-chandler who sent him to Reval with a letter to a merchant at fifteen. He broke his shin at sixteen falling from a ladder on the quay and it set badly. He learned German from the harbour and Latin from a friar. He has served the Schomakers since 1341. His winter in the same Nunne lane as the spinner Magdalena is a quiet bond between them.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough for a stool at a merchant's counting table.
- **Fear:** That his limp will bar him from sea trade.
- **Contradiction:** He is loud-voiced and shy.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He writes letters home to his mother and never sends them.
