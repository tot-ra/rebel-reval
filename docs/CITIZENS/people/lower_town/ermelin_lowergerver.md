## Biography
Born 1328 on Vaimu, eldest of the Lowergerver children. She was taught numerals by her father, spinning by her mother and Estonian by the tannery journeymen's songs. Her father thinks of her marriage; she thinks of the market. In the week after Easter she met Beke van Lemego at the church door and the two began to walk home together.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To choose her own husband, or none.
- **Fear:** Being sent away to a stranger in Lübeck.
- **Contradiction:** She wants freedom and keeps her mother's rules to the letter.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has sung Estonian songs to the journeymen and learned a verse she may not repeat at home.
