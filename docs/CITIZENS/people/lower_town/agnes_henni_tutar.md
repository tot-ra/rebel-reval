## At a glance
- Broad through the shoulders like a boatman, with a thin reedy voice that surprises people.
- Left-handed, and the household has long since stopped telling her to switch.
- Sets the table backwards on purpose and nobody has noticed in two years.
- Hums when she scrubs, always the same four notes.

## Biography
Agnes was born in 1318 on the coast near Kolga, the daughter of Henni, a fisherman. She learned to row, mend nets and gut herring, all with her left hand, which her mother called an old bad habit. She came to Reval at seventeen when a storm took the boat, and took service with a brewer's widow, then with Lutke van Brunswik in 1339. She is the best cook in the house by far, and has not told anyone. Henn Lauri poeg is a countryman, and she speaks Estonian with him at the well.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough for a net and a share in a boat at Kolga.
- **Fear:** Storms, and being sent back to a place with no work.
- **Contradiction:** A strong woman with a thin voice who says little and does everything.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides a net-needle under her mattress, her dead father's.

## Work and money
She earns about 2 marks a year and keep, plus a pair of shoes at Michaelmas. She saves a schilling a month in a clay pot under the hearth. A bad month is a spoiled batch of fish.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A maid with a net to buy has no time for sides. She would feed anyone at the back door and carry a basket for a neighbour. She says her Paternoster in Estonian and leaves a fish-head on the hearthstone for the house-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with Henn and the fishwives; Low German with the household.
- **Delivery:** Thin, reedy and quiet; speaks in short phrases.
- **Sample lines:** "Sit, I'll bring it." "The fish are late." "Mine ei tea." (I do not know.)
- **Verbal tic:** Hums a four-note tune between sentences.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the price of fish and which boats are in. She would trade it for a net-needle. She believes the false rumour that the sea will freeze late this year.
