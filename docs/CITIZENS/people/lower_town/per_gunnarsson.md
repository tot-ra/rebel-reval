## Biography
Born 1330 on a Swedish fisher's boat family; his father Gunnar was lost in a storm and his mother sent him to Ulf at ten. He has served two years and learned to knead and bank the fire. He sings in the loft after prayers and is shushed. He thinks the oven is a sleeping dragon.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A barrow of his own.
- **Fear:** The master's geese, and the dark of the wood store.
- **Contradiction:** Loud, yet creeps like a mouse to the pantry.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He took a honey cake once and still feels it.
