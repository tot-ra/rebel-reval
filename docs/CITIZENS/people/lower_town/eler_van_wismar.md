## Biography
Born about 1331, he was a sickly child fed on goat's milk. At ten he went with his father to the gate and has stayed. He learns the keys by touch and the tallies by notch, since he cannot yet read.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hold the great key.
- **Fear:** The dark arch at night.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet, yet watches all.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has copied a key in wax.
