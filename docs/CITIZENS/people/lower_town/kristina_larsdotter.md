## At a glance
- Sells at the shop window and knows every cook and maid on Pikk by name.
- Hoarse from calling prices over the harbour wind, though she rarely leaves the lane.
- Keeps the account with the Harju miller on notched sticks, quarter-day by quarter-day.
- Has a long memory for who paid and who forgot.

## Biography
Born 1289 in Åbo (Turku), daughter of a ship's chandler named Lars, and brought to Reval in 1311 to keep house for an uncle. She married Ulf in the early 1320s. She worked the bread window from the first week and learned Low German from haggling. She survived the cold winter of 1337-38 with two mouths too many, and the memory marks her thrift. Of her children, [Birgitta](../../ledger/lower_town/pikk.md#hh-lt-osm-w200453920) is the one that works beside her.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see the quarter-day accounts square.
- **Fear:** A bad harvest and a mob at the shutters.
- **Contradiction:** Hard bargainer who cannot refuse a hungry child.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows where Ulf goes at night and has said nothing.

## Work and money
Shop sales yield about 9 marks monthly. The miller takes quarter-day credit. A bad month: the mill delivers late.

## Faction and belief
None; she wants only steady flour. A small favour is a loaf; a large one she negotiates; informing would ruin her. Devout, with a St Lucia candle in December.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who owes. She would trade it for a better flour price. She believes flour sellers cut the sacks with chalk, which is false.
