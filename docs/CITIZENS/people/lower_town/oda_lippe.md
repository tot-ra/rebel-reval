## Biography
Born on Pikk in 1332, the third child. She learned to count with her sister and to shout from the quay. At Candlemas the neighbour's gander bit her calf, and she has plotted revenge ever since: salt, a net, a bucket.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To beat the gander.
- **Fear:** The cellar in the dark.
- **Contradiction:** Brave with geese, afraid of a mouse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a stolen honey-cake in the loft.
