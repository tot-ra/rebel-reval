## Biography
Kaur was born in 1330 in a Munga cottage, son of Jakobi, a porter who died of the lung-fever. He was bound to Mikk at eleven, to learn the grain trade. His elder sister Ann works in the same house as a maid.

He learned to count on his fingers from a market-woman. Mattes the clerk says the boy has a head for numbers; Kaur does not say anything.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To become the one who counts the grain.
- **Fear:** The tally rod and Mattes's ruler.
- **Contradiction:** Prim in speech, a thief of sweet crumbs.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows exactly how many sacks are missing from the cellar.

## Faction and belief
He has no faction; he believes in numbers. He would do a small favour for a roll, never a large one. He crosses himself when he passes the harbour cross.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with Ann; Middle Low German to others, precisely.
- **Delivery:** Clipped, short sentences, exact numbers.
- **Sample lines:** "Forty-one sacks, not forty." / "That is the third time." / "Üks, kaks, kolm."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the cellar's true count. He would trade it for a fresh roll. He believes, wrongly, that sums can be wrong only if someone is lying.
