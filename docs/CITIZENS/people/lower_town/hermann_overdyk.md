## Biography
Hermann was born in 1297 at Kampen in the Netherlands, the son of a skipper trading to the Baltic, and grew up in the Reval run. At fifteen he spent a winter in the harbour with Estonian fishers and learned their tongue. He took command of a small cog at twenty-nine. He keeps the ship, not the politics, and refuses every invitation to join a cause or a guild. In 1338 he married a Reval burgher's daughter, who died in childbirth; the child with her. Since then he has lived on the ship.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A hold full of Gotland salt and no quarrels.
- **Fear:** Becoming involved.
- **Contradiction:** Genial with every man he meets, close to none.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He pays the harbour porters in beer to hurry his cargo through first, which the other skippers resent.

## Work and money
He earns about 15 marks a voyage in freight and pays the crew a fifth. He owns his ship free. A bad month is a storm in the Gulf.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He declines every cause with a courteous excuse. He would carry a letter for a friend, and would not inform. He prays at St Nicholas and trusts the sea more than any council.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German with merchants; Estonian with porters; Latin in bills.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy but clear; light humour.
- **Sample lines:** "Tomorrow I sail, so today I am blameless." "Take your time, but not much." "Kuule, sõber!" (Listen, friend!)
- **Verbal tic:** Says "as it happens" before a refusal.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every Gulf pilot and porter. He would trade it for a quick discharge. He believes the false rumour that Estonian fishers can call fog.
