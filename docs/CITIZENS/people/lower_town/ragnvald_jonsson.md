## Biography
Born in 1318 in Kalmar, the son of a salt-factor's scribe, Ragnvald came to Reval at sixteen on a Lübeck cog as the master's ship-boy and left it for a counting room when a clerk died of a fever. Albert hired him in 1338 for his beautiful hand and his readiness to work for a bed and a mark a month. He writes the daily book in Low German, reads Latin enough for charters and sends letters to his mother in Swedish. He is saving to buy a stall. He has no family in Reval, and the household is his.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Enough silver to open a small shop and send for his sister.
- **Fear:** A bad entry that costs him the post.
- **Contradiction:** Meticulous in the master's book and loose in his own drawer of pins.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sells small wares at a few pfennig profit without Albert's leave.

## Work and money
He earns 1 mark a month plus board and keeps perhaps 3 öre from his own sales. He buys pins, needles and ribbon from a pedlar at the harbour. A bad month is a stolen sack of pins.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A guest in the town, he thinks politics are for those with property. He would give small help but would not risk his post. He prays at St Olaf's and keeps a Swedish custom of a candle on St Lucy's.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the cargo of every ship in harbour. Would trade it for a fair price on a stall. Believes the false rumour that the Order will take over the harbour.
