## Biography
Born about 1315 in Dortmund, a draper's son. He married young, as a clerk in minor orders, and lost his wife Alheyd in childbed in 1341; the infant, Hinrik, was baptised in haste by Arend van Bocholt when Lambert was away at the Coastal Gate with a dying sailor, and now lives with Alheyd's sister in Lübeck. Lambert took priest's orders that autumn, was sent to Reval, and has served at St Olaf's since Michaelmas. He is lonely and speaks of it to nobody.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be trusted by the Estonian half of the parish.
- **Fear:** That his debt to the smugglers will be known.
- **Contradiction:** Preaches honesty, buys cheap wax.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** His cheap candles are harbour wax from Novgorod, passed through the gate by night.

## Work and money
He draws a priest's share of the parish dues, about 8 marks a year, plus fees for masses. He rents a small loft on Pikk for 1 mark a year, where he stores candles bought from Lübeck skippers and sold at the porch for a penny less than the going price.

## Faction and belief
Church core, sincere but young. A small favour: a mass. A large one: he would hide a fugitive in the loft. Informing: against his oath. He believes in the poor, and fears the bishop.

## Voice
