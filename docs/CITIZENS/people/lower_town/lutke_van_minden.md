## At a glance
- A broad-shouldered youth with a dark close-trimmed beard that makes him look twenty-five until he speaks.
- A thin, reedy voice that cracks at the worst moment.
- Sells tripe and tallow ends from the back door at prices that make honest butchers frown.
- Wipes his knife on his sleeve, always the left.

## Biography
Born in 1326 in Minden, Lutke was sent to Reval at eleven as the ward of a cousin who died in the next winter. Thomas Langhe ([card](thomas_langhe.md)) took him on for his strong back and his father's name. He is in his sixth year, a year from journeyman. He slept through the great frost of last January in the slaughterhouse loft and still dreams of it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy a stall of his own at the Vene market.
- **Fear:** Dismissal for what the other apprentices suspect.
- **Contradiction:** Bold in the yard, hides when the master frowns.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sells offcuts to Estonian women at a cut price and gives Thomas a share he thinks is fair.

## Work and money
No wage; board, a pair of clogs and a pfennig on feast days, plus his hidden pennies from offcuts. A bad month is one without cattle.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He wants coin and does not care who takes the town. He would do a small favour for pay, refuse a large one and inform for a good price. He crosses himself at the Dominicans and spits for luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Rough Low German.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy; the voice breaks when he is angry.
- **Sample lines:** "Cheap, cheap, take it." "I never touched it." "Leck mi."
- **Verbal tic:** "Honest."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which maid sells which scraps. He would trade it for a cut of salt. He believes the false rumour that a butcher's boy who kills a white calf is cursed.
