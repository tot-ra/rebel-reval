## Biography
Taniel was born on the Harju shore in 1271 and learned to make nets from his father Kaspar. He survived the famine of 1315-16 by moving to town, where nets sold. His fingers, long and clever, tie a perfect knot in the dark. Retired now, he lives in a corner of his son's house. Men of the Harju rising hold his kin's land on the Harju road and use him as a messenger.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To die in his own bed, and see his grandchildren fed.
- **Fear:** The Danes burning the villages of his kin.
- **Contradiction:** He is the quietest man on Harju and the most used.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He carries word between the lane and the Harju road by knot-signs on a fishing net.

## Faction and belief
He aids the Harju Kings because his kin are held to it, not for love. A small favour he gives, a large one he cannot refuse, informing he would never do. He knots a charm into every net.
