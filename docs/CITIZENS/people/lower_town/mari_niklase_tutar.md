## Biography
Born 1319 in a Harju village; her father, bound to a manor, sent her to town in 1336 as a maid to ease the labour-tax. She has served the Snelles since 1339. The tremor began after a night nursing the infant Lambrecht through a fever.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Enough for a dowry so she can marry free.
- **Fear:** Being sent home to the manor.
- **Contradiction:** Thrifty but cannot refuse a beggar.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Sends a pfennig a month to her mother and lies about it.
