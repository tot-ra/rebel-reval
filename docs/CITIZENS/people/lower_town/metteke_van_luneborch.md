## Biography
Metteke was born in Lüneburg in 1320, a salt-boiler's daughter, and came to Reval at sixteen with a cousin to find work. She served in two houses before Hille hired her in 1340. A year ago, while carrying washing to Dunkri, she warned a merchant that a creditor was coming for him, and he avoided the visit. She has since asked for small favours in return, and he resents it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Enough savings to open a washing-house of her own.
- **Fear:** Dismissal without a character.
- **Contradiction:** She serves the Hanseatic house yet keeps her own counsel.
