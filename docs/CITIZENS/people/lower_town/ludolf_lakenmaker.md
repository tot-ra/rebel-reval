## Biography
Born in 1313 in a weaver's cottage in Soest and trained on a Westphalian loom; he came to Reval at twenty on a cog because a cousin had a bench to rent. The Amt fraternity buried his cousin and took Ludolf in. He wed Abele Fromme three years ago, and their son Bartold was born at Candlemas. The loom is rented from a master who takes a third of every ell, and the cloth he weaves belongs to a merchant he has never met. A weaver's reed with one cracked tooth, kept for luck, hangs above the loom.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A loom of his own and a seat at the Amt feast table that is not at the bottom.
- **Fear:** Estonian piece-workers undercutting him, and fire in the drying yard.
- **Contradiction:** He resents being treated as a debtor yet keeps the debt carefully.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** A year ago Grete Hovesche paid his arrears to the fraternity, and he has been in the circle since.

## Work and money
He earns about three schillings an ell of coarse cloth after the master's third; roughly nine marks a quarter. Wool comes from a Hanseatic factor via the rented loom. He owes the master fourteen schillings and the fraternity a small subscription.

## Faction and belief
He sympathises with the guilds because the Amt gave him a burial plot and a bench; he is no zealot. A small favour he does; a large one makes him sweat; he would pass a rumour, not a name. Crosses himself before work and wears a hazel twig in the loom.

He knows which factor pays late and who watches the tenter-yard. He believes, wrongly, that the Estonians will burn the fulling mill on St George's Night.
