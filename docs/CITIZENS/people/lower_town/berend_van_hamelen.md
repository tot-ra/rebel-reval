## Biography
Born 1289 on the Lai of his father, a carpenter. He raised roofs in the lean years after the war and helped the Dominicans lay a floor. He married Gesa, who died of a fever in 1335. In 1337 his hands failed in winter and he gave the shop to journeymen and moved to his son Nicolaus's house. He still goes to the carpenters' yard to tell them the trusses are wrong.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A last roof he can say is his, perhaps the church porch.
- **Fear:** Dying in a bed he has not paid for.
- **Contradiction:** Prides himself on being unsentimental, yet carved the wheel for Hildebrand.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Knows his son's debts exist from the way Nicolaus counts coins.

He keeps a pension in kind from his old journeyman, a few shillings a quarter, and his tools, which he will not sell.

## Faction and belief
None; the carpenter's craft taught him not to care who holds the keys while the roof leaks. Prays to St Joseph. Touches the lintel before entering.

## Voice
