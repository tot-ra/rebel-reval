## At a glance
- A round-bellied young woman with a high narrow forehead and rosy cheeks, always sweeping with her left hand.
- Known for a flat, even voice and for being the best at laying a fire.
- Oddity: she never raises her voice, yet servants down the lane obey her.

## Biography
Born 1322 on a farm near Visby on Gotland, the daughter of Torgil, a fisherman who drowned in 1333. At sixteen she came to Reval on a herring cog with a shipmaster cousin who found her a place in a Lower Town house. She has worked for the Stenhus family since 1339, and speaks Swedish only to herself and to a pedlar who passes in autumn.

She is homesick in a practical way: she knows the price of a passage home and has saved two örtug of the sum.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A wool cloak of her own, not a hand-me-down.
- **Fear:** Winter at sea, and being sent away if she breaks something.
- **Contradiction:** She hates the noise of the market and is happiest haggling in it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides her coins in the horn comb, in a hollow she cut herself.

- **Spring 1343 disruption:** Flour is dear and the market is crowded and cross; she shortens her errands and keeps her purse inside her gown.

## Work and money
Wage: board and eight öre a quarter, plus an Easter gift of linen. Her savings are two örtug and a few pfennig. Small purchases at market she makes with a pfennig at a time, buying cheaply from other maids. A bad month is a lost purse.

## Faction and belief
No faction; she has no interest in the Rat and little understanding of the quarrels. For a small favour she lends her bucket; for a large one she would ask the mistress; she would not inform. She prays to St Eric of Sweden at the back of the church, and leaves a bowl of porridge for the farm elf, whom she misses.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish to herself; Low German in the house, with Gotland vowels.
- **Delivery:** Monotone and level, with even stresses; she almost never raises or lowers her voice.
- **Sample lines:** "The fire is lit." / "The fish is two pfennig. It was three." / "Jag vill hem till sommaren." (I want to go home in summer.)
- **Verbal tic:** Hums three notes before she speaks.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which stalls give short weight and what the cooks say about the family. She would trade it for a bigger cloak. She believes the false rumour that Swedish ships are barred from Reval harbour this year.
