## Biography
Born 1325 in Stockholm to a salt-hauler; he shipped as a boy to Reval and was left behind with a fever in 1338. Stepan took him as apprentice and made him journeyman in 1342. He learned Low German on the quay, which is why it is hoarse.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save for a berth on a Gotland ship.
- **Fear:** A master who would sell his time.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to Stepan yet courts every harbour offer.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Has a promised berth he has not told Stepan about.
