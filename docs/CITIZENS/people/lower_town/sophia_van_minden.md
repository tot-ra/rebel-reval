## Biography
Born 1286 in Minden in Westphalia, brought to Reval at fifteen to marry into the trade, which turned out to mean waiting four years for Frederik, who was away. She married him in 1319, bore six children, and raised three. She has the booming voice of a captain's daughter, a gift she inherited and has never learned to lower. She runs the house with firmness and fairness.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Mette married and Dietrich settled.
- **Fear:** Her husband's credit failing in the grain months.
- **Contradiction:** Loud and brisk, yet shy of reading aloud.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She buys cheap candles for the servants and bills the household for good ones.

## Faction and belief
None. She leaves politics to her husband and the church to the priest. A small favour she gives loudly; a large one she negotiates; informing she thinks unseemly. She keeps the feast of St Sophia with a spun-flax offering.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German, always at volume.
- **Delivery:** Booming, deep, with a laugh at the end of a sentence.
- **Sample lines:** "Come in, come in, shut the door!" / "Mari, my girl, those pins are fair." / "Cousin, you are thin again!"
- **Verbal tic:** "Quite so."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows every marriage and debt on Pikk. She would trade it for a good match for Mette. She believes that a woman who spins at the window is thought modest, which is partly true.
