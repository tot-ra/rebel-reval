## Biography
Born 1334 on Vaimu. As eldest son he is meant for the tannery, and he knows it. He prefers the stable cat and the yard-wall where he sits unobserved. He has a favourite hiding-place behind the lime-barrels.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be left alone with the cat.
- **Fear:** The tan-pits at night.
- **Contradiction:** He hates the tannery and copies his father's walk.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He talks to the cat in a language of his own.
