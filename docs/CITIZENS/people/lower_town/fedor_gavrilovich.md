## Biography
Fedor was born in 1293 in a village near Novgorod, a fur-trapper's son. He carried bales on one shoulder from age ten, which gave him the tilt. He came to trade in Reval in 1319, and learned Middle Low German by arguing with furriers.

He believes in fair weights and in small kindnesses. He tells others to buy from Ulrika Olofsdotter, a maid on Munga who sells him honest thread.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To retire to a house on the Volkhov.
- **Fear:** Dying in a foreign inn.
- **Contradiction:** Generous with advice, mean with silver.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He suspects Mikula the dyer is one of theirs and has never said.

## Work and money
He buys pelts from Novgorod caravans and sells to German furriers; about three hundred marks a year pass through. A bad month is moth in the squirrel skins.

## Faction and belief
He is a sympathiser because his trade lives on open roads. A small favour he does, a large one with advice, informing never. He keeps an icon of St Nicholas in his bale-chest.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Russian in the court; Middle Low German in the market.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, drawn out, slow with numbers.
- **Sample lines:** "A fair price is the one both sides complain of." / "Buy thread from Ulrika, she does not cheat." / "Nu, tak."
- **Verbal tic:** "So, so."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the pelt prices at Novgorod and the thread prices at Munga. He would trade it for a good pelt. He believes, wrongly, that the Order's tax will fall on furs.
