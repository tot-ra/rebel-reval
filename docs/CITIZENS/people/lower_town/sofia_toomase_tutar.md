- A pale, slight child with straw-blond hair who speaks so softly people lean down.
- Counts everything, courses of stone, steps to the well, planks in a cart, using notches she cuts with her left hand.
- Fears the carter's goose and has a long, one-sided feud with it.
- Oddity: her father lets her use his rule on Sundays, though he tells no one.

## Biography
Born in 1332, after her mother had stopped expecting more. Her father's workshop is her playground: she sits on the stone pile and counts what the journeymen have cut. She is the family's tally-keeper by accident, and she knows numerals from her father's marks, though never a letter. Last summer a carter's goose chased her to the well, and she has avoided the carter's yard since, taking the long way round past the tannery.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to go to the quay by herself.

- **Contradiction:** Tiny and silent, yet boldly corrects an adult's count.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides a smooth white stone under the loft board and tells it her worries.
