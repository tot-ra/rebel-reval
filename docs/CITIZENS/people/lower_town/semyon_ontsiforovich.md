## At a glance
- Sits behind the wax counter and weighs without being told.
- Monotone, never excited, which makes customers trust him.
- Pear-shaped, with a rolling, wide-legged gait he is teased for.
- Counts candles aloud as other boys count birds.

## Biography
Born 1330 on Tolli, elder son of Ontsifor and Olena. He learned to weigh at six and to haggle at nine. He has never left Reval, and his Novgorod is his grandmother's stories. He is the son his father hoped for, and he knows it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to go to Novgorod with a cart.
- **Fear:** His father's disappointment.
- **Contradiction:** He is calm with customers and cries when a candle breaks.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a second set of weights he made himself, to test his father's.
