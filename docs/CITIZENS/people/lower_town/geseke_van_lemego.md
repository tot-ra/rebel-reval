## Biography
Born in Lemego in Westphalia in 1294, Geseke came to Reval as a girl with an aunt who spun for the Dunkri houses. She married Ghert Kleine in 1320 and raised three children, burying two in the fever years. She lost the tooth to a hard crust in 1331. She took in spinning for Rataskaevu weavers and, when the taproom's back room lay empty, put four wheels in it. She knows the street through the women who bring flax. She lives above the taproom with her husband, her children, his aunt and a maid.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A spinning-room that pays its own way, so her husband's debts do not govern the house.
- **Fear:** A cold hearth in winter, and her daughter's silence.
- **Contradiction:** She hates gossip as sin and runs a house of it for her own purposes.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She decides which talk from the taproom goes on, and to whom; the circle calls her by a nickname she has never heard.

## Work and money
She spins about 4 lispfund of flax a year into thread at 6 pfennigs the hank; her income is about 10 marks, which keeps the house when the taproom is slack. Flax comes from Harju peasants by cart; thread goes to the weavers on Nunne. A bad month is wet flax.

## Faction and belief
She is core Hanseatic because her thread rides Hanse carts. A small favour, a word passed; a large one, a roof for a night; to inform she would sooner burn her own flax. She says the Ave on each spindle-turn and burns a wisp of flax at the Midsummer fire.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German, low and calm.
- **Delivery:** Low and unhurried; each sentence ends with a short pause, so the room waits.
- **Sample lines:** "Sit, girl, you hold it wrong." "I hear nothing; I spin." "Dat geit al." (That will do.)
- **Verbal tic:** Sucks the gap in her teeth.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who drinks and what they say. She would trade this for three months' flax on credit. She believes the false rumour that the Order has bought flax for sails.
