## Biography
Born 1313 in Stralsund, daughter of a lighter-master who drowned in 1331. His lighter went to her uncle; Richardis stayed to work it, because a woman may not sail a cog but may row a lighter between quay and ship. She came to Reval in 1336 with a skipper's crew and was left behind with a debt. She lodges with Jakob's family for 3 marks a year and keeps the fund of the drowned in a tin box.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A lighter of her own.
- **Fear:** The sea at night, the box going empty.
- **Contradiction:** She distrusts men's work and runs the men's burial fund.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** The fund is short by two marks; she covers it from her own wages.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic dependent: she works for the Hanse's boats and fears losing the place. Small favour: a lift in the lighter; large: no; informing: she would if wages were threatened. Prays to St Nicholas and throws a coin into the roads for luck.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which ships carry how much and which crews are unpaid. She would trade it for a place on a cog. She believes the false rumour that women bring storms.
