## Biography
Born 1321 in Reval, daughter of the Swedish shipwright's hand Torgil, who fell from a hull on the Coastal Gate quay in 1336 and left debts for his burial. A Lübeck skipper's agent paid the funeral and the priest, and the debt was written against her. She went into service at fifteen and has served here since 1339. She carries a quarter of her wages to the agent each Michaelmas. A year ago a gatekeeper brought her a pie and asked a small question, and the bond tightened.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear her father's name and her own ledger.
- **Fear:** Being named as the one who passed a message.
- **Contradiction:** Hates the debt, yet defends the men who hold it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has carried an unsigned letter twice and knows she cannot say to whom.

## Faction and belief
A dependent: she acts to clear a debt, not out of belief. A small favour she does; a large one she dreads; informing she has already done once. Church-going, with a bone charm from her mother.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which sailors drink where. She would trade it for her father's debt forgiven. She believes the Danes will cancel Hanseatic debts, which is false.
