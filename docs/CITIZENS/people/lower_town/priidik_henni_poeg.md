## At a glance
- Slight and weathered, with an ash-blond beard and a mole at the corner of his mouth.
- Gravelly voice, the product of fog, shouting and cold.
- Does small kindnesses on the quay and keeps count of them.
- Always the shortest man on a crew, and the first up the mast.

## Biography
Priidik was born in 1295 at Pirita, the youngest son of a Henn who fished from a flat-bottomed boat. He shipped as a boy on a Reval cog in 1308 and has been the topman ever since, because he was the lightest. A fall from the foremast into the sea in 1330 gave him a hacking cough that settled into his gravelly voice. He lives in the harbour berths from spring to autumn and in a fisherman's loft at Pirita in winter, with his brother's family. He keeps a ledger in his head of favours done and owed.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see a nephew shipped as an apprentice in Lübeck rather than a topman.
- **Fear:** Falling again; his knees will not take another.
- **Contradiction:** Warm to strangers, but resentful when favours are forgotten.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once let a runaway serf stow away in a cask on the way to Visby, and has never told.

## Work and money
He earns about 5 öre on voyage; port day-work pays a few pfennig. Berth costs a pfennig a night. A bad month is a fever in the lungs.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He wants no part of a rising; the quay is his world. He would carry a message for a friend and would not inform. He keeps a charm of dried seaweed in his cap and prays to St Nicholas.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian; a few German sailing words.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly and low; a cough between phrases.
- **Sample lines:** "Up I go, then, like a squirrel." "I carried it, I tell you, I carried it." "Aga nii on." (But so it is.)
- **Verbal tic:** Coughs into his fist before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every rope on three ships and which sailors are sick. He would trade it for a warm meal. He believes the false rumour that a mole near the mouth marks a man who will die by water.
