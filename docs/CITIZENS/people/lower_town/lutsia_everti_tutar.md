## Biography
Born in 1302 in a village near Rapla, Lutsia was brought to Reval as a girl of ten and apprenticed to a spinner. She married Toomas Kauri poeg at twenty and has borne ten children, buried two. She is distantly kin by marriage to the Swedish spinner Anna Birgersdotter, a link neither can trace. She keeps the house together while Toomas worries about the roof, and sells a few pins and whorls from a box on the sill.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Bread for eight children and a roof that does not leak.
- **Fear:** A hard winter, and a debt that takes the house.
- **Contradiction:** She sings constantly and never lets anyone see her counting.

## Work and money
She spins three skeins a day for the Snelle house at a penny a skein and earns a few pfennigs more on pins. She buys flax from a carter. She sells bone pins and whorls, which her eldest sons carve, for a farthing each. A bad month is a sick child.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction. For a small favour she gives thread; for a large one she weighs her children; she would not inform. She keeps St Olaf and the hearth-spirit alike, and ties red thread on the cradle.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who is spinning short skeins and who is selling thread. She would trade it for flax. She believes, falsely, that wool shorn at the new moon never takes the dye.
