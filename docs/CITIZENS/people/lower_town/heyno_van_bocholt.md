## Biography
Heyno was born in Bocholt in 1305 and came to Reval at nineteen as a cooper's journeyman. He never took the burgher oath and so cannot sit in the guild. He set up his own bench on Nunne in 1335. The failed harvest of 1340 pushed him to borrow grain from the weaver Gyse Hardekop.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the 15-mark grain debt and buy his citizenship.
- **Fear:** Debt-collectors and the council's inspectors.
- **Contradiction:** Exact in measures, loose in quarrels.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He suspects Magdalena's errands but pockets her rent.
