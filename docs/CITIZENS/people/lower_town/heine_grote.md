## Biography
Born in 1327 in Reval to a German carter named Grote who died when Heine was two. He was nursed by an Estonian shore-woman and spoke Estonian first, then learned the German of the street from the quay boys, which came out in her lilt. His mother apprenticed him to Jaan for seven years. He still owes an old courtesy to a boy who trained under the previous master.

## Motivation
