## Biography
Born 1338 in the small house on Katariina käik, the first child. A cold winter at two left him small and coughing; his father's cupping and his mother's broth pulled him through. He minds the shop door when his mother goes to market, and he knows the price of salt, which he says to strangers.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hold his father's razor, once.
- **Fear:** The smoke from next door, which he thinks has eyes.
- **Contradiction:** He is shy with adults and shouts at the chimney.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He hides sweet roots in a cracked pot beneath the loft ladder.
