## Biography
Nicolaus was born in 1321 in Reval, the son of a boatwright who died in 1332 of the lung-fever. His mother took in washing; the Dominican friars taught him letters and Latin in return for carrying water. In 1335 he was bound as a boy to a merchant, then came to Godeke van Stralesund in 1339 as clerk. The chilblains came from three winters in an unheated counting room.

He sleeps in a closet next to the counting room and eats with the household.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough for his own stall at the Rathaus steps.
- **Fear:** Making a mistake in a total and being blamed for the loss.
- **Contradiction:** He is shy in speech yet bold in writing, once amending a letter to save his master from a bad bargain.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has read the letters that mention "friends after mass" and has told no one.

## Work and money
He earns six marks a year with board, and keeps a small purse of schillings for pens and gall nuts. He once bought oak-galls with Rein Tanieli poeg to halve the cost; the sharing lapsed after a quarrel over weights. A bad month is one where the ink freezes and the letters are late.

## Faction and belief
He keeps out of faction because a clerk who is seen to lean loses his place. He believes in orderly accounts. A small favour he does gladly; a large one he refuses; informing he might, if his place were at risk. He wears a small lead pilgrim's badge from St Olaf's.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German in the house; Latin for letters and prayers.
- **Delivery:** Husky, hesitant, sentences trail off, speeds up when sums are involved.
- **Sample lines:** "It is eleven and four, no, twelve." / "I only copy what I am given." / "Min Gott, de Tied."
- **Verbal tic:** "As written."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows each merchant's debts and which letters were answered. He would trade it for a place elsewhere. He believes, wrongly, that Lübeck will send a fleet.
