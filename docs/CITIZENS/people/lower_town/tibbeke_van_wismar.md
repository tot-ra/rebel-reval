## Biography
Born in Wismar in 1280, she came to Reval at twenty with a spinning master's license and set up as a gold-thread spinner on Pikk. She never married; the Lippe family took her in after the fire of 1328 burned her shop. She has watched three councils come and go, and speaks of the wars of 1313 as if they were last winter.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Gottschalk in the council before she dies.
- **Fear:** A siege; she remembers the last.
- **Contradiction:** She is gentle with children and hard with her circle.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She passes the circle's word to Heyno van Bremen at the church door.

## Faction and belief
Core Hanseatic: because the town's peace is her life's work. A small favour: a word; a large one: a night's shelter; to inform: she would refuse unless the town's gates were at risk. She crosses herself thrice and burns a sprig of juniper in the sickroom.
