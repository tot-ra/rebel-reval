## Biography
Born 1333 on Tolli, younger son. Spent his summers on the Coastal Gate quay, where sailors teach him the numbers of ropes. He has no learning but he has a head for tallies. His brother is a shopkeeper; he wants to be a skipper.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To sail.
- **Fear:** The sea he loves, at night.
- **Contradiction:** He talks of leaving and weeps when his mother is sick.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once untied a skiff and drifted a hundred yards before he could row back.
