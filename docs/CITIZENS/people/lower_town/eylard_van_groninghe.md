## At a glance
- Keeps Lauri's timber accounts and reads the letters that arrive by Lübeck ship.
- Squints at every page, and is the only man on Pikk who holds a ledger at arm's length and then too close.
- Voice scratched raw from reading customs lists on the quay in the wind.
- Believes in the order of the Hanse the way a pilgrim believes in a road.

## Biography
Born 1321 in Groningen, third son of a cloth dealer, shipped to Reval at fifteen with a letter to a merchant on Lai and the instruction to be useful. He trained under a master who later passed him to Lauri's timber trade as the Estonian carpenter wished to keep accounts a German could read. He learned Latin from a Dominican brother who taught him for a candle a week. He misses the damp flat country of home and the sound of church bells over canals. He lodges in the attic over the workshop, and has not yet made his place.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A burgher's right and a seat at the Kindergilde table.
- **Fear:** Being sent home a failure, or blamed for a sum that does not tally.
- **Contradiction:** Believes in order and law, yet gladly writes two sets of figures when the master asks.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has read a letter meant for another man and told no one.

## Work and money
His pay is board, a bed and 8 marks a year; he sends 2 home. He owns a psalter, a wax tablet and a knife. A bad month is a missing invoice. He owes a friend a favour of silver he cannot repay.

## Faction and belief
A core Hanseatic man out of gratitude and ambition: the guild fed him, taught him, and will make him. For a small favour he will copy a list; for a large one he will carry a sealed packet; informing he does already, in a clerk's tidy way. He is devout but practical, saying a paternoster before each ledger.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German with merchants; Latin for prayers and charters; no Estonian beyond a few market words.
- **Delivery:** Hoarse, rapid, clipped, with a cough at the end of a sentence.
- **Sample lines:** "The figures do not lie, only men do." / "Who has the dividers?" / "Per Deum, not before noon."
- **Verbal tic:** "As written."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows what each Pikk merchant owes and who is behind. He would trade it for a recommendation to a master. He believes the Order is already in the town in disguise, which is false.
