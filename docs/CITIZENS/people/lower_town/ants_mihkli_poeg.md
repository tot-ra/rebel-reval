## Biography
Born in 1274 in a Harju village and brought to town at twelve by a master tailor who taught him the needle. He has sewn for fifty years, for the lanes and the watch. His wife Gesa married him in 1297. They had four children; two survived to leave.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To pass a clear house to his daughter.
- **Fear:** A bailiff at the door for another man's debt.
- **Contradiction:** Cautious all his life, yet he stood surety for a stranger.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has not told Gesa how much he owes.

## Work and money
He sews plain garments for a few pfennigs. He owes Frederik van Brunswik six marks, paid about three. Wolter van Hamelen owes him a fine of eighteen marks. He holds Segebode Corte's shears for eight marks.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A poor old man wants to be left alone. A small favour is a patched seam; a large one he would refuse; he would inform if his debts were forgiven. He prays at St Olaf's.
