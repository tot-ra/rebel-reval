## Biography
Timofei was born in 1289 in Novgorod to a church-reader. He learned letters in the choir and came to Reval in 1320 as a court scribe. He lost his wife and son in the fever of 1330 and has not remarried.

He keeps the court's accounts and also a second list of names that never goes in the book. He reports to Irina Ontsiforovna, a household servant on Pikk, who has the older line of kin in Pskov.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see the Novgorod road kept open until he dies.
- **Fear:** An audit of the court by the council.
- **Contradiction:** An exact man who writes two truths.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** The second list has fourteen names.

## Faction and belief
He is active because the Novgorod trade is his life. A small favour he does, a large one with Irina's word, informing he does not. He fasts in Lent and kisses the icon.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Russian in the court; Middle Low German in the market.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, slow, repeats figures.
- **Sample lines:** "Forty-one, forty-one, I wrote it twice." / "The book says one thing." / "Gospodi pomilui."
- **Verbal tic:** "As written."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the court's debts. He would trade it for a safe road for the court. He believes, wrongly, that the Danes will close the harbour.
