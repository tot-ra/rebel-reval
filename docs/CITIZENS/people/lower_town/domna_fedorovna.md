## Biography
Born in 1336 in Reval, daughter of Fedor, Grigory's brother. Her mother died in the fever of 1339 and Fedor in the lung fever of 1341. An Estonian market-woman minded her for two years, which is where the sing-song came from. Now she lives at her grandmother's oven.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To catch the speckled hen.
- **Fear:** The dark under the stair.
- **Contradiction:** Bold in the yard and silent in bed.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She talks to her mother's wooden spoon at night.
