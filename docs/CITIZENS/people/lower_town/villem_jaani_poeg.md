## Biography
Born in 1305 in a village near Jüri, Villem was sold by his father at nine to a Harju manor. He ran away at nineteen and came to town, where he was taken in by the carpenters' yard. The winter of 1341 was bad: the lane froze, the well stopped, and a neighbour's hearth went cold; he hauled firewood on his back through three weeks and lost two toes. Hinrick took him on as a servant that spring, and he has stayed. He sleeps in the loft above the workshop and says it is warm.

## Motivation

## Faction and belief
No faction; he cannot afford one and the manor lords would hang him if he were found. He would lie for a friend, hide for a neighbour, and tell nothing to the Rat. He prays to St Olaf for the cold to pass.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; he points and nods in German.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, with clipped ends.
- **Sample lines:** "Up, up, it holds!" "Cold? No, not cold." "Mind the rung, master."
- **Verbal tic:** Says "up" for everything.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which roofs lean and the routes of the manor riders. He would trade it for a safe word. He believes the manor will send men for runaways at midsummer.
