## Biography
Born 1273 in Revel, daughter of the stone-house keeper after whom she is named; she never married. She was a tawer, working alum-white leather for gloves and purses, from fourteen until her hands failed in 1335. Tyde's mother, her sister, took her in. She sits at the washing-trough with Estonian women and learned their tongue. In Lent 1342 Sibbe Hovesche paid her Easter tithe when she was short; since then he has asked small things of her.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be useful and independent until she dies.
- **Fear:** Being a burden and a debtor.
- **Contradiction:** She resents Sibbe and goes on telling him.
