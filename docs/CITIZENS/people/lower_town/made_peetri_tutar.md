## Biography
Made was born in 1289 in a Rävala village and came to Reval at sixteen to serve a chandler on Lai. She took over the dip-vats when he died and ran them herself for twenty years, selling to St Olaf's and to ship chandlers. A fall on the wet floor in 1339 ended her work. Albert, whom she had supplied with wax, took her in when she could not pay her rent, and she has kept the household's candles and keys' arrangement ever since. Nobody remembers whether she is kin or charity; she answers to "Mother Made" and likes it.

## Work and money
She earns nothing now; her keep is her pay. She sells candle-ends at 1 pfennig and gives half away. A bad month is a missing key.

## Faction and belief
No faction. Too old and lame to risk. She would give a candle but not a night's shelter. Pious at St Olaf's; keeps the old custom of burning a wax stub for the dead on All Souls.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; a lilting Low German with the clerks.
- **Delivery:** Sing-song; each sentence rises at the end.
- **Sample lines:** "A candle is a small sun, child." "Mind the stair!" "Tule siia, tule." (Come here, come.)
- **Verbal tic:** "Ei-ei" before a warning.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows which churches buy wax cheaply and which maids steal ends. Would trade it for an arm on the stairs. Believes the false rumour that wax from a hanged man's candle lights ghosts.
