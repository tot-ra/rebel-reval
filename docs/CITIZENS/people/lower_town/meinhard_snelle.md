## At a glance
- A tall sandy man with a squint in the left eye, a missing front tooth and a voice that sets pigeons off the roof.
- He leads the night watch from the town hall, and keeps the gate rota in his head.
- Odd for a burgher watch sergeant: he swears by the Danish king and holds the town's keys.

## Biography
Born in 1313 in Reval to a burgher armourer, he was a boy on the wall in the Danish levy of 1325. The viceroy's steward gave him bread and a place in the watch, and he remembers it. He rose to sergeant in 1338. He is brave, loyal and not clever.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be captain of the castle guard one day.
- **Fear:** The town and the crown fighting, and being made to choose.
- **Contradiction:** Sworn to the town's watch and loyal to the viceroy.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He leaves a postern unbarred on certain nights when a friend from the hill asks.

## Work and money
He earns twelve marks a year and keep, and a fine's share on watch arrests. He owes two marks for a new jack. A bad month is a quiet one, with no fines.

## Faction and belief
He is loyal to the crown out of gratitude for bread. He would warn a Danish friend for a small favour, open a postern for a large one, and would inform on a rising. He prays to St Olaf and spits at the Order.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German, the watch's barked commands.
- **Delivery:** Booming, even in a whisper, with a whistle through the gap.
- **Sample lines:** "Who goes there?" "Curfew, off the street." "Dat sal wi seen."
- **Verbal tic:** Clears his throat like a horn.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every gate's weak bar. He would trade it for a place at the castle. He believes the Order has spies among the stable-boys, which is false.
