## At a glance
- Moves through the house without a sound; the floorboards seem to forget her.
- Unhurried speech that makes servants and children trust her.
- Known as the best at starching a coif and the worst at being thanked.
- Oddity: an illiterate maid who remembers every word she overhears, because she cannot write it down.

## Biography
Born in Reval in 1309 to a cooper's family named Kniphof, one of six children. Her mother died of a wasting cough in 1341, and [Gottschalk van Deventer](./gottschalk_van_deventer.md) paid for the funeral candle and a mass. That kindness bought her ear. She entered the Wise house in 1336 and has served there since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough for a cottage and a plot of cabbage.
- **Fear:** Dismissal without a character; being named for what she hears.
- **Contradiction:** She resents Gottschalk's hold and still reports to him because she cannot repay the candle.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sits at the edge of a three-person cell, and has never told [Meinhard Wise](./meinhard_wise.md) that his table is overheard.

## Work and money
Wage 1 mark a year plus keep and a pair of shoes at Michaelmas. She keeps 5 schillings under a floor-board. A bad month is a broken platter docked from her pay.

## Faction and belief
She serves the Hansa because the Hansa buys her house's bread and because of one obligation. A small favour she does; a large one she resents; informing she already does. She prays to Our Lady and carries a rag of her mother's apron.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German only.
- **Delivery:** Low, unhurried, rarely rising.
- **Sample lines:** "I did not hear." "Bread is on the table." "Mother used to say, who counts, owns."
- **Verbal tic:** Folds her apron corner while speaking.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows what is said at the Wise table. She would trade it for the debt's release. She believes the false rumour that the Order will pay informers in silver.
