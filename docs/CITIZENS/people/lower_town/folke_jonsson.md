## Biography
Born in 1322 in a Swedish fisher's house on the Kopli shore, Folke was bound to Gunnar at twelve and finished his years at nineteen. He stayed on at a journeyman's wage because Gunnar needs him and promised him the bench. He has walked every shoemaker's lane in Reval and knows who charges what. A lasting grievance: Tideman Schomaker on Nunne sells shoes at prices that Folke cannot match with honest leather, and Folke suspects stolen or smuggled stock. He has no proof and is sharp with himself for guessing.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To inherit Gunnar's bench and pay the master's roof loan off for him.
- **Fear:** That the bench goes to a stranger.
- **Contradiction:** A fair-minded man who is quick to suspect others of cheating.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has been keeping a tally of Tideman Schomaker's pairs, with a notch for each.
