## Biography
Born 1329 in the house on Laboratooriumi. Her sister [Mett Toomase tütar](mett_toomase_tutar.md) was always the quick one and Sofia the baby, so Katarina took the useful role: errands, water, small sums. She learned prices from the market stalls with her mother and numbers from a tally-stick. The winter of 1342, with Toomas short of work and the harbour iced, she asked to take a day place at the cooper's household and brought home a loaf every evening. She is proud of it and does not say so.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be the one who keeps the family fed through the lean weeks.
- **Fear:** The neck mark being seen, and a market stall-keeper laughing.
- **Contradiction:** Stays out of every quarrel, yet remembers every slight to the day.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has been short-changing herself on the weekly tally to bring home more for the table.

## Work and money
Day wages are 2 pfennig and a meal; she brings the bread home. Rye costs her more each week, and she reports it. Her mother's coin keeps the household afloat; her small share is the difference between porridge and bread.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A maid in other people's houses learns that politics belongs to those who can lose nothing, and she has lost her place once already. She would carry a small thing if asked kindly; she would not inform. She says her Paternoster in Estonian and leaves a crust for the house spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; she understands more Low German than she admits.
- **Delivery:** Nasal and flat; counts as she speaks.
- **Sample lines:** "Rye is up two pfennig." / "I did not take it, mistress." / "Kuule, the bell."
- **Verbal tic:** Taps the tally-stick against her thigh.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows every price, who is buying more than usual, and who is short. She would trade it for a place at a better table. She falsely believes the cooper hides a hoard of silver in a barrel.
