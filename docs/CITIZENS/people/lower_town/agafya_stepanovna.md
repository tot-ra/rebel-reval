## Biography
Born 1306 in Pskov, a priest's servant's daughter, brought to Reval in 1330 with a merchant household. Stepan bought her contract, and she has been in his house since. She was recruited at a funeral in 1336 by a Pskov man who asked only that she remember names. Over time she became the one who decides what is passed on.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A small house in Pskov to die in.
- **Fear:** Exposure and a rope.
- **Contradiction:** Shy in daylight, ruthless at night.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Reports on Stepan's guests too.

## Faction and belief
An informer for Pskov and Novgorod interests; her reason is her priest's dying wish that she keep faith with the old city. She would pass on a word, a name; she would not harm the household. Orthodox, devout.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Russian in private, plain Low German in the hall.
- **Delivery:** Flat monotone, steady.
- **Sample lines:** "There is bread." "I heard nothing; the pot was loud." "Slava Bogu."
- **Verbal tic:** Never says "I".

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who visits and when. Would trade only for Pskov's good. Believes that Hanseatic ships will close the Narva road.
