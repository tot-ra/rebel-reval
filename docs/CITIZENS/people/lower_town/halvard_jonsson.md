## Biography
Halvard was born in the winter of 1330. His mother Ragna died of lung fever when he was five, and he remembers her only as a smell of caraway. The night he was six and burned with a fever, a neighbouring tailor helped his father carry him to the herb-wife by lantern; he remembers a beard that smelled of chalk and wool and nothing else. Cecilia raised him with a stick she never used. He is now a year into his father's bench, bad at filing, good at sums.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the shop's tally and to sell, not forge.
- **Fear:** That his father's eyes will fail before he can take over.
- **Contradiction:** He scorns the bench, yet fights anyone who calls his father's knives cheap.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once stood outside Erik Andersson's shop on Pikk jalg for an hour, memorising the price board.
