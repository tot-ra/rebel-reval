## At a glance
- Loudest of the maids, who laughs at a volume that carries from the well to the wash-house.
- Attends every funeral on Pikk, Lai and Vaimu, mourners or not.
- Sympathises with the guilds because they keep her master's house in work, and passes on what she hears.
- Has a small squint, which makes people think she is looking at someone else.

## Biography
Born 1318 in Reval to a salt-carter named Eerik, who died in 1330. Her mother took in washing, and Margareta went into service at twelve. She learned to cook with an old widow on Vaimu who taught her that the quickest way to know people is to feed them. She has served here since 1340. She was baptised, married no one, and has a sister in Rakvere she writes to through a priest's clerk. She fell into the whisper-circle by accident, at a funeral where she overheard something she was not meant to.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A name that people mention warmly, and a small kitchen of her own.
- **Fear:** Being thought a gossip and dismissed.
- **Contradiction:** Loud as a bell, yet keeps the one secret that matters like a stone in the mouth.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She recruited Dietrich Zierenberg a year ago with a bowl of soup and a kind word, and now fears he will tell.

## Faction and belief
A sympathiser: she likes the order that keeps her master's table full, and enjoys being the one who knows. A small favour is a message, a large one a night's watch at a door; informing she calls "passing the word". Christian, with a tin cross and a pinch of salt over the door.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian in the kitchen; Middle Low German to merchants, loud and eager.
- **Delivery:** Booming, quick, laughs mid-sentence.
- **Sample lines:** "Old age takes them all, and we sit by the bier and say so!" / "Soup first, questions after." / "Oh, I never said that, did I?"
- **Verbal tic:** "As I heard it."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who died, who mourned, and who did not. She would trade it for a seat nearer the fire. She believes the Dominicans have a secret tunnel to the hill, which is false.
