## Biography
Born in 1317 at Rannamõisa to the boatman Albert. Came to Reval in 1338 at her sister's call and has served in the taproom since. The scar is a fever sore from the winter of 1340. She learned German from the guests.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be paid on time, and to dance at the St John's fire.
- **Fear:** A hand on the stairs at night.
- **Contradiction:** A bright talker who tells no one anything true.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides a charm under her bed to keep the watch away.
