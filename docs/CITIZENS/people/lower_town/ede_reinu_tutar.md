## Biography
Born 1312 in a fishing hamlet near Viimsi; her voice went hoarse calling across the harbour wind to her father's boat. At fourteen she came into town as a scullery girl, and in 1333 Hartwig van Minden's late wife took her on as maid. Her right hand began to shake after the cold winter of 1342–43, when she and Katharina van Hildensem fed two braziers in the lane. Reinu was her father's name; she is no kin to others of that forename. A year ago Ell, a girl from Hobusepea, did her a small kindness, and since then Ede has carried talk out of the house.

## Motivation

## Faction and belief
Black Cloaks by the favour of a friend. Her root grievance: Danish levies take her father's fish. Her reason: Ell did not make her feel a servant. She would pass a small word, never poison. She keeps the old rites: milk on the threshold, a salt pinch in the fire.

## Voice

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who calls on her master after dark. Would trade it for peace from Ell. Believes falsely that merchants' ghosts walk the counting-room.
