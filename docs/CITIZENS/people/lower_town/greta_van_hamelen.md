## At a glance
- Spins with a wheel at the kitchen window, in the maids' company, not in the parlour.
- A level monotone that never rises, even at a spilled bowl.
- Known for fine thread sold to Lai weavers.
- Oddity: a wealthy wife who spins for coin and keeps it separate.

## Biography
Born in Hameln in 1292 and brought to Reval at eight by a merchant father. She married [Thomas Blyde](./thomas_blyde.md) in 1324. She has borne five children and buried one.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A safe future for the children and a debt-free house.
- **Fear:** A fall in the market.
- **Contradiction:** Cool in manner, deeply anxious.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows about the stonemason's loan and has been pawning thread to repay it.
