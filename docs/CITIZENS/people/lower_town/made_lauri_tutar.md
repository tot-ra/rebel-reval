## Biography
Made was born in 1320 in a hamlet near the Harju forest, daughter of Lauri, a charcoal-burner. Her grandmother taught her the first-milk offering. She came to town at fifteen when her father died, and Liis took her into the dairy in 1338. At a grove on the Pirita road she met the keeper of the old rites, who now asks what is said in German houses about forests, felling and the Church.

She reports in the open air, never in a house.

## Work and money
She earns two marks a year and board. Her token costs nothing; the porter Priidik once paid her a penny for a warning. A bad month is when the milk sours and Liis docks her.

## Faction and belief
She belongs to the old ways because her grandmother did and because the forest feeds the poor. A small favour she does at once, a large one with the keeper's word, and informing is her whole job. She prays to the Virgin and to the hearth in the same breath.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian in the dairy; Middle Low German at the door.
- **Delivery:** Husky, low, sleepy, never repeats herself.
- **Sample lines:** "I heard nothing, Frau." / "The milk is first for the house." / "Metsa isa hoia."
- **Verbal tic:** "As it was."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who in the lane says what about felling. She would trade it for a safe grove. She believes, wrongly, that the new bell was cast with a spirit trapped in it.
