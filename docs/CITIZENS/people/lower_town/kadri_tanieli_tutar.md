## Biography
Born in 1306 in a Harju village to a weaver named Taniel; sent to town at fourteen as a spinner's girl. She married Marten Kristjani poeg in 1327 and has borne eight children; six live. The winter of 1342 took her cow, her last hen and a month of sleep. She spun for sale all that winter, sitting up with the baby on her lap, and her yarn bought the rye. She keeps the family on a thread of her own making and does not expect thanks.

## Motivation
- **Want:** One winter in which nobody goes hungry and Priidik is apprenticed properly.
- **Fear:** The bailiff and the brewer meeting in the same lane.
- **Contradiction:** Deeply devout, yet she lies by silence for her husband's pride.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows from the goldsmith's wife that the betrothal ring was never there; she lets Marten tell the story and quietly spins double.

## Work and money
She sells about two pfund of yarn a week to cloth-weavers on Nunne and Sauna for a few pfennigs a skein, a quarter of the household's income. She buys rye and onions with it. The money-box holds three marks and a little over; the house owes six to the brewer. A bad month is when the baby is ill and the spindle sits idle.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A mother of six wants the lane at peace and will join no one. A small favour she does; a large one for a child; she would inform only to save one of hers. She prays to Our Lady at St Olaf's and ties a red thread on the baby's wrist.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; plain Low German in the market.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, even, hardly raised; a flat pleasant drone.
- **Sample lines:** "Sit, child, the thread will keep." "Mis siis, so what?" "Rye first, then pride."
- **Verbal tic:** Hums under her breath between sentences.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the price of rye on every stall and who sells short. She would trade it for a clear debt. She falsely believes the Rat is about to ban unsealed yarn.
