## At a glance
- A small woman with a voice like a cello, which surprises everyone who meets her at the stall.
- Counts every coin twice and trusts neither the journeyman nor the husband with a full purse.
- Her nose was broken once and set slightly right of true, and she tells the story differently each time.
- Speaks Swedish in the kitchen and a careful, slow German at the market.

## Biography
Helga was born in 1283 in Nyköping, the daughter of a herring-salter, and crossed to Reval at twenty-two with her brother Sten, a shipwright's hand, after a bad winter. A hawser struck her face at the Coastal gate in 1306 and broke the nose; she was by then already promised to [Nicolaus van Luneborch](../../people/lower_town/nicolaus_van_luneborch.md), who had met her at the fish-quay. They married that year. Her daughter Ragna, now in Visby, came home to lie in during the winter of 1342 and the boy was baptised by [Tyde Sasse](../../people/lower_town/tyde_sasse.md); Helga's brother Sten was buried from the same church in the autumn. She holds the household together now, and is the only one of the house who knows what the silver is for.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep Ragna's boy in the family and see him once on a Visby ship.
- **Fear:** Her husband's night walks ending in a fine, and the house on Vana turg with it.
- **Contradiction:** She scolds the maids for waste but leaves bread on the cellar step for the stray cat.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps a purse of six marks sewn into the straw of the bed, unknown to Nicolaus, for a passage to Visby.

## Work and money
She cures, smokes and sells the lesser fish, perch, flounder and smoked herring, from a second board next to her husband's, and keeps the purse. Salt is her largest cost, about a mark in four of her takings. The household's silver is mostly hers to count; she has refused Nicolaus a second loan since the Berend van Wismar bond. A bad month is a late thaw that spoils the salt.

- **Network:** [Tyde Sasse](../../people/lower_town/tyde_sasse.md), priest of the Holy Spirit: baptised her grandson and said the funeral office for her brother. The bond is warm but formal; she brings him smoked fish at Lent and does not confess to him what matters.
- [Anna Everti tütar](../../people/lower_town/anna_everti_tutar.md), spinner on Kuninga: they know each other from the market, greet by name and trade the day's prices. Helga likes the girl's quick tongue and checks her weights anyway.
- **Others:** Next door is the household of [Wolter van Hamelen](../../people/lower_town/wolter_van_hamelen.md), who buys her smoked fish; on Raekoja is the baker [Marfa Yeremeyevna](../../people/lower_town/marfa_yeremeyevna.md), whose hens she tolerates.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She is a Swede and a wife, and has seen two Danish levies and one Swedish one; she trusts none of them. For a small favour she gives fish; for a large one she gives her opinion and nothing else; to inform she would say she had not heard. She crosses herself at every church she passes and sets bread on the sill at Midwinter, as her mother did.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish at home; slow, careful Middle Low German at the stall.
- **Delivery:** Warm baritone, unhurried; she lets silences do the work.
- **Sample lines:** "Pay what you owe and go with God." "Lena, the brine is low, again." "My husband shouts, I count; the street knows which of us is paid."
- **Verbal tic:** "Ja, ja, so."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the price of every fish on the board and every fisher's debts, and who has lately sold in the night. She would trade it for news of Visby ships. She believes, wrongly, that the Dominicans keep a secret cellar of salt.

## Game hooks
- **Ambient role:** Second board at Vana turg by day; kitchen and yard at evening.
- **Interaction:** Speaks Swedish to a visiting Swede or Finn and warms; to Kalev she is polite and brisk, selling smoked fish, and will ask for a hook to hang the fish in the smoke.
- **Barks:** "Smoked flounder, fresh from the fire!" / "Mind the cat, boy." / "Bar the door, there's trouble in the lane."
- **Quest touch:** She will pay for a pair of iron hooks for the smoke loft.
- **St George's Night:** She bars the larder and sits with a knife in the kitchen; she would hide a Swede or an Estonian child before a German officer.
