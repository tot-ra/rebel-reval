## Biography
Born in 1322 in a village beyond the Harju gate, where his father Lembit held a bound strip until the manor's reeve took it for dues in 1334; the family fled to town with a cart and a cow that was sold within the month. A parish priest taught Lauri his letters in exchange for carrying water. He was hired at sixteen by Jaak Lauri poeg, who wanted a clerk of his own people. His sister Dorothea cooks in the same house.

He has never been back to the village, but the road is in his head.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be needed by something larger than a cook's accounts.
- **Fear:** A bound man's fate for his father's kin, and being caught with a message that cannot be explained.
- **Contradiction:** He is trusted with money and secrets because he never hurries, and is secretly impatient.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Taniel Kaspari poeg paid his school fee two winters back and recruited him a year ago for the Harju Kings with a small kindness. He resents being treated as a debtor and carries words anyway.

## Work and money
Wages are 2 marks a year and board. He copies invoices for others at a pfennig a line to save for a grave plot for his mother. A bad month is a missed delivery.

## Faction and belief
A courier for the Harju Kings, motivated by his father's strip of land, not by hope of a kingdom. A small favour: carry a word. A large one: stand at a gate. He would not inform. He prays at St Olaf and keeps a pinch of the home field's earth in a pouch.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with kin, Low German with guests, Latin in books.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses; never raises his voice.
- **Sample lines:** "I will say it once. Then once more." / "Sums do not lie, masters do." / "Jah. Tulen."
- **Verbal tic:** Repeats the last word of a message before he answers.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the rhythm of every gate watch and the hours of the carts. He would trade it for his mother's burial plot. He believes the false rumour that the Harju Kings have a French captain.
