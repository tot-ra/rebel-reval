## Biography
Ragnhild was born in 1320 in a fishing hamlet on Nuckö, the Swedish coast, daughter of Olof, a fisherman. Her father and two brothers were lost in a November gale in 1338. She came to Reval on a salt boat that winter and lived in a loft on Apteegi with a widow, close to the cap-maker Stepan Fedorovich, until Cecilia hired her as a kitchen maid in 1340.

She thinks before she speaks, partly because her Middle Low German is learned and partly because she was told as a child that quick words drown boats.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough for a cow and a share in a boat back on Nuckö.
- **Fear:** Hunger returning, the winter of 1339 again.
- **Contradiction:** Sharp with money but gives bread to anyone cold.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sells the house's candle ends and offcuts and keeps the pfennigs in a hole behind the hearth.

## Work and money
She earns two and a half marks a year and board. Her side trade nets about a mark a year. She buys onions and salt fish at the market, and sells tallow ends and rabbit skin scraps to cap-makers. A bad month is one when she must spend her hearth-hole coins.

## Faction and belief
She stays out of faction because her trade runs between all of them and because losing three men to the sea taught her which fights matter. She would do a small favour for a neighbour, think hard about a large one, and would not inform. She carries a cord with a fishbone knot and prays to St Nicholas for sailors.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish when alone or praying; Middle Low German slowly.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses before answers; low volume.
- **Sample lines:** "...The beer is ready." / "I will think on it." / "Ja, det är sant."
- **Verbal tic:** A long breath before the first word.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which stalls give short weight and which houses throw out good scraps. She would trade it for a bag of salt. She believes, wrongly, that a ship's cat brings a gale.
