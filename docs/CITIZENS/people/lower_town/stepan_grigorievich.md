## Biography
Born 1301 in Pskov, came to Reval at sixteen with a cart-train and stayed. The Rat took him for the Nunnery Gate in 1333 on a Hanseatic merchant's word. He married Domna Timofeyevna in 1331. In 1340 he borrowed 45 marks from Meinhard Wise to dower his sister Agafya, and the debt shapes every choice. In Lent this year the young priest Lambert van Dortmund, passing through the gate at night, blurted out a sin to him as one blurts to a stone; Stepan, an Orthodox layman, could say nothing, and Lambert has avoided his eye since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To pay off Meinhard Wise before Michaelmas.
- **Fear:** Losing the gate, and with it the house.
- **Contradiction:** He is the Rat's man and a guest of the Russian trade.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows what Lambert confessed and will not repeat it; it weighs on him more than the debt.

## Work and money
He earns 10 marks a year from the Rat with perquisites, and a share of his wife's brewing. He owes 45 marks. A bad month is a fine for a missed toll.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic dependent: the Rat's pay and Meinhard Wise's loan are his chains. Small favour: leave the wicket unlocked; large: no; informing: he would, if he were asked by Meinhard. Orthodox, with a pinch of bread on the threshold.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which carts are late and who comes in at dusk. He would trade it for his debt. He believes the false rumour that the nuns hide grain.
