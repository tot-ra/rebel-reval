## Biography
Born 1307 on the Swedish coast near Åbo and brought to Reval at eighteen by an aunt after a betrothed fisherman was lost at sea in 1325. She has served on Pikk since 1332 and never married. She brings her worries to the priest of St Olaf, Lambert, because he listens and does not bargain. She tells herself she goes for the sacrament.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Someone to speak with who will not repeat it.
- **Fear:** Dying alone and unshriven.
- **Contradiction:** Calm with children, yet lies awake with terror.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has not yet confessed the thing that sends her to the priest.
