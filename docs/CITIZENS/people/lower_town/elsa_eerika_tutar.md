## Biography
Born about 1309 near Rannamõisa on the coast, the daughter of Eerik, a fisherman who died in the wars of 1313. Brought to town as a girl of nine by an aunt who washed for the Dominicans. She worked for three households before the van Soests took her on seven years ago. A husband, a carter, left for the Harju roads and was never heard of; she does not discuss him.

She lives in a corner of the wash-house and calls the whole house "ours" without irony.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be paid on the day it is due, and to be asked her opinion once.
- **Fear:** Her hand failing, and with it the lye-tub.
- **Contradiction:** Uncomplaining in work, merciless in the privacy of her tongue about the family's habits.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has kept two silver pfennigs sewn into her hem since Lent for her own burial.

## Work and money
Two marks a year and board, paid at Martinmas. Tools: lye-brush, beetle, copper kettle, hatchet. She owes nobody and is owed three weeks' wages in kind. A bad month is a split copper.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A woman with no kin and one pair of hands cannot afford loyalties. She would pass a small word, never a large one, and inform on nobody. She hangs a sprig of rowan over the wash-house door and attends Mass.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with friends; short German with the house.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, low, blunt.
- **Sample lines:** "The water is hot." "Ära kiirusta, do not hurry me." "Wood is wood."
- **Verbal tic:** Clears her throat before speaking.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who does not pay their washerwoman and who bleeds in the wash. She would trade it for a warm cloak. She believes, wrongly, that the well at the Sauna is cursed.
