## Biography
Born in 1323, the son of an Estonian carter, Hindrek was taught Latin by a Dominican lay brother who found him sweeping the friary. At fifteen he was bound to a Pikk clerk, who trained him and later also trained Eylard van Groninghe. He moved to Albert's counting room as journeyman at nineteen. His German remains stiff; he gets by with trade phrases and the clerks' help.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To become a notary's scribe.
- **Fear:** Being found out as poor at German.
- **Contradiction:** A cheerful face over a deep anxiety about his speech.
