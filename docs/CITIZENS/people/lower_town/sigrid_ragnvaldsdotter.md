## Biography
Born 1286 in Kalmar, daughter of a rope-maker, Ragnvald. Came to Reval at nineteen with a Swedish skipper's household and stayed when it left. Married Jakob Aino poeg in 1327; their Judit came in 1330. She has spun for three merchants' wives since 1320, all in the same lane. She noticed the boundary stake between her yard and Gerburg van Soest's had moved sometime after the winter of 1337; Gerburg's father, who moved it, is dead, and the family is wealthy.
