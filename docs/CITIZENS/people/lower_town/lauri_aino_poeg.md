## Biography
Born 1332, the first son after two lost babies. He has been fed by sailors' scraps since he could walk and knows the Danes from the Lübeckers by their stems. Carries messages for the fishmonger on the corner and keeps the pennies in his mouth. His father hopes he will be a ship's boy; his mother wants him to learn a trade.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be a ship's boy, to see the Sound.
- **Fear:** A flogging for losing a message.
- **Contradiction:** Brags of leaving, stays to feed Made.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Sleeps some nights in a beached boat.
