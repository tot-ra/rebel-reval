## Biography
Born in 1316 to Laurents, a Harju salt-carrier, Magdalena spun for a Lai-lane weaver until Albert noticed her in 1336, rubbing a bloody thumb. They married at St Olaf's the next spring. She kept the habit of spinning at the door because her hands go restless without it, and the maids learned long since not to object. Taniel was born in 1339, Elisabet this winter. She runs the keys, the larder and the maids' wages and has been the one steady thing in a house whose master vanishes some nights.

## Motivation

## Work and money
She spins 2 skeins a day for the household and sells nothing; her real work is housekeeping, about 3 marks a month for bread, ale and wages. A bad month is a grain price rise.

## Faction and belief
No faction. Her politics are her keys: a rising would cost her stores. She would give small aid, refuse a large risk and would not inform. She keeps Catholic feasts and rubs the hearth with ash on Midsummer.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows what each maid steals and who in the lane is behind on rent. Would trade it for a discreet midwife. Believes the false rumour that a left-handed spinner brings luck.
