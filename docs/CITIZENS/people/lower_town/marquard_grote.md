## Biography
Born 1323 in Reval, the son of a minor Kindergilde merchant who died young. He was apprenticed at fourteen to Frederik and has been in the counting-room since. He learned Latin from a Dominican. He attended his father's funeral and a second funeral at which a whisper drew him in. He believes the guild order protects men like his father.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A partnership and his father's name restored.
- **Fear:** Poverty and a master's displeasure.
- **Contradiction:** Precise and measured, yet keeps secrets for friends.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has made fair copies of a letter he was not meant to see.

## Faction and belief
Core Hanseatic from gratitude and prudence: the guild made his father's name and will make his. A small favour he does; a large one he carries; informing he does as a clerk. Devout, with a psalter he reads before sleep.
