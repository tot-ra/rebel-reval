## Biography
Magdalena was born in 1319 near Reval, daughter of a carter's hand. Her mother died in the winter of 1331; the hospital of the Holy Spirit fed the family on a merchant's bequest. Since then she believes the guilds feed the poor and the manors do not. She came to Paul's house in 1337.

Her brother Rein works in the same house. A year ago they stood at the same funeral of a merchant's widow, and she heard a whisper about the cell.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be useful to the council that fed her family.
- **Fear:** Fire in the Lower Town and manor men at the gate.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to the Hansa, yet fond of the house that may be betraying it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has noticed Paul's late visitors and has not yet decided to tell.

## Faction and belief
She is Hanseatic because the guilds fed her family. A small favour she does, a large one she weighs, and informing she may do, unhappily. She says an extra Ave for the Rathaus.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with her brother; Middle Low German otherwise.
- **Delivery:** Flat monotone, no rise or fall.
- **Sample lines:** "The council fed us." / "I heard it, I did not like it." / "Jumal hoidku."
- **Verbal tic:** "Noted."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who visits Paul after curfew. She would trade it for the hospital's good word. She believes, wrongly, that the Harju men will burn the Rathaus.
