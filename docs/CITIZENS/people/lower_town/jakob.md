## Biography
Born 1308 on the coast, son of a net-maker. He came to Reval at fifteen to learn sailmaking from a Swede. After a failed harvest last year he bought grain from Thomas Blyde on credit. He rents a shed from Lambrecht van Minden for his canvas and firewood. His lodger Kaur spins the flax for his thread.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear Blyde's grain debt before winter.
- **Fear:** Interest unspoken and heavy.
- **Contradiction:** A thin voice, a heavy temper.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has said nothing of the rotten spars in the shed.
