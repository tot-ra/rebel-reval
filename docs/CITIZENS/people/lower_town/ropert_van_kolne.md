## At a glance
- Hears the parish's German confessions and some of its fears.
- Cuts goose quills and mixes oak-gall ink for sale.
- Speaks in a thin reedy voice that carries oddly in the nave.
- Lonely, and a little afraid.

## Biography
Born about 1284 in Cologne, a tanner's son, and ordained in 1310. He came to Reval in 1319 after the great fire of a Cologne parish, and has been chaplain at St Olaf's for twenty-four years. He buried half of the 1339 hunger. He has never been a vicar, and knows why. He writes a clear hand and is often asked to draw up wills for sailors.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A quiet death in the parish house.
- **Fear:** Dying unshriven, himself.
- **Contradiction:** Strict in the confessional, soft in the sacristy.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He doubts that restitution cleans the soul, but demands it anyway.

## Work and money
He draws about 5 marks a year from the parish dues, plus will-writing fees of a few schillings each. He cuts and sells quills for a pfennig a bundle, and sells ink in horn pots. Once with Lambrecht Wise he bought a lot of oak-galls and parchment skins, and they split it to beat the price.

## Faction and belief
Church core, tired and honest. A small favour: a will. A large one: sanctuary. Informing: never. He keeps old Rhenish saints' days.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Latin at the altar; Low German everywhere else.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy, slowly rising.
- **Sample lines:** "Pay what you owe first, daughter." / "A bundle of quills, one pfennig." / "Herre, erbarme dich." (Lord, have mercy.)
- **Verbal tic:** Says "Well then" before bad news.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who made restitution and for what. He believes the bishop will send a visitor after Pentecost.
