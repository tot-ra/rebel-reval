## Biography
Born in 1307 in Reval, the son of a carter named Villem. He broke his nose at fifteen when a horse kicked in a stall; it was set by a barber who laughed. He came to Siim's stable in 1331. He has no family left in town and does not speak of the rest.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be master of a stable of his own.
- **Fear:** A lame horse and a ruined name.
- **Contradiction:** Gentle to horses, rough with young rivals.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has watched Thomas Overdyk's cart and thinks he sees stolen oats, but has not told Siim.

## Work and money
A mark a year and board; fees on hired horses. Tools: curry-comb, hoof-pick, pitchfork. He owes one mark to a farrier. A bad month is a lame horse.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A servant must please a master. He would do a small favour and inform on a thief only if sure. He keeps the horse-saints and spits for luck.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows each horse in the lane by its hoofbeat. He would trade it for a stall. He believes, wrongly, that the Vogt is buying every horse for the Danes.
