## Biography
Ilsabe was born in 1310 in a village near Kampen, came north with a salt-dealer's household in 1328 and was left in Reval when he died. She worked in two kitchens before Cecilia took her in 1336. Her husband, a carter, drowned in the harbour in 1334, and she has not looked for another.

She is the one who knows the street: who buys what, who is ill, who has a new hat.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy a small plot with a hearth before she is too old for the basket.
- **Fear:** The chill of an empty larder.
- **Contradiction:** She quarrels with everyone at the market yet gives away her last onion.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She skims a few pfennigs from every purchase, to hide them in a stocking.

## Work and money
She earns three marks a year and board; the skim brings in another half mark. She buys at Vana turg and the fish-landing; sells nothing. A bad month is one where she must pay full price for rye.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction because the market is her country. She trusts people who pay on time. A small favour she does for a fair word, a large one never, and informing she would not do for free. She hangs a St Martha medal in the kitchen.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who is in debt by what they buy. She would trade it for a good fish. She believes, wrongly, that Lübeck is sending ships with cheap salt.
