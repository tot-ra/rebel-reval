## Biography
Born 1326 in the Laboratooriumi house, the second of three living sisters. She learned to spin on her mother's knee and then at the German houses where Elin delivers wash. At fifteen she took a spinning place with a cloth-weaver's family, and that spring a German maid called [Grete Hovesche](grete_hovesche.md) gave her a pair of mended shoes when her own split in the snow, and then a name to pass a message to. A year later she knows more than the maid does about who in the lane listens. She now helps spin for two households and sits at her father's table with her mouth shut.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be needed and trusted by people who can do something with what she knows.
- **Fear:** Her father learning, and the humiliation of the shoes being called a debt.
- **Contradiction:** Hates owing a kindness, yet passes news to people she barely likes to pay it off.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She holds the circle's way of meeting after mass, and the merchant [Lambrecht Stenhus](lambrecht_stenhus.md) waits on her sign.

## Work and money
She earns about 2 pfennig per day-skein from a weaver and hands most to Elin. Flax comes from Pskov traders at the market; wool from the Harju fells. A bad month is a late weaver; she then borrows from her mother. She owes no silver, only the debt she believes she owes Grete.

## Faction and belief
Her Hanseatic leaning is the loyalty of a guild household: the town's order feeds her father, and she wants it kept from the knights and the hill. A small favour she carries; a large one she weighs coldly; informing she would do if it kept her family safe. She keeps St Olaf's feast and ties a red thread on the cradle post.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; quick market Low German outside.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, words tumbling, then sudden silence.
- **Sample lines:** "Not today, mistress." / "I heard nothing. I never do." / "Kuule, tell them the wool is late."
- **Verbal tic:** "Quick, quick," at the end of a sentence.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who orders what thread, therefore who is mourning, marrying or hoarding. She would trade it for an end to her debt. She falsely believes the Order's riders will not harm Estonian girls.
