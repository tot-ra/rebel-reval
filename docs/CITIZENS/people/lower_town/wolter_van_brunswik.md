## Biography
Wolter was born in 1278 in Reval, the son of a Brunswick merchant and his Estonian housekeeper whom he married late; the boy was nursed and raised in Estonian and learned German from his father's clerks. He went to Novgorod at sixteen and returned at thirty with a fortune in furs and a limp that has healed. He was elected councillor in 1322. His sons died in the fever of 1334, leaving only the clerks he has trained as his heirs. He is, by the lights of Vene, the most respected old man on the street, and the most careful.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the house's trade alive through whatever comes after the spring.
- **Fear:** A siege that closes the Novgorod road, or a mob in his counting-room.
- **Contradiction:** A councillor of the Hanseatic town who sends his servants to Estonian priests.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has begun to share what he hears with a small circle of others who also want the trade to outlive whoever holds the walls.

## Work and money
He trades furs, wax and cloth with Novgorod and Lübeck, and lends silver to younger merchants at modest interest. A good year clears eighty marks. He keeps a ledger in a hand no one else reads. A bad month is a closed road or a sunk cog.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic core, by seat and by habit. His own reason is that trade must outlast whoever holds the walls; his branch is hedging, not loyalty. For a small favour he would write a letter; for a large one he would test the asker first; to inform he would lend, not give. He keeps the Estonian rite of a pinch of salt on the lintel and a Latin psalm at night.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Latin in council and prayer; Low German in a lilt; Estonian to the kitchen.
- **Delivery:** Sing-song, with an old man's economy.
- **Sample lines:** "A bale is a bale, and a word is lighter." "Sit, boy, the quill will wait." "I did not say I agreed. I said I heard."
- **Verbal tic:** "As the sea goes."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows Novgorod prices, the council's votes and who is hedging. He would trade it for a safe road. He believes, wrongly, that the Order will not enter Reval.
