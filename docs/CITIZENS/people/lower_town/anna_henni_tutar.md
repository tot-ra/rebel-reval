## At a glance
- The maid who does the water, the wash and the slops, and never seems to sit.
- Known on the lane for refusing to buy a thing she has not tested with her teeth.
- Speaks in a flat murmur; strangers think she is ill, and she is only tired.
- Is not in anyone's faction because she has no hour to spare for it.

## Biography
Born 1314 on a manor near Jüri in Harju to a labourer named Henn, who sent her to town at fourteen when the harvest failed, to eat at someone else's table. She has served three houses on Pikk and Lai; the longest, this one, since 1337. She was promised to a carter in 1338 who left for Pärnu and never wrote, and she cannot read in any case. She has put away three marks in a stocking behind the wash-house, hoping to buy a plot of her own in the suburb one day. She lives in the kitchen loft with two other maids.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A hearth of her own and a roof that cannot be taken.
- **Fear:** Dismissal in a lean month, with no one to take her in.
- **Contradiction:** Counts every pfennig on tallies in her head, yet cannot pass a beggar without giving the heel of bread.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She saw Lauri come in at dawn with his cap hidden and a stranger's cloak, and kept silent.

## Work and money
Wages are 4 marks a year with board, paid at Michaelmas; she has already spent a quarter on shoes. Savings of three marks hide in a stocking. A bad month is a short-tempered mistress and a docked wage.

## Faction and belief
None; she has no time and no one who has asked. If asked for a small favour she will carry a message; a large one she cannot afford; informing would end her place if found out. She keeps St Olaf's feast and leaves a bowl of milk on the sill on St John's eve.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with the other servants; short, correct Middle Low German to masters.
- **Delivery:** A flat, tired murmur; trails off at the end of sentences.
- **Sample lines:** "Water's cold again, and the fire's out." / "Ask Magdalena, she's honest." / "Ma ei oska öelda. I cannot say."
- **Verbal tic:** "If there's time."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who in the house is awake at night, and what each person eats. She would trade it for a day off. She believes salt thrown into the fire keeps the plague away, which is false.
