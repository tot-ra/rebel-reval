## Biography
Born 1287 in the harbour, a cooper's daughter; she served in a Harju road inn from the age of twelve. She married Tõnis in 1326 and has carried the taproom ever since. She lost two infants and raised Lauri. In 1339 she threw a German merchant's apprentice out for a groping hand and the Rat fined her; she has not forgotten.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Lauri married to a decent girl and the house paid off.
- **Fear:** A search of the cellar by the Rat's men.
- **Contradiction:** Hard to customers, soft to beggars at the back door.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Knows exactly what sits beneath the third barrel.
