## Biography
Born in 1268 in a village near Rävala, the son of Matt, a fence-maker. He came to town after the 1313 troubles and set stakes for the Rat's surveyors for thirty years, until a fall from a ladder in 1338 broke his hip. The Holy Spirit house took him in as a poor man of the parish; he sleeps on a straw pallet in the men's hall. He carves spoons and whistles, and has a small savings of 4 marks hidden in the thatch.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be useful and to die in the hall with his name said.
- **Fear:** Being moved to the pit for the dead without a priest.
- **Contradiction:** He owes the house his bed yet keeps his savings hidden.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows a boundary stake behind the hospital was moved by a neighbour's father.

## Faction and belief
None; he is old and dependent. For a small favour he would carve a whistle; for a large one he would refuse; he would not inform. He prays in Estonian and leaves a crust under the hearth for the house-spirit.
