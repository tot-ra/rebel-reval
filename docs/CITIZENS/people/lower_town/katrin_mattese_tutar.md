## Biography
Katrin was born in 1310 in a fishing hamlet near Merivälja, the daughter of Matti, a netmaker. A hard winter in 1326 sent her to the town, and she took service with a Rataskaevu widow who taught her to scrub, mend and bargain in German. She has been in the van Munster house since 1337, when the young merchant took it over. She has never learned letters, but she counts coins faster than the clerks. The loft she sleeps in was built against the tavern wall, and the chimney there has smoked since the winter before last.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A dry loft and a warm winter, and a small purse of schillings for a cottage near Merivälja.
- **Fear:** A fire; a quarrel that costs her the place.
- **Contradiction:** She hates the smoke yet cannot complain; she knows whose chimney it is and says nothing.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden three marks in a stocking behind a loose beam.

## Work and money
She earns about 2 marks a year, a pair of shoes and her keep, and sells knotted herring nets in winter for a few pfennigs. Her stocking holds three marks she has not told anyone about. A bad month is a loft with wet bedding, or a stolen kerchief.

She leaves a loaf on his step at Candlemas, so he cannot say he was not warned.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She stays out of politics because an Estonian maid who speaks in a merchant's house is soon in the Vogt's. She would carry a small thing for Magnus, but nothing for a stranger. She says her Paternoster in Estonian and burns a pinch of rye for the household hearth-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian in the loft and market; Low German with the household.
- **Delivery:** Sing-song, soft, short sentences.
- **Sample lines:** "The smoke knows its way." "I did not hear." "Ei oska öelda." (I cannot say.)
- **Verbal tic:** Coughs before speaking.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the house's comings and goings and which stairs creak. She would trade it for a dry bed. She believes the false rumour that the tavern chimney is to be raised at the council's cost.
