## At a glance
- A knife-smith who has become a small lender, and whose stoop shows both trades.
- Speaks only Swedish; customers bring a child or a maid to translate.
- Sells blades at a fair mark and lends the proceeds to those who ask politely.
- Keeps three generations under one roof on the slope of Pikk jalg.

## Biography
Born in 1292 in Visby on Gotland, the son of [Birger Magnusson](birger_magnusson.md), who left after the war between town and countryside. He apprenticed with a Visby cutler and came to Reval in 1318 with a chest of tools. He married [Ulrika Jönsdotter](ulrika_jonsdotter.md) in 1320. He set up the forge on Pikk jalg in 1322 and has taught his son Knut the trade. He has never learned Low German beyond the numbers. Now he lends more than he forges.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see his son Knut a master and the house debt-free.
- **Fear:** A bad harvest that sinks his debtors.
- **Contradiction:** He is a hard bargainer at the anvil and a soft touch at the table.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He lent 30 marks to a crown clerk without a witness.

## Work and money
He sells knives for 4 to 8 pfennigs apiece and holds bonds worth 36 marks. A bad month means no iron and unpaid debtors.

## Faction and belief
None; he lends to all sides and wants to be left alone. For a small favour he would make a knife; for a large one a loan; he would not inform. He prays at St Olaf and keeps Midsummer fire.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish at home; numerals and gesture with the Germans.
- **Delivery:** Nasal and slow, with a rising tone for questions.
- **Sample lines:** "A good blade holds an edge." "Jag väntar, I wait." "Pay when you can."
- **Verbal tic:** Tests an edge with his thumb.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who owes whom on Rataskaevu and Pikk jalg. He would trade it for peace. Believes falsely that the crown will cancel all debts to Swedes.
