## Biography
Born in 1329 in the Sauna street house, Mikk grew up behind Madis and has been pushed out of every game since. He took to the casks at nine and to the stable next door at ten, where the ostler's horses let him sit in the straw. He was baptised at St Olaf's and knows the Estonian of the lanes and a plain Low German. He trusts animals and dislikes crowds. He is the one the neighbours send for when a horse is down.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To work with horses, not casks, and to be paid for it.
- **Fear:** Being left to the brew-room for life, and Madis's sarcasm.
- **Contradiction:** He is the strongest boy in the household and cries over a lame colt.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has been taking a heel of the household's bread to a stray dog in the lane.

## Work and money
He earns board and a farthing a day, and keeps what customers tip, a pfennig or two. He owns a pocket-knife and a button-bone. A bad month is a broken hoop.
