## Biography
Born in 1314 in a village near the Narva ford where Russian, Votic and Estonian were all spoken in a lane; she picked up the Estonian cadence there. She came to Reval at twenty with her sister Irina in the train of a Pskov merchant, and married Gavril in 1337. Stepan was born in 1338, Agafya last spring. She spins for the house and for sale.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A quiet house and a safe road for her children.
- **Fear:** An accusation against Gavril.
- **Contradiction:** She distrusts the Germans yet speaks their tongue best of anyone in the house.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hears Gavril leave after curfew and prays rather than asks.
