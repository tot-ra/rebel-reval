## Biography
Kersten was born in Reval, the son of a notary's assistant who was fined by the viceroy's bailiff for an unpaid gate due in 1335 and died in debt the next year. He was apprenticed to Segebode at fifteen on the strength of his hand. He sleeps in the counting room and has not slept eight hours together since. He serves the circle because he saw what a toll can do, and because he likes being the one who knows.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be Segebode's heir in the counting room and pay off his late father's name.
- **Fear:** A mistake in a list that sends someone to the Vogt.
- **Contradiction:** He hates being obliged, and runs a web made of small obligations.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a coded list of the circle in the lining of his tablet case.

## Work and money
Segebode pays him five marks a year, board and a tunic. Kersten spends nothing and sends two marks to his sister.

## Faction and belief
Kersten believes in order, and the Hanse supplies it. A small favour he does at once; a large one he writes down; informing he does only when a name is already doomed. He prays quickly and without feeling.

## Voice
- **Registers:** German at table; Latin in letters.
- **Delivery:** A flat tired murmur that never rises.
- **Sample lines:** "That is not on the list." / "I saw nothing." / "Ja, ja. Another one."
- **Verbal tic:** "As written."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every toll figure and every name in the circle. He believes the viceroy's clerks sell the tolls to Dorpat.
