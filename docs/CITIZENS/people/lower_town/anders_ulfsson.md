## At a glance
- A pale, narrow man with a moustache and chin beard and ears that stand out from his head, which he hides under a cap.
- Speaks slowly with long pauses, which makes merchants think he is deciding and clerks think he is asleep.
- Has never been wrong on a column of figures.
- Walks home from church with a spinner twice his age.

## Biography
Anders was born in 1315 in Stockholm, the son of a customs-clerk, and sent to Reval at eighteen when his father died, to learn the Hansa trade. He spent a year at Visby learning Low German before he arrived. [Wolter van Brunswik](../../people/lower_town/wolter_van_brunswik.md) took him in 1338 as a clerk for the Swedish and Gotland accounts, and he has kept them since. He is slow, correct and silent. He lives in a loft and keeps no company but the ledger and one Sunday walk.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be made factor of a Visby house and return to Sweden with a pension.
- **Fear:** A clerical error that costs his master a cargo.
- **Contradiction:** He is slow in speech and fast with figures.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a second private ledger of the house's debts, in Swedish, in case he is ever asked.

## Work and money
He earns his board, a gown at Christmas and two marks a year, and saves one. He owes nothing and is owed four pfennigs by a sailor. A bad month is a sunk cog from Visby.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A Swede in a German house watches and says nothing. For a small favour he would check a column; for a large one he would pause for a long time; to inform he would forget the Low German word. He says the Swedish paternoster and keeps a St Birgitta medal.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish in prayer; slow Low German in the house; Latin on paper.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses before each clause.
- **Sample lines:** "The sum is... correct." "I will check it again, if you wish." "That is... not how it was written."
- **Verbal tic:** "Hm."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows Gotland prices, Swedish ship dates and the house's debts. He would trade it for a Visby post. He believes, wrongly, that the Swedish king will send a fleet.
