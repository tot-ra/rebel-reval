## Biography
Born in 1310 in a village near Maardu, the daughter of Villem. She came to town at fifteen after a bad harvest and has served three houses. Jaan's household took her in 1338 after the death of her husband, a boatman, in the roads. She sleeps by the kitchen hearth.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A bed of her own and a day she is not called.
- **Fear:** Dismissal and the road.
- **Contradiction:** She hates the work and would not leave the children.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has opened the locker under the floor and closed it, and is trying to forget what she saw.
