## Biography
Born in 1329, the eldest of Albert van Paderborne's ([card](albert_van_paderborne.md)) children, Gerburg was set to wash linen at six and to carry baskets at ten. Her mother Taleke ([card](taleke_van_revele_westfal.md)) lets her keep a pfennig a week. She is clever with numbers and has been told that a girl does not need more. She met Kunigunde, the retail trader, at the church door in Easter week and now walks home with her, and from her hears that the coif-makers' trade needs quick hands.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A place with a coif-maker in Pikk, before her father arranges a match.
- **Fear:** Being married off to a widower to clear a bond.
- **Contradiction:** Claims she hates the house, and hides crusts for the lame kitten in the loft.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has seen her father's tin box and knows how many bonds are in it.

## Work and money
She earns no wage, only a pfennig a week and her keep; her mother's washing and her own baskets bring in a mark a month. A bad month is one where the linen is late.
