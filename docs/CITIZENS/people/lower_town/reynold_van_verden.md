## Biography
Born on Pikk in 1329, the eldest. A chaplain saw his quick Latin at seven and put him in the St Nicholas school. His father hopes for a clerk or priest; his mother for a merchant. He is torn and has told neither.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To finish Donatus and begin Cicero.
- **Fear:** Being apprenticed to the quay.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet, yet vain of his Latin.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He copies passages from a forbidden romance into his tablets.
