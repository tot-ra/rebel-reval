## Biography
Born 1313 in Reval to an Estonian fisherman, Taniel, who died when his boat capsized in 1327. At fourteen Jüri was apprenticed to a German barber on the Sauna street, who taught him shaving, cupping, bleeding and setting bones. He set up his own shop in 1336 in a small house on Katariina käik, after marrying Anna, a retail trader's daughter, in 1335.

For two winters his loft has been smoked out by the chimney of his neighbour the potter, who will not raise it. He keeps a pitch-rag on the loft wall as a score.

## Motivation

## Work and money
He charges one pfennig for a shave, two for a haircut, three for a tooth, and five for setting a limb. Income about ten marks a year; Anna's stall adds six. He buys razor steel, linen and salves from the market. He owes a Sauna street smith one mark. A bad month is a fever in the lane that frightens the customers.

## Faction and belief
He has no faction; he serves anyone with a collar to shave. For a small favour he treats a child free; for a large one he would hesitate; he would not inform, but the Vogt could make him. He prays at the friary and crosses himself with the razor hand.

## Voice

- **Delivery:** Low and unhurried, soothing; he talks while he works.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who is ill and who has been beaten; he keeps it close. He would trade it for a chimney cap. He believes the false rumour that bleeding on a Friday brings a fever.
