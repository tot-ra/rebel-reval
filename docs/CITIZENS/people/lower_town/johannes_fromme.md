## Biography
Born 1300 to a German shipwright's apprentice and an Estonian fisher's daughter who died when he was nine. An Estonian nurse at Kopli finished raising him; she gave him the cadence. He learned boats on the Kopli beach and set up in Aida in 1328 with a borrowed adze. The failed harvest of 1341 left him in debt for grain. He now has two lodgers who pay his rent, and he is wary of anything that might bring the Vogt to the door.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear his grain debt before autumn and finish the pinnace he started for himself.
- **Fear:** Losing the house.
- **Contradiction:** Hates quarrels but keeps every grudge in a ledger of the mind.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** The smoke from next door is warming his loft; he has stopped complaining.

## Work and money
