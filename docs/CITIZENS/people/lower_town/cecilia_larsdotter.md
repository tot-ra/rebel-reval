## Biography
Born in 1325 in a Swedish fishing hamlet at Nuckö, on the western shore, Cecilia lost her father Lars to a winter ice crossing when she was nine. Her mother sent her to Reval at fifteen with a cousin's cart-driver and a letter nobody read. She was scullery help at the Dominican friary guesthouse for a year, then came to the van Deventers at Michaelmas 1341 on a half-wage. She still speaks German with Swedish vowels and is laughed at in the lane for it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To learn to sew a proper seam and be promoted from the scullery.
- **Fear:** The cold cellar, rats, and being sent home with nothing.
- **Contradiction:** Hides her ears, yet sings aloud when she believes herself alone.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has carried a letter from her mother in her bodice for two years because she cannot ask anyone to read it.
