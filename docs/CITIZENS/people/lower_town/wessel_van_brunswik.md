## At a glance
- Big, soft-bellied priest who blushes to the tonsure whenever he is caught out.
- Keeps the parish accounts in a neat Latin hand and knows what every altar costs.
- Bites his nails to the quick; his thumbs are calloused from worrying them.
- Shares a party wall and a smoky grievance with the candle-maker next door.

## Biography
Wessel was born in Brunswick in 1305, the third son of a draper, and was given to the Church because there was no shop for him. He studied at the cathedral school, was ordained in 1330, and came to Reval as a chaplain in 1334. He became resident priest in 1339 when the old priest died. In 1337 his child, born to a woman now dead, was baptised in haste by a lay hand and buried within a week; that is the great quiet sorrow of his life. He is a good manager, an uneasy confessor, and a man whose faith is steadier than his nerves.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A new roof for the nave and a quiet parish.
- **Fear:** Failing a penitent and being found out for the old sin.
- **Contradiction:** He preaches poverty and notices every coin.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps the dead woman's name in the church's obit book under a false initial.

## Work and money
He lives on fees, offerings and a share of the tithe. A mass for the dead pays four pfennigs; a wedding, a few schillings. He owes nothing but a debt of conscience.

## Faction and belief
He is core to the Church. His belief is plain: mercy first, ledger second. He would give a small favour, a blessing; a large one, sanctuary; he would never inform on a confession, though the Vogt has asked.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German with parishioners; Latin at the altar.
- **Delivery:** Warm baritone, slowing when troubled.
- **Sample lines:** "Sit, my son, and tell it slowly." "Dominus vobiscum." "Smoke again, God help me."
- **Verbal tic:** Pinches his thumbnail before a hard answer.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the parish's debts and sins. He would trade none of it, but would share a loaf. He believes that the Dominicans are stealing his tithes, which is false.
