## Biography
Rein was born in 1319 in Reval to an Estonian carter. He learned his letters from a parish clerk and was taken by Paul in 1334 as an apprentice. At twenty-one, in the winter of 1341, he shaved two pfund off a delivery of wax to the Novgorod court to cover a gambling debt. Ashamed to tell his own priest, he went to the court's chaplain Dmitri Stepanovich. Dmitri refused absolution until the wax was made good. Rein found the silver and delivered it.

His sister Magdalena works in the same house.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A trading share of his own, and a name that no priest can recall.
- **Fear:** Dmitri telling the story to anyone.
- **Contradiction:** An honest hand with a dishonest year.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He still owes a few schillings to a dice-player at the harbour.

## Work and money
He earns five marks a year with board. He once bought oak-galls with Nicolaus Snelle to halve the price; the share lapsed after a quarrel over weights. A bad month is a delivery rejected for short weight.
