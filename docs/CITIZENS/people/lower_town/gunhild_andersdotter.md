## Biography
Gunhild was born in Reval in 1309, the daughter of a Swedish sailor and a laundress who worked beside Estonian women at the Härjapea stream. She picked up their cadence and keeps it under her German. She married once and was widowed within a year, and has served in houses since. She has been with the Schomakers since 1338.
