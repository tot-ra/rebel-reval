## Biography
Born on Pikk in 1329, the second child and first daughter. She learned numerals from her mother's shop tally and was whipped for adding in church. Her squint was noticed at three, and a priest said it was the saints' mark; she disagrees. She now keeps her mother's till on Saturdays.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To run the books.
- **Fear:** A marriage at fourteen to a dull widower.
- **Contradiction:** Precise and rule-loving, yet she lies fluently to avoid chores.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has found an error of two marks in her father's account and not told him.
