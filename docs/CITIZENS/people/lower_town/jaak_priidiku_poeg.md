## Biography
Jaak was born on a farm near Kuusalu. His father Priidik, bound by a manor's labour-dues, paid a priest to place him in Reval at nine so that one son would not be bound, and Jaak has seen his father twice since. He sleeps on a straw pallet in the forge loft beside Halvard. His mother's herding songs are his only inheritance, and he sings them to the fire.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to strike the hammer, not just turn the wheel, and one day to own a knife that is his.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to the manor if he breaks something costly.
- **Contradiction:** He is loud and bold yet terrified of the dark yard at night.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once cracked a whetstone and hid the pieces in the well-bucket.

## Work and money
He earns his keep and a pair of shoes yearly; his father's fee was paid in rye. He has no coin, only a bone button and a bent nail he calls his treasure.
