## Biography
Born about 1321 on the Pirita ferry, the son of Kristjan the ferryman. At nine he pulled a pot of boiling pitch off a hearth to save his sister and scarred his hand for life; his father said it was the only brave thing he ever did. The ferry failed after a flood, and Mikk came to town as a reed-seller's boy, then was bound to Albert at Michaelmas 1340. He learned shawm and fiddle in three years and now plays the second part at most weddings. He still sleeps in the workroom on a straw tick, and keeps his savings in a hollow rafter.

## Work and money
As journeyman he is paid a quarter of each fee, about half a mark a month, with board. He buys reeds from a harbour marsh-cutter, gut from a butcher and rosin from a Lübeck skipper, and sells small stock at a fair price: a reed for a pfennig, a string for two. He owes nothing.
