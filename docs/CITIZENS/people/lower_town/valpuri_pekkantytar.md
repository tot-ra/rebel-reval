- Small, lean and quick, with rosy cheeks that look healthier than her pay.

- Wants nothing to do with Ludolf's back-lane habits.

## Biography
Valpuri was born in 1307 near Turku, the daughter of Pekka, a fisherman who sold herring to Reval skippers. At sixteen she crossed with a skipper's cargo and was put into service in a Harju manor kitchen. She learned the cadence of Estonian from the cook there, and it has stayed in her German ever since. A manor fire in 1331 sent her to town, and she has been cook's help in the van Munster house since the master took it over. She has outlasted two cooks and a mistress.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A wage large enough to send a few schillings home to her sister at Turku each year.
- **Fear:** A fire in the kitchen, and a bad name in a foreign town.
- **Contradiction:** She is proud of her Finnish and uses her Estonian lilt to be understood.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She makes a small bowl of porridge each night for the house-spirit, which the master does not know.

## Work and money
She earns about 2 marks a year and keep, plus Easter and Christmas gifts. She buys flour from the miller on Vana turg when the master is short. A bad month is a burnt batch of bread, or a lost spoon.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A Finn in a German house has more to lose than to gain by sides. She would feed anyone at the back door, hide nothing, and carry nothing for a stranger. She says her Paternoster in Finnish and leaves milk for the household spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish when alone; Low German with an Estonian lilt for the household.
- **Delivery:** Sing-song, quick, warm; rises at the ends of phrases.
- **Sample lines:** "Sit, eat, speak after." "Salt is cheap, trouble is dear." "Hyvää huomenta." (Good morning.)
- **Verbal tic:** Says "ah, so" before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who eats what and who skips supper. She would trade it for flour at a fair price. She believes the false rumour that the Hansa will close the Turku skippers' berths.
