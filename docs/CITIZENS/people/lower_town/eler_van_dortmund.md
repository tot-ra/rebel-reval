## Biography
Born on Toompea in 1329, the son of a German scullion in the Danish viceroy's kitchen. His father died when Eler was nine; the steward's wife kept him on turning the spit until the steward found him a place with a Lower Town shop. Beke van Lemego took him at thirteen for a small premium and no wages. He sleeps under the counter and goes up the hill every Friday with a basket.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be taken on as a journeyman Krämer before he is sixteen.
- **Fear:** The word thief; the steward's wife once said he might become one.
- **Contradiction:** He is afraid of the Danish bailiffs, yet obeys them first.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He counts the number of men who come in at Vespers, and tells the falconer's lad.

## Work and money
No wage; board, bed, a pair of shoes each spring. He is given 2 pfennigs a feast day by Beke and keeps it in a knot of his shirt. He owes a debt of kindness, which he cannot spend.

## Faction and belief
He loves the hill because it fed him. Asked for a small favour he will count carts; for a large one he would tremble and do it; to inform, he already does. He crosses himself and carries a pebble from the castle chapel.
