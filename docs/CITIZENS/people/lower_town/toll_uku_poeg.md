## Biography
Tõll was born in 1270 in a hamlet near the Harju road and came to Reval as a boy during the building of the Dominican walls. He learned the lathe from an old carpenter and made bowls and spoons for forty years. In the troubles of 1313 he lost a brother to a Danish levy; in the hard winter of 1334 he lost his wife Kaie to fever. He moved in with Marten and Eeva when his eyes failed for fine work. Now he sits on the doorstep with a knife and a block of birch, making spatulas and spoons for sale, and tells stories to anyone who sits.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To die in the house he helped build and to see his grandsons fired a kiln of their own.
- **Fear:** Becoming a burden; the loss of his sight.
- **Contradiction:** He tells everyone to keep quiet and tells the lane everything.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He taught Jüri the old counting rhymes and knows what they mean.

He makes spoons and spatulas worth a pfennig each, a few per week. Birch blocks come from the carter. He gives most coins to Eeva.
