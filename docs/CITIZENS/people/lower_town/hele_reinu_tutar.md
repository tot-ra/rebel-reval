## Biography
Born in the winter of 1337, the late child of Rein and Marga, Hele grew up in steam and the smell of birch. She learned to walk on the bath-house floor and to count on the wooden tally-tags of the benches. Her grandmother Lutsia taught her a rhyme about a fox. A goose next door pinched her in the autumn; since then she has declared war.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To stand on the stove-room step and be taller than the goose.
- **Fear:** The dark under the stairs and the sound of the Rat's watch.
- **Contradiction:** She is brave with geese and hides behind her mother from priests.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides crusts in a crack by the stove for a stray cat.
