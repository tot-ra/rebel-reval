## Biography
Born in 1303 in the harbour fishers' lane, brother to Wendla. He took to horses at twelve, mucking the stalls at a Pikk merchant's, where he rose to ostler. In 1338 a horse kicked a clerk and he was dismissed, though the horse was blameless. Wendla spoke to Hinrick and he was taken into the carpenter's yard, where one mare hauls the beams. He has kept that mare for four years and knows her moods better than his own.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** The mare falling sick.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet among horses and tongue-tied among people, but a skilled listener.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows the mare was borrowed from a Pikk house and never returned.
