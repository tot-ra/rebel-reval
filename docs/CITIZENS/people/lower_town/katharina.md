## Biography
Born in Reval in 1321, daughter of a Toompea laundress who washed for the viceroy's household. She absorbed a quiet loyalty to the Danes at her mother's knee. She entered the Blyde house at sixteen.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A place on Toompea herself, one day.
- **Fear:** The mob and the council's rough men.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to a crown that never knew her name.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She carries news to a falconer's boy on Toompea each week.

## Faction and belief
Her loyalty to the crown is her mother's. A small favour she does; a large one she weighs; informing is her work. She prays to Our Lady.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, blunt.

- **Verbal tic:** Rubs her lip.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows what is said in the Blyde kitchen. She believes the false rumour that the viceroy will tax only merchants.
