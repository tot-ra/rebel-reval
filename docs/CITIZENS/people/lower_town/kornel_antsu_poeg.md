## Biography
Born 1308 on a Harju manor, the son of Ants, a tenant farmer who was bound to the land in the flogging year of 1325. Kornel ran away that autumn, hid in a charcoal-burner's hut, and came to Reval in 1326. He worked as a porter at the harbour until a barrel crushed a toe, then took the van Campen post in 1334. His mother is still on the manor and sends word through carters. Hiding a father's bondage under a servant's name is the great labour of his life.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy his mother free, which costs 6 marks the bailiff will not name.
- **Fear:** A manor man recognising him in the market.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to the house's children and sympathetic to the people who might take it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has helped two runaways hide in the woodpile.

## Work and money
He earns 2 marks a year with board. His pack brings in another mark: thread from Kunigunde's shop, ribbon from a packman. He has saved two marks toward his mother's price. A bad month is a closed gate and an empty pack.

## Faction and belief
Harju Kings by blood and bond: a father bound, a mother held. Asked for a small favour he gives it; a large one, he gives if it frees someone; informing he refuses. Folk practice: a copper coin for the hearth-spirit; church for the children.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with carters; Low German to the master; patter when selling.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, rapid.
- **Sample lines:** "Three pfennig, no less, but it is a good ribbon." "Water's in, mistress, wood's stacked." "Ei, ei, ära muretse."
- **Verbal tic:** "Fair price."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which manors beat their bound men and which carters bring news. He would trade it for a name that can free his mother. False belief: that the Order will pay off the bailiffs.
