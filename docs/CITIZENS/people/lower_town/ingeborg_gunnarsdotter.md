## Biography
Born 1300 in Kalmar, daughter of a brewer. She came to Reval with her husband's cousin's family and married Nils in 1330. She brews small beer in the yard behind the shop and sells it by the stoup. The cold-sore scar is from a winter fever at sixteen.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A copper kettle of her own.
- **Fear:** A spoiled brew; the fever in the children.
- **Contradiction:** Whispers, but wins every argument.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She waters the beer sold to carters.
