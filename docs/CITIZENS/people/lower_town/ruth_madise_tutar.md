## At a glance
- A wiry, ruddy girl with straw-blond hair and a sing-song voice.
- Left-handed, which her mother tries daily to correct.
- Carries her father's dinner to the gate and stays to watch the carts.
- Oddity: she can name every guild's colours from a cart's tarp.

## Biography
Born 1334, after the sons died, and treated by her father as his luck. She spends mornings with [Lutsia Madise tütar](lutsia_madise_tutar.md) and afternoons at the Short Hill Gate carrying bread to [Madis Jaani poeg](madis_jaani_poeg.md). The gate-guards let her count carts. She picks up phrases of Low German and sings them in Estonian rhythm.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to ring the gate bell once.
- **Fear:** Her mother's hard hand and the dark under the stairs.
- **Contradiction:** Sings all day and cannot keep a secret for an hour.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She saw a rider cross the postern by night and has told only Lutsia, who forgot.
