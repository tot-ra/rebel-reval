## Biography
Born 1299 in a Harju village; came to town as a girl; married Evert in 1321. Her chimney has smoked into her neighbour's loft for two winters. A year ago Lutsia Andrese tütar helped her through a hard season with a sack of meal, and brought her into the circle.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A full pantry.
- **Fear:** Eight children without their father.
- **Contradiction:** Resents her debt to Lutsia yet keeps the meetings.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Evert does not know she belongs.
