## Biography
Born in 1324 in a Harju manor village near Kose, the daughter of Taniel, a ploughman bound to his lord. Her father and two brothers were taken for the Danish levy in 1337; one never returned. She ran away at fifteen with her mother's blessing and hid among carters on the road, arriving in the lane in 1339. Kadri Leho tütar took her on for her keep and asked nothing.

She has not seen her mother in four years and sends word through carters.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To bring her mother out of the manor.
- **Fear:** A bailiff's man recognising her.
- **Contradiction:** The laughing, trusting face of a girl who has told one lie after another for four years.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She carries word between the lane and the Harju villages, using the baby's cradle as cover.

## Faction and belief
Core of the Harju Kings' town circle, because of her father and brother. She would carry a message for a stranger, hide a runaway for a friend, and inform on nobody. She keeps the old rowan-sprig over the cradle and prays at St Olaf's.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with friends; careful German.
- **Delivery:** Low, unhurried, laughs between phrases.
- **Sample lines:** "Easy now, easy." "Kes seal on, who is there?" "My mother would say so."
- **Verbal tic:** Laughs before bad news.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the carters' routes and the manor bailiffs' habits. She would trade it for her mother's name on a free list. She believes, wrongly, that the Danish king will free the serfs if asked.
