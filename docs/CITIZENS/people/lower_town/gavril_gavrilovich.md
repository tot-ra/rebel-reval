## Biography
Born in Pskov in 1312, the son of a silver-weigher, he learned Latin from a Catholic monk on the Novgorod road. He came to Reval at twenty-two with a letter from a Novgorod merchant and set up a stall, paying the Rat a yearly fee. He married Marfa Ontsiforovna in 1337 and their daughter Agafya is one year old. His chimney quarrel with the priest next door is two winters old and neither has taken it to the Vogt.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the Novgorod road open and the silver moving.
- **Fear:** A siege, and a stall confiscated as foreign property.
- **Contradiction:** A man of numbers who risks all for a rumour.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sends coded notes about gate watches and grain to Novgorod.

## Work and money
A fee of one per cent on changes yields about 60 marks a year. He owes 25 marks to a Novgorod partner and is owed 40 by Pikk merchants. A bad month is a clipped coin.

## Faction and belief
Active for Pskov and Novgorod: his clients and kin are there. A small favour: a rumour; a large one: a night's lodging for a stranger; to inform: a coded note. He prays before an icon and keeps bread and salt at the door.

## Voice

- **Delivery:** A flat, tired murmur.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who hoards silver. He trades for road news. He believes the false rumour that the Rat will ban foreign coin.
