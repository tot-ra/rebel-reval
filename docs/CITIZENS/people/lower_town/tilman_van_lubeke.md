## At a glance
- A tall old man bent forward from the shoulders, speaking in a flat, tired murmur that makes the Rat strain to hear.
- Sat on the council for twenty years and now sits more than he speaks.
- His house is large, with twelve under the roof, yet his purse is thinner than it looks.
- Marks every page he signs with a small cross beside his name.

## Biography
Born in 1277 in Lübeck, the third son of a cloth dealer. He came to Reval at nineteen as a factor's helper and married into a family that traded to Novgorod. He broke his shin on the ice at the 1321 harbour, when the Rat's galley turned over. The Rat co-opted him in 1323. His daughter Hille married a Stralsund merchant in 1339; he borrowed 3 marks for her dowry and still has not repaid. In 1341 he put silver into a boat voyage that went badly. Now he is rich in house and poor in coin, and he keeps both quiet.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hand the Pikk house to a clerk he trusts and die solvent.
- **Fear:** The shame of a public debt.
- **Contradiction:** He lectures young clerks on thrift and has lent his name to a skipper who disappoints.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He is in debt to a tallow-boiler and a Russian trader, and the council does not know.

## Work and money
Imports salt and cloth, exports wax. He owes 5 marks to Ontsifor and 3 marks to Kristjan, and is owed 20 marks by Lübeck partners. A bad month means a servant dismissed and the upper floor shuttered.

## Faction and belief
Core Hanseatic from habit and duty: the Rat is his life. For a small favour he would lend a key; for a large one a letter; to inform he would give a name only if the house were threatened. He fasts, lights a candle and trusts the Rat.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German; Latin for charters.
- **Delivery:** A flat, tired murmur, slow.
- **Sample lines:** "We shall write it down." "A debt is a letter waiting to be read." "Dat is so, dat is so."
- **Verbal tic:** Exhales before every figure.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows which councillors are in debt and which skippers cheat. He would trade it for time. Believes falsely that the Order will pay Reval's grain debts.
