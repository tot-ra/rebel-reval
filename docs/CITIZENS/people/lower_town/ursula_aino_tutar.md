## Biography
Born about 1315 in a fishing hamlet near Viimsi, the daughter of Aino, a net-mender. She came to town at fifteen after her father drowned, and entered service with Albert and Berta in 1334. The scar over her brow came from a hen's spur when she was seven. She has served here nine years, is paid in kind and silver, and has never been late.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To pay for her mother's winter peat and a place in the parish ground.
- **Fear:** A mistress who dismisses her.
- **Contradiction:** Flat of voice, warm of hand.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps back one pfennig a week from marketing for her mother.
