## Biography
Born on Pikk in 1307 to the Blyde family of linen-weavers, married Johannes van Verden at twenty-one. She built a yarn trade on the side, employing three outworkers. After the winter of 1341 she kept the household in porridge from yarn money while Johannes's ship was late.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A trade for her daughters, and a place for her sons.
- **Fear:** The loss of the Pikk house.
- **Contradiction:** She hates gossip yet takes part in a whisper.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows her husband sells news, and has not said.

## Work and money
Yarn pays about 25 marks a year. She buys wool from carriers, pays outworkers 2 schillings a week, and keeps coins in a jar. A bad month is wet wool.
