## Biography
Born in 1282 in a Harju village, Anna lived through the great hunger of 1315-1317 and the unrest after, and moved to Reval in 1320 when her husband Toomas took work at the harbour. He died in 1337. She spun wicks for the chandlers until her eyes weakened and now helps with the dips. She raised Kristjan, who went to sea; she is quietly glad he came back. She tells her grandchildren the old tales about the grove and the thunder-father.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To live to see Ruth walk, and to be buried at St Olaf's beside Toomas.
- **Fear:** Hunger returning, and being a burden.
- **Contradiction:** She laughs at hardship and weeps at a spilled bowl of milk.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has an old charm of rowan and red thread for the baby, which Kristiina does not know of.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction. For a small favour she gives a story; for a large one she gives a charm; she would not inform. She keeps St Olaf and the grove-father both.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the old herb-charms and the names of the lane's dead. She would trade it for a warm shawl. She believes, falsely, that a hard spring means a mild winter.
