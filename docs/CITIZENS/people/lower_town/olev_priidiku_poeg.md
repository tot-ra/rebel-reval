## Biography
Olev was born in 1315 in a Harju hamlet, the son of a bell-ringer's helper, and came to town at ten to serve in the church. He learned the bells from an old sexton and the grave-digger's trade from the same man. He took minor orders at twenty and has lived in the church house since. He is proud of his clean church and sells wax ends on the side to buy his sister's dowry.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A dowry for his sister back in Harju.
- **Fear:** Being thought a thief for the candle-ends.
- **Contradiction:** A man of the Church who still pours ale on a stone at midsummer.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps count of the priest's debts.
