## Biography
Born in 1325 in a fisherman's shed by the harbour; her father Peeter went down with a boat off Naissaar in 1337, her mother died of the winter cough two years later. A neighbour woman took her in; at sixteen she was hired by Gesa Siimu tütar for a mark a year. The back teeth went in a bad winter, when a tooth-worm swelled her jaw and the barber pulled three at once; Gesa dosed her with clove oil and paid the barber out of the housekeeping. She knows she owes the mistress and tries to pay in noise and labour.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A dowry-chest of her own and a husband who owns a boat.
- **Fear:** The cough that took her mother, and the barber's pincers.
- **Contradiction:** Loud with everyone, silent in church.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has been giving fish-scrap and bread to a runaway boy sleeping in a net-loft on Rataskaevu.

## Work and money
A mark a year, board, shoes at Martinmas (hand-me-downs). She spends on onions and a ribbon. She has eleven pfennigs, a horn comb and her mother's iron thimble. A bad month is one where the mistress's temper is short, which means her teeth ache.

## Faction and belief
No faction. Politics is for people with time to sit down. A small favour she does; a large one she would do for the runaway boy; she would inform if she were threatened with the barber's chair, which she says is a joke. She says a Hail Mary at the door of St Olaf's and ties a thread to the well-sweep for luck.

## Knowledge and rumours
