## Biography
Born 1331, heir to the house, sent at seven to the clerk's school. He learned his letters quickly and his Latin faster. His father wants him on the quay; his mother wants him in the Church. He copies psalms for pocket money, and is beaten for his left hand.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To read the books in the Dominican library.
- **Fear:** His father's disappointment and the teacher's switch.
- **Contradiction:** Obedient in speech, stubborn in the hand.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has copied a page of a forbidden song he overheard at the quay.
