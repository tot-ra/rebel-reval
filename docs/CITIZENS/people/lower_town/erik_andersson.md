## Biography
Born in 1318 at Visby, the son of a boatman. His father was lost at sea in 1330 and he came to Reval with an uncle to learn the knife trade. He joined [Magnus Birgersson](magnus_birgersson.md)'s forge at fifteen and has stayed ten years. He listens to harbour talk and dreams of better pay. A captain from the coast bought blades from him in the winter, and he has not said no.

## Motivation
- **Want:** His own forge and a wife.
- **Fear:** The Rat's hangman.
- **Contradiction:** He is loyal to Magnus and also sells behind his back.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sold six blades to a raider's agent last month.

## Faction and belief
Secret sympathiser of the harbour raiders, from love of coin and sea. For a small favour he would sell a blade; for a large one carry a message; he would not inform. He prays like a sailor.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish with the family; rough Middle Low German on the quay.

- **Sample lines:** "A good edge pays." "Jag vet inte, I know nothing." "Ask Magnus."
