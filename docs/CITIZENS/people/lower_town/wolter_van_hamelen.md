## Biography
Wolter was born in 1300 in Hameln on the Weser, the son of a cloth-trader who sent him to Reval at fourteen to a Novgorod merchant house. He trained under a master who later also trained [Niklas Uku poeg](../../people/lower_town/niklas_uku_poeg.md), and under whom [Meinhard van Campen](../../people/lower_town/meinhard_van_campen.md) had served before. He bought the Vana turg house in 1328, took a Goslar merchant's daughter, [Gyse van Goslar](../../people/lower_town/gyse_van_goslar.md), to wife in 1326, and was elected to the council in 1338. Last year the council fined him 18 marks for salting a wax cargo past the weigh-house, and [Ants Mihkli poeg](../../people/lower_town/ants_mihkli_poeg.md) stood surety. He has not repaid it. He now sleeps three hours a night.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the 18 marks quietly before Michaelmas and keep his seat.
- **Fear:** A day in the council when someone asks who stood surety for him.
- **Contradiction:** He sits in judgement on weights and fines while owing one himself.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He leans toward those who think the council has sold the lanes for tolls, a leaning he has never voiced; and he has twice seen a neighbour leave by the back lane at night and told no one.

## Work and money
He ships cloth and Novgorod wax and furs by cog and cart; the porters at the strand are his only labour of consequence. A good season clears fifty marks; this spring the wax is held at the weigh-house and carriers are scarce. He owes the council 18 marks, which [Ants Mihkli poeg](../../people/lower_town/ants_mihkli_poeg.md) stands for. A bad month is a late cog or a porter who will not carry.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic core: his money and his seat depend on the Hansa's privileges. His own reason is the 18 marks and the fear of shame, not the privileges. For a small favour he would speak for a man at the council; for a large one he would hesitate until he saw who asked; to inform he would write nothing down. He lights a candle at St Nicholas for a brother who died at Novgorod and keeps a pinch of Weser earth in a box.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Latin in the council and on letters; soft Low German at home and in the market.
- **Delivery:** Almost a whisper; slow, so that others lean in.
- **Sample lines:** "A fine is a fine; a debt is a debt; a man is a man." "I did not say so, you did." "Come in out of the lane, and shut the door."
- **Verbal tic:** "As it were."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the council's votes before they are announced, the tariff of every gate, and which neighbour walks at night. He would trade it for time on the 18 marks. He believes, wrongly, that Toompea will not interfere.
