## Biography
Born in 1319 to a herder in the Rapla hills, she was taken in when a manor-lord felled the grove where her mother left offerings. Her mother died soon after. She came to Reval at seventeen and entered Gerdt's house at twenty. She keeps the old rites: bread under the threshold, a drop of ale in the wall, a pinch of salt at the boundary stone. She leads a small circle of women and men who do likewise.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** The priest finding her bread.
