## Biography
Ursula was born in 1329 as the second girl in the family. At nine she broke her nose when a shelf of unfired bowls fell on her in the workshop; Marten set it with his thumb and it healed crooked. She now helps Eeva with the drying racks but, since last autumn, earns bread and a few pfennigs by scrubbing at the tavern of Olof Ragnvaldsson, three doors down. She walks home each night with leftover crusts for the little ones. Her ambition is to learn her mother's herb lore properly, but she has been told to wait.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To have her own bundle of silver pennies in a pouch.
- **Fear:** Drunk men who grab at girls in the tavern; the broken nose has not made them gentler.
- **Contradiction:** She is brave with carts and dogs but cannot bear to be teased.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has seen Jüri leave at night and says nothing, because she wants a secret of her own.
