## Biography
Born in 1285 in a village near the Pirita river, the son of a man called Aino who kept a few sheep for a Harju manor. When the manor took the flock for a debt in 1306, Eerik walked into Reval with a loaf and found work at the friary well. The brothers made him a lay brother in 1309; he has been there ever since, rising before the bell to haul water and last to bed.

He built the friary's carp pond in 1318 with his own hands, and tends it with a patience he shows no human. He is left-handed, and in his first year a novice master tied the hand behind him at table. At work he forgets and uses the left.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A full pond at Easter next year and no heron on the bank.
- **Fear:** Being replaced by a younger servant when his back goes.
- **Contradiction:** A man of vows who hates waste more than sin; he will rake a dead fish out of the water before blessing a living one.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sets a pinch of the first catch back into the pond each spring, a practice from his village that he calls "feeding the water" and tells no friar.

## Work and money
He draws no wage; the friary feeds and clothes him. His carp go to the infirmary and the Friday table; some surplus goes through Brother Arnold to the market. He lends a few pfennigs to the servants, which he never asks back. A bad month is a late frost that kills spawn.

## Faction and belief
He belongs to the Church by habit and gratitude; he has no politics, only a dislike of manors. For a small favour he gives a fish; for a large one he gives a day's work; he would not inform. He prays at the church but speaks to the water in the old words.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; he understands German commands but answers in Estonian.
- **Delivery:** Low and unhurried, with long pauses; never raises his voice, even at herons.
- **Sample lines:** "Water knows who is hurrying." / "Ei ole kala, kui ta on kuiv." (A fish is no fish if it is dry.) / "A heron is a lord with long legs."
- **Verbal tic:** Taps his beard twice before replying.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which Estonians in the lane are hungry and which are hoarding. He would trade it for a day's rest in winter. He believes the false rumour that the pond's carp are blessed by St Catherine and cannot die.
