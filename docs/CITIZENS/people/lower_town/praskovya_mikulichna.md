## Biography
Praskovya was born in 1335 in Reval and named for her mother. She was a sickly baby who has since grown into a wiry child. She carries messages to the milk-women's doors for a crust, and hides from the goose behind the vat-yard gate. She has never been outside the walls. She speaks Russian at home and a quick Low German in the lane, in a thin high voice that carries.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To beat the goose and have a doll.
- **Fear:** The goose, thunder, and the dark behind the stair.
- **Contradiction:** Fearless with grown men and terrified of a bird.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She feeds the goose a crust every morning and tells nobody.
