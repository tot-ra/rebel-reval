## Biography
Born on the Finnish coast near Turku in 1319, Tuulikki came to Reval at fifteen on a fish-boat after the herring failed at home. Her father Lasse drowned in 1334; her mother lives with an aunt and receives a few pfennigs each quarter. She served in two houses on Pikk before Geseke Lippe took her on at Michaelmas 1341. She learned Middle Low German at the pump and Estonian from the market; neither tongue is hers. The Harju kings reached her through a woman at the well who spoke Finnish and asked for nothing, until a year ago when a stranger asked her to listen. She lives under the Dunkri roof in a loft shared with the other maids.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Enough silver to buy her mother a cow, and a day free of anyone's orders.
- **Fear:** Being found out, and being sent back across the gulf to a hungry coast.
- **Contradiction:** She respects Geseke Lippe, who is fair to her, and still passes on what Geseke says.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps a notched stick in her bedding, one notch for every guest who dined with the councillor.

## Work and money
Wage is about 2 marks a year with board and a pair of shoes at Michaelmas; most goes to her mother by the harbour's fish-carriers. She has about 9 schillings saved in a knotted cloth. Her only property is a wooden comb and a St Olaf's medal. A bad month is a docked wage after a broken jug.

## Faction and belief
She is a Harju Kings informer because the manor-bound peasants are people she would have been. A small favour is a message, a large one she would refuse. She says a Finnish blessing over the bread and leaves a crumb at the hearth.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish with the cooks and in the Finnish corner at St Olaf, Middle Low German with masters.
- **Delivery:** Low and unhurried, never raised, with a rising end that sounds like a question.
- **Sample lines:** "Yes, mistress, at once." "I only carry the jug." "Hyvä on." (It is good.)
- **Verbal tic:** A soft "mm" before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who dined where, and which guests left early. She would trade for her mother's safety. She believes the false rumour that the Order is already in Wesenberg.
