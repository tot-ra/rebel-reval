## Biography
Born 1311 on Lai's far end to a butcher's younger daughter and a drover; kin of Otto van Hildensem the Fleischer, whom she calls uncle and he calls a connection. Reared in a house of cleavers; she left it gladly. At twenty she was spinning for the weavers by the Viru side and, in 1331, married Nicolaus van Hamelen. Hildebrand came two years later. In January 1343 the fuel gave out, the cellar froze and she and Ede, the maid from the big house two doors off, kept two braziers alive in one lane for a month. She does not speak of that. She spins now for a woollen weaver at four pfennig the pfund.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To have a door that closes on a full cellar, and a husband who sleeps.
- **Fear:** That her father-in-law finds out about the surety.
- **Contradiction:** Tells the children thrift is virtue, yet buys the best flax.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows the sum of the fine because she found Stepan Fedorovich's tally in Nicolaus's coat and put it back.

## Work and money
Spins about two pfund a week at four pfennig the pfund, plus gifts at Christmas; spindle, distaff and a borrowed wheel. Buys wool from a Lai weaver's agent on a month's credit. Pays the lodgers' board into the chest. Her piece-work brings in perhaps two marks a month and covers meat on Sundays. A bad month is one when the weaver is slow to pay.

## Faction and belief
None. Politics belong to men with spare hours, and she has none. She would pass on a small favour if it cost no coin; she would never inform. Prays to St Anne for the children; wears a knot of rowan under the apron, which she does not call a charm.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German with family; a few Estonian words to Ede and the street.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, even, unhurried; answers a question after finishing her row.
- **Sample lines:** "Aye, it is spun." "Put that down, Godeke." "Dat helpt nich, dat is, wat dat is."
- **Verbal tic:** "As it is."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who owes whom on Lai from yarn deliveries. Would trade for flax. Believes falsely that the baker's loaves are short-weighted by a pfund on Fridays.
