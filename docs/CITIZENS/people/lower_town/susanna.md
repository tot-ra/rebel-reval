## At a glance
- High, quick voice that rises at the end of every sentence.
- Chilblains crack her knuckles each March.
- Known for bargaining at market; fishwives respect her.
- Oddity: baptised with a German name though her mother called her something else.

## Biography
Born at Viimsi in 1319 and brought to Reval at ten when her mother died. She served a fishwife before entering the Deventer house at eighteen. She learned market tongue fast and prices faster.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry a miller and have a loft of her own.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to a manor and bound.
- **Contradiction:** A sharp bargainer who gives away her own supper.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides an escaped bondsman's cousin's message in her scarf.

## Faction and belief
No faction; she does small favours and fears larger ones. She leaves milk for the house-spirit and goes to Mass.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with fishwives, Low German elsewhere.
- **Delivery:** High, quick.
- **Sample lines:** "A penny less!" "Not today, thank you." "Mu ema ütles..."
- **Verbal tic:** "Look here."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows market prices and who sells short weight. She believes the false rumour that a ship of grain is hidden at the Coastal Gate.
