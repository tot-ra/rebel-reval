## Biography
Gyse was born in 1331, the winter her brother died, and her mother clung to her. She learned numbers before letters, from her mother's rhyme, and by eight was setting out bowls in sums of ten. She dislikes the harbour smell and the porters' jokes. Last Michaelmas she found that the bowls in the cupboard were two fewer than her tally and said nothing. She now watches who comes and goes.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to keep the shop's tally in writing, as a clerk does.
- **Fear:** The loud men who come in drunk.
- **Contradiction:** She is meek at the table and bossy with Yda.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows her father leaves after curfew and counts the nights.
