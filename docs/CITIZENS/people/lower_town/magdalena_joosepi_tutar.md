## Biography
Magdalena was born in 1315 in a hamlet near the Tartu road. A fever at nine left her with a swollen belly and a stiff hip. Her parents died in the winter of 1334 and she walked to Reval, found work spinning, and rented the corner from the cooper in 1338. She was brought into the Black Cloaks by a woman she met at a funeral, and has carried messages ever since.

## Faction and belief
Courier for the Black Cloaks. Her root grievance is her parents' death in a winter the town refused to help. She would carry a message, not a weapon. She keeps the Church.

## Voice

- **Verbal tic:** Winds the spindle while speaking.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which lanes the watch avoids. She believes the Order will come before May, which is false.
