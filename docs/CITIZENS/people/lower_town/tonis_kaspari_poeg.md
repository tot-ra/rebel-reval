## Biography
Born about 1304 in Harju, the son of Kaspar, a farmer. At fourteen he came to the church as a bell-boy and was trained by the old sexton Ott, who also trained Olev. He took the tonsure at twenty, and has kept the keys for nineteen years. He does not read, but knows by memory which key opens which chest.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be buried within the church wall.
- **Fear:** Losing a key.
- **Contradiction:** Silent, yet knows everyone's business.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a spare key to the tower hidden under a loose stone.

## Faction and belief
Church core. He believes in the church and the keys. A small favour: a lamp. A large one: he would not open the tower. Informing: never.
