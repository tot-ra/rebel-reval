## Biography
Born 1320 in a farm village in Harju; his father Mihkel died of a winter fever, and the manor bailiff took the land for debts. He walked to Reval at fifteen, slept under a cart at the Cattle Gate, and was taken on at the van Campen yard because he could soothe a spooked mare. In 1339 a cob kicked out his back teeth. He has never forgiven the bailiff and has told no one why.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stall of his own, and a good horse he owns.
- **Fear:** That the manor's bailiff has a ledger with his name in it.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to the stable, sympathetic to people who might burn it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He carries word between a few hands, never written, and has never learned all the names.

## Work and money
He earns 2 marks a year with board and a cast-off cloak. Oats from the Cattle Gate carters; shoes from the smith on Müürivahe. His savings are one mark in a sock. A bad month is a lame horse.

## Faction and belief
Black Cloaks by grievance, not by oath: the bailiff, the closed gates. A small favour he does at once; a large one he thinks about overnight; informing he refuses. He crosses himself and leaves a bread crust for the stable cat.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with servants, Low German with the family and customers.
- **Delivery:** Clear and carrying, a stable-yard call.
- **Sample lines:** "Easy now, easy." "No, master, the bay was not ridden." "Hobune on rahulik."
- **Verbal tic:** "Steady."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who rides out at night and where. He would trade it for a good bridle. False belief: that the Order's horses are already in Toompea stables.
