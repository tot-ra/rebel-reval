## Biography
Born 1300 on Lai, son of a ropemaker. A Dominican friar at St Catherine's taught him letters as a child because he sang well; the Latin stayed after the singing went. He kept the Sand Gate from 1329. He married Sibbe van Luneborch in 1330; Segebode followed in 1332 and Ermelin in 1336. In the last year his wife has come home from market later and quieter, and he has not asked why.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the gate and see Segebode into a clerk's post.
- **Fear:** What his wife is doing when she is not at the market.
- **Contradiction:** Reads Latin but cannot read his own wife.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once let a cart through unsearched, and the cart is part of Gerdt's gossip.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic dependent, since the Rat holds his keys. Beneath that, he keeps his ears open at the funerals. He would do a small favour, hold a gate, but fear for his family keeps him from a large one. Devout; he sings the Salve in the arch.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German; Latin with the chaplain.

Knows who enters from Harju. Believes falsely that Danes watch the gates by night.
