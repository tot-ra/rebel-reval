## Biography
Yda was born in 1332, small and loud from the first hour. Hille tried to tie her left hand to correct it and gave up. She learned numerals from Gyse, sums being a game between them. She sings the Church hymns she hears and invents verses. She has been told to lower her voice in church, and cannot.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to sing in the choir at St Olaf's.
- **Fear:** Thunder and her mother's temper.
- **Contradiction:** She wants to be heard and cannot keep a secret.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has seen her father leave at night and told the neighbour's dog.
