- Booming voice from a gaunt chest; shushed on every stair of the house.
- Youngest of the two clerks; copies letters and runs them to the quay.
- Carries tidings from the counting room to the unmarried merchants, and thinks himself a spy in a ballad.
- Eats like a wolf and never fills out.

## Biography
Born in 1325 in Hameln on the Weser, the fourth son of a shopkeeper who could not apprentice all of them. At twelve he was shipped to a cousin of the van Minden family in Lübeck, learned his letters and ledgers there, and came to Reval in 1341 on a salt cog. His voice broke early and never settled; at the counting-room desk it rattles the shutters. [Winand van Minden](winand_van_minden.md) has taken him on for board and 6 marks a year. In Lent 1342 an older clerk took him to a supper of unmarried young merchants and he was flattered into promising to tell them what the household's visitors said. He now lives on that promise.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A name among the Blackheads, then a stall of his own before he is thirty.
- **Fear:** Being found out by Winand, and being sent back to Hameln.
- **Contradiction:** A man who cannot whisper has chosen to be a secret-keeper.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He reads the letters he copies, and has passed the Brotherhood the names of three councillors who fear the grain levy.

## Work and money
Six marks a year and board; he spends it on ink, a better knife and now and then a pot of ale. He owes his cousin in Lübeck 2 marks for passage. A bad month means no spare candles and cold hands over the parchment.

## Faction and belief
He is an informer for the Blackheads out of ambition, not conviction. For a small favour he would carry a note; for a large one he would copy a letter; to inform on the Rat he already does. He prays brusquely and believes in luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German always; Latin only on parchment.
- **Delivery:** Fast and far too loud, then abruptly silent when he catches himself.
- **Sample lines:** "Who said that? I did not hear it, I was in the next room." "Three marks, and a candle." "Dat is wol so."
- **Verbal tic:** Clears his throat before every secret.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows which councillors fear the levy and which skippers are late. He would trade this for a recommendation. Believes falsely that Toompea has already sent for Order knights.
