## Biography
Born 1325 in a fishing village near Åbo. Her father drowned in 1336; her mother died the next winter. An uncle, a sailor, carried her to Visby, then to Reval, and put her into the Zierenberg house in 1341 for a year's wage, then left on a cog. She has not seen him since. She is repaying the household with careful work and, lately, with news.

## Motivation

## Work and money
Pay is 5 schillings a year, board and a gown. She sends nothing home; there is no home. A bad month is a sick master: the maids work doubled.

## Faction and belief
Her tie to the Hanseatic order is that of the fed to the feeder. A small favour she grants quickly; a large one she makes hard; informing she might do if safety demanded. She keeps Swedish saints' days and blesses her bed with salt.
