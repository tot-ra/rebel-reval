## Biography
Born about 1313 in Reval, a joiner's daughter (the Vrie name). She went into service after her father's death, and has been with the Timmermans six years. She claims to be related to Katrin through a marriage no one can trace.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband with a trade.
- **Fear:** Staying a maid.
- **Contradiction:** Nosy, yet closed-mouthed.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She is saving for a dowry in a hole in the wall.
