## Biography
Born in 1273 in Reval to a fisherman Gerdt. She sold herring in the market at ten, pins at twenty, and thread and needles since. Married Ants in 1297. She lost two children and raised two. She learned weights from a Lübeck pedlar, and has never been cheated twice.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To outlast her rivals and see her daughter settled.
- **Fear:** A stolen tray and a cold winter.
- **Contradiction:** A woman who shouts to sell and whispers to lend.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She quietly lends pennies to servants at a half-penny a week, and hides it from Ants.

## Work and money
She makes a mark and a half a month at best. She buys from merchants by the gross and sells by the piece. She owes the wholesaler a half-mark.

She knows who buys what and who cannot pay. She would trade it for a good bargain. She believes, wrongly, that the Order will ban peddlers.
