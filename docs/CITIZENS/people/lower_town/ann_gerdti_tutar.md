## Biography
Born in 1324, Ann was the first child of Gerdt Pauli poeg ([card](gerdt_pauli_poeg.md)) and Kadri ([card](kadri_hendriku_tutar.md)). She learned to spin at six and German at the gate, where she carried her father's dinner. Last winter she helped nurse her brother Henn through a cough and sat with her mother for a week without sleep. Since then she has taken over the delivery of yarn to Nunne and counts the pfennigs herself.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see a ship sail out of the harbour with her aboard.
- **Fear:** Being married to a gatekeeper's friend and spending life at a door.
- **Contradiction:** Laughs loudly, plans quietly.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has asked a Swedish skipper about a place as cook on his boat.

## Faction and belief
No faction. Her politics is the sea. She would do a small favour for a laugh, refuse a large one and talk to anyone about anything. She prays to St Olaf for sailors, and wears a blue bead against drowning.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; bright Low German with buyers.
- **Delivery:** A warm baritone, quick and laughing.
- **Sample lines:** "Three pfennigs, sir, and a good price." "Father says the gate stays shut." "Ma ei karda." (I am not afraid.)
- **Verbal tic:** "Honestly!"

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which skippers hire cooks. She would trade it for a berth. She believes the false rumour that a woman aboard ship brings luck.
