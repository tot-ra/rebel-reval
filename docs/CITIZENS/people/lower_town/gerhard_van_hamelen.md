## Biography
Born 1322 in Hameln on the Weser, a distant cousin of the gatekeeper's line, though Nicolaus van Hamelen never says so. He learned writing from a notary, came to Reval in 1340 and entered Hartwig's counting-room. He sleeps over the stables and pays himself in tidy sums.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry well and keep a ledger of his own.
- **Fear:** Being caught short by the master's audit.
- **Contradiction:** Preaches honesty and shaves the weights.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sells wax scrapings and oddments at a fair price from a back stall.
