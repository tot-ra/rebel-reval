## Biography
Grigori was born in 1333 in Reval, the first child of Mikula and Praskovya. He speaks Russian at home and Low German in the lane, and slowly in both, with long pauses that adults mistake for dullness. His father put him to the small vat at eight, where he has learned to read colour by eye. He has never seen Novgorod. He draws what he sees with a burnt stick on the vat-yard wall.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To make a red nobody has seen before.
- **Fear:** Fire in the vat-yard, and the dark corner behind the vats.
- **Contradiction:** A boy who says little and notices everything.
