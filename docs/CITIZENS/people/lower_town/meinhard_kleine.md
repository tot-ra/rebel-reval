## Biography
Born 1322 to a Reval saddler, a distant relation by marriage of Jaak Lauri poeg. After the saddler died in 1336 he was apprenticed to a clerk, learned numerals, then Latin, and in 1341 became clerk to the merchant Siim Villemi poeg on Suur-Kloostri. He lodges with the gatekeeper's family because it is cheap. Winters have left his knuckles split.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A desk of his own and a wage of ten marks.
- **Fear:** Becoming a clerk at fifty with a cough.
- **Contradiction:** Hates cheats but copies figures that others would call tricks.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has noticed Gerhard's sums and kept a notched list.
