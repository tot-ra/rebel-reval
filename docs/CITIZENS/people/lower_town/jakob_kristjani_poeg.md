## Biography
Born in 1335 on Sulevimägi, Jakob is the first child of the tallow-boiler's house and has had a candle-stub in his hand since he could stand. He knows the sound of the pan about to catch. He spends his days between the shed and the lane, where he is the keeper of three cats and a grudge against one dog. His grandmother Anna tells him stories he half believes.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to skim the pan like his father.
- **Fear:** The fire-bell, and the dog.
- **Contradiction:** He is solemn about everything and laughs at once at a fallen bucket.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has named all three cats and told no one.
