## At a glance
- Lean, freckled, grey-stubbled, with a pock-scarred forehead and a warm baritone that makes bad news sound like a lullaby.
- Smells of tallow and smoke, which the whole lane can detect at forty paces.
- Works on borrowed tools every week to pay for his own.
- Settles every quarrel with a jug of beer and a shrug.

## Biography
Born in 1274 at Turku in Finland to a Swedish soap-boiler, he came to Reval in 1300 on a salt cog. He served the town's old chandler, married Ulrika Knutsdotter in 1306, and set up his own vat in 1315 after the wars. A bad tallow harvest in 1341 cost him his moulds and brought him to Siim Jaagu poeg's door for four marks. A daughter died in 1325; he never speaks of her.

Now he works a narrow plot of 186 square metres with one servant and an apprentice, and shares the lane's smell without apology.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To redeem his tools before his hands fail.
- **Fear:** Dying with the pledge unpaid, and Ulrika left to beg.
- **Contradiction:** A proud craftsman who works for a tavern keeper's kitchen weekly and calls it a favour.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once let a runaway serf sleep in the tallow shed for three nights, and has never told Ulrika.

## Work and money
He buys tallow from the butchers and wax by weight from the harbour, sells candles by the dozen at five pfennigs. A good month brings six marks; the pledge costs him one. His tools are in Siim's cellar for four marks, which he pays off in dipping weekly.

## Faction and belief
No faction. An old foreigner who needs every customer cannot afford a side. He would give a small favour for a beer, a larger one for kin, and would never inform. He keeps St Lucy's day with candles in the window.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish at home, slow Middle Low German in the street.
- **Delivery:** Warm baritone, drawn-out vowels, a laugh before a grievance.
- **Sample lines:** "A candle is a short hour." "Ja, ja, it will pass." "Have a jug."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who burns the most light after curfew. He would trade it for a pledge returned. He believes, wrongly, that wax from the Order's lands is cheaper this year.
