## Biography
Gesa was born in 1312 in a Harju hamlet, the daughter of a woodcutter called Rein. At nineteen she was pouring tallow in a candle-house when the pot tipped; the burn blistered for a month, and a spinner from the Harju road, [Ell](../../people/harju_road/ell.md), brought goose-fat, a bag of moss and a charm. Gesa took the kindness and then learned it had a price. She came to Kaur's brewhouse in 1336. A year ago she was brought into the grove circle, and since then she has been careful about what she says and where she stands.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be owed nothing by anyone.
- **Fear:** Being named in the church or in the council.
- **Contradiction:** She keeps the old rites and confesses to a priest who hates them.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She carries messages and small bundles from the yard gate to the grove on certain nights, and fills the cup at the Midsummer rite.

## Work and money
She earns her keep, a gown at Christmas and a mark a year, which she keeps in a bundle of yarrow. She repaid eight örtug to the priest for the restitution and owes nothing else. A bad month is a burn or a scolding.

## Faction and belief
Cult of Metsik, secret cell member, by obligation not zeal. Her own reason is the burn: kindness was offered when no one else offered, and she cannot return it. For a small favour she would pass a bundle; for a large one she would hide a person for a night; to inform she would take the cost on herself. She says the paternoster and touches a doorpost for the grove.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian in the loft and at the gate; careful Low German to the household.
- **Delivery:** Soft, almost whispering, trailing off.
- **Sample lines:** "I did not hear you." "The dipper is on the shelf, master." "Ell sends nothing."
- **Verbal tic:** "As you say."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the grove paths, who comes at night to the yard gate, and the priest's weaknesses. She would trade it for a release from the debt. She believes, wrongly, that the old priest of St Olaf has been poisoned.
