## Biography
Egbert was born in 1327, the first child of [Wolter van Hamelen](../../people/lower_town/wolter_van_hamelen.md) and [Gyse van Goslar](../../people/lower_town/gyse_van_goslar.md), and his father's whole hope. He was set at a desk at ten and found he could do sums on the left but not on the right; the curate who beat him for it was dismissed the next year. Last winter his father sent him for six weeks to the brewer Kaur's malt-house to learn to judge grain; there [Jaan](../../people/lower_town/jaan.md), a boy of fourteen, covered for his mistakes. He is now the junior in the counting-room and a regular at the church door on Sundays.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To have a beard his father respects and a ship of his own.
- **Fear:** Being asked to read aloud in the counting-room.
- **Contradiction:** He talks like a merchant and is frightened of the strand.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once weighed a short measure of malt and let the brewer's boy take the blame; he has been paying it back in small coins ever since.

## Work and money
He earns his board and a schilling at each quarter-day. He owes six pfennigs to the baker's man for a bun, and nothing else. He carries samples between his father's counting-room and buyers on Raekoja. A bad month is a stern review.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He is sixteen and wants only to be taken for a man. For a small favour he would carry a sample; for a large one he would ask his father; to inform he would blush. He says his prayers loudly and has a pewter St Nicholas medal sewn into his cap.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German; a try at his father's whisper when he is nervous.
- **Delivery:** Husky and cracking, trying to be deeper than it is.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows his father's prices and which bales come in tomorrow. He would trade it for a friend. He believes, wrongly, that the town council can overrule the Danish viceroy on tolls.
