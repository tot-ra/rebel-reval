## Biography
Born in 1319 on a Harju farm in the year after the Danish levy, daughter of Kaspar, a free tenant with a poor field. At fourteen she was sent to town after a thin harvest to earn her keep, and found a place with Wendla in 1334. The burn came the next year, and the family shouted for a day and then forgot. She has been three years in this loft. Her mother's village is a day's walk; she goes home at Michaelmas with eggs, thread and news.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Enough saved by Michaelmas to buy her mother a milch goat.
- **Fear:** A bad winter in the village and a letter that arrives too late to help.
- **Contradiction:** Exhausted and curt at home, but sharp and cheerful at the market, where she chooses to be liked.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has one mark and three örtug sewn into the hem of her second gown, and has told nobody, not even Wendla.

## Work and money
She earns a mark a year and keep, plus about three pfennig a week from the market. She buys ribbon ends and pins from a pedlar and resells them at a fair margin; eggs come from her mother's hens. She owes nothing. She saves every örtug and trusts no crock. A bad month is Lent, when nobody buys ribbon.

## Faction and belief
No faction, because there is nothing to gain and one mark to lose. She would carry a message for a friend and forget it. She believes in St Olaf's help and puts a feather in her mother's thatch each Michaelmas.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who in three lanes pays late, from the market. She would trade it for a good word with the cook. She believes the old man's wife was a witch, which is false and which he laughed at.
