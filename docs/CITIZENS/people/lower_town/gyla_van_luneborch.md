## Biography
Born in 1321 in Lüneburg to a salt-boat family, she came to Reval at fifteen with an uncle who then drowned off Naissaar. Her voice has been hoarse since the day she called for help across the wind. Gyse Hardekop took her in at seventeen. She sleeps on a pallet in the kitchen with Ermgard.

She now sells small things from the household's remnants.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband with a boat of his own.
- **Fear:** Wind on water.
- **Contradiction:** She fears the sea and always looks at it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps her uncle's tin whistle in her shoe and has never told anyone.

## Work and money
Wages are 2 marks a year with board and shoes. Her pins and ribbon ends bring 8 pfennigs a week. She sells at a fair price. A bad month is a lost purse.

## Faction and belief
None: servants are dismissed first. She prays to St Nicholas for sailors, and spits thrice when she hears a gull at night.

## Voice

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the sailors' gossip. She would trade it for a ribbon. She believes the false rumour that Naissaar is cursed.
