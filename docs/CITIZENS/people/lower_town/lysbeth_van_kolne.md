## Biography
Lysbeth was born in 1277 in Cologne and came to Reval at twelve as a hosier's apprentice with a cousin. She married the hosier in 1295 and ran the shop after his death in 1320, taking in knitters from the Pikk. She learned numerals from the shop-tallies and never needed more. She sold the stock in 1335 when her hands slowed, and moved to her nephew Lutke's house on Saiakang as its keeper. She counts the household's candles each night and writes nothing, since she cannot.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep a roof over the household until her nephew finds his feet.
- **Fear:** A pawn ticket that cannot be redeemed.
- **Contradiction:** She scolds Lutke for show and covers his debts from her own purse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has a small hoard of 12 marks, sewn into her mattress, that nobody knows about.

## Work and money
She lives on a small rent from her old shop, about 12 schillings a quarter, and a share of the household's keep. She buys wool from a Vana turg dealer and knits for neighbours at a pfennig a row. A bad month is a damp store that spoils the wool.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A widow who wants a quiet roof has no politics but peace. She would give bread to anyone, shelter no one without asking Lutke, and carry nothing for a stranger. She says her rosary on her fingers and keeps a bit of rowan over the door.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German in the house; low and husky with the neighbours.
- **Delivery:** Husky, deliberate, slow; she counts under her breath.
- **Sample lines:** "Two loaves, not three." "A candle is a day's wage." "Mine Gode." (My God.)
- **Verbal tic:** Taps her keys when disapproving.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows what each household buys, what it burns and who is behind. She would trade it for quiet. She believes the false rumour that the council will tax stockings by the pair.
