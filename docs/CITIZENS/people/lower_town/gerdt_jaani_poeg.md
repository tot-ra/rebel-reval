## Biography
Born in 1297 to Jaan, a carpenter who had bought burgher rights during the wars of 1313 to 1325 when the town needed hands, Gerdt inherited the bench and the rights. He trained under a master who also trained Hinrick Kristjani poeg, and he owes Hinrick an old, unspoken courtesy. He married Gertrud Marteni tütar in 1328. He has two sons, Mattes and Siim, an aunt, Alit, and two maids. Because he speaks only Estonian, German clients deal with his wife. He took a night-watch spear in 1335 to stay in the town's good graces. His father moved a boundary stake between their yard and the cooper's, and Gerdt has never found a good way to mention it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hand a sound business, a straight roof and a burgher's name to Mattes.
- **Fear:** Losing the burgher rights if the Hanseatic men turn on Estonians.
- **Contradiction:** A watchman who notices every silence but never asks about them.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He heard a whisper at a funeral and has told no one, not even Gertrud.

## Work and money
He earns about twenty marks a year from building, roofing and joinery; oak and pine come by cart from the Harju forests, nails from smiths. He owes nothing but is short of stone, since Kadri Leho tütar has held it back.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic by trade, with no part in any plan. A small favour he does; a large one he weighs for Mattes; he would not inform, since an informer must first admit he has noticed. A pine sprig over the door at midsummer.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; German through Gertrud.
- **Delivery:** Hoarse, slow, drops the voice.
- **Sample lines:** "Oak does not lie; people do." / "Three knocks, and it answers." / "Ma ei mäleta, I do not remember."
- **Verbal tic:** Three knocks on the nearest wood.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which beams in town are rotten and which silences matter. He believes, wrongly, that the old Hanseatic quarter will be fortified by Mayday.
