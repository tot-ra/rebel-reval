## Biography
Born in 1326 on Dunkri, the second son of Ghert and Geseke, Ropert has worked the cellar since he was nine. At thirteen a runaway keg took the skin from his chin, and he kept rolling it to the door. He learned his father's tally-sticks and then numerals from his sister. Last year he began supplying small beer to the fish-gutters of Vana turg on credit, which he reckons as his own business. He joined a circle through a sailor who drank on credit in the taproom. He sleeps in the family loft.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be the master of the cellar before his father is, and to have his own name on the sign.
- **Fear:** His father's lowered voice.
- **Contradiction:** He swaggers in the lane and flinches at the cellar stair in the dark.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He hid a sailor's bundle behind the third keg for two nights and has not said what it held.

## Faction and belief
He is an active Hanseatic youth because the Hansa buys his beer and he likes the sailors. A small favour, a keg moved; a large one, a night's watching; informing he would do for a purse. He crosses himself at each cellar door.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which ships drink on credit. He would trade this for a seat at the sailors' table. He believes the false rumour that the Rat will close the taverns at Whitsun.
