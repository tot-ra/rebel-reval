## Biography
Born 1305 into the van Brunswik merchant family. She spun at her mother's side, learned numerals from a tally-board, and was married at nineteen after a settlement her husband still pays on. She bore five children and buried none. During the hard winter of 1342 she opened the pantry to three poorer neighbours. She now runs the house with a firm, warm hand.

## Work and money
She manages the household budget, around 100 marks a year; she buys grain by the last, wax by the pfund, cloth by the ell. Her spinning is for linen shifts. A bad month is a delayed ship and short stores.

## Faction and belief
No faction; the house is her cause. A small favour she grants freely; a large one she weighs with her husband; informing she would not do. She keeps the old Easter customs and a bowl of milk at the cellar door.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the cost of every item in the household and which neighbours are in debt. She would trade it for a good marriage. She falsely believes her husband's insomnia comes from a curse.
