## Biography
Born about 1306 on a Harju farm near the Pirita, the daughter of Toomas, a cowherd. Her family were free but poor; at twelve she was sent to town to a dairy-woman on Lai, learning to churn and salt in the cold cellar behind the Dominican friary. She lost her back teeth one by one, the first to a toothache in the sour spring of 1329, and the rest to the hunger years. She married Albert the piper in 1333, and since then her own trade has been the steady wage of the house. Her mother died in the hunger of 1339, and Margareta has never since wasted a drop of milk. She sells now from three cows' worth of cream bought from Harju women.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A firm stall of her own at the Lai market, and a good indenture for Kaur.
- **Fear:** Disorder: unrest empties the merchants' kitchens and her pails come home full.
- **Contradiction:** Gentle in speech, hard on weights.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Dmitri Stepanovich told her at Lent something he told no priest; she keeps it behind her teeth.

## Work and money
She clears about 1 mark a month in a good one, most of it in örtug and the rest in kind. She buys salt by the lispfund from the harbour, cream from Harju farm women, and sells to the kitchens of the Lai merchants on standing order. Debts: half a mark to a cooper for pails. A bad month is a late frost, when the cows dry and the Harju women bring no cream.

## Faction and belief
Sympathiser, not a plotter. The guild houses are her customers and their order protects her stall from Toompea tolls. She would pass a small word, refuse a large errand, and would never inform on a neighbour. She keeps a saucer of milk under the step for the house-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; she speaks German by pointing and counting.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses, so listeners lean in.
- **Sample lines:** "Butter is... a day old. Take it or... leave it." / "I heard nothing. I... have ears." / "Piim läheb hapuks." (Milk turns sour.)
- **Verbal tic:** Presses her tongue into the gap in her cheek before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which Lai houses are buying more than usual and which cooks are dismissed. She would trade it for a clean cellar pail. She believes, wrongly, that the Danes plan to burn the Lower Town market.
