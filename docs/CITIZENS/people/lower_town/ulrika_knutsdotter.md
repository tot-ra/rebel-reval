## Biography
Born in 1283 at Åbo to a Swedish smith named Knut, she married Sven Svensson in 1306 after he lodged with her father. She spun for the Åbo cathedral before coming to Reval, and her yarn has outlived two chandlers. One daughter died young in 1325, and one son went to sea in 1331 and has not written, though neither of them can write. She learned no German, because she never needed it; the lane learned Swedish instead.

She sits at the front window each day with her wheel and her cup.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Sven's tools redeemed and her own name on a good bolt of wick.
- **Fear:** Dying in a foreign town with nobody to pay for her candles.
- **Contradiction:** Refuses to learn German, but understands every word spoken in the lane.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has been giving Marga Simoni tütar a few pfennigs' worth of flax on the quiet since January.

## Work and money
She sells wick-yarn by the hank at two pfennigs, around two marks a month. Tools: distaff, spindle, wheel, hackle. She owes the flax-seller one mark. A bad month is a rotted flax bale.

## Faction and belief
No faction. An old woman at a wheel does not take sides; she would do a small favour for kin, no more, and would not inform. She keeps a bone charm over the window and prays to Saint Birgitta.

She knows every family relationship on the lane. She would trade it for good flax. She believes, wrongly, that the Vogt's new wife is a Dane in disguise.
