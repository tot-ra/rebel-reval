## Biography
Born 1335 in the house on Katariina käik. At four he fell from the loft and cut his chin. At five he chalked a wall for his father. This winter the goose of the neighbouring yard chased him across the lane and took his cap. He wants to be a mason like his father, and has not noticed that this means lime.
