- Average height and plain, with a voice already as gravelly as a man of fifty.

## Biography
Lambert was born in 1326 in Stade, the son of a salt-boat captain who died when Lambert was nine. His mother sent him to a cousin in Reval at thirteen, and he was bound to Lutke van Brunswik. He has two years left on his term. During the hard winter of 1342 he nearly died of a lung fever, and an old woman at the Holy Spirit hospital nursed him back, and he repays her with bread. He supervises Sivert, and in the winter he taught him how to tie knots.

## Work and money
He earns no wage, only bed, bread and two shirts. His master owes his guild a term-fee. He saves a pfennig a week. A bad month is a cough, or a lost bale.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A boy two years short of freedom has no time for sides. He would carry bread to anyone, and nothing else. He says his Paternoster in German and touches the hospital doorpost.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German only; gravelly and flat.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, low, deliberate; he coughs between phrases.
- **Sample lines:** "Sivert, leave it." "Two years more." "Dat is nich mien." (That is not mine.)
- **Verbal tic:** Clears his throat before every answer.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who is ill at the hospital and who brings gifts. He would trade it for a warm cloak. He believes the false rumour that the hospital will close at Pentecost.
