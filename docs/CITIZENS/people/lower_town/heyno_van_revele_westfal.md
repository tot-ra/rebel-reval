- Tallest man on his stretch of Dunkri, and the loudest in a whisper.
- Carries a short hazel stick with forty-odd notches, one for each detour round a tavern door.

## Biography
Born in Reval in 1289, the son of a Westphalian cloth-buyer, Heyno inherited a house, a half-share in a cog and a loud voice. He married Gyla Olde in 1315 and had four children; two lived. In 1341 he married his eldest, Alheyd, to a Pernau factor and borrowed 45 marks from Tõnis Jaani poeg to meet the dowry. The marriage held; the debt has not. Last year he stood surety for Rein the furrier's fine of 12 marks and has watched Rein avoid him since. He fills the house with apprentices because they are cheap labour and a house with nine mouths looks respectable. The wind has been unkind to the rye trade for two springs.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the 45 marks before Michaelmas without selling the cog-share.
- **Fear:** Seeing his debt read aloud in the Rat, and his name beside Rein's.
- **Contradiction:** A loud man who whispers when he lies, and he lies about money.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He is two quarters behind on interest to Tõnis, and has started to notice who notices.

## Work and money
He ships rye and cloth, mostly to Lübeck, and sells salt to the Dunkri houses. A good year yields 150 marks. He owes Tõnis 45 marks at interest he will not read aloud, and is owed 12 by Rein through a surety. Gyla's laundry income, about 8 marks a year, keeps the bread bought. Bad months mean unsold rye.

## Faction and belief
A Hanseatic sympathiser, because the cog-share is his. A small favour, a letter carried; a large favour, he would delay; to inform, only if the debt were called. He keeps St Nicholas's feast and nails a horseshoe over the counting-room door.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German in trade; Latin in charters; a few Estonian words with the labourers.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, loud when cheerful, dropping to a rasp when worried.
- **Sample lines:** "Weights are weights, Berend, and yours were short." "Not a word to Gyla, lad." "Dat sall wi seen." (We shall see.)
- **Verbal tic:** "Mark me."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who owes Tõnis and by how much. He would trade this for a delay on his own bond. He believes the false rumour that Tõnis is selling bonds to the Order.
