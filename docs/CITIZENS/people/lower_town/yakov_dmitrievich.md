## Biography
Born 1329 in the Lai cellar. At eleven he was put to rolling casks; his left shoulder rose crooked from lifting. The gauging rod came from a drover who owed Dmitri for a month's beer and could not pay. Yakov lends it to Nicolaus van Zwolle when the big house needs it, and borrows it back.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To measure casks as well as his father.
- **Fear:** Becoming a stooped cellar man.
- **Contradiction:** Cheerful by nature yet bitter about the rod.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has marked the rod himself with a notch of his own.
