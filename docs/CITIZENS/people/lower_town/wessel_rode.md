## Biography
Born 1317 in Lübeck to a cloth-seller's family that went bankrupt in 1330. Apprenticed to a notary and taught Latin by the Dominican friars, he came to Reval in 1338 as clerk to the merchant Johannes van Luneborch on Lai, whose counting-house lies nine metres off. He lodges with Nicolaus van Hamelen. Two winters ago he first saw a clerk's fee taken by a Danish agent; that was the first small bruise.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be admitted to the Kindergilde and own a ledger.
- **Fear:** Being found out and sent back to Lübeck with nothing.
- **Contradiction:** Believes in the council's liberties yet spends his evenings working against its current course.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He copied a letter without being told to and burned the copy.

## Faction and belief
He sympathises with the Hanseatic line that the council should govern alone. He would copy a letter, keep silent, but not carry arms. Prays the office hours.
