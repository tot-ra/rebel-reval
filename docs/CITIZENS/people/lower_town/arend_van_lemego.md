## At a glance
- A thin, reedy voice that squeaks when he is excited about a line of figures.
- Writes the best Latin hand in the street.
- Known for supplying copied bills to merchants on credit.
- Oddity: a clerk called a "craftsman" because he treats penmanship as one.

## Biography
Born in Lemgo in 1319, son of a notary. He came to Reval in 1338 with a Lübeck letter and entered the van Deventer house. He joined an unmarried merchants' fellowship that meets after mass; it is not yet the Blackheads, but he already calls it so.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Burgher rights and a wife with a dowry.
- **Fear:** A mistake in a ledger that costs a cargo.
- **Contradiction:** Meticulous in figures, careless with promises.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has lent small sums to young clerks to bind them, and he is one of the circle's recruiters.

## Work and money
Wage 5 marks a year plus board; he earns extra copying bills. He supplies Lambrecht van Minden's accounts on quarter-day credit. A bad month is an unpaid copy fee.

## Faction and belief
His sympathy is for the unmarried merchants' fellowship, which wants a hall and a seat; he is motivated by ambition. A small favour yes, a large one he weighs, informing he sells. Church-goer without fervour.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German; Latin in writing.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy, quick.
- **Sample lines:** "Credit to the quarter-day." "I only copy what I am given." "A hand is a fortune."
- **Verbal tic:** Cracks his knuckles.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who owes. He would trade it for a recommendation. He believes the false rumour that a hall is already bought.
