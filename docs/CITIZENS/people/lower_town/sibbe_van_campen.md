## Biography
Born 1308 in Kampen, a rope-walk hand's daughter, she came to Reval at nineteen with a cousin who married away and left her. Never married; she has worked grain, fish, bricks, cloth. She lodges at Johannes's because it is the cheapest roof within reach of the quay.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A permanent place in one household.
- **Fear:** Illness without wages.
- **Contradiction:** Hoards gossip but gives it away.
