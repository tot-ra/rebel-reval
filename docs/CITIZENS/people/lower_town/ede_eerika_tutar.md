## Biography
Ede was born in 1320 in a village on a manor east of Reval, daughter of Eerika, a tenant. In 1339 a bad harvest left her father owing the manor rye, and the steward spoke of binding the family to the land. Her parents sent Ede and her brother Lauri to town that spring so that at least two would be free. A mason's wife on Harju road found them places; Godeke's household took them in 1340.

She carries a cell's whispers because the villagers who remain are bound. She does not call it rebellion. She calls it keeping count.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To get her father and mother out of the manor before the next harvest.
- **Fear:** That Lauri will say something loose over ale and the family will be named.
- **Contradiction:** She serves a councillor faithfully and would burn his house for the right reason.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps a knotted cord of eleven knots, one for each bound man she knows by name.

## Work and money
She earns three marks a year, board and a pair of shoes at Michaelmas. She sends one mark a year home with Taniel the carter's boy. Her debts are only the borrowed linen for a shroud for a village aunt. A bad month is one when the manor sends for the family's rye.

## Faction and belief
She is for the Harju Kings because her family is bound, not because of a king. She would carry a small message at once, a large one if her brother were out of the house, and would never inform. She leaves a bit of porridge on the sill for the house spirit and crosses herself at church.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with her brother and at church; Middle Low German to the household, in short words.
- **Delivery:** Husky, low, short sentences, breaks off if anyone approaches.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which manors have stewards who hand out flogging and which have kind ones. She would trade it only for passage. She believes, wrongly, that the Order will free bound peasants if they rise.
