## Biography
Mett was born in 1285, daughter of Laurentsi, an Estonian wool-dealer in the Lower Town. She married Mikk at eighteen and has outlived three children. She never moved to a wealthier idleness and spins every day. The household jokes that the maid Kadri has the same eyebrows; they know of no kinship.

She spins for neighbours at cost and gets in return a small hoard of goodwill.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Ruth married to a decent man.
- **Fear:** Mikk's sleeplessness turning to madness.
- **Contradiction:** Rich enough to hire spinners, yet spinning herself.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a purse of twenty marks in the loom.

## Work and money
She spins two pfund of wool a week and charges nothing among neighbours. Her hidden purse of twenty marks comes from selling the household's old linen. A bad month is when Mikk sells wool she wanted.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction and calls politics men's talk. A small favour she does at once, a large one for family, informing never. She puts a wool knot under the cradle of every new neighbour's baby.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; Middle Low German in the lane.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy, unhurried, ends sentences with a sigh.
- **Sample lines:** "Take it, it is only wool." / "A woman remembers what a man forgets." / "Küll ta tuleb."
- **Verbal tic:** "Well, well."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows whose child is whose, and who owes whom wool. She would trade none of it. She believes, wrongly, that her husband's sleeplessness is a curse.
