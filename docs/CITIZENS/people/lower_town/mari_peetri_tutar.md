- A small girl, small even for six, with ears that stick out under her cap and a shout you hear in the next lane.
- Known for racing along the rope-walk holding the end of a cord.
- Oddity: she is sure the tar kettle breathes at night, and says good evening to it.

## Biography
Born in the spring of 1337 in the room above the rope-walk, she was the first child of Peeter and Eeva to live past a year. She learned to walk on loose cord and to count on the knots. She has a brother, Ain, five, whom she bosses, and a baby sister, Veronika, whom she guards. Her grandfather Taniel carves her a new wooden spool each Easter.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to turn the big wheel.
- **Fear:** The tar kettle going out, and the dark behind the shed.
- **Contradiction:** Loud with everyone and mute with the priest.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden her grandfather's spare knife under the loose board.
