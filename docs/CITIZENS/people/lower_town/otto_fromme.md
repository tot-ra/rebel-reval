## Biography
Born in 1331 on the quay, the son of a German journeyman and a mother who died of a winter cough when he was two. An Estonian wet-nurse and her family kept him until he was nine; that is why he sings his words. A cousin in Lübeck wrote to [Winand van Minden](winand_van_minden.md) and bought him an apprenticeship for 2 marks in 1340. He sleeps by the counting room stove, fetches, carries and sands the ink, and has been beaten for the left hand and for the cheese.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A full belly and, one day, a coat with a fur collar.
- **Fear:** The loft stairs in the dark, and being sent back to the quay.
- **Contradiction:** He loves the maids' kitchen warmth yet longs to be a clerk like Reimar.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has hidden three bent nails and a silver pfennig under a loose board.
