## Biography
Born about 1318 on a Harju farm, the younger daughter of Leho. Her sister Kadri went to a mason's yard; Kristiina went to Pikk at sixteen. She has been with the Timmermans nine years. She learned soap-making from the cook.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy her own cow.
- **Fear:** Being sent home.
- **Contradiction:** Stubborn, yet generous.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sells soap that belongs to the house.
