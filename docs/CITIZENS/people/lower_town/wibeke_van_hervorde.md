## Biography
Wibeke was born in 1306 in Herford in Westphalia, the daughter of a linen-weaver, and came north at sixteen with a merchant's family who used her as a nursemaid. She learned Estonian from the cook in her second house. Her first employer died in 1332, her second turned her off for a missing spoon, and in 1337 she entered the van Munster household. The tremor began after a fever in 1340. She buys pins and thread from pedlars at the Sand gate and sells them to neighbours, a small trade the master tolerates.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough to stop working before her hand fails altogether.
- **Fear:** A tremor that spills a cup in front of the master.
- **Contradiction:** She is the most trusted seller on the lane, and sells at less than she should.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has been quietly stitching a small bag of marks into her mattress.

## Work and money
She earns about 2 marks a year and keep, plus 10 to 12 schillings a year from her tray. She buys at the Sand gate by the dozen and sells singly; a pin is one pfennig. A bad month is a damp tray, or a customer who forgets to pay.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A tray-seller who takes a side loses half her buyers; she takes none, and says that is her only politics. She would lend a spool, hold a note for a night, but not carry a secret. She says her Paternoster under her breath and keeps a bit of rue in her apron pocket against the evil eye.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German in the house; Estonian with the market women.
- **Delivery:** Soft, almost whispering; she waits for the room to quiet.
- **Sample lines:** "A pfennig, and it is yours." "Mind the needle." "Ma ei tea." (I do not know.)
- **Verbal tic:** Says "if you please" after every price.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who buys needles, who sews shirts for a coming journey and who stops buying. She would trade it for a quiet corner. She believes the false rumour that the Hansa will forbid pedlars at the Sand gate.
