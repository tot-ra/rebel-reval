## Biography
Born 1276 in Visby. Came to Reval as a girl and learned the tawer's trade. Boiled glue until her hands failed. Her daughter, Gunnar's wife, died; Karin stayed on to keep his house.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be buried beside her daughter.
- **Fear:** Being a burden.
- **Contradiction:** Sharp-tongued, soft-hearted.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She lent Gunnar her savings.
