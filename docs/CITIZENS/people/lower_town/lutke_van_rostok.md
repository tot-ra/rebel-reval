## At a glance
- A slight man in a stained apron whose pauses are so long customers think he has forgotten the question.
- His cookshop sells pea stew, baked fish and rye dumplings to porters and ship crews.
- Cannot read; carries every price, debt and recipe in his head.
- Owes eight marks for his sister's dowry and has not mentioned it in a year.

## Biography
Lutke came from Rostock in 1318 as a ship's cook on a Baltic cog and left it at Reval when the master died owing him wages. He took service in a merchant kitchen on Pikk, learned to roast and to salt, and in 1322 married Hille van Kolne, a spinner's daughter with some savings. With her help he rented the Müürivahe house and opened the cookshop in 1326. In 1338 he borrowed eight marks from the merchant Paul Tõlli poeg to dower his sister Gese, who married a cooper in Rostock; the match held, the debt did not shrink. Now he is a settled burgher with a name for honest portions and a habit of listening.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To pay off Paul and see Heilwig well married before the shop gets too much for him.
- **Fear:** A bad winter, a fire in the kitchen, being caught on the back lane.
- **Contradiction:** He condemns smugglers and takes the cheapest fish without asking where it came from.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** After curfew he sometimes meets a porter at the Sand Gate lane, trading what he hears in the shop.

## Work and money
He makes perhaps two marks a month in a good season. Fish comes from the boats, rye from a carter, peas by the sack. His margins are thin; he sells on tick to ship crews and has about three marks outstanding.

## Faction and belief
He is active for the Hanseatic side because the guilds are his customers and his creditors. He would pass a rumour as a small favour; he would also inform if a gate were threatened. He keeps the Church fasts and leaves a first spoonful for the hearth.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German in the shop; rough Estonian with porters.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses.
- **Sample lines:** "Hm. Peas or fish." "Dat is een Sake. Wait." "I will not say, I will not say."
- **Verbal tic:** Wipes a spoon on his apron before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who eats on credit and who is quietly broke. He would trade it for safe passage. He believes the rumour that the Order will send ships before the rising, which is false.
