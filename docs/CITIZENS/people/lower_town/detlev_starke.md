## Biography
Detlev was born in 1297 in Stralsund to a net-mender and was put on a cog at eleven. In 1329 a Visby skipper beat him and docked his pay; a Hanseatic factor on the Reval quay, who paid fair and in silver, took his side before the town bailiff. Since then he has done the factor's small errands, then those of the men above the factor. He cannot write, so he remembers: names, ships, faces, who drank with whom. The rope-burn on his neck is from a parted stay in 1336. He believes the Hansa is the only master that keeps its word, and the circle is how that word is kept.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be remembered by the Hansa when he is too old to hand a line.
- **Fear:** A parted stay, a lost post, a wrong name said aloud.
- **Contradiction:** A man who cannot read who keeps the best memory of letters in the harbour.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He recruited a young clerk with a small kindness and does not like how it grew into a debt.

- **Spring 1343 disruption:** Gate-closings and the levy talk raise his work: he relays who is buying rope and wood, and whom Toompea's men are asking after.

## Work and money
He earns about 6 öre a month on voyage, plus a little silver from the circle that he does not count as wages. He pays the berth, sends nothing anywhere, and keeps a small purse in a hole in the inn wall. A bad month is a quiet quay.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic circle, core. His reason is pay and fairness: the Hansa kept faith when a skipper did not. He would pass a message for a small favour, a night's watch for a larger, and he would inform on anyone who threatens the quay's peace. He prays to St Nicholas and carries a lead medal of the saint.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German only; keeps his tongue short.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, low and slow; rarely volunteers.
- **Sample lines:** "I saw nothing, but I would know it again." "A kindness is a rope, friend. Hold it loosely." "Dat schal so wesen." (So it shall be.)
- **Verbal tic:** Closes one eye when he remembers a name.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which crews drink with which clerks and when ships' manifests change. He would trade it only to the circle. He believes the false rumour that the Order has already hired a spy among the harbour pilots.
