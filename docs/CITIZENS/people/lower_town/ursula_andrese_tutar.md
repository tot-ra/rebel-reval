- A gangly girl with dark blond braids who raps each pot with a knuckle before the kiln.
- Known for sorting good pots from bad by the ring.
- Oddity: she speaks in a flat monotone even when excited, which makes her jokes land late.

## Biography
Born 1332 in the house by the kiln, the first child to live. She learned to wedge clay at five and to turn a small bowl at eight. Last winter she carried water and spun when her mother was ill and has been the household's steady hand since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To throw a jug as good as her father's.
- **Fear:** A cracked firing, and a cough in the house.
- **Contradiction:** She scolds her little sister for dirt and is filthiest of all.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a tiny lopsided pot she made at the back of the kiln, firing it with the others.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She believes in the fire spirit and in St Catherine on her wheel, who looks like a pot.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; some German at market.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, level, quick; no lift on questions.
- **Sample lines:** "That one is cracked." / "Father says the chimney is fine." / "See kruus on kena." (That cup is nice.)
- **Verbal tic:** Raps her knuckle on whatever is near.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which pots will break and which hens lay under the loft. She believes the false rumour that a pot dropped on St John's Eve will bring a husband.
