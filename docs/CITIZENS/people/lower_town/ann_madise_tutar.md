## Biography
Born in 1319 in a fishing hamlet on the Harju coast to a net-mender, Madis, and his wife, Ann was sent into Reval at fourteen when the herring failed. She worked first for a baker on Vene street, then came to the van Deventers in 1339. Her younger sister Liis remains on the coast. In the bad winter just past she walked home with half her wage in a fish-barrel. She lives in the loft over the workshop and is trusted with the keys to the herring cask.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough silver to buy a cow for Liis.
- **Fear:** Being turned off when the shop's money runs out.
- **Contradiction:** Seems slow, and is the one who has noticed the shop is failing.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides two pfennigs a month inside the hem of her gown and tells no one the sum.

## Work and money
Wage is four marks a year plus board and a pair of shoes at Michaelmas, paid late. She buys at market on the household's credit. A bad month is one when Margarete cannot pay her on the quarter day.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She has no spare day for politics and trusts no one who talks fast. She would do a small favour, refuse a large one and keep silent if asked to inform, because she cannot think what to say. She prays at St Olaf and pours a little milk by the threshold on St George's Day.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home and market; slow careful Low German in the house.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses, as though weighing each word.
- **Sample lines:** "Herring is dear." "I will say it when I have thought." "Ma ei oska öelda." (I cannot say.)
- **Verbal tic:** A long "Well..." before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows what the house eats and what it can no longer afford. She would trade it only for a day off. She believes the false rumour that the herring come late this year because of a drowned priest.
