## Biography
Born 1332 in Pskov, son of a carter who died on the road; his mother placed him with Stepan in 1340 to learn a trade. He cried for a month and has since learned to block a cap in an hour. He sleeps in the workshop on the felt.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To make a cap on his own.
- **Fear:** Being sent home to Pskov to starve.
- **Contradiction:** Silent with adults, sings in the yard when he thinks he is alone.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Saves scraps of fur for a hat for his mother.
