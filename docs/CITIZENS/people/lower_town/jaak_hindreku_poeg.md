## Biography
Born in 1262 in Reval, when the town wall was still going up in stone. He trained as a cooper and worked at the harbour for fifty years, and bought the burgher's right his son now holds. He lost his wife in 1329 and has lived with Siim since. He remembers the wars of 1313 as his own, and the spring talk of 1343 as another turn of an old wheel. He walks slowly and talks slower.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be buried beside his wife at St Olaf's.
- **Fear:** Dying alone in a loud house.
- **Contradiction:** A man of eighty-one who flirts with the servant of a hard-working chandler, and is embarrassed by it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has promised Cecilia the bed-chest, and not told Siim.
