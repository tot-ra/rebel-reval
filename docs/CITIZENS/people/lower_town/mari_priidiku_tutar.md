## Biography
Born in a fishing hamlet east of Reval, the fourth of seven, Mari came to town at fourteen with a bundle of linen and her mother's blessing. She served a baker's widow on Viru, learned to bake rye and to bear a temper, and entered Ell's household at Michaelmas 1338. Her roundness is a source of pride: a girl who is round in the famine years is a girl who has been fed. She sings alto-low over the pots, a warm baritone she was teased for as a child, and has learned to keep it under the stair when the master is in. She is paid in food, clothing and six marks a year, and three of the marks go home to her mother by a fish-carter who comes each quarter.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A dowry for a fisher husband, a cottage with a pig and a day without a fire to light.
- **Fear:** Losing her place to a cheaper girl, and the fish-carter not coming.
- **Contradiction:** She sings aloud in the loft and goes silent when the master enters the kitchen.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps three pfennigs in a hollow beam and a lock of her father's hair in her sleeve.

## Work and money
Six marks a year, paid at Michaelmas, less a gown. She buys herring, peas and greens for a household of eight and gets a pfennig's discount for each bundle she carries herself. She lights the fire before Prime and is the last to bed. A bad month is one when the herring runs thin and the master's temper shortens; then she makes a stew of peas and crusts and says nothing.

## Faction and belief
None. Politics belongs to those who can afford to lose a day. A small favour she gives with a crust; a large one she cannot; informing she would never, since nobody tells her anything worth informing. She leaves a crust at the hearth for the house spirit and spits thrice at the threshold when a bad word is spoken.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the price of every cask by what she hears and which of Jüri's customers pay late. She would trade it for a place by the fire. She believes the viceroy eats children, which she had from a drunk fisher and has never questioned.
