## Biography
Born in Lüneburg in 1296 into a salt-boiler's family; her sister died young and left a niece, [Telseke van Luneborch](./telseke_van_luneborch.md). She came to Reval at twenty, worked in the Amt of washerwomen, and married [Gottschalk van Deventer](./gottschalk_van_deventer.md) as his second wife. She kept her Amt place when she became patrician, which the neighbours find odd and she finds practical.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Her stepdaughters well wed; her niece safe.
- **Fear:** Her husband's temper if he guesses she hears too much.
- **Contradiction:** Obedient wife, independent tradeswoman.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Her husband's favours are listed in her head; she has told no one.

No faction; her husband's politics are his. A small favour she does, a large one she weighs, informing she will not. Devout, with a charm of salt in her apron.
