## Biography
Pärtel was born in 1322 to a Lasnamäe boat-builder, Kristjan, who put him on a coaster at fifteen. Two summers hauling lines left him with the gait and a rope scar. When the skipper drowned in 1340, he walked into Albert's counting room with a letter from a priest and a clean hand, and was taken on. His eyes tire quickly now; he reads close to the candle and keeps his face too near the page.
