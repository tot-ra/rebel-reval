## Biography
Born in 1295 in Lübeck, the son of a clerk, ordained in Riga in 1319 and sent to the Reval convent in 1325. He writes the convent's charters and says its Masses. He has buried eleven sisters. He prefers the sacristy to the pulpit, and the pulpit to the street.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To finish a clean copy of the convent's charters before he dies.
- **Fear:** A fraud found in the old rent-roll.
- **Contradiction:** A confessor sworn to silence who keeps a private list of his penitents' fears.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** One charter of 1297 has an erased line about a fishing-weir, and he has not told the nuns.

## Work and money
He owns nothing but a psalter and a cassock. His stipend of two marks a year is paid by the convent. He pays no rent. He borrows for ink.

## Faction and belief
Church, core. His root is obedience; his branch is listening. A small favour he gives; a large one he weighs by the seal of confession; he will not inform, ever. He keeps the Hours and believes in the old protective rite of the threshold only as a custom.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Latin in liturgy; Low German in sermon and counsel.
- **Delivery:** Clear, carrying, patient.
- **Sample lines:** "Tell me what troubles you, slowly." "Pax, my son, and pay your debt." "Bring it back before Pentecost."
- **Verbal tic:** Pauses and looks at his fingernails before replying.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who is afraid and of what. He would trade it for a clear copy of a lost charter. He believes, wrongly, that the Danish viceroy will confiscate convent rents.
