## Biography
Timofei was born in 1301 in Pskov and moved to Novgorod as a young man to trade flax. He first wintered in Reval in 1330. A year ago he was sick of a fever at the court and the sailor Yakov Fedorovich paid the physician; Yakov asked a small word in return, and later a larger one.

He now sits in a circle he did not choose. His scar is from a rope-cutting knife.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear his debt of honour and go home to Pskov.
- **Fear:** A search of the court's books.
- **Contradiction:** A careful man tied to an unplanned oath.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has carried one letter for Yakov and read it.

## Faction and belief
He is a sympathiser out of debt, not love. A small favour he does at once, a large one under protest, informing he would refuse. He prays before the icon and keeps a rope knot as a charm.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Russian in the court; Middle Low German in trade.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy, apologetic, drops his eyes.
- **Sample lines:** "I do not like to owe." / "The flax is good, the price is what it is." / "Prostite."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which ships are due and which rope-walks cheat. He would trade it for his debt. He believes, wrongly, that Yakov will be hanged.
