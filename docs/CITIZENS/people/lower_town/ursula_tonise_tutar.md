## At a glance
- Heavy and slow, with a voice that never rises or falls.
- Senior among the maids; the others come to her about wages, thieves and fevers.
- Flushes crimson at a compliment and says it is the fire.
- Works for the Hanseatic side because it feeds her, and keeps it quiet.

## Biography
Born in 1308 in a farmstead near Rävala, the eldest daughter of Tõnis, a tenant under a Danish vassal. A bad harvest in 1322 sent her to Reval at fourteen, where she scrubbed for a baker before she entered [Winand van Minden](winand_van_minden.md)'s father's service. She stayed through his death and the son's arrival. She learned Middle Low German at the kitchen door and quietly kept the habits of both houses. A man she loved died at the 1343 midwinter ice, a carter; she grieves as she cooks. Now she is steady and nearly indispensable.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A tiled hearth in a house of her own, and to keep her name clean.
- **Fear:** Being replaced by a younger maid, and cold.
- **Contradiction:** She serves Germans and prays in Estonian.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She carries stray words from the house to a man in the guild, for a few pfennigs and a promise of her place.

## Work and money
She earns 3 marks a year with board, plus two shirts. She sews on feast days for pfennigs and has 6 marks sewn in a hem. A bad month means rye bread and no cheese.

## Faction and belief
Active on the Hanseatic side because the guild pays her and shields her place. For a small favour she would carry a word; for a large one she would hide a parcel; to inform she already does, in small measure. She keeps the Christian fasts and leaves milk for the house-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; plain Middle Low German with the household.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, even, never hurried.
- **Sample lines:** "The bread will not bake itself." "Ma ei lähe, I will not go." "Wait, I will ask."
- **Verbal tic:** Says "so" before every sentence.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who comes and goes at the house's back door and who owes whom. She would trade it for a promise of a place. Believes falsely that the new Toompea clerk is a spy for the Order.
