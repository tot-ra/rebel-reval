## Biography
Born in 1310 in a farmstead on the Pirita road, the daughter of Pärt, a tenant. She came to Reval at fifteen after a failed harvest and served a brewer, then a baker, then the van Lubeke house, where she has been for nine years. She has no husband; her one suitor drowned in a boat at the Coastal Gate in 1336. She lifts, scrubs and carries, and talks to the horses.

## Motivation
