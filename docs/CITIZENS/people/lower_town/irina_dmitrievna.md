## Biography
Born 1264 in Pskov, daughter of Dmitri, a tallow-boiler. Married Semyon, a Novgorod candle-maker, in 1284; moved to Reval in 1290 when he took the agent's post. Semyon died in 1312. She ran the candle shop for a decade and handed it to her son Ontsifor. She remembers the Danish stewards before the present ones, and the wars of 1313-1325 as an endless cost of wax. She loves Reval and Pskov equally, and the city has taught her to say so only at home.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be buried beside Semyon, and to see Pskov's road open.
- **Fear:** Outliving her grandsons.
- **Contradiction:** Scolds the Germans and sells them candles.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps a Pskov silver penny from her father in the hem of her shawl.

## Work and money
Retired; the shop pays her board; she sells tapers at feasts for about 1 mark a year. A bad month is a fever.

## Faction and belief
A sympathiser for Pskov and Novgorod: not for politics but because they are home. Small favour: a candle; large: a message; informing: she would blame her hearing. Orthodox, with beads and a hearth charm.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Russian at home; blunt Middle Low German.
- **Delivery:** Clear, carrying.
- **Sample lines:** "I saw the Danes come and I saw them go." / "Wax is wax, children." / "Nee, nee."
- **Verbal tic:** "Remember."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who in the town speaks Russian and who pretends not to. She believes the false rumour that a Novgorod prince will arrive by ship.
