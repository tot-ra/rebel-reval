## Biography
Born in 1319 in a hamlet by Lake Ülemiste, the daughter of a carter called Paul, Magdalena carried water yokes from age eight, which dropped her left shoulder. She came to Reval at seventeen when her father died, and the Kleines took her in because she could count. She learned numerals from the taproom tallies and the Pater Noster from the sexton. She learned from a customer, the scribe Frederik, that oak galls make ink, and began to gather them on the Toompea slope. She lives in the loft above the taproom with the other woman of the house.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stall of her own for ink and soot, and her sister freed from the manor.
- **Fear:** Dismissal, and the long road back to the lake.
- **Contradiction:** She is honest in sums and secretive about her gall-trade, which her mistress would call theft of time.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps 4 marks and a wax tablet of figures in a hollow beam in the loft.

## Work and money
She earns 1 mark and 6 schillings a year with board; the ink trade adds 1 schilling a month. She owns a pouch, a tablet and a wooden comb. A bad month is a fall on the slope.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction; she watched what happened to a carter in 1337 who joined something. A small favour, for coin; a large one, no. She keeps St Olaf's feast and leaves a gall at the roof-beam for luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with the other servants; German, clipped and exact.
- **Delivery:** Clipped and precise; a sentence is a sum.
- **Sample lines:** "Three galls, one pfennig, fair." "I did not take it." "Täpselt nii." (Exactly so.)

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows where the galls are best and who buys ink. She would trade this for a word to a scribe. She believes the false rumour that the oaks on the slope are cursed.
