## Biography
Born 1316 on the coast near Turku, son of a fisherman named Olli. At seventeen he shipped on a Hanseatic cog, was paid off at Reval in 1338, and Hartwig van Minden took him as groom and porter after the boat share he held was lost. Hartwig feeds him, houses him in the stable loft, and trusts him with the keys to the yard. He has no kin in town, no Finnish priest, and no woman; the nearest he comes to confession is Lambrecht Wise, the chaplain, who speaks slowly enough for him.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A boat share again, and a name his own.
- **Fear:** Dismissal, and being alone in a winter street.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to a house that thinks little of him, because the rest of the world thinks less.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has heard his master's name spoken in a whisper he cannot place.

## Faction and belief
Core only in name: his place is Hartwig's, and Hartwig is Hanseatic. He would carry a bundle, stand a door, but not take up a spear against a neighbour. He keeps a Finnish charm, a carved bear tooth, and crosses himself twice.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish to himself, careful Low German to others.
- **Delivery:** Clipped and precise, each word weighed.
- **Sample lines:** "Master is in the counting-room." "I carry; I do not ask." "Minä en tiedä." (I do not know.)
- **Verbal tic:** "So."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who visits the master at night. Would trade it for a letter to Finland. Believes falsely that Estonians poison wells.
