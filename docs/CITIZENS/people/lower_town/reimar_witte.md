## Biography
Born in 1318 in Reval, the second son of a burgher cooper on Vene who could not leave the shop to him. At eleven he was sent to the parish school at St Nicholas, where a priest taught him Latin and the hand. At sixteen he entered [Winand van Minden](winand_van_minden.md)'s counting room and rose to senior clerk when the previous man went to Lübeck. In the spring of 1342 an older merchant of the unmarried men's circle spoke to him after mass about "friends who remember kindness", and he has attended their suppers since without taking a vow. His father died in the autumn and left him his mother's debts; he sends 3 marks a year to her.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A trusted post with Winand's house, then his own stall by thirty.
- **Fear:** Being asked to choose between Winand and the circle.
- **Contradiction:** He guards the strongroom key faithfully and gossips over wine.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has copied one of Winand's letters for the circle, and has not decided whether to do it again.

## Work and money
He earns 10 marks a year with board and a share of petty commissions. He sends 3 marks to his mother and owes 2 marks to a tailor. A bad month means no new ink and mending his own hose.

## Faction and belief
A Blackheads sympathiser: he likes the supper table and the sense of belonging to the unmarried young men of the trade. For a small favour he would carry a message; for a large one he would hesitate; to inform on Winand he would refuse. He prays at St Nicholas and keeps a small tallow candle for his father.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German; Latin for forms and prayers.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy, rising at the end of sentences as if asking permission.
- **Sample lines:** "The count is right, the count is always right." "I have not heard that." "Dat schal wol, so be it."
- **Verbal tic:** Taps the key ring twice.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the house's bonds, its debts and what is in the strongroom. He would trade it for a safe word to the circle. Believes falsely that the Rat will call a levy on the burgher households at Pentecost.
