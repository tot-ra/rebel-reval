## Biography
Born in 1330 in a fisher village on the Viimsi shore. His father Kornel drowned in 1340 and his mother placed him with Peeter Tanieli poeg for seven years of service, with a fee of two marks that Peeter paid in advance. He has been in Olevimägi since Michaelmas and sleeps in the shed.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be a journeyman at twenty and send wages to his mother.
- **Fear:** The water.
- **Contradiction:** He loves the rope and hates the river it goes to.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He cannot swim and tells no one.
