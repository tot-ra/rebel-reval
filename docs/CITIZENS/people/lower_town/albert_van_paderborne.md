## Biography
Born in 1305 in Reval, the son of a Paderborn journeyman, Albert took the bench at twenty-five when his father's heart failed. He married Taleke van Revele Westfal ([card](taleke_van_revele_westfal.md)) in 1328 and has raised six children, the youngest still at the breast. He turned from stitching to trading when the harbour stalls began paying for quantity, and in the autumn of 1342 lent out forty-two marks on two roofs after the autumn gales. Since then he has joined a quiet circle of guild men and passes on what the porters hear, a duty he took because debt makes a man careful of the council.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To place Gerburg and Detlev well and take a stall by the Sand Gate.
- **Fear:** A bad year in which his debtors cannot pay and his creditors can.
- **Contradiction:** A man of order who has bound himself to a secret.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows the circle's superior is a porter, and has been told never to speak to him in public.

## Work and money
Income about six marks a month from shoes sold in lots to the harbour, plus interest. Leather from the tanners by the stream, thread from the stalls. Credits: twenty-four marks to Toomas and eighteen to Jaan, both lent in autumn 1342, each half paid. Debts: none, he boasts. A bad month is a wet one that rots the leather.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic, active: debt has taught him that the council keeps the roads open and the stalls paid. He acts out of caution, not love. He would carry a small message, refuse to hide arms, and inform if his bonds were threatened. Church: St Nicholas; a candle each Saturday for the shoemakers' dead.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German only, curt with debtors.
- **Delivery:** A flat, tired murmur, rarely above a whisper.
- **Sample lines:** "Michaelmas is not far." "I do not ask; I only count." "Dat geit, dat geit."
- **Verbal tic:** "As agreed."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which roofs in the quarter were mended with borrowed silver. He would trade the knowledge for leather at cost. He believes the false rumour that the Rat plans to cancel debts at the new mayor's election.
