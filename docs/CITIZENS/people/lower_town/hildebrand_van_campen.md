## Biography
Born 1297 in Reval to a Westphalian saddler who came east for the Danish king's wars and stayed. Apprenticed at thirteen to a Beutler on Müürivahe, whose workshop he later bought with his wife's dowry. During the harsh winter of 1316 he sold purses for bread. The lesson stuck: money is a debt someone has forgotten. He took the house on Müürivahe in 1328, was admitted to the Rat in 1339 for his steady hand with the accounts, and has since sat in more disputes than he has won. Last spring he was fined 12 marks for a wall-side encroachment and let a neighbour stand surety. He is a cousin, distantly, of the van Campen on Sauna and has never worked out how.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To leave six children a house and a name in a city that stays German and quiet.
- **Fear:** That a rising brings the Order or Toompea's men into the lanes and the Rat loses what little it can still refuse.
- **Contradiction:** Preaches caution at council while keeping a circle that is not cautious.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has quietly gathered a few people who think the town should not be a gift to either side. He has not told his wife.

## Work and money
He cuts oxhide and sheepskin bought from tanners at the Smiths' Gate, sews belts, purses, sword-hangers, and sells to merchants and the harbour captains. Income about 90 marks a year, of which the household eats most. He is owed 12 marks by Bengt Magnusson, and owes 12 marks of fine to Toomas Kauri poeg's generosity. Hens run loose in the yard; eggs are small trade.

## Faction and belief
His belief is that cities survive by being too useful to destroy. A small favour he gives freely; a large one he weighs for a month; informing he would do only against an outsider. Prays at St Nicholas, sets a pinch of grain at the hearth before the harvest.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German at council and shop, a few Latin tags from hearing mass, no Estonian beyond market words.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, rising at the end of a figure.
- **Sample lines:** "Twelve marks is twelve marks, neighbour, and I do not forget a mark." "Hens are hens; a jug will mend it." "Say less in the street, say it twice at home."
- **Verbal tic:** "As I count it."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who owes whom on three streets. He would trade it for a safe word at the Rat. False belief: that the Order's agents are already quartered on Toompea.
