## Biography
Born in a fishing hamlet near Kopli in 1324, one of seven. She came to Reval at twelve to be hired out; her sister [Ruth Antsu tütar](./ruth_antsu_tutar.md) followed her into the Minden house. She learned German fast and silence faster.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband with a boat and a cottage.
- **Fear:** Hunger and a bad master.
- **Contradiction:** Serious and quiet, but loves a mean joke.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows the chaplain is afraid of something he will not name.
