- Long moustache and beard on a lean frame; speaks almost in a whisper.

## Biography
Born in Novgorod in 1321 to a cloth-seller, he was sent to Pskov to learn writing and then to Reval to Gavril's counting room at nineteen. He learned Latin there from the Dominican lay brother. He sells small lots on the side.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Capital for his own stall.
- **Fear:** Being searched at the quay.
- **Contradiction:** A careful clerk who gambles on one cargo.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** His stock is smuggled amber.
