## Biography
Lembit was born in 1334 in the winter his grandmother died, and the family held that a new life had replaced an old one. He took to the yard quickly, earning a scar on the knee from a pot-shard at five. He learned to count on the kiln bricks and to spit through the gap in his teeth. Last autumn Marten began to let him carry clay buckets from the cart. He is now the family's runner, sent to the market, the well, and back. In the evenings he sits with Tõll and learns to whittle.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to fire the kiln alone.
- **Fear:** The dark loft and the hens on Katariina käik.
- **Contradiction:** He boasts he is brave and then sleeps with the baby's cradle rope in his hand.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has buried a clay horse he made in the corner of the yard and will not say why.
