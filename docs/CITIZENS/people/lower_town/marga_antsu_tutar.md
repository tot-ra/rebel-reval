## Biography
Born in 1320 in a fishing hamlet near Kopli, the daughter of Ants, a net-mender, Marga walked into Reval at nine after her father died of fever. She was boarded in a lane off Rataskaevu and survived the winter of 1337, when the water-pump froze for five weeks and half the lane left on a cart. A cousin by marriage found her work in a Dunkri kitchen. She joined the van Groninghe house at Martinmas 1340. She has never been paid in anything but coin and bread; she likes that. She lives in the shared maids' loft.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A bed with her own blanket, and a wage she need not share with a cousin who found her the post.
- **Fear:** A freezing winter, and the pump-lane.
- **Contradiction:** She hates waste but cannot stop giving food away at the back door.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She feeds a lame boy in the yard behind the stable, a stray from the Harju road, with her own bread.

## Work and money
Her wage is 1 mark and 4 schillings a year with board, and shoes at Michaelmas. She keeps 5 schillings sewn into her shawl. She owes nothing and owns nothing. A bad month is a day without bread. A cousin occasionally asks for a small loan she cannot refuse.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction; the cold taught her that rulers change and winter does not. A small favour she gives gladly; a large one she would think about only for the boy. She keeps St Olaf's feast and leaves bread on the stone for the house-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian when alone and with the cook's helpers; slow German with masters.
- **Delivery:** A sing-song Estonian cadence under the German; soft, rising at the ends.
- **Sample lines:** "The wood is stacked, mistress." "I did not touch it." "Küll see läheb üle." (It will pass.)
- **Verbal tic:** A little "ee" before a sentence.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which pumps freeze first and which cellar doors do not lock. She would trade this for work for the boy. She believes the false rumour that the Rat plans to wall up the old pump lane.
