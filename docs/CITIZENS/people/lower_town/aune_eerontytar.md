- Speaks almost in a whisper; the house leans in to hear her.
- Bites her nails to the quick; her thumbs are hard as horn.

## Biography
Born near Turku in 1317 to a fisher family; her father drowned in 1335, and she came to Reval on a trading boat in 1338 as a deck-hand's cook. She entered the Minden house as a scullion and rose to maid. She sends a tiny sum home each Christmas by a Finnish skipper.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough to go home and buy a share of a net.
- **Fear:** Being sold, beaten, or sent away.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet and yielding in the house, fierce about her own savings.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has saved 7 marks, stitched in her mattress.

## Faction and belief
No faction; she sees politics as a weather. Small favours she does; large ones she cannot; informing she fears. She is baptised and carries a bone charm from her mother.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the house's linen and who visits at night. She would trade it for safe passage home. She believes the false rumour that Finns will be expelled.
