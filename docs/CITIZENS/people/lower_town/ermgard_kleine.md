## Biography
Born in 1322 on Dunkri over the taproom, the first child of Ghert and Geseke, Ermgard learned numerals from her father's tally-sticks and the Pater Noster from her great-aunt Fenne. She was betrothed at seventeen to a harbour factor who drowned off Naissaar; since then no one has asked. She took to carrying messages for a customer who tipped in pfennigs, and found that listening was a trade of its own. The circle took her on a year ago. She lives above the taproom with her family.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband of her choosing, or none, and a taproom of her own.
- **Fear:** Being married off to settle her father's debts.
- **Contradiction:** She serves drunks without a flicker and cannot bear her father's raised voice.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sets a certain jug on the sill when a customer wants to be seen leaving unseen.

## Work and money
She earns no wage but board and a share of tips, about 2 schillings a month; she has 14 schillings hidden in a stocking. She buys nothing. A bad month is a customer who leaves without paying.

## Faction and belief
She is a Hanseatic dependent, since her father's custom is the Hansa's. A small favour, a jug on a sill; a large one, an hour's lie; informing she would think on. She prays to St Nicholas and says nothing to Fenne's herbs.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German, flat and precise.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, even, almost bored.
- **Sample lines:** "Two stoops, one for the corner." "I did not hear that." "Dat is nich mien." (That is not mine.)
- **Verbal tic:** "As you say."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who sat where and who left by the back. She would trade for a word with a Lübeck factor. She believes the false rumour that the Order pays well for tavern gossip.
