## Biography
Born in Reval in 1269, the daughter of a candle-chandler, Fenne married a pewter-caster, Hinrik Grote, in 1290 and learned the craft beside him. He died in 1328; she cast spoons for another decade and left the moulds to her nephew's store. She lived through the fire of 1313, the Danish tax of 1317 and the rye-less winter of 1322. She was brought to Ghert's house in 1338 when her knees failed. She lives in the room by the taproom hearth.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To die in her own bed and be buried beside Hinrik in St Nicholas's yard.
- **Fear:** A cold room, and being parcelled out to a hospital.
- **Contradiction:** She scolds Ghert about debt and has never told him she keeps 6 marks sewn into her mattress.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She quietly added a mark to Ghert's hoard last winter and told him it was a find.

## Work and money
She has no income save a small rent from the pewter moulds, about 2 schillings a quarter. She keeps 6 marks in her mattress. She owns a spoon, a rosary and a hazel stick. A bad month is a cold fire.

## Faction and belief
She is a Hanseatic sympathiser because the Hansa bought her spoons. A small favour, a word; a large one, a night's shelter; she would never inform. She keeps St Nicholas's feast and sprinkles a bit of salt on the threshold.

## Voice

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who owed what in 1313. She would trade this for a warm shawl. She believes the false rumour that the Danes will tax hearths.
