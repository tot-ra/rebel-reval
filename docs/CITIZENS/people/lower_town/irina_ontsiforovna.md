## Biography
Born in 1308 near Narva, elder sister of Marfa. She came to Reval with her at twenty-one and stayed as servant when Marfa married, since no dowry could be found for her. A Novgorod merchant at the guest chapel asked her to say who came to the house; she agreed for a reason she keeps to herself.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A bed of her own and a name.
- **Fear:** Being sent back across the border.
- **Contradiction:** She loves Marfa's children and betrays their father's visitors.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She reports on Gavril himself, since his night-walks worry her.
