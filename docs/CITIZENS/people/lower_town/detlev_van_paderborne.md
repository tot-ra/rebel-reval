## Biography
Born in 1331, second child of Albert van Paderborne ([card](albert_van_paderborne.md)), Detlev began pegging soles at nine. The scar came at ten, when a last hurled across the shop by his father struck him; neither speaks of it. He spends his free hour on the Sand Gate quay watching the cogs unload and believes a boat is a cleaner thing than a shoe.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A berth as a ship's carpenter's boy.
- **Fear:** His father's silence and the smell of wet leather.
- **Contradiction:** Slow with words, quick with his hands.
