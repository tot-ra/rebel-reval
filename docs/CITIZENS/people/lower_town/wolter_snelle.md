## Biography
Born 1311 at Aida, son of a fishmonger who bought burgher right in 1318. Inherited the yard at twenty-one and spent the first winter paying a brother's debts. In 1342 he was fined fifteen marks for selling stale herring on a feast day, and the furrier Rein stood surety. He has not repaid, and he knows it. He married Wendele in 1337 and employs a clerk to write what he cannot.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To repay Rein before Rein asks.
- **Fear:** The Kämmerer's inspector at the stalls.
- **Contradiction:** Boasts of his honest weights while fixing the scale.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Lent the clerk's wage to cover a short week.
