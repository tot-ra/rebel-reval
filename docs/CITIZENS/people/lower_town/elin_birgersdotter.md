## Biography
Born in 1316 in a fishing hamlet on Gotland, the daughter of a boatman named Birger. She came to Reval at fifteen as a maid on a Hanseatic cog, and learned there that a quiet servant hears all. She entered [Magnus Birgersson](magnus_birgersson.md)'s household at twenty and has been there since. A year ago she began doing small kindnesses for people who could be useful, and found herself at the head of a circle.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Safety for her mother on Gotland and a room of her own.
- **Fear:** Dismissal and being named.
- **Contradiction:** She orders merchants yet obeys a mistress.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She remembers the circle's every name and tells no one the whole.

## Faction and belief
Active on the Hanseatic side because a Hansa cog fed her family. For a small favour she would carry a message; for a large one she would hide a person; to inform she would give one name, never all. She prays at St Olaf and leaves bread for the sea.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish at home; plain Middle Low German.
- **Delivery:** Hoarse, even, quiet.
- **Sample lines:** "I did not hear that." "Se, look, it is nothing." "Come after mass."
- **Verbal tic:** Touches her left cheek before a name.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the circle's members, though not each other's. She would trade it for her mother's safety. Believes falsely that the Rat keeps a list of all servants.
