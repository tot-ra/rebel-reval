## Biography
Henn was born in 1318 in a village near Rapla, the third son of Lauri, a smallholder. A hard harvest and a manor levy in 1338 sent him to town for a season, which became five years. He found work hauling water for a brewer and then wood for Lutke van Brunswik. He sends half his wages to his mother when a carter goes by. He has learned Low German slowly, one word at a time, and speaks it the same way.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A plot of his own and a quiet wife, in that order.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to the manor.
- **Contradiction:** He is the strongest man in the house and the least likely to say so.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has an unredeemed pledge in his mother's village, a cow.

## Work and money
He earns about 2 marks a year and keep. He sends 5 schillings a year to his mother. A bad month is a split yoke or a late carter.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A free Estonian houseman with a bound brother in the country has more to lose than to risk. He would carry a sack for anyone, and speak to no one about it. He crosses himself at the church door and leaves a crust of rye at the well.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with Agnes and the carters; slow Low German with the household.
- **Delivery:** Slow, with long pauses; he finishes a word, then thinks.
- **Sample lines:** "I carry. It is heavy. I carry." "The master knows." "Ei oska öelda." (I cannot say.)
- **Verbal tic:** Says "yes... yes" before replying.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the carters' news and which roads are muddy. He would trade it for a pair of boots. He believes the false rumour that bound men will be given land in Reval.
