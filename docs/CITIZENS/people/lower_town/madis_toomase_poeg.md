## Biography
Born in 1323 in Reval, the first child of Toomas and Lutsia, Madis was at the mash-tun at eight and in charge of the evening trade at sixteen. He has his grandmother Kaie's head for the licence and his father's gentleness with customers, and is impatient with both. He learned carving from an old pin-maker on Müürivahe and has been selling his work quietly for two years.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save eight marks and open a second stall near the Viru road.
- **Fear:** That the Michaelmas bond will take the house before he has the stall.
- **Contradiction:** He is the family's best brewer and wants most to give brewing up for a trade with clean hands.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has a purse of twelve schillings buried under the hearth-stone.

## Work and money
He earns his board and a pfennig a day, plus whatever the pins bring: about four schillings a quarter. He buys bone from the butcher. A bad month is a sour cask and a fine for selling after curfew.

## Faction and belief
He has no faction. For a small favour he gives ale; for a large one he demands a share; he would not inform for love of coin, only for his family's safety. He goes to Mass and leaves the first foam on the hearth.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the count of every ale-house customer on three streets. He would trade it for a licence. He believes, falsely, that the council will give new licences after St George's.
