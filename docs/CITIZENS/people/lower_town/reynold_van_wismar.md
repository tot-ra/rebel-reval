## Biography
Born about 1324, the eldest son. His grandfather's debts shamed the family, so Reynold spent his boyhood in the counting-houses of Lai learning to write. At seventeen he sailed as supercargo to Visby with a share of cloth. At eighteen a Toompea clerk told him that a smart young man could earn more than a father's wage. He accepted, and has told no one.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy his father out of debt and then out of the gate.
- **Fear:** That the Rat will find his notes.
- **Contradiction:** Writes to the Crown to save his father's town.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He reports gate traffic to the castle, learned from Berend's tally.
