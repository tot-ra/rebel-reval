## Biography
Born 1309 in Reval to a Russian carter and a mother who died in the winter of 1315. An Estonian nurse, Maret, raised her, which is why her German sings. She learned brewing in a neighbour's yard, married Stepan Grigorievich in 1331, and bore Marfa in 1333 and Olena in 1339. She brews under the Rat's licence in the gatekeeper's name, which cost a fee she has not forgotten.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A second copper and a malt-store of her own.
- **Fear:** A bad barley year, and the Rat's eye on a woman brewing.
- **Contradiction:** Generous to children, a hard creditor.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She waters the last barrel of each batch and charges full price; she calls it keeping the peace.

## Work and money
She buys malt from Aida carts and brews about 20 barrels a week, selling at 2 örtug a measure. Income about 5 marks a month. Taverns settle at quarter-days. A bad month is spoilt malt.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which tavern waters and whose barrels leak. She would trade it for malt. She believes the false rumour that the Rat will tax brewers by the copper.
