## Biography
Born in 1297 at Lübeck, Hildebrand was the younger son of a skipper and learned the Baltic from his father's deck. He took command at thirty and bought half of a cog, the _Marienknecht_, on a loan from a Lübeck merchant. He keeps a Reval berth at the harbour inns when the ship lies in port. He learned Latin for the bills of lading and reads and writes well. The tremor began in 1339 after a storm kept him at the helm for two days; he says it is a draught and hides his hand. He is a quiet member of the Hanseatic circle that keeps the Reval trade out of Toompea's and the Order's hands.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To pay off the Lübeck loan by Michaelmas and to see his son take the helm.
- **Fear:** A lost cargo, a failed hand.
- **Contradiction:** Prompt and exacting with the crew, dependent on every favour of the circle.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Twice he has held cargo back as a lever against a buyer, and has been uneasy ever since.

## Work and money
He earns about 20 marks a voyage in freight and shares a third of that with the Lübeck lender. He owes about 60 marks on the ship. A bad month is a lost cargo.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic guilds, active. His reason is debt: the Hansa gives freight and credit, the Crown and the Order take both. He would carry a letter for a small favour, hold a cargo for a large one, and report the movements of foreign ships. He prays at St Nicholas and keeps a pewter medal of the saint in his hat.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German; Latin in bills.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, giving orders; slows to precision when counting.
- **Sample lines:** "A cog is a promise with a keel." "The tide does not wait, nor do I." "Dat is mine Sake." (That is my business.)
- **Verbal tic:** Steadies his right hand with his left.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which ships carry which cargo, and who pays late. He would trade it for credit. He believes the false rumour that the Order has bought a fleet in secret.
