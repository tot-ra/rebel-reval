## Biography
Born in 1297 at Turku to a fisherman named Mikko, Pekka learned Low German as a boy on the Åbo wharf and came to Reval at twenty. The nose was broken in 1325 by a German carter who objected to his prices. He learned numerals from a Dutch pedlar, who taught him that numbers are all a pedlar needs. He sells needles, thread, bone combs, small knives and brass rings off a tray, in the lanes of the Lower Town and on the quay. He has a wife in Turku and two sons, whom he sees every second winter. The memory of the sons keeps him honest in his weights.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stall of his own under the quay arcade.
- **Fear:** Losing the tray to theft or the guild's fine.
- **Contradiction:** He cannot read but guesses the price of any cloth by touch.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once sold a fake silver ring and has been trying to make good on it with discounts.

## Work and money
He earns about 3 öre a month and pays a pfennig a night for the berth. Stock costs him half the takings. A bad month is a stolen tray.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He wants only to sell. He would carry a small package, and would not hide a fugitive. He crosses himself at St Olaf's and carries a Finnish rowan-charm in his pouch.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish in his head; Low German to sell.
- **Delivery:** Husky and low, cajoling.
- **Sample lines:** "Needles, sharp as sin, a pfennig for three!" "Count with me, mistress, count with me." "Hyvä, hyvä." (Good, good.)
- **Verbal tic:** Taps the tray with his left forefinger.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who has money and who pretends. He would trade it for a good pitch. He believes the false rumour that a crooked nose brings luck at trade.
