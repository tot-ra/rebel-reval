## Biography
Born in Greifswald on the Baltic coast, she came to Reval at sixteen in the kitchen train of a visiting Danish knight and stayed in Toompea service, learning Estonian from the scullery women. In 1337 she was dismissed with a quarter's wage unpaid and a charge of a missing silver spoon that was never proved. Reynold Gude, the falconer, had seen who took it and made the charge vanish. She entered Beke van Lemego's house on Pikk the same winter, and has never been asked to repay him except in small errands.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A wage she holds in her own hand and a bed that is hers.
- **Fear:** Being named a thief; the spoon would be remembered by anyone who wanted to remember it.
- **Contradiction:** She is the loudest voice in the house and the one that most needs silence.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She carries one message a month from Pikk kitchens to Toompea, and has recruited two others by kindness.

## Work and money
Board, a bed, two shifts a year and a wage of about 6 schillings a quarter. She keeps 4 pfennigs back for candles and a mass for her mother, and owes no money, only a debt of favour.

## Faction and belief
She is no royalist; she is a debtor. Asked for a small favour she will carry a word; for a large one, she would hesitate and probably open a back door; to inform on Beke she says she cannot, and fears she might. She prays to St Anne and ties a red thread to the hearth hook for luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German in the house; Estonian with Toompea maids.
- **Delivery:** Booming, quick, with a laugh she then covers with her hand.
- **Sample lines:** "Mind the step, it bites!" / "I carry water, not tales, master clerk." / "Ach, Gott, nich al wedder."
- **Verbal tic:** "God's wounds, what next."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which Pikk houses have a ladder to the roof and which keep dogs. She would trade this for the end of her debt. She believes the false rumour that the Danish viceroy has hanged a maid for theft.
