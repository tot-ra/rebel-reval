## Biography
Born on Pikk in 1325, the eldest child, he began at the harbour at twelve checking bales for his father. He learned Latin at St Nicholas and numbers at the quay. At sixteen he took his first small cargo to Visby on credit and returned with a profit of four marks; he has spoken of nothing else since. A cold, wet spring has taken his voice.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A ship share of his own before twenty.
- **Fear:** Being thought his father's clerk, not his son.
- **Contradiction:** He talks of duty but sneaks off to watch the shipwrights.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has promised more credit to a skipper than his father knows.
