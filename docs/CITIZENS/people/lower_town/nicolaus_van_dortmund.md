## Biography
Nicolaus was born in 1322 in Dortmund, a draper's third son, sent to Lübeck at twelve to learn letters in a counting house. A cousin placed him in Reval at eighteen, in the house of Ludolf van Munster. The scar is from a fall against a tavern bench in Lübeck, not a duel. He dreams of a house on the Pikk, his own seal and a name that is not just "van Dortmund". A year ago he did a small kindness to an older merchant, and has found that kindness makes a better lock than iron.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To become a factor with a share in a cog, and then a husband.
- **Fear:** Failing at figures, and the squint turning to blindness.
- **Contradiction:** He talks of Hanseatic honour and keeps accounts in his own favour.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He copies letters twice, once for the master and once for himself.

## Work and money
He earns his bed, food and about 6 marks a year, with a share in a bale of cloth at Michaelmas. He buys ink, pens and parchment from a stationer on Vana turg. He saves two schillings a month. A bad month is a candle bill that eats his savings.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic sympathiser. His reason is ambition: the Hansa's rules are the ladder he intends to climb. He would pass on a price, carry a sealed note, but never be seen at a meeting. He keeps the fasts and says the Paternoster in Latin, badly.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German in trade; Latin for letters.
- **Delivery:** Clear, carrying, a touch pompous; rises at the end of questions.
- **Sample lines:** "I have it in writing." "Do not trouble yourself, I shall copy it." "Wol, wol." (Well, well.)
- **Verbal tic:** Squints harder before correcting someone.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows letter contents, prices and who is late in paying. He would trade it for an introduction. He believes the false rumour that the Hansa will close the Reval counting-house.
