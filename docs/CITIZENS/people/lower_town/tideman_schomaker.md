## Biography
Tideman was born in Reval in 1282 to a shoemaker and took over the bench in 1310. He joined the shoemakers' brotherhood under St Canute's umbrella and married Bela van Lemego in 1304. A failed harvest in 1340 on the rye strip he rents beyond the Viru gate forced him to borrow grain from the joiner Jaan Jaani poeg, and the interest grows. In 1339 he put five marks into a boat voyage that sank. He now hires clerks to copy and carry for the merchants who trade his shoes, and keeps the balance by selling cheap.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the grain debt before he dies and leave the bench to a steady hand.
- **Fear:** The joiner's patience ending; a bad rye year.
- **Contradiction:** He scolds waste and keeps ten people at table.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He buys a few cheap hides without asking where they came from.

## Faction and belief
Dependent on the Hanseatic creditors; he passes on what clerks tell him and nothing more. He would refuse to inform on a neighbour. He keeps all Church fasts.
