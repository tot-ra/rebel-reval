## Biography
Born about 1314 in a fishing hamlet near Pirita, the sixth child of Toomas, a net-mender who died in the hard winter of 1325. She came to town at twelve and was taken into the van Soest kitchen at nineteen. She learned German by being scolded in it. A younger brother went to the Harju manors and was bound there; she has not seen him in four years.

Now she sleeps by the kitchen door, owns a bone comb and one good scarf, and sends a few pfennigs to her mother.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A bed of her own in a room with a door.
- **Fear:** Dismissal in a hard spring, with nowhere to go but the Hospital of the Holy Spirit.
- **Contradiction:** She hates the house's gossiping, yet listens at every door because knowing is the only insurance she has.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sets out a thimble of milk each Thursday for the house-spirit, behind the water barrel, where the mistress cannot see.

## Work and money
Two marks a year and her keep, paid at Martinmas; she saves half. Tools are the yoke, two buckets, a lye brush, and a scrubbing stone. She buys her own scarf and shoes. A bad month is a broken bucket, docked from pay.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She would do a small favour for any who ask kindly, a large one for none, and would inform only if threatened with dismissal. She keeps the old milk-offering and the Christian calendar together.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who comes late at night to the kitchen door and who is owed money. She would trade it for news of her brother. She believes, wrongly, that the new Vogt takes children for the Danish fleet.
