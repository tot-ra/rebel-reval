## Biography
Kadri was born in 1313 in the Lower Town, daughter of Andrese, a day labourer. In the grain-price winter of 1340 the council put her father in the stocks for taking sacks off a cart, and the cold cost him his hearing; he died a year later. Kadri came to Mikk's house in 1341.

Magdalena Joosepi tütar, a spinner on Nunne, gave her a loaf and a candle at her father's funeral; a year ago she asked for something in return.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see the council answer for the stocks.
- **Fear:** Mikk's ears at the window.
- **Contradiction:** Resents owing a favour yet pays it with her life.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides the circle's counters in the flour bin.

## Faction and belief
She leans Black Cloaks for her father's stocks. A small favour she does at once, a large one for the cause, informing never. She keeps a rowan twig.

## Voice

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who is watched at the Sand Gate. She would trade it for a name. She believes, wrongly, that Mikk is the council's spy.
