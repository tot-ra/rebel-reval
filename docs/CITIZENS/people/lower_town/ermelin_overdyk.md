## Biography
Born 1336 on Lai. Her father taught her to count by notching a stick, and she now counts carts when he lets her sit in the arch. She loves her brother Segebode and hates the goose that guards a neighbour's stall.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To count every cart in Reval.
- **Fear:** The goose.
- **Contradiction:** Counts quietly but screams at the goose.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Knows how many keys her father has: three.
