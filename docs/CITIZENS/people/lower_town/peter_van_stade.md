## Biography
Born 1334 in the great house, the second son. He was a slow talker as a toddler and a slow walker, and the household learned to wait. He has helped turn malt since six and prefers the loft to the lane. At eight he decided that the brewhouse cat, a grey tom called Grau, was stealing his place on the warm kiln wall; they have been at war since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** The warm spot on the kiln wall, undisputed.
- **Fear:** The cellar rats, and his mother's silences.
- **Contradiction:** He appears calm and is the most stubborn child in the house.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has been feeding Grau scraps for a year and calls him "enemy" to cover it.
