## Biography
Born in 1324 in a cottage near Visby, the daughter of a fisherman named Knut. When the fish failed in 1340, her mother sent her to Reval to a cousin. She entered the Birgersson household two years ago. She has learned Low German from the market, and thinks she speaks it well. Her pleasures are the Saturday soup and the Sunday dance by the harbour wall.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To eat well and to dance.
- **Fear:** Her mother's illness and the fish failing again.
- **Contradiction:** She is hungry in a house with a full pantry.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She takes crusts to a lame boy at the quay.
