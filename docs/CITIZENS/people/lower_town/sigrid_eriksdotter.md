## Biography
Born at Kopli in 1315 to a Swedish fish-salter, she went into service at twelve and has served three households. A betrothed carter fell from a load in 1338 and the marriage never happened. She took service with Jöns Gunnarsson three years ago and has kept her place by being quick and honest. She is saving for a market stall of her own and has a hoard, notched on a hazel stick, a notch for every schilling.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stall for thread and ribbon at the Viru market, with her name on it.
- **Fear:** Being caught in a fib, since her face always betrays her.
- **Contradiction:** She sells and haggles boldly but cannot tell the smallest untruth without turning red.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Eleven notches on the stick. She has told nobody how many.

## Work and money
Wage a mark and a half a year, plus shoes. She buys thread, ribbon and needles from a Krämer on the lane and resells at a pfennig above cost to neighbours. Profit: three schillings a month.

## Faction and belief
No faction; her only politics is the price of salt. A small favour she does; a large one she refuses; she would never inform, since she would blush and be caught. She keeps a hazel sprig over her pallet.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who owes whom a pfennig along the lane. She believes, wrongly, that a clove of garlic in the shoe wards off fever.
