## Biography
Ragnvald was born in 1335 in the weaver's house on Sauna street, the first boy after two girls and the first to live past the cradle. He was named for a Gotland great-uncle his grandfather Magnus speaks of. He can count to thirty in Swedish, sing three verses of a sea-song, and throw a stone at a goose. His duty is to carry the spools to the loom and to watch his baby sister when his mother is at the market.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To beat the goose and to be allowed in the weaving-shed.
- **Fear:** The goose, and the dark loft.
- **Contradiction:** He is the bravest boy in the lane and cries when the baby does.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has hidden a goose-feather from the next yard in a crack in the wall as a trophy.
