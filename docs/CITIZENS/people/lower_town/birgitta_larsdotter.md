## Biography
Born in 1263 on the shore of Gotland, the daughter of a fisherman named Lars. She married a fisherman and followed him to Reval in 1300; he drowned in the autumn of 1318 when the herring boats were caught off Naissaar. She sold fish at the Fish Market for twenty years. A bad winter in 1341 left her with swollen knees and the Holy Spirit house gave her a bed. She still goes to the market on her own two feet.
