## Biography
Born in 1337, the third of the household's children, Albert was named for his godfather, who brought him a wooden horse at his christening. He has been bitten by the goose twice, and has not forgiven it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To catch the goose by the tail.
- **Fear:** The goose, the dark cellar and thunder.
- **Contradiction:** Bold with the hens, small with the goose.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He feeds the goose crumbs when no one looks, hoping for peace.
