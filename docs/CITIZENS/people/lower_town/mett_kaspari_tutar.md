## Biography
Born in 1320 in a village near Rapla, the sixth of nine children of a tenant called Kaspar, Mett was sent to Reval at fourteen when her father could not pay the rye rent. She scrubbed pots at a bakehouse on Viru, then came to Dunkri at Candlemas 1340, where Geseke Lippe taught her to count in numerals on her fingers. She learned the pedlar's trick from the cook: nothing is waste if you can find the person who wants it. She sends home 3 pfennigs a month and has spoken to a village priest only once. She lives in the shared loft with the other maids and the other servant.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy her sister out of manor service, a price of about 4 marks she has never said aloud.
- **Fear:** Dismissal, for stealing, which she does not do.
- **Contradiction:** She is honest in trade and sly in conversation, and thinks no more of the one than the other.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** A small bag of silver under a loose floorboard, 2 marks in weighed pieces.

## Work and money
Her wage is 1 1/2 marks a year plus board; the pedlar's trade adds about 6 schillings. She buys stubs from the church sexton's boy, sells eggs with the cook's leave and takes a quarter. A bad month is a fox in the hen-house. She owes no one.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction. A servant who cheats may be dismissed; a servant who whispers may be hanged. A small favour she will do for a price; a large one she would not. She goes to St Olaf and puts a drop of milk on the doorstep for the house-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with the other maids; fast Middle Low German with masters.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, swallowing endings, so that the German she speaks sounds like birdsong.
- **Sample lines:** "Fair price, fair price, take it or leave it." "The hen laid it, not I." "Mul on kiire." (I'm in a hurry.)
- **Verbal tic:** "Fair price."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who buys what, and who has stopped buying. She would trade this for a few pfennigs. She believes the false rumour that the Rat will ban back-door trade at Whitsun.
