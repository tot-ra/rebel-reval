## Biography
Born in 1303 on a Harju manor to a smith's helper named Eerik; the manor-lord bound the family to the plough when Evert was twelve, and he walked to town in the winter of 1316 and was never fetched. He worked for a carter, then a brewer, and for twelve years has been household man for Niklas Uku poeg. His sister Mai stayed on the manor; her husband is about to be bound, and Evert sends her two pfennigs at Michaelmas by a carter. He has no wife and no child. He lives for the order of the cellar.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A pair of proper leather shoes, and to see his sister out of the manor.
- **Fear:** Being fetched back as a runaway if the Rat ever checks the old ledgers.
- **Contradiction:** A runaway who is the most trusted keyholder in the street.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has seen a letter with black wax come from Lübeck, and watched Niklas burn half of it unread by anyone else.

## Work and money
Board, a straw pallet, a mark a year and a pair of shoes at Martinmas when the master remembers. He buys nothing but a candle and a knife-whetstone. He puts every örtug beyond a mark by in a cracked clay jug under the pallet.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A bound man cannot afford a cause, since an unclear name can send him back to the plough. A small favour he does; a large one he refuses; he would inform if he were promised his sister's freedom. He leaves a crust under the cellar step each Thursday for the house-spirit, and crosses himself afterwards.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at the lower table, a little halting Middle Low German to buyers.
- **Delivery:** High, quick, a bird's voice, that jumps up when he is nervous.
- **Sample lines:** "Seven, eight, nine, ten casks; all there." "Ma ei näinud, I did not see." "Mind the third stair."
- **Verbal tic:** Counts under his breath, even in talk.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows what is in every cask and bale and who comes by the cellar door at night. He would trade it for a letter saying his sister is free. He falsely believes the Rat keeps a book of runaways at the Viru gate.
