## Biography
Born in 1314 in a Reval cooper's household, she was spinning flax by nine and married Ludolf at twenty-six. She spins coarse yarn for the weavers on the lane and sells the surplus at the market. A year ago, short of rye when her husband's arrears were called, she was helped by a merchant, Godeke van Stralesund, and by a porter, Lars Folkesson, each with a sack and a favour. She has been paying that debt in word-carrying ever since. She gave birth to Bartold at Candlemas in the bed she shares with a loom.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Rye in the cupboard, a loom of her own for Ludolf, and Bartold christened in a clean gown.
- **Fear:** Being found out and the fraternity turning Ludolf out.
- **Contradiction:** She hates owing anyone yet will not call the debt a debt.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She is the circle's go-between to three men who think they report to Godeke alone.

## Work and money
She earns about eight schillings a month from yarn; flax is bought at market, wool from a carter. The family owes the loom's master fourteen schillings. A bad month is one where Bartold is ill.

## Faction and belief
She leans Hanseatic out of need: they buy yarn and feed the poor at Martinmas. A small favour she does at once; a large one she does after weighing Bartold; informing she would do on a shut door. She keeps a cross of rye straw at the cradle.
