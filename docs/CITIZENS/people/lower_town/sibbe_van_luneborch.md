## Biography
Born 1301 in Reval to a van Luneborch family of small traders, kin to the great Johannes van Luneborch two doors off, though he has not spoken to her in years. She married Henneke Overdyk in 1330 and lost an infant in 1334. In 1342 a wool-factor from Riga, a man who buys yarn for the Order's mills, offered a few silver pfennigs for the day's talk of who buys and who sells. Elisabet Lunge, a spinner on Toompea, arranged it. Sibbe pocketed the first coin and asked herself whom she was robbing; the answer was no one yet.

## Faction and belief
Her root grievance is the Danish tax on yarn; her reason to act is silver and a quiet pride. She would repeat a price, but would never put anyone in danger. She keeps a rowan charm for the children.
