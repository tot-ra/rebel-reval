## Biography
Born 1309 at a fishing village near Åbo, daughter of a net-mender, Gunnar. Her brother Nils took ship at sixteen on a Hanse cog out of Reval; in 1336 the cog was taken by sea-robbers, the crew's wages unpaid, and Nils chose the robbers over the dock. Anna came to Reval in 1337 to look for him and found only a rumour; she took service with the Folkessons. A year later a porter on the Western coast road passed her a word from Nils and asked for another. She has given him word of ships since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Nils alive, once.
- **Fear:** That her tales get a sailor hanged, or the infant hurt.
- **Contradiction:** She is gentle with Kristina and sells her master's schedules.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She tells Priidik which of Erik's cargoes are insured and when they sail; she has never named a sum.

## Work and money
Wage 2 marks a year with board; she sends one mark a year to a church in Åbo for Nils's soul, in case. She buys nothing but thread. A bad month is a master's temper.

## Faction and belief
Secret sympathy with the brethren: not for plunder, for Nils, and because the Hanse pays its crews last. Small favour: a whisper; large favour: she would refuse; informing: she would say nothing and burn the lamp. St Olaf, and a charm of salt over the threshold.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which ships carry Erik's grain and when. She would trade it for news of Nils. She believes the false rumour that the Hanse hangs every sea-robber.
