## Biography
Born 1300 at the lime kilns beyond the town, son of Ants, a kiln hand, and Veronika, who carried and slaked the lime beside him. Paul was put to the masons at twelve, hauling stone for the Dominicans' new work at St Catherine's, and learned cutting from a German journeyman who beat him and taught him well. He married Wendla in 1334. In 1341 he bought a share in a small cargo boat bound for Visby with salt and hides, and the boat went down in a gale off the islands. He still has the share receipt, a tally stick he cannot read but will not burn. He joined the Estonian night-watch squad in 1340 for the steady pay, which stops the debt growing. He lives in a cramped house on Katariina käik with his wife, mother and children.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Clear the 12 marks to Meinhard van Campen before Michaelmas and put Kaspar to a mason's bench in a guild shop.
- **Fear:** Being seen to fail in front of his mother; losing the watch pay.
- **Contradiction:** Proud of his fine work, yet underprices it every time.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has twice seen men on the watch round take coin to look the other way, and has said nothing, so is not clean himself.

## Work and money
Day pay is a few pfennig; a hearth job earns perhaps half a mark. Lime comes from the kilns, stone from the Lasnamäe quarry carters, timber on credit from carpenters. The watch brings a small steady wage. Debt: 12 marks to Meinhard van Campen, patient so far. A wet month kills building work and leaves him living on the watch pay alone.

## Faction and belief
Nominally within the Hanseatic order of things because the guilds' masters hire him, but he holds no role. He would do a small favour for anyone who paid; a large one only if it did not cost the watch pay; he would inform only if threatened. He keeps St Olaf's feast and leaves a crust of bread at the hearth for the house spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home and on site; Low German with masters and watch officers.
- **Delivery:** Booming, even when whispering; slow, with a pause for his own jokes.
- **Sample lines:** "A wall wants patience, not hurry." "Twelve marks is a wall I have not built yet." "Kuule, poiss, hold the line straight."
- **Verbal tic:** "Eh, so."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who is out after curfew and which cellars have been recently dug. Would trade it for a lighter debt. Believes, falsely, that Meinhard van Campen is preparing to call the debt in at Whitsun.
