## At a glance
- The tallest maid in the bakehouse and the best at shaping loaves.
- A clear voice that carries through the oven's roar.
- Remembers every winter she has survived, and who helped.
- Keeps out of politics because her wage is her whole hope.

## Biography
Born 1312 in Reval's Swedish quarter, the daughter of a barrel-hooper named Anders, who died in the hungry winter of 1316-18 when she was four. She was raised by an aunt, served in three houses, and has been here since 1336. A dog tore her ear at nine, and she has loved dogs since. She survived the bad winter of 1337-38 in the same lane as Ingeborg and shares bread with her.

## Faction and belief
None; politics is a rich woman's pastime. A small favour she grants, a large one she sleeps on, informing she disdains. She keeps St Lucy's candle and leaves a crust for the house-spirit.
