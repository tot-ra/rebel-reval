- A big bearded man at the tide-line with his sleeves up and a paddle in his left hand.

- Odd: a man in a woman's trade, which he does because wash-women would not take on a Finn.

## Biography
Born 1310 in Porvoo, he fell out of a Finnish fishing crew and came to Reval in 1335. The wash-women would not take a stranger, so he worked alone and cheaper. He rents a corner of Johannes's loft. He is cheerful out loud and watchful in his head.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A copper kettle of his own and a drying yard.
- **Fear:** A complaint to the Vogt over his prices.
- **Contradiction:** Says cleanliness is honest but buys lye from men who ask no questions.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Sells soap that is not quite his own.
