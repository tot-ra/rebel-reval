## Biography
Born in Bocholt in 1326; his father, a burgher, died after a bad debt to a Riga agent. The agent offered to cover it if Arend would report small matters from Reval. He entered the Blyde house in 1341.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear his father's name and the debt.
- **Fear:** Exposure.
- **Contradiction:** Honest in the ledger, a spy in the street.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has reported three small items to a Riga agent at Easter.

His tie is a debt, not belief. He would pass a small thing, resist a large one, and inform because he already does. Devout and guilty.
