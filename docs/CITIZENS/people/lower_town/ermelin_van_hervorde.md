## At a glance
- A thin, brown-eyed girl who talks in a husky rasp and is always asked to lower her voice.
- Spends mornings in the kitchen and storerooms, as patrician girls do before marriage.
- Counts linen and spices with her mother and is quicker at it than either admits.
- Oddity: a burgomaster's daughter who prefers the stable yard to the hall.

## Biography
Born 1329, the eldest, with a sister's patience she did not inherit. She learned numerals on the tally-board by six and the storeroom keys by ten. Last winter she carried bread to the neighbours while her mother was unwell. She is learning the household's work under [Gyse van Brunswik](gyse_van_brunswik.md) before marriage, and she knows she will be traded in a settlement.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To choose where she lives; failing that, to be useful.
- **Fear:** A betrothal to a man twice her age.
- **Contradiction:** Prattles in a husky voice, then freezes when asked to speak in company.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sneaks out to feed a stray dog behind the stable.

## Work and money
She has no income; her mother gives her 2 pfennig on feast days. She learns the household's budget: grain by the last, wax by the pfund.

## Faction and belief
None. She is fourteen; faction is a word for her father's table. She would carry a message if asked kindly, tell no one, and cannot imagine informing. She says her prayers with her sister and leaves milk for the cellar spirit.

## Voice

- **Delivery:** Husky, fast, then hushed.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the keys and the stores. She would trade it for an afternoon alone. She falsely believes the cellar spirit steals spoons.
