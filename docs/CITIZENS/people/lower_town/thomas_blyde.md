## Biography
Born in Reval in 1285, third son of a cloth-merchant. He ran a ship to Visby and joined the Rat in 1330. He married Greta van Hamelen in 1324 and has five children. A roof collapse in the winter of 1342 forced him to borrow, which he hides.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To leave the house and the seat to his sons.
- **Fear:** Creditors, a bad harvest, and a divided Rat.
- **Contradiction:** A lender who hates owing.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He owes nine marks to a stonemason, due at Michaelmas, and has paid half.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic from purse and habit. He would give a small favour, call a large one a loan, and inform only to save the Rat. Pious, with a gift to the Dominicans.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German; Latin in council.
- **Delivery:** Soft, almost whispering.
- **Sample lines:** "A sack will be found." "Let us not shout." "Michaelmas is not far."
- **Verbal tic:** Whistles on s.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who owes grain. He would trade it for votes. He believes the false rumour that his roofer was bribed.
