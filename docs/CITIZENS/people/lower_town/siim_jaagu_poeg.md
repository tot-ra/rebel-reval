## Biography
Born in 1290 in Reval, the son of Jaak, a craftsman who saved for a burgher's right and bought it for Siim in 1318. He trained in a tavern on Rataskaevu under a master who had already trained Nicolaus van Wismar and went on to take Siim. He took a half-share in a boat voyage in 1339 that sank off Gotland, leaving him thirty marks in debt to the market gardener Hildebrand van Hildensem. His wife died in 1334.

He keeps the house on Lühike jalg with his aged father, three servants and a clerk.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To pay Hildebrand before he is called a defaulter.
- **Fear:** Losing the burgher right and being thrown back among the labourers.
- **Contradiction:** An Estonian who plays the German burgher, yet prefers to speak Estonian after the third cup.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** The sunk boat was carrying smuggled iron, and he has never told Hildebrand.

## Work and money
He buys malt and hops from [Kaur](../../people/lower_town/kaur.md) and sells ale at a pfennig a cup. A good month clears about six marks. He owes Hildebrand thirty, and holds Sven Svensson's moulds for four. A bad month is a watch levy of ale.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A debtor cannot afford a side. A small favour is a free cup, a larger one a postponed debt; he would inform if the watch asked, and regrets it in advance. He keeps the feasts at St Olaf's.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home, German with guests.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, loud, hearty.
- **Sample lines:** "A cup on credit is a cup in the wind." "Mis sa ütled, what do you say?" "Pay on Saturday."
- **Verbal tic:** Thumps the counter.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who drinks too much, who owes, who has lately bought iron. He would trade it for a postponed debt. He believes, wrongly, that Hildebrand intends to seize the tavern.
