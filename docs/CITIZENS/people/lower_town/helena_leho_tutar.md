## Biography
Born 1308 in a fishing household by the harbour. At nine she spun beside her mother; at fifteen she sold ribbon at the fair in the Old Market. She married Tõnis in 1329. She bore six children; she buried none. Last autumn the roof loan weighed on her and she took in more thread. She keeps the accounts of the household in her memory and sings them to herself at the wheel.
