## Biography
Born 1316 in a village on a Harju manor, where her father Villem was bound to the lord's fields. When the manor tightened the labour dues in 1331 she was sent to town to earn her own bread and his. She served two houses before this one in 1340. Her brother was flogged at the manor for leaving without leave, and she heard of it from a carter; that is the root of her grievance. She carries what is whispered to her on market days, and speaks little.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see her father unbound and her brother home.
- **Fear:** A flogging, and the household finding the notes.
- **Contradiction:** Soft and meek in the hall, firm as iron in the cell.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides a message in the lining of her basket twice a month.

## Faction and belief
A courier of the Harju Kings: not from love of any king, but from anger at the dues and the flogging. For a small favour she carries a word; for a large one she hides a man; for informing she says nothing at all. She keeps St George's day with a prayer and leaves milk on the doorstep for the house-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with kin and carters; Middle Low German to masters.
- **Delivery:** Soft, almost whispering.
- **Sample lines:** "Fish, mistress, and a loaf." / "I carry only what is given." / "Ära muretse. Do not worry."
- **Verbal tic:** "If you please."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which gates are slack and which carters are honest. She would trade it for her father's release. She believes the Order will free the serfs, which is false.
