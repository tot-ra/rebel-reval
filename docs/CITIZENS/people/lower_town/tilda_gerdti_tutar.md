## At a glance
- A long-limbed girl with a booming voice who minds the hens, the baby and the lane.
- An old burn scar on the back of one hand, from the woad vat's iron lid.
- Shouts the hens home each dusk and is heard at the Viru Gate.
- Is always the one who is blamed.

## Biography
Born in 1331, third child of Gerdt Pauli poeg ([card](gerdt_pauli_poeg.md)) and Kadri ([card](kadri_hendriku_tutar.md)), Tilda was taught to count hens before letters. At nine she tried to lift the woad vat's lid when her father was at the gate and burned the back of her hand; he has not allowed her near it since, and she goes to look at the blue steam anyway.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to carry her father's dinner to the gate every day.
- **Fear:** The cellar, and Ann marrying and leaving her the baby.
- **Contradiction:** Loud, and afraid of the dark.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a stray kitten in the hen-house.
