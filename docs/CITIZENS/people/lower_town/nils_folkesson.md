## Biography
Born 1300 at Söderköping in Östergötland, son of a cartwright. He sailed to Reval in 1325 to join a cousin and learned the oak-and-iron trade from a German master. He married Ingeborg in 1330 and took over the yard on Kuninga when the master died without heirs. Two winters ago the neighbour Ell Jaagu tütar's chimney began to smoke into his loft; he has said nothing to the Vogt. His children are Kristina, Nils and Margareta, and the maid is Ragnhild.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To train his son as a master and keep the yard in the family.
- **Fear:** A cracked axle on a cart that kills somebody, and with it his name.
- **Contradiction:** He is a master of a craft that thrives on disputes over carts, but avoids any dispute himself.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sold a defective axle to a carter last winter and has not forgotten.

## Work and money
A cart wheel sells for about a mark; a new cart for four. Oak and ash come from Harju forests; iron tyres from the smiths at the Smiths' Gate. A bad month is a wet one with no carters.
