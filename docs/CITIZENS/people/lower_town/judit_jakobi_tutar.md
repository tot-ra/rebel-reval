## Biography
Born 1330 on Vaimu, late child of Jakob and Sigrid. She learned numbers from the stones in her mother's yard-pot. She has never had a doll; she has a stick with a rag. She is the reason the house works.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stall of her own.
- **Fear:** Being married off to a porter.
- **Contradiction:** Bossy with younger children, shy with grown women.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She saw a hemmed sack passed and did not tell.
