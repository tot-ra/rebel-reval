## Biography
Born in 1334 behind the bakehouse, the eldest. He was made to stand on a crate at six to reach the dough board, and he has not gotten off it since. He has delivered loaves along Müürivahe, Vene and the harbour since seven, and his voice, trained in the wind, is rough. He was bitten once by the miller's dog and now carries bread-crusts for it. He is meant to learn the oven, and is already bored of it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to light the oven alone.
- **Fear:** His father's quiet look at the end of the month.
- **Contradiction:** Trusted with the money and not with the fire.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has seen his father leave by the back lane and counts the nights.
