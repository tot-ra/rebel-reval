## Biography
Gyse was born in Hildesheim to a salt-merchant's family and came to Reval at sixteen to marry Segebode in 1308. She learned Estonian from a nursemaid and kept it as a private tongue with her cook. She buried three infants and bore a daughter and a son who now live in Lübeck. She has run the household accounts since 1315 and spins wool each day from habit rather than need, because the wheel lets her think. She knows the town's wives by their debts, and she has been on the receiving end of more thanks than her husband has ever collected.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep her husband alive and the house solvent until the grandchildren can be fetched from Lübeck.
- **Fear:** A rising that ends in fire on Harju, and being left a widow in a hall full of strangers.
- **Contradiction:** She is the best-informed woman in the street and says almost nothing.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She lent a small sum to the town clerk Gerhard van Bremen without telling Segebode, to make a friend of him.

## Work and money
She manages the household stores and thirteen mouths: rye, salt herring, malt and candles. A bad month is a late ship; a worse one a levy. She spins wool for her own household and for the nuns of St Michael as a gift.

## Faction and belief
She backs the guilds for her husband's sake. A small favour she returns, a large one she asks for in kind, and an informer she would never be. She prays to St Anne and leaves the first bread to the house spirit, as her Estonian nurse did.
