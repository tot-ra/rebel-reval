## Biography
Born about 1308 on a Harju farm, he came to town after a bad harvest. He dug graves for the hunger of 1339 until his shoulders ached. He tolls the hours and rings for the dead. He does not read, but knows the names of the dead from the bell strokes.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A pig, and a wife who tolerates one.
- **Fear:** Digging for a child.
- **Contradiction:** Jolly, yet at ease only with the dead.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows which graves are shallow.
