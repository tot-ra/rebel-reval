## Biography
Henn was born on Harju in the cold spring of 1332 and survived a fever at four that his mother still counts as a miracle. He was put to his father's trade last Michaelmas. He can drive a small hoop and sort staves by sound, and he counts the numerals on the stave bundles faster than the clerk. He is the eldest of three brothers and takes it seriously.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To drive the big hoop alone before his twelfth birthday.
- **Fear:** The goose, and his father's flat silence when a cask leaks.
- **Contradiction:** He wants to be grown, and sulks when sent to bed with his brothers.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once dropped a hoop-band down the privy pit and said the apprentice Peeter did it.
