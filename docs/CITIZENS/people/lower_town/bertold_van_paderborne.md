## Biography
Bertold was born in 1322 in Paderborn, the son of a cloth-merchant, and came to Reval at seventeen to [Wolter van Brunswik](../../people/lower_town/wolter_van_brunswik.md) as a Hansa apprentice. He shares the name of Cord van Paderborne, the fishmonger's journeyman, and no kinship either of them can prove. He reads the accounts aloud because the old master is hard of hearing, and the habit has made his voice enormous. At a funeral at St Nicholas last winter he whispered a question to a knight on Toompea, and the whisper has not stopped.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be a Hanse factor in Novgorod with a house of his own.
- **Fear:** The Danes or the rebels closing the road before he has a share.
- **Contradiction:** A man with the loudest voice in the house who keeps the quietest secret.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He passes what he reads aloud, edited, to a small circle who want to be on the right side when the rising comes.

## Work and money
He earns his board, a gown at Christmas and two marks a year. He saves half a mark and lends the rest to the other clerks at no interest. He owes nobody. A bad month is a master's rage or a blotted page.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic sympathiser: his advancement lies with the Hansa. His own reason is ambition for Novgorod; the circle is a ladder, not a creed. For a small favour he would read a letter slowly; for a large one he would copy it wrongly; to inform he would write nothing and speak softly. He says the Latin psalms aloud and a rhyme from Paderborne for luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Latin for the accounts; booming Low German for the hall; a learned whisper for secrets.
- **Delivery:** Booming, even and steady, then suddenly hushed.
- **Sample lines:** "Forty marks, two öre, three örtug!" "Shh, sit, we do not shout here." "I only read what is written."
- **Verbal tic:** "As read."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every price the house pays and the gate-keepers' times. He would trade it for a place in Novgorod. He believes, wrongly, that Toompea's knights will open the gates to the Hansa.
