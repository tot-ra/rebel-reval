## Biography
Born in the first winter after the great grain failure, Kaur is the first child to live in the house on Oleviste. He learned numerals by counting his father's fees in notches on a stick, and learned that numbers do not lie. He has not learned letters, and is not asked to. He now tends the hens and goes with his father to feasts as a bearer of the fiddle case, sitting under a table until called.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to play the fiddle, not just carry it.
- **Fear:** The tide-bell at night, and a certain gander on Pagari.
- **Contradiction:** Flat of voice, restless of hands.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has played the Dutchman alone, once, and told no one.
