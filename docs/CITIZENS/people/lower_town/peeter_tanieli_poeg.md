## Biography
Born in 1296 on Olevimägi as the son of Taniel Eerika poeg, the ropemaker, he was walking the rope-walk at five. In the war years he made cables for the Danish ships. He married Eeva Marteni tütar in 1329, after the rope market recovered, and has buried one child.

Now he runs a rope-walk behind the house, rents a shed from a merchant, and employs his father and an apprentice. He has a reputation for fair measure.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To leave Ain a rope-walk and a name.
- **Fear:** A debt called in before the harvest and his father's breath gone.
- **Contradiction:** He is a lender who fears being owed, and a borrower who fears the note.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has twice seen the baker Ulf Eriksson leave by the back lane after curfew and has told no one, because he knows which door he might need open.

## Work and money
A fathom of good cable fetches 1 örtug. He borrowed 15 marks from Erik Folkesson to pay a dowry for his sister; the marriage went ahead and the debt remains. He lent Gunnar Knutsson 30 marks in the autumn of 1342 for a new roof; the bond falls due at Michaelmas and Gunnar has paid about half. He rents a shed from Frederik van Brunswik for tools and firewood at a rate neither considers fair to themselves. A bad month is a ship that does not come.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every ship that wants cable. He would trade it for a loan. He believes the false rumour that a rope-walk law is coming.
