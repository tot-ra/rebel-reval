## Biography
Tilda was born in 1332 in the brewhouse, the third child to live, and has carried water since she could walk. The well in the back yard is shared with the Swedish weaver and the German merchant, and she knows its rota better than the housewives. She helps her mother with the baby and her father with the cooling troughs. She is tired, and has not yet noticed that she is.

## Motivation
- **Want:** One whole night's sleep, and a new scarf.
- **Fear:** Falling in the well, and her father's shouting when the roof leaks.
- **Contradiction:** The quietest girl in the house, and the one who knows everyone's business.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sleeps with a loaf-heel under her pillow in case the bread runs short.
