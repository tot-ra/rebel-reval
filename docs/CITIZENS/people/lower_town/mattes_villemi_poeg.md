## Biography
Mattes was born in 1314 to an Estonian tailor on Munga. A priest taught him letters; he went to Mikk's house as a boy of twelve and rose to clerk by twenty-two. He lost the tooth in 1335 when he slipped on the ice by the harbour. The squint came from years of copying by tallow light.

He is distantly related by marriage to Dmitri Stepanovich of the Novgorod court, and neither can trace it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A trading share of his own before thirty.
- **Fear:** Going blind at the desk.
- **Contradiction:** Cynical about merchants, yet works like one.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a second set of tallies of what Mikk does not enter.
