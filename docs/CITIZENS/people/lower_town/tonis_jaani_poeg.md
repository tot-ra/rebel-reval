## Biography
Born 1286 in a Harju village; his father was a freeholder who lost his land to a manor after the war of 1313. Tõnis came to Reval in 1314 with a donkey and carried malt for a brewer. Twenty years of saved coin bought the cellar lease. He saw his brother flogged on Toompea in 1317 for resisting a levy, and the brother never worked again. He married Katrin in 1326, and their son Lauri is his only child.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A tavern his son can inherit without a German landlord.
- **Fear:** The Rat revoking his tavern licence.
- **Contradiction:** Lends to the men he resents.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** The Black Cloaks' scrip is kept under the third barrel.

## Faction and belief
Core, though he holds no sword. His reason is his brother's flogging, and a taproom that is a refuge. He would pass a word or hide a man; he would not inform. He leaves a first cup on the sill for the house-spirit.
