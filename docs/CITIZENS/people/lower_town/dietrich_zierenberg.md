## Biography
Born about 1279 in Lübeck, he shipped as a boy on herring cogs and was twice shipwrecked off Bornholm before he turned twenty. He came to Reval in 1301 as a factor, married up in 1305, and rose by the Gotland run. His first wife, Margarete van Rostok, died in 1327; he raised her brother's three orphans, Ghese, Claus and Sophia, as his own. He married [Berta van Stade](berta_van_stade.md) in 1329. A burgomaster since 1336, he is thought rich and is, though most of it floats.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see the Gotland trade pass undiminished to Claus van Rostok.
- **Fear:** Owing anyone for anything; dying with the last ship unreturned.
- **Contradiction:** Hates debts, yet one small kindness has bound him to a secret.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** A year ago a maid returned a lost purse at the Coastal Gate and a few weeks later asked him to listen; he has been listening ever since.

## Work and money
His cargoes of cloth, wax and salt return perhaps 150 marks a year. He extends credit settled at quarter-days. A bad month is a wreck: then pledged plate pays the crew.

## Faction and belief
A core man of the council order, by office and by fear of the Danish tax. His sympathy with the rising's complaints is a private matter, set against old loyalty to Lübeck. A small favour he repays double; a large one he refuses, then thinks about; informing he has never done. He gives alms at St Nicholas's and keeps sea-charms from his years afloat.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German in business; Latin in prayers.
- **Delivery:** Thin and reedy, with long dry pauses.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows every captain and cargo in the Gulf. He would trade it for a clean ledger. He falsely believes the Order has already bribed a Rat clerk.
