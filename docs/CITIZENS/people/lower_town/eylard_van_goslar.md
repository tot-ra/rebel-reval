## Biography
Born in 1318 in Goslar in the Harz, the son of a mining clerk. At fourteen he came by Lübeck to Reval to serve [Tilman van Lubeke](tilman_van_lubeke.md)'s house on a cousin's word. He rose quickly, because his hand is clear and his nerves are quick. A year ago he attended a funeral at which a few men whispered the same phrase; he joined what followed without a vow. He has since done small kindnesses to people the circle might use. He lives in the clerks' garret, writing a second set of notes.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be indispensable to the Hanseatic circle and then to the Rat.
- **Fear:** That someone he helped will say so aloud.
- **Contradiction:** He prides himself on gratitude yet collects it like a debt.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a notebook of whom he has helped, and what each might do.

## Work and money
He earns 8 marks a year with board and spends 2 on kindnesses: bread, ale, a pair of shoes. A bad month means no ale for those who might be useful.

## Faction and belief
Hanseatic by dependence: the guilds fed him and he repays. For a small favour he would carry a note; for a large one he would use a kindness owed; to inform he already does. He prays at St Nicholas and fears the rising.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German; Latin on parchment.
- **Delivery:** Husky, quick, dropping in pitch for secrets.
- **Sample lines:** "A small thing, no more." "You owe me nothing, truly." "Wol, wol, dat is good."
- **Verbal tic:** Says "truly" after every kindness.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who owes the circle and who fears it. He would trade it for rank. Believes falsely that Toompea's viceroy will close the gates before Easter next.
