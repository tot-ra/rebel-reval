## Biography
Born 1314 in Reval to a Swedish fishing family, the second of five. Her father died in 1330 and the household fell apart. She served three houses and has been here since 1338. In the winter of 1337-38 she and a neighbour's maid shared one rye loaf between three houses and learned each other's hunger. She has kept the habit of sharing, and of counting every crumb.
