## Biography
Born in 1298 in Visby, the daughter of Jon, a fisherman. She came to Reval at twenty with a cousin's family and married [Magnus Birgersson](magnus_birgersson.md) in 1320. She bore five children and buried two. The tremor began after a fever in 1337. She spins at the doorstep, sells yarn, and has spoken only Swedish since she arrived, though she understands market German.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry Knut to a good girl and keep Gunhild near.
- **Fear:** A fever in the house.
- **Contradiction:** She scolds her sons yet slips them bread.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides coin in the spindle's hollow whorl.
