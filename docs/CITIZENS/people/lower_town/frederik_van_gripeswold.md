## At a glance
- A tall scribe with protruding ears and a close beard, who writes with his left hand and curls his wrist over the line.
- He is the chancery's best copyist and its worst timekeeper.
- Odd for a scribe: he owes a confessor a restitution he paid, and still resents the priest's right to demand it.

## Biography
Born in 1299 in Greifswald, son of a notary; he came to Reval in 1325 as a copyist and has stayed. He once took forty pfennig from a deposit and was refused absolution by the chaplain of St Nicholas until he repaid it. He has been honest since and keeps the receipt. He lives in the town hall and dines with the scribes.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stable post under the next Stadtschreiber.
- **Fear:** Failing eyesight, which has begun.
- **Contradiction:** Meticulous in the hand and careless in the day.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps the receipt of his restitution in the lining of his hood.

## Work and money
He earns twelve marks a year and keep. He does private copying for merchants at a pfennig a line and keeps the money. He owes nothing. A bad month is a week with no copying.

## Faction and belief
No faction: he copies for all and wants to stay clear. He would copy a letter for a small favour, hide a copy for a large one, and does not inform. He prays before each charter.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German in the hall; Latin in charters.
- **Delivery:** Nasal and precise, a little hurried when he is late.
- **Sample lines:** "I will have it by Vespers." "Hold the rule steady." "Dat is al ghescreven."
- **Verbal tic:** Blows on the page twice.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which merchants sign with a mark and which with a hand. He would trade it for a better inkhorn. He believes the chancery cat is a witch's familiar, which is false.
