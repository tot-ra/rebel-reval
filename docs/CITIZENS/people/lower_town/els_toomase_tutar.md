## Biography
Els was born in 1334 in the brewhouse and learned to whistle before she learned to speak. She keeps the small children in line, plays hop-and-catch in the lane, and sings the psalms she hears at St Olaf's, with different words. She is hopeless at carrying water and good at carrying news.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To sing at a feast, and to win the goose war.
- **Fear:** The dark brew-room, and her father's debt-face.
- **Contradiction:** The loudest in the house and the first to hide.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has a bone whistle hidden from her mother.
