## Biography
Ingeborg was born in 1328 in Reval, a year behind Helga, whose dresses she wears the next season. She learned to spin by copying her sister and to sing from her grandfather Magnus. She has a gift for making friends and no sense of whom it is wise to be friends with. In Easter week she met Margareta Olofsdotter at the church door and now they share the walk and the news.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to sell a bit of her own thread and to keep a friend of her own.
- **Fear:** Being married off to a stranger, and her father's temper when the shed is mentioned.
- **Contradiction:** The chattiest girl on the street, who has kept her father's night work to herself.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a ribbon she bought from the Estonian spinner under her pillow because her mother disapproves of such luxuries.

## Work and money
She spins one and a half skeins a day and keeps a pfennig and a half of ribbon money. She buys her small things from the Estonian seller and gets honest weight. A bad week is a lost skein.

## Faction and belief
She has no faction. For a small favour she carries a note; for a large one she tells her sister; she would not inform, because she cannot keep her face still. She prays to St Catherine and knots her hair-ribbon three times for luck.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which maid is in love and which seller is honest. She would trade it for a new ribbon. She believes, falsely, that a swallow's nest over the door brings a husband.
