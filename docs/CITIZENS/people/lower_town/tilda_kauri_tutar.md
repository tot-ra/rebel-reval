## Biography
Born 1323 on a farm beside the Kopli road; her father, a Kauri of another stock, died of the bloody flux in 1336 and the landlord took the best cow. At fourteen she came to town with a cousin and was taken by Elin as a maid for her board and 4 schillings a year. She learned Low German at the market and Swedish words from Elin. She sleeps on a bench by the kitchen hearth, and thinks of the family as hers more than the farm.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A cow of her own and a husband who owns a plot, or at least a roof.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to the farm if the house loses its coin.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to the mason's family, and counts how much longer she must stay.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps a bundle of 14 pfennigs inside a roof-beam, and her dead father's carved bone token.

## Work and money
Pay is 4 schillings a year, board, and a pair of shoes at Michaelmas. She spends on thread and a church candle. A bad month means thinner porridge and no shoes.

## Faction and belief
None. A maid has no vote and little time. She would do a small favour for the household, hesitate over a large one, and refuse to inform on the family. She leaves a crust at the threshold and tells the beads Elin gave her.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who comes and goes at the master's door by night. She would trade it for a better pair of shoes. She falsely believes the flux returns every seventh spring.
