## At a glance
- Sturdy, tanned like a boat-hand, with a flat, tired murmur that makes everything sound like an apology.
- Minds the candle-shop counter and the porridge pot at once.
- Walks home from church with old Jaak Hindreku poeg, which the lane finds hilarious.
- Her right hand shakes at day's end.

## Biography
Born in 1316 at Nyland to a fisherman Erik, who sold her labour to a cousin at fourteen. She came to Reval in 1335 and has served the chandler's house for six years. A suitor drowned in 1340; she wears his bone ring on a cord. She learned German in the shop and has never been asked to forget Swedish.

She sleeps above the shop, among bundles of wicks.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep a place where she is not shouted at.
- **Fear:** Her trembling hand costing her the weights.
- **Contradiction:** Tired to the bone yet always willing to walk the long way home.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Old Jaak has promised her his bed-chest if she will sit with him at the end.

## Work and money
One mark and a half a year with board. Tools: scales, a ledger she cannot read, a candle-snuffer. She keeps six pfennigs. A bad month is a short count in the till.

- **Network:** [Jaak Hindreku poeg](../../people/lower_town/jaak_hindreku_poeg.md), retired craftsman: they met at the church door the week of Easter and fell into the habit of walking home together; she likes his slow voice and his stories.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A woman with one good place cannot risk it. She would carry a message across the lane and no further, and would not inform. She keeps Saint Lucy and a candle for the drowned.
