## Biography
Born in the winter of 1328 in the room over the quay. The bad winter of 1341 killed the family's pig and two neighbours' infants; she spun through it and was proud. Her mother taught her the drop spindle at six. She sells yarn to a net-maker on Aida and has never held the coins; her mother keeps them.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A spinning wheel of her own, which a guildswoman has promised her cheaply.
- **Fear:** Being married at fifteen to a man who beats.
- **Contradiction:** Generous with advice, stingy with thread.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Hides a bone needle she found in the harbour mud and will not give back.
