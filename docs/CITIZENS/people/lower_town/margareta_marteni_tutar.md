## Biography
Born in 1332, second of the line; she has been the nurse of every baby since Katrin. She learned to walk the lane with a child on her hip and has never, she says, had a full night's sleep.

## Motivation
- **Want:** One whole morning in bed.
- **Fear:** The winter cough, and the goose.
- **Contradiction:** Tired as an old woman, quick as a cat when the baby cries.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides bread-ends in the loft for Liis, because she does not trust the hearth supply.
