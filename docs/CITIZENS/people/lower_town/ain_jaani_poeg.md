## At a glance
- A sturdy, rosy boy with straw hair who sweeps shavings from first light and talks faster than he works.
- Left-handed in a shop built for right-handers; his plane strokes are neat, but his father hates it.
- Keeps a pet jackdaw on the roof, which steals pegs.
- Fears the dark under the stairs and the iron of the smith's hammer.

## Biography
Born in the winter of 1331/32, the first child of Jaan and Triin, Ain has known no other room than the workshop's. At six he sorted pegs; at nine he held boards for his father; this winter he cut his first dovetail, badly. His father tried to bind the left hand to his belt as a cure, and Triin took the cord away. He plays marbles of clay with his brother Lembit in the lane when no one is looking.

## Motivation
