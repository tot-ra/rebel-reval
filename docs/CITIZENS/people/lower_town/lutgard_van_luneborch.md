## Biography
Born about 1302 in Reval, the niece of the fishmonger Nicolaus van Luneborch, in a family that sold herring on Vana turg. She married Berend in 1322. She has borne eight children and buried one, and sold three years' yarn to pay for the roof. She spun for the weavers on Nunne in her girlhood and does so still.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To marry Geseke well and see Reynold safe.

- **Contradiction:** Quick to blush, slow to give way.

## Work and money
She spins for Nunne weavers at a few pfennigs per skein and brings in about 1 mark a month. Flax comes from Harju women; wool from the town market. She owes half a mark to a flax-seller.
