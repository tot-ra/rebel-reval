- Sits by the kitchen fire with a bowl of rushlight wicks she does not need to cut.
- Left-handed in a world that frowned on it; her high, quick voice scolds the maids in two breaths.
- Known to the whole lane as the one who gave out candle-ends at Candlemas.
- Oddity: a woman craftsman in a merchant house; no faction because she has seen three wars and no one's side won.

## Biography
Born in Reval in 1261, daughter of the Wise who first kept a wax workshop on Lai. She never married; she says the workshop was husband enough. She lived through the wars of 1313 to 1325, hid in the church of St Nicholas, and rebuilt the workshop after a fire. When arthritis forced her out of the trade she moved into her nephew's house, where she is quietly in charge of the candles and the kitchen.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To die in her own bed, with the candle of her own making.
- **Fear:** Fire; hunger; another siege.
- **Contradiction:** She scolds the household for waste but gives to every beggar at the door.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps a hoard of eleven marks in a candle mould beneath the stairs, meant for [Werner Wise](../../ledger/lower_town/lai.md#hh-lt-osm-w200516965).

## Work and money
Retired; the household supports her and she contributes her hoard and her tallow knowledge. She buys fat from the butchers on Vaimu and sells candle-ends for a few pfennigs. A bad month is fat prices rising and the price of wicks.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She has no use for kings or councillors; they all burn the same candles. A small favour she does; a large one she refuses; informing she would never. Prays at the Candlemas altar, believes in saints and hearth-spirits equally.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German, plain.
- **Delivery:** High and quick, with sudden stops.
- **Sample lines:** "Mind the flame." "I saw the Danes come and I saw them go." "Eat, you thin things."
- **Verbal tic:** "Hear me."

## Knowledge and rumours
She remembers the old streets, who built what, and how the fire of 1317 started. She trades stories for company. She believes the false rumour that a witch lives on Sauna street and curdles the milk.
