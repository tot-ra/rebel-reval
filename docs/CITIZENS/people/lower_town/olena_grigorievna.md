## Biography
Born in 1332 in Reval, elder daughter of Grigory, who died on the Narva road when she was eight. She remembers his laugh and the smell of his coat. She learned to count loaves at five and to count debts at eight. Her mother is her teacher and her grandmother her guardian.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to carry the long peel.
- **Fear:** Her mother crying at night.
- **Contradiction:** Silent among adults and bossy among children.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides one rusk each day under the oven-step for the street cat.
