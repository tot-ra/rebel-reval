## Biography
Born 1319 in a Swedish fishing family at Reval's harbour, the fifth of eight, put into service at eleven because the table was full. She served a fishmonger's widow and then the bakers from 1338. She lost the tooth at seventeen when a spoiled cook flung a crust, and has smiled with her lips since. She walks the market every day and has learned that a fair price is whatever two women agree at the stall.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save a mark for a lace collar.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to the harbour house.
- **Contradiction:** Careful with every pfennig, yet buys a ribbon each month she cannot afford.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She skims a pfennig from the bread money on market days and replaces it by Saturday.
