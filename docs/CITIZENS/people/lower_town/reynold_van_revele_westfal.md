## At a glance
- Thick, straight eyebrows meeting over the nose, so he looks permanently suspicious.
- High, quick voice that cracks at the end of sentences.
- Known as the boy who runs the fastest from the counting-room to the quay.
- Oddity: his name says Reval and Westphalia at once; his father was Westphalian and he was born here.

## Biography
Born in Reval in 1331 to a Westphalian cooper who died of lung-fever the winter he turned nine. A priest placed him with [Lambrecht van Minden](./lambrecht_van_minden.md) for seven years of service. He sleeps in the loft above the stable with Uku Kaspari poeg, and carries tales from the quay to the kitchen.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To become a merchant and have a ship.
- **Fear:** The master's belt and a rat in the loft.
- **Contradiction:** Brave on the quay, terrified in the dark.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has a stolen wooden cog he carved and hides in the hay.
