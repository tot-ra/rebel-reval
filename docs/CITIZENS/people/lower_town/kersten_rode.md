## At a glance
- A stocky, clean-shaven youth with a faint pock-scarred forehead and a nasal voice.
- Keeps the burgomaster's horses and rents spare stalls to visiting merchants.
- Sleeps in the hayloft with a knife under the straw.
- Oddity: a stable-boy who holds the whisper of half the carters in town.

## Biography
Born 1320 in a carter's yard by the Sand Gate; he had smallpox at five and lived. He mucked stalls for a carter at ten, and in 1338 the Zierenberg house took him on. He talks with the harbour carters and horse-dealers and hears things. A year ago he heard a name at the stable door and has been quiet since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stable of his own, with six stalls and a lease.
- **Fear:** A fire in the loft; losing the horses.
- **Contradiction:** Loyal to the master's horses more than the master.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He rents the second stall to a stranger at night for cash.

## Work and money
Pay is 6 schillings a year and board. Oats come from Harju farms, hay from the meadows by Kopli. A bad month is a lame horse.

## Faction and belief
Active in the Hanseatic order's sense: he passes word along the carters' route. His reason is simple: trade keeps horses fed. A small favour he grants for a coin; a large one for a name he trusts; informing he does when asked by his master. He rubs a horseshoe on the stable door before each feast.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German with carters.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, blunt, clipped.
- **Sample lines:** "Mind the grey, she bites." / "Not my horse, not my business." / "Hoof is lame, master."
- **Verbal tic:** "Aye, aye," under his breath.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who rides out at night. He would trade it for oats and a lease. He falsely believes the Vogt keeps a spare cavalry in the hill's stables.
