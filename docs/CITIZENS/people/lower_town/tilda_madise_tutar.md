## At a glance
- A sturdy girl with light-brown hair, wind-reddened cheeks and a voice so low people ask her to repeat.
- Spins with her mother at the window and sells thread at the market on Saturdays.
- Has twice refused a suitor; the lane has theories.
- Oddity: she is the family's best haggler and the one who whispers.

## Biography
Born 1322 in the narrow house on Laboratooriumi, eldest of three living daughters. At eight she was set to the distaff beside [Kristiina Mikku tütar](kristiina_mikku_tutar.md). A cooper's son courted her in 1340 and a boat-builder's journeyman in 1342; she turned both away. The bad winter of 1342 shaped her: she carried thread through snow to keep three houses from going without, and she will not be rushed into a marriage that drops her into another woman's kitchen.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A loom of her own and a trade not run by a mother-in-law.
- **Fear:** Ending up with a husband who drinks the thread money.
- **Contradiction:** Soft-voiced, yet the toughest bargainer in the house.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has a standing arrangement to spin extra for a Pskov carter and pockets the difference.

## Work and money
She earns about 2 schillings a month and gives most to her mother. Thread sells by the skein at the market; wool comes from the stalls by the pfund. A bad month is no Saturday buyers.

## Faction and belief
None. Her father's post and her mother's tongue are enough. She would do small favours for friends and nothing for strangers. She keeps St Olaf's day and a spoonful of porridge for the hearth.

Knows who is marrying and who owes. She would trade it for a loom. She falsely believes the cooper's son cursed her.
