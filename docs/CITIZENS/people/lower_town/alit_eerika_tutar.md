## Biography
Born in 1272, she twisted rope for the harbour for forty years. She remembers the Danish levies, the siege winters of the 1310s and the hunger of 1315. Her brother Jaan, Gerdt's father, moved a boundary stake between the yards; she saw it, and she has said nothing. She lives in her nephew's house with her own cot by the stove. She has a feud with a kind of knot: the Reval hitch, which she says is ugly.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see the boys grown and the house sound.
- **Fear:** Hunger, and dying in the cold.
- **Contradiction:** She scolds the maids and slips them coins.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows about the boundary stake.
