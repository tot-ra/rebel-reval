## Biography
Born 1338, a winter child who coughed her first year and has been watched ever since. She learned to walk by holding the kiln wall. She is left-handed, which her mother mends by tying a ribbon on the right, and the ribbon is always off.

## Motivation
- **Want:** The rooster gone and a doll with a face.
- **Fear:** The rooster, the dark loft and the cough coming back.
- **Contradiction:** She is timid with strangers and fearless with fire.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has buried a button in the yard and tells nobody where.
