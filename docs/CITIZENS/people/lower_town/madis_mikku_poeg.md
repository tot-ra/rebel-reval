- Sallow from the counting room, though he grew up on the shore.

## Biography
Born in 1322 in Reval, the son of a fishmonger named Mikk. He learned his letters from a parish priest and entered [Tilman van Lubeke](tilman_van_lubeke.md)'s household at fifteen. His voice roughened after a winter fever and has never smoothed. He lives with his mother in a small room off Tolli on feast days. He keeps a modest account of his wages in a leather purse.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To put by 30 marks and open a stall.
- **Fear:** Dismissal when the house's debts surface.
- **Contradiction:** He distrusts the clergy yet trades with one.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has lent 2 marks to a fellow clerk and not been repaid.

## Faction and belief
None; he is neither German nor rebel and wants his stall. For a small favour he would count; for a large one he would hesitate; he would not inform. He prays at St Olaf.

## Voice

- **Sample lines:** "A fair weight is a fair weight." "Osta sealt, buy there." "Count it twice."
