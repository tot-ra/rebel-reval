## Biography
Peter was born in Bremen, orphaned at nine, and shipped to Reval at eleven in the care of a skipper who knew Segebode. He cannot read, but he can count the weights of a lispfund of wax at a glance, and he remembers the price of every candle on Harju. He has been bound for three years and has four to go. He sleeps in the warehouse loft among the bales.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To own a warehouse and a ledger.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to Bremen on a cog.
- **Contradiction:** He is shy with people and ruthless with prices.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once split a wax delivery with another apprentice and kept the savings.
