## Biography
Peter was born in Bremen, orphaned at nine when the sweating fever took both parents, and shipped to Reval at eleven in the care of a skipper who owed Segebode a favour. He cannot read, but he can tell a lispfund of wax from a pound of tallow with his eyes shut, and he remembers the price of every candle on Harju. He has been bound for three years and has four to go. He sleeps in the warehouse loft among the bales, where the smell of salt and wax is the nearest thing to home. His voice broke early; he sounds like a carter and is teased for it by the clerks.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To own a warehouse and a ledger of his own, and to read it.
- **Fear:** Being sent back to Bremen on a cog, and being cheated of his term.
- **Contradiction:** He is shy with people and ruthless with prices.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once split a wax delivery with another apprentice and kept the savings, a sin for which he has twice confessed.

## Work and money
No wage; board, a tunic and a promised purse of two marks at the end of his term. He weighs wax, salt and iron at the warehouse beam and notes the weights on a tally stick. He saves tips of a pfennig from carters he does not cheat. A bad month is one with a late cog; then the warehouse is empty and the clerks send him to the quay to wait.

## Faction and belief
None. He is too small and too poor, and the clerks' whispers pass over his head. A small favour he does for a pfennig; a large one he refuses; informing he has not been asked. He prays to St Nicholas for fair winds and kisses the tally stick at night.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the weight and price of everything in the warehouse and which clerk slips out at dusk. He would trade it for a lesson in letters. He believes Jaan cheats at the beam, which is true only on Tuesdays.
