- Splits cart hire and a day's harbour labour with [Johannes van Verden](johannes_van_verden.md)'s man every week.
- Wears a moustache and chin beard to look older.

## Biography
Born in 1324 in Reval to Taniel, a porter who carried salt on the quay. A parish priest taught him letters in exchange for carrying water, and at fourteen [Tilman van Lubeke](tilman_van_lubeke.md) took him on as a runner and then as clerk, because his sums were cleaner than any German boy's. He has lived under the counting room's slanted roof since 1340. His father died last winter of a crushed chest, and his mother lives in a lane off Sulevimägi. He sends her 2 marks a year.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be called by his own name in a German ledger, not "the Estonian".
- **Fear:** That Tilman's debts will end the house.
- **Contradiction:** He is proud of his Latin yet hides it from carters.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a second tally of the house's debts and knows its true size.

## Faction and belief
None; as an Estonian in a German house he keeps his head down. For a small favour he would translate a letter; for a large one he would refuse; to inform he would lie. He prays at St Olaf and keeps a birch twig for his father.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian in the lane, Middle Low German in the house, Latin on parchment.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, careful, never raised.
- **Sample lines:** "The sum is the sum." "Ma ei vaidle, I do not argue." "Half the cart is mine."
- **Verbal tic:** Counts on his fingers aloud.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the house's debts and which carter is honest. He would trade it for a good word. Believes falsely that Tilman will sell the house to the Order.
