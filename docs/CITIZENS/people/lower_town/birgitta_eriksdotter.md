- Left-handed, which the cook calls clumsy and the nuns call quick.

- Runs a tiny trade of her own in thread, ribbon and eggs, mostly to the convent round the corner.

## Biography
Born 1312 in Nyland, Finland, daughter of a cooper, Erik. At six a sow bit her ear while she minded it; she learned to watch animals and people alike. Came to Reval at fifteen with her mother's cousin, and has served in three houses. She came to the Folkesson house in 1339 and remained, because Ulrika pays on quarter-day and the cook does not shout. She is not a rebel; she is a saver, with twelve marks' worth of ribbon in a chest.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stall of her own on the Holy Spirit steps.
- **Fear:** The tremor spreading to the left hand.
- **Contradiction:** She is thrifty and gives away her best egg to any child who asks.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** The ribbons are made from her own savings, and some from offcuts she takes from the cloth-room; she tells herself the mistress would not mind.

## Work and money
Wage 3 marks a year with board, plus linen and shoes. Her sideline adds perhaps 2 marks. She buys thread at the Lai stalls and sells to the sisters. A bad month is a lost parcel or a chill that lasts.

## Faction and belief
No faction: a servant has enough masters. For a small favour she carries a message, for a large one she asks the nun, to inform she is too frightened. St Olaf's, and a charm of red thread against fever.
