## Biography
Born about 1317 in Harju. Her family were bound; at fourteen she fled to town and was hired without questions. She has since been bent over tubs at the Timmermans. A cooper, Reimar Fatbinder, once gave her a barrel hoop to save her family, and she has paid for it since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear her debt to the cooper.
- **Fear:** Being named an escaped serf.
- **Contradiction:** Meek, yet watchful.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Her family is still bound on a Harju manor.
