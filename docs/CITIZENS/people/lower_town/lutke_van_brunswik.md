## Biography
Lutke was born in 1303 in the narrow Saiakang house, the son of a Brunswick spice dealer who settled there, died in 1322 and left debts. Lutke sailed as supercargo on Lübeck cogs and carried furs from Novgorod, then came home in 1331 to the house and the yard his father had fenced. A bad cargo of wax in 1337 left him owing, and his aunt took over the keeping of the household. He still stands in the Kindergilde yard and talks of ships, but has not owned a share since 1337. He pays his pledges with Saturday labour at his creditor's warehouse.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To regain his scales and a share in a cog before he is fifty.
- **Fear:** The stake in the yard coming to light, and being named the surety who did not collect.
- **Contradiction:** A man who talks of ships and sweeps another man's warehouse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Most of his news comes from who watches his door.

## Work and money
He deals in small lots of wax, tallow and herring, clearing perhaps 40 marks a year, much of it to interest. His scales and weights are pledged to Albert for 6 marks. He stood surety for Mikk Simoni poeg's 9-mark fine and has not been repaid. A bad month is a shortfall that sends a servant to the pawn shop.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A man in debt has no use for a side, he says, and a side has no use for him. He would lend a hand to anyone who paid, and talk to anyone who listened. He lights a candle to St Nicholas for ships, and spits over his left shoulder when a skipper laughs.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German in trade; Latin only for formulae.
- **Delivery:** Booming, theatrical, loud for the street; drops to a mutter when a creditor passes.
- **Sample lines:** "I have cogs in my head, friend." "Never trust a quiet man's scale." "Hol di fast!" (Hold fast!)
- **Verbal tic:** Starts every sentence with "Now then."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the harbour gossip, which cargoes are late, who has pawned what. He would trade it for a week's interest. He believes the false rumour that the Hansa will ban all small merchants from Novgorod.
