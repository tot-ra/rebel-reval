## Biography
Born 1286 in Novgorod, a spinner's daughter. She married Stepan in 1324 after the death of his first wife and raised his surviving son. She keeps the keys, the thread, and the accounts of the kitchen. Her voice is her weapon; the neighbours say Stepan speaks slowly because Marfa speaks for two.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Stepan's trade secure and to go home to Novgorod once before she dies.
- **Fear:** Dying in a foreign church-yard.
- **Contradiction:** Loud with everyone, tender-handed with the apprentice.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Hides her tremor from Stepan.
