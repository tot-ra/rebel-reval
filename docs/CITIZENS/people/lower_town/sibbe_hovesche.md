## Biography
Born in Reval in 1312 to a Hovesche family of grocers. She learned scales and weights in her father's shop, and at nineteen married [Lambrecht van Minden](./lambrecht_van_minden.md) with a dowry of the shop's goodwill. The shop moved to the front room of the Lai house, and she has run it alone since. Meinhard Wise's circle drew her in a year ago when she lent him weights; she has said yes ever since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A sound business to leave to Berta and the boys.
- **Fear:** A husband's politics bringing the house down.
- **Contradiction:** She keeps strict accounts and takes stock from sources she does not ask about.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Her pepper and thread sometimes come from a harbour lighter at odd hours; she suspects it is smuggled and does not ask.

## Work and money
Shop turnover about 150 marks a year; margin on pepper 1 schilling a pound. She buys from Lübeck merchants and the odd lighter. A bad month is a seizure of uncustomed goods.

## Faction and belief
Her Hanseatic loyalty is commercial: free roads mean full shelves. She would pass a message, hold a package, and carry a name to Meinhard but would not inform on her husband. She keeps all fasts and a sprig of lavender in the till.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German.
- **Delivery:** Low, unhurried, rarely looking up from scales.
- **Sample lines:** "Weight is weight." "Come back at Michaelmas, then." "I never ask where the pepper comes from."
- **Verbal tic:** Taps scale-pan twice.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows every maid's wage and every wife's debt. She would trade it for a favourable word with the guilds. She believes the false rumour that her husband has a mistress in Riga.
