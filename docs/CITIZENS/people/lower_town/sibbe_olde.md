## Biography
Born about 1280 in Reval. She married Jakob in 1298. She bore seven and buried four. In the winter of 1342 the lane ran short of firewood and she shared the household's pottage with her neighbours. She still counts the thread in her head, to the pfennig, though she cannot write.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** Being a burden.
- **Contradiction:** Merry, yet counts every skein.
