## Biography
Sivert was born in 1331 in Lübeck, the son of a ship's carpenter who sent him to Reval at ten to learn trade. He came with a letter and a cousin's name, and was taken in by Lutke van Brunswik, who was short of a boy. He has slept in the loft since, with Lambert Overdyk. He is learning weights, bills and who is who on the harbour. He has not yet been beaten, which he counts as luck.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see a ship go out and know what is in her hold.
- **Fear:** The pawnbroker's dog, and being sent home.
- **Contradiction:** He boasts of the harbour and is afraid of the dark stair.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has kept a bent nail he found on the quay in his shoe for luck.

## Work and money
He earns no wage, only bed, bread and two shirts a year. His master owes his father's guild a term-fee. A bad month is a spilled bag of pepper.

## Knowledge and rumours
