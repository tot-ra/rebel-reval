## Biography
Born 1332 on Lai. Henneke's friend the chaplain Lambrecht Wise hears his Latin on Saturdays, and a Dominican teaches him the psalms. His father hopes he will clerk; his mother says nothing. He sings in the choir at St Olaf's.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** His father's sad face.
- **Contradiction:** A choirboy who hates being looked at.
