## Biography
Born in 1318 in Kalarand, hired out at twelve as a kitchen girl in a German household, where she learned Low German from the cook. She came to Kaspar in 1339 after a quarrel with the mistress. She survived the winter of 1341-42 with Anna Aino tütar's shared stool and Ulrika Folkesdotter's soup.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A wage in coin, not in bread.
- **Fear:** Being dismissed without a reference in the cold.
- **Contradiction:** She keeps the house, yet dreams of leaving it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She keeps one mark sewn into her mattress.
