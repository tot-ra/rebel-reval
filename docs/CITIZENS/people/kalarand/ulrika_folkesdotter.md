## Biography
Born in 1316 on Gotland, brought to Reval at fourteen as maid. She came to Gunnar's house in 1338. The tremor began after the hard winter of 1341. She has few countrymen to talk with.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A kind hearth and a language to share.
- **Fear:** Being sent home.
- **Contradiction:** Cheerful, yet lonely.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She is teaching Olev Tanieli poeg Swedish sailors' songs.
