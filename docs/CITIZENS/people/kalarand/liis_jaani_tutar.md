## At a glance
- Sells hot pea pottage and fried herring from a brazier at the beach end of the lane.
- Cooks for hire at weddings, wakes and the odd Lower Town kitchen.
- Oddity: left-handed, so every ladle and knife in her stall is set backward.

## Biography
Born in 1288 in the hinterland, sent at ten as kitchen girl to a Lower Town household, where she learned to season, salt and stretch a pot. She married Olev Tanieli poeg in 1314, and in the hunger of 1315 fed the pair of them on bark flour. She has lost one child to fever and raised Villem and Laurents. Now she cooks for hire three days a week and keeps the brazier the other four.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A proper shop with a roof and a hearth of her own.
- **Fear:** Cold winters and hunger; the smell of a pot that scorches.
- **Contradiction:** She feeds half the lane yet cannot sit still to eat.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She water-thins the pottage on days when the pea-sack runs low.

## Work and money
She makes about two marks a year selling pottage and three on hire. Peas cost a mark the barrel, salt dearer; fuel is driftwood. A bad month is when the harbour freezes.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She cooks for whoever pays. She would give a small favour, not a large one, and never inform. She whispers a charm over the pot against scorching.

## Knowledge and rumours
