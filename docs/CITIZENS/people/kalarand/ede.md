## At a glance
- Ferries cargo between anchored ships and the quay in a flat-bottomed lighter she inherited.
- Rents a shed to Olev Tanieli poeg and bargains over it each quarter.
- Oddity: a woman running a boatman's trade; the lighter-men grumble and still hire her.

## Biography
Born in 1280 at Kalarand to a lighter-man. She rowed with her father at eleven, married the boatman Mihkel at twenty, and buried him in 1331 when a sling snapped. Since then she has worked the lighter, kept the plot, and taken in three souls. She has no children living.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hand the lighter to someone who will keep it.
- **Fear:** A lighter-man's widow is a poor target; she fears being bought out.
- **Contradiction:** She trusts the water more than her neighbours.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Her husband's name is cut under the thwart.

## Work and money
A lighter run pays an örtug or two; about six marks in a year. Shed rent from Olev adds three örtug a quarter. Debts: pitch owed to a boat-builder. A bad month is ice.

## Relationships
- **Household:** [Gerdt Lembitu poeg](../../people/kalarand/gerdt_lembitu_poeg.md), servant, handy with horses; [Helena](../../people/kalarand/helena.md), lodger and spinner, loud; [Lutsia Hindreku tütar](../../people/kalarand/lutsia_hindreku_tutar.md), servant who keeps the house.
- **Network:**
  - [Gunnar Bengtsson](../../people/kalarand/gunnar_bengtsson.md), lighter-man: she suspects him of undercutting the going price with stolen or smuggled stock; no proof, so far. She watches his loads.
  - [Olev Tanieli poeg](../../people/kalarand/olev_tanieli_poeg.md), neighbour: he rents a shed for tools and firewood at a rate neither considers fair to themselves. They haggle every quarter-day and are friends.
  - [Kaspar](../../people/kalarand/kaspar.md), harbour pilot: the lane's children play together despite the parents' coolness; she lets it pass, he pretends not to see. She is wary of his silence.
- **Others:** [Lars Knutsson](../../ledger/kalarand/western_coast_road.md#hh-kr-kalarand-12), ropemaker, sells her rope.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A small favour she gives; a large one she charges for; informing, never. She spits in the water before the first run each spring.

## Voice

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows each ship's load and who undercuts. She would trade it for a good price on rope. She believes the Swede steals fodder; unproved.
