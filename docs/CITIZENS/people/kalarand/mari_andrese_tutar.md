## Biography
Born in 1333, after the baby who died, Mari was late to speak and then spoke seldom. Her father says she was listening. At six she found a drowned fisherman's boot at the tide-line and told no one for a day, then led her mother to it. At eight she took in the gull. She keeps the stall dips in a row and knows if one is missing. She is slower than Elsa, and has stopped minding.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be let alone to watch the boats.
- **Fear:** Being rushed to answer; the pause is where the answer lives.
- **Contradiction:** She hates being hurried yet is the first to see danger.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She saw Eeva leave a bundle by the gate at night and told nobody.
