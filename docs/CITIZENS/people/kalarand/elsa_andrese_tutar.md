## Biography
Born in 1331 in the lean-to on the Western coast road, the first child after a boy who died two days old. At seven she learned to count on her mother's tally sticks; at nine a lighter-man named her "the Gull" after she screamed a fisherman out of a squall. The cold-sore scar came at eleven after a bad Lent. She hates the goose of the neighbouring plot, which has pecked her on the same shin three times. She minds Kristiina and carries the basket to the market with her mother.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be taken to the Lower Town market and be allowed to keep the stall alone for one afternoon.
- **Fear:** Dogs, and the day her mother's hand shakes too much to sell.
- **Contradiction:** She wants to be a grown trader, yet still sleeps with her sisters in one blanket.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides a bent copper ring found at the tide-line in a crack in the hearthstone.

## Work and money
She earns nothing but is worth a hired girl: two örtug a month if her mother had to pay for the carrying. She is given a pfennig on feast days and saves it for ribbon.
