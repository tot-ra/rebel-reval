## Biography
Born in 1307 on the shingle, Tõnu went to sea at ten with his father Lembit and now owns half a skiff with his brother-in-law. He married [Elisabet Hinricku tütar](../../people/kalarand/elisabet_hinricku_tutar.md) in 1334. They have four living daughters, Kadri, Susanna, Els and Elisabet. He sells to the fishmongers of the Lower Town, and when the boat goes out he leaves the house to the neighbour.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A full skiff of his own and a dowry for four girls.
- **Fear:** A bad herring season and a drowned crew.
- **Contradiction:** A man loved by his neighbours who hides his debts from them.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He half-owns a skiff he cannot afford to repair.

## Work and money
A good day is a barrel of herring worth three schillings; a bad one nothing. Salt from the Lower Town costs more each month. He owes a schilling on the half-share. A bad month is a gale.

## Faction and belief
None: the sea is neutral. A small favour is a fish; a large one depends on the tide; he would not inform. He pours the first fish-blood back into the sea.
