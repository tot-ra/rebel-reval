## At a glance
- Takes ships over the bars into Reval roads by the colour of the water, and is paid by the keel.
- Passes ship names, cargoes and Danish boat movements to the Harju circle; resents it.
- Oddity: a man who speaks barely above a whisper makes his living by being heard on a deck.

## Biography
Born in 1279 on a Harju manor, bound to the soil, and ran at seventeen after a flogging for a lost ox. He hid in the fishing beach, took a job as a boat boy, and learned the channel from an old Swede. By thirty he held his own pilot's boat. The ear was nicked by a hook at twenty. He has no wife and no children now; a woman died of fever in 1330. He rents beds to two lodgers and keeps two servants, and the household is the nearest he has to family. A year ago Kornel Antsu poeg paid for caulking when Kaspar's boat sprang a seam, and then asked for a small thing in return, and another.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To die in a bed on the beach, not hauled back to a manor.
- **Fear:** A bailiff recognising him; a runaway is property.
- **Contradiction:** He despises being used, yet keeps the work because the circle remembers he is bound by birth.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows which Danish guard-boat sleeps on the night watch, and has told only one man.

## Work and money
A pilotage fee is two or three örtug a keel, paid in silver by weight; about five marks in a year. Lodgers' rent adds one. He owes a Lower Town ropemaker half a mark. A bad month is fog: no ships, no fees.

## Faction and belief
Informer for the Harju Kings, never a fighter. His root is the manor and the flogging, and he acts from fear of being sent back, not love of kings. A small favour he grants; a large one he refuses; informing is already what he does. He keeps a pinch of grave-earth in his boat.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only, spoken near a whisper.
- **Delivery:** Soft, almost whispering, with long looks.
- **Sample lines:** "The bar moves with the wind." / "Say nothing, then I heard nothing." / "Ships come, ships go."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which ships carry grain, silver or arms, and the Danish boats' hours. He would trade it only for a safe bed. He believes Toompea will raise the poll tax on beach folk; unproved.
