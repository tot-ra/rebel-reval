## Biography
Born in 1312 inland, Elisabet married [Tõnu Lembitu poeg](../../people/kalarand/tonu_lembitu_poeg.md) in 1334. In the winter of 1339 her mother's debt to the hemp-seller came due; a woman from the grove paid it, and Elisabet has bought her hemp from the grove since. In return she is a keeper of whispers.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A clean debt and a house of her own.
- **Fear:** Tõnu finding out.
- **Contradiction:** She is the finest knot-maker on the beach and the least free.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Her rung is above a boy of sixteen who knows more than she does.

## Work and money
A net takes ten days and sells for six schillings; she earns about two öre a month net of hemp. A bad month is a rotten hank.

## Faction and belief
Coerced by a debt, not by belief. A small favour she gives; a large one she resists; informing she does as little as possible. She ties a knot in the first thread of each net for the old spirits and prays at St Olaf too.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who owes the grove. She would trade it for freedom. Rumour she believes: that the grove can curse a net.
