## Biography
Born in 1304 at Kalarand, worked as a deckhand on Gotland cogs and learned the harbour raiders' trade. He came ashore at thirty and hauls cargo. He moves goods for the Vitalienbrüder and keeps a wary eye.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A share of a good haul.
- **Fear:** A customs search.
- **Contradiction:** Laughing, yet watchful.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** A crate under his bed is not cargo.

## Faction and belief
Core of the Vitalienbrüder. His root is the harbour's unequal tolls; he acts from appetite for plunder. A small favour he gives; a large one for a share; informing, never. He crosses himself at sea.
