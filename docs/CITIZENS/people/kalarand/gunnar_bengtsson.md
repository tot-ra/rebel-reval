## At a glance
- Runs a lighter and sells small things (tar, nails, rope-ends) from a shelf by the door.
- Left-handed, which means his oars are rigged opposite to the lane's.
- Oddity: speaks only Swedish and makes himself understood with a nasal bark and gestures.

## Biography
Born in 1303 on Gotland to a ship's carpenter. He crewed a cog to Reval at twenty and stayed after the master drowned. He bought a lighter on credit, repaid it in eight years, and now rents beds to a lodger, a porter and a servant. His wife died of a fever in 1335. He sells tar bought in bulk.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be taken for a lawful tradesman, not a foreigner.
- **Fear:** A guild or council action against Swedes.
- **Contradiction:** He is honest and suspected of cheating.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** His tar comes from a Gotland cousin, never taxed.

## Work and money
A run pays two örtug; about five marks a year; the tar shelf adds two. Lodgers' rent adds one. A bad month is a seized load.
