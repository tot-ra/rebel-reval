## Biography
Born in 1307 at Kalarand to a carter. He worked from boyhood with horses at the carriers' yard. After a flogging of a friend for hiding a bound man, he started listening to the quiet men. He now carries messages in his head, not on his body.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stall of his own to keep a horse.
- **Fear:** A flogging; being named.
- **Contradiction:** He calms horses all day and is restless at night.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He leaves oats for a man hiding in the hayloft once a week.

## Faction and belief
A sympathiser of the Black Cloaks. His root is the flogging he watched; he acts from loyalty to a friend. A small favour he gives; a large one only if it spares a man; informing, never. He crosses himself at a horse's death.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home, Low German in the yard.
- **Delivery:** Husky, light, quick.
- **Sample lines:** "Whoa, easy now." / "Oats are cheap, ask no more." / "Walk with me, friend."
- **Verbal tic:** "Easy, easy."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who rides where and which cart carries what. He would trade it for a good stall. He believes a white horse brings death; false.
