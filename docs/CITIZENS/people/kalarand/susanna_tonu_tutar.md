## Biography
Born in 1336, Susanna was told to use her right hand and refused; her father, a left-hander himself, stopped them. She sorts shells by colour and keeps them in a crack in the wall.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To finish her shell line.
- **Fear:** Someone moving her shells.
- **Contradiction:** She is calm and cries at a spilled bowl.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She talks to the sea when no one is near.
