## Biography
Born in 1316 at Kalarand, orphaned at fourteen. He digs ditches, carries stone and hauls sand. He lodges with Gunnar for a mark a year.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** Illness that keeps him from the gate.
- **Contradiction:** Precise in speech, clumsy in love.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He is courting Helena, who laughs.
