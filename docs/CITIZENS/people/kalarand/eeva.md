## Biography
Born in 1314 in Kalarand, hired out at nine to wash nets for a lighter-man. She came to Kaspar in 1338. The stoop and hoarseness came by twenty-five. In the hard winter she survived next to Elisabet Hinricku tütar, sharing a bed of nets for warmth.
