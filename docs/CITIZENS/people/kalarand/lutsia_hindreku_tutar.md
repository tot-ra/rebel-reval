## At a glance
- Cooks, washes, scours and runs the house for Ede.
- Speaks so slowly that listeners finish her sentences wrongly.
- Oddity: a rolling, wide-legged gait like a sailor ashore.

## Biography
Born in 1312 in Kalarand, hired out at eleven. She came to Ede at twenty and has stayed. The gait came from carrying water yokes since childhood. She survived the hard winter with Anna Aino tütar's help.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband with a boat.
- **Fear:** Going hungry in winter.
- **Contradiction:** Slow to speak, quick to act.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has begun to hope.

## Work and money
Her wage is five örtug a quarter and board; she saves nothing. A bad month is no pay.
