## Biography
Born in 1329, the younger son. At six he was the feverish child Olev and Andres carried to the herb-wife in the night. At twelve he was bound to a Lower Town cooper for seven years; the master feeds him but takes his wages. He learned weights from a quarrel and caution from a loss.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To make a barrel that holds.
- **Fear:** The master's strap, and a ruined cask.
- **Contradiction:** He hates the yard yet loves the smell of oak.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He pockets one hoop-withy a week and sells it for a pfennig.
