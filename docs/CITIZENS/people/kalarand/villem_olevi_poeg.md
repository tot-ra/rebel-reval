## Biography
Born in 1322, the first son of Olev. He carried his first stone at eleven and has not stopped. He survived the winter of 1341-42 beside Andres Lauri poeg, hauling frozen ballast for a half-pay; they have not discussed it. He has never had a wage he did not hand to his mother. He sings in the church at St Olaf, where nobody sees his face.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A wage of his own and a girl who does not laugh.
- **Fear:** Being his father, bent at sixty.
- **Contradiction:** He is the strongest in the lane and the most afraid of speaking.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once carved a wooden spoon for a girl in the lane and burned it when she laughed.
