## Biography
Born in 1323 in a Harju hamlet, her family burned out in a manor dispute. She came to Reval at fifteen and found a spinner's bench at Ede's. The booming voice came from shouting across fields. She was asked a year ago to carry a skein and has done so since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To bring her sister from the manor.
- **Fear:** A search of her skeins.
- **Contradiction:** She shouts, but hides the most.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Her skeins carry cords that Paul Tõlli poeg reads.

## Faction and belief
A courier for the Harju Kings. Her root is the burned hamlet; she acts from love of her sister. A small favour she gives; a large one she may; informing, never. She says the Ave Maria at the spindle.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only.
- **Delivery:** Booming, quick, laughing.
- **Sample lines:** "Skeins for Vene, come along!" / "Don't ask, it's wool." / "Hush, who is listening?"
- **Verbal tic:** "Hey, hey."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who reads cords and who delivers. She would trade it for her sister. She believes the viceroy has fled; false.
