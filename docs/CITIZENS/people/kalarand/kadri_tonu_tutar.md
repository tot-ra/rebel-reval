## Biography
Born in 1335 on the shingle, Kadri is the eldest. She learned to gut herring at five and now minds her sisters. She wants to go out in the skiff, which her father forbids.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To go in the boat.
- **Fear:** Her father's anger.
- **Contradiction:** She is the loudest of the girls and hides when it thunders.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has swum in the shallows when told not to.
