## Biography
Born in 1313 at Kalarand to a net-mender. His father died when he was nineteen; he took up day work and sleeps in Kaspar's loft. He learned knots from his father and still ties a dozen no sailor knows. The grey came early, after a winter of 1341 when his savings went on his mother's burial.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A quarter-share in a fishing boat.
- **Fear:** A bad winter that eats the savings again.
- **Contradiction:** He speaks in a monotone yet sings softly in his sleep.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has eleven örtug in a knotted rope under the loft boards.

## Work and money
He makes about three marks a year plus pfennigs for mending; lodging costs one. His savings stand at eleven örtug. A bad month means skipping the evening porridge.

## Faction and belief
