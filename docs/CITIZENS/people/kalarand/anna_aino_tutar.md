## At a glance
- Sells needles, thread, salt, tallow dips and ribbons from a basket and a plank on trestles at the beach end of the road.
- Keeps accounts on notched tally sticks in a pouch that never leaves her belt.
- Oddity: a voice with no rise or fall, which makes her prices sound like law.

## Biography
Born in 1304 on the beach, the daughter of a woman who sold smoked fish from a basket. She learned prices at nine and weights at twelve. The nose was broken at fourteen when a loaded hatch slammed in a lighter's hold; nobody set it. She married Andres Lauri poeg at twenty-one, and when his wages failed in the hunger of 1341 she borrowed two marks of thread stock from a Lower Town wholesaler and has repaid it, penny by penny, since. She now sells from the trestle by day and carries her basket into Lower Town on market days.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To own a lockable chest and a covered stall, a roof over the stock.
- **Fear:** The tremor spreading to her left hand; a scale she cannot hold is a shop she cannot keep.
- **Contradiction:** She trusts no one with credit, yet half her stock sits in the neighbours' debts.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has sixteen örtug sewn into the hem of the winter gown and has told no one, not even Andres.

## Work and money
Margins are tiny: a pfennig on a needle, three on a dip. Her stock from the Lower Town wholesaler costs about three marks a quarter; she turns it twice and clears perhaps two marks a year, roughly what Andres earns on a good run. She lends to neighbours on tallies and rarely loses; on a bad month she sells her own thread. She buys salt from lighters, thread from Hanseatic traders, tallow from a beach butcher.

## Faction and belief
No faction; she cannot afford any politics. A small favour she grants if she is paid; a large one, no; to inform, she would ask what the information was and sell it only if it hurt no neighbour. She prays at St Olaf and hangs a rowan twig above the stock.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; she knows a handful of Low German prices.
- **Delivery:** Flat, steady, no emphasis; her prices carry across the beach.
- **Sample lines:** "A pfennig the needle, three the dip." / "I wrote it on the stick, so it is true." / "Come back when you have it."
- **Verbal tic:** "Written, then."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who owes whom on the beach to the örtug, and when each ship's wife has money. She would trade it for a place under cover. She believes Liis Reinu tütar waters her oil; it is false, she merely buys better.
