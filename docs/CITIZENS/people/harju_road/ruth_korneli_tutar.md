- Short, stocky, ruddy woman, left-handed, with deep laugh lines.
- Sings under her breath in Estonian and speaks German to the butcher with the same lilt.
- Keeps Veronika's house and hen while the old woman walks the herds.
- Believes, quietly, that her mistress is up to something, and has decided not to know.

## Biography
Born 1318 in a Harju village, she came to town at fifteen when her father could not feed her, and was taken into Veronika's household for a coat and board. She has stayed ten years.

She learned German at the butcher's counter and her mistress's habit of counting. She sends a pfennig a month to her mother and has never been paid in coin otherwise.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband with a steady trade and a roof of his own.
- **Fear:** Being turned out if Veronika is fined again.
- **Contradiction:** Jokes constantly and tells no one anything that matters.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has seen strangers come to the back door at dusk and has bolted it behind them without looking.

## Work and money
Board, a yearly coat, and two pfennigs at Easter; she sends one home. She has never saved more than three pfennigs.
