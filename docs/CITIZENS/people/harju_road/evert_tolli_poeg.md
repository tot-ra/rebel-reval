## Biography
Born 1338, the loud one. The squint was noticed at two, and his mother put a hand over the good eye to see what he would do; he laughed. He has never been sick for long, which his brother resents.

## Motivation

- **Fear:** Being left alone in the loft.
- **Contradiction:** Fearless with dogs, terrified of the dark.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He ate the heel of bread meant for his sister, and blamed the hen.
