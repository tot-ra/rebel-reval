## Biography
Born 1304 in a Harju village near an oak her grandmother called "the elder", she married Siim in 1327 and moved to Harju road. Her first daughter, Marta, died at two of a fever in 1332; Ell kept the old way of mourning, bread and salt at the hearth for nine nights. The priest at St Olaf's scolded her; she did it anyway.

Since 1340 she has walked, one night in the month, to an oak outside the town, and has brought others. She spins for three Lower Town households and says nothing.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the old rites alive for Marta's sake.
- **Fear:** The priest, the Vogt, her husband's silence turning into questions.
- **Contradiction:** The calmest woman on the lane, and the only one who breaks the law monthly.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She is a link in a small circle, and has told no one outside it, not even the confessor.

## Work and money
Spins for a pfennig and a half per skein; about fifteen pfennigs a week. Wool comes from the households. She pays for the children's bread with it. She owes nothing.

## Faction and belief
Cult of Metsik, secret sympathiser. Her root is Marta's death and the priest's scolding; her branch is memory. She acts from grief, not zeal. A small favour she gives in bread; a large one she would not give outside the circle; informing she would refuse and bear the cost. She goes to Mass and to the oak.

## Voice

- **Delivery:** Flat, monotone, steady.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows which oaks outside town are still tended. Believes, falsely, that the Danes will fell the grove before summer.
