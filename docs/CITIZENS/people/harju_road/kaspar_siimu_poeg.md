## Biography
Born 1330. At seven he was kicked by the mill horse, which left the scar; at ten it bit his ear. He has loved it ever since. His father taught him the hopper and the toll. He wants to drive a cart.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To drive a cart through the gate.
- **Fear:** His father's temper over short weight.
- **Contradiction:** Careless with himself, careful with the horse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once sold a handful of toll-grain for a ribbon.
