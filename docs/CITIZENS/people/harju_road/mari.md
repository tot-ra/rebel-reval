## At a glance

## Biography
Mari was born in 1287 by the Harju road, daughter of the boat-builder Evert, and came to the harbour at fourteen. She married a carter, Jaan, who died in 1330 when a load of casks slid on the hill below the Smiths' gate. Her daughter Aino died in childbed in 1328 and her son Andres in the autumn of 1337, drowned off the harbour mole; she raised their children, [Triin Aino tütar](../../people/harju_road/triin_aino_tutar.md) and [Madis Andrese poeg](../../people/harju_road/madis_andrese_poeg.md), and keeps her old father, [Evert Marteni poeg](../../people/harju_road/evert_marteni_poeg.md), under the same roof.

In the winter of 1338 the yard's master, a burgher she calls only "the master", cut her wage to scraps; a woman from the grove brought barley and a promise. Mari has been the grove's ear since. Now she mucks out the master's stalls and keeps what she hears.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the shed-room roof dry and the two children fed until Triin is married and Madis has a trade.
- **Fear:** The master turning her out; being named as the one who talks.
- **Contradiction:** She hates being a debtor, yet repays the grove's barley with gossip because nothing else she owns has value.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has never told the grove who her father is leaning toward, because she suspects, and does not want to know.

## Work and money
She earns about three öre a month in coin and a bowl of meal at harvest; carters tip her a pfennig for a well-rubbed horse. Tools: a hoof-pick and a currycomb with three teeth missing. She counts the yard's lamp-oil and the number of farm-hands at [Thomas Overdyk](../../people/lower_town/thomas_overdyk.md)'s stable in her head every night. A bad month is a lame horse she did not catch.

## Faction and belief
Mari is an informer, not a believer in the cult's creed; the grove fed her when the poor-box did not. A small favour she gives gladly; a large one (hiding a runaway) she refuses; she repeats nothing she has not heard twice. She prays at St Olaf and hangs a rowan twig over the stable door.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; she understands a little Low German from carters but never answers in it.
- **Delivery:** Low and unhurried, never raised; she speaks to horses at the same pace as to people.
- **Sample lines:** "Leave him with me. He kicks left." / "Three loads for the Viru gate and none for the Smiths'. Odd, that." / "Pole minu asi." (Not my business.)
- **Verbal tic:** Ends statements with "so it goes", flatly.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which carters carry what. She would trade it for a roof beam or an apprenticeship for Madis. Rumour she believes: that the Danish viceroy has a cart of silver to hire Livonian mercenaries.
