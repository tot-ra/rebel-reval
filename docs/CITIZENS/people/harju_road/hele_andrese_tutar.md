## Biography
Born 1310 in a coast village, married Joosep in 1332. She lost two babies before Hindrek, and bore Mett last spring. The tremor came after the second.

She spins steadily, minds the infant, and keeps an eye on the road when her husband goes out.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Mett walk.
- **Fear:** Her husband not coming home.
- **Contradiction:** Practical and calm, yet she will not look at what she knows.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has found a knife under the shed floor and put it back.

## Faction and belief
None. She keeps out by choice: the baby is more than her husband's debts. A small favour she would do; a large one she would not; informing she would do only for Hindrek's sake. She prays at St Olaf's and ties a red thread on the cradle.
