## Biography
Born 1282 in a coast village, she twisted nets from age ten and moved to Reval in 1305. She worked for a net-seller at the harbour for thirty years, took in cordage at home after, and retired when her fingers stiffened in 1338. She lives with her daughter Berta and son-in-law Olev.

She is distantly related by marriage to Ruth Korneli tütar and to Mari the ostler, but nobody can trace how.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Magdalena married to a decent man.
- **Fear:** A hard winter; being a burden.
- **Contradiction:** She claims to hate gossip and keeps a better record of it than the church.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has guessed what Olev does at night and has hidden his boots twice.

## Faction and belief
None. She has seen too many rises to trust one. A small favour she will do; a large one never, for the child's sake. She says her rosary on a cord of knots and also ties a knot against the evil eye.
