## Biography
Born 1291 in a Harju village, he drove for a manor until 1318, when he bought a cart and ran. He was free by the town's law within the year. He married Mett in 1322. Sofia was born in 1331; a second child died young.

He drives from the quay to the merchants' yards, to the gates, and sometimes beyond.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A second horse, so he can run two carts.
- **Fear:** An axle breaking on the Pärnu road with a merchant's wine aboard.
- **Contradiction:** He is cautious about everything but his own credit, which he stretches to the quarter-day.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** One of his merchants asked him to carry something not on the bill, and he did.

## Work and money
Charges per load; a good week yields five schillings, paid at quarter-days. Fodder costs a schilling a week. The shed rent from Joosep is eight pfennigs a quarter. He owes the wheelwright for a new axle.

## Faction and belief
None. His root is the road; politics is weather. A small favour he will do for the price of a load; a large one never; informing would cost him four customers. He crosses himself at the gate and keeps a horseshoe over the door.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian; market German for weights and loads.
- **Delivery:** A flat, tired murmur.
- **Sample lines:** "Load's on, price is the price." / "Axle's sound, don't ask twice." / "Hüva, hüva." (Fine, fine.)

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows what merchants ship and when. Believes, falsely, that the Order will close the Pärnu road.
