## At a glance
- Small for seven, pale and sallow, with brown hair that stands up at the back.
- Speaks in a flat even voice that makes every sentence sound like a decision.
- Counts things: stones, hens, steps to the well.
- Fears the goose at the corner.

## Biography
Born 1336, a winter child. At four he coughed all of the bad winter of 1340 and his father and Olev, the neighbour, carried him through the dark to the herb-wife. He remembers the swaying and the smell of smoke. Since then he is allowed by the fire, and watches.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To have a stone of his own that nobody takes.
- **Fear:** The goose at the corner, and the dark when his father is away.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet, yet the only one who will tell the grown-ups they are wrong.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has hidden a smooth grey stone under the loft bed and has told Evert it is a saint's bone.
