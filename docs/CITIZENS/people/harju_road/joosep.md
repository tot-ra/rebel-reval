## Biography
Born 1306 on a gate-farm, he took over three strips in 1330. Hail destroyed the rye in 1339; he borrowed three marks of seed-silver from a smith's widow who turned out to be a Black Cloak. He has paid the interest in errands ever since. He fell ill that winter with a fever that left his voice gravelly.

He lives with Hele and two children in a house with a shed, which Taniel rents out, and which his circle uses.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the three marks and be nobody's link.
- **Fear:** His son Hindrek being asked to carry a message.
- **Contradiction:** A coerced man who gives orders, and does not believe in any of them.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has told the circle he cannot read, so nothing is written.

## Work and money
Three narrow strips; a garden. The shed rent to Taniel is eight pfennigs a quarter, which neither thinks fair. The three marks are owed to the circle; he has repaid about one in errands. A bad month is a missed sowing.

## Faction and belief
Black Cloaks, coerced. His root is the hail and the debt, not the guilds. He acts because he is owed nothing and owes three marks. A small favour he will do; a large one he cannot refuse; he would not inform because he would have to say who recruited him. He leaves a handful of seed at the field's corner.
