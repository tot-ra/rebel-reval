## Biography
Born 1337, the year her father planted the rowan. At three she coughed through a winter and her father's neighbour Siim carried her to the herb-wife in the dark. She remembers the swaying and the smoke. Now she is strong, and spends the days between the brewhouse, the garden and the lane.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed into the brewhouse.
- **Fear:** The miller's horse.
- **Contradiction:** Talkative with plants, silent with people.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She buried a broken cup under the rowan as an offering.
