## Biography
Born 1329 in the loft on Harju road, the first to live. Her grandfather put a spindle in her hand at six and noticed that she used the left, and was cross for a month. Her mother let it be. By ten she could spin a pfund of wool a week, and the bad winter of 1340 taught her what a pfund of wool is worth when there is no rye.

She lives now by the spindle, the small loft, and the three younger children she minds when her mother carries the wash.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Leather shoes, and a place in a household where she is not sent out in snow in clogs.
- **Fear:** Being put into service with a man who counts the bread she eats.
- **Contradiction:** She is the family's quietest member and the best at lying to a creditor.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** The shoe-money, eleven pfennigs, kept in a split under the hearth stone, and her plan to spend it at Michaelmas.

## Work and money
Spins for a Lower Town household at three pfennigs the pfund of thread. She earns about eighteen pfennigs in a week when work is steady. Wool comes from the households; she returns thread and gets paid at the door. Eleven pfennigs saved.
