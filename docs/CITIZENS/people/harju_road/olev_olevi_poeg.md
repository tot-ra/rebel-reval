## Biography
Born 1307 in a gate-farm on the Harju road, the only son of Olev the elder, who died in the cattle plague of 1332 and left the strips and a debt. Olev married Berta in 1334 and moved her mother Ede in the same winter. When Magdalena was born in 1337 he planted a rowan at the corner of the plot.

The winter of 1340 taught him that a neighbour can be a better granary than a lord. When Siim Niklase poeg carried his feverish daughter through the dark to the herb-wife, Olev owed a debt he cannot repay in coin, and he knows it.

He now works three strips and a garden, sells peas and rye in the market, and sits in a cell of the Black Cloaks he does not call by name.

## Work and money
Three strips of rye and peas; flax in the garden. A good year brings eleven schillings of grain sold; a bad year brings debt to the miller. Berta's ale adds two or three schillings in a week. Ground-rent is paid at Michaelmas. He borrows an iron-shod ditching spade from Joosep and has done so four times this spring.

## Faction and belief
Secret sympathiser. His root is the 1332 levy on his father's strips, not the guilds. He acts from obligation: someone helped once and now he repays. A small favour he will do, a large one only if it is for Tõll's or Siim's family. He would not inform. He leaves the first sheaf of rye in the field for the old spirits.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows which strips will flood, and which of the gate-farms are being eyed for the levy. Believes, falsely, that the Harju kings will arrive with horses before Whitsun.
