## Biography
Born 1328 in the house on Harju road, the eldest of four. She learned to spin at her mother's knee and to count at the mill. The bad winter of 1340 took her grandmother and left her with a cough; she survived on porridge and a cloak lent through the winter.

She spins now alongside her mother and sells thread at the market, and lately she has noticed that her mother does not come home when the bell rings.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To have a cloak of her own.
- **Fear:** Her mother's silence turning to trouble.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet at home and the loudest laugher at market, with her hand over her cheek.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Twice she has followed her mother partway down the road and turned back at the ditch.
