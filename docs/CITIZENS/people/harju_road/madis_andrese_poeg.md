## Biography
Madis was born in 1330, the son of Andres, who drowned off the harbour mole in 1337. His grandmother [Mari](../../people/harju_road/mari.md) took him on her hip and never put him down. The drowning left him with a fear of loud water and a habit of whispering. He keeps the hens and the household's one goat, carries eggs to the market, and wonders when somebody will give him a trade.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be taken on as an apprentice, any trade, by anyone who will not shout.
- **Fear:** Raised voices; the harbour; being left alone with the dark.
- **Contradiction:** He is afraid of everything and yet is the one who goes out at night to count the goat.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has seen his cousin Triin leave at night and says nothing.
