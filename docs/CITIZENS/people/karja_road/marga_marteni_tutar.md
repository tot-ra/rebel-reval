## Biography
Born in 1317 on a farm outside the gate, Marga came to town at fifteen and married [Mikk Madise poeg](../../people/karja_road/mikk_madise_poeg.md) in 1336. She bore Mattes, Jakob and Susanna. She buys milk from the burghers' cows by the pail and sells curds and butter. Half of what she earns goes to rent and half to the Amt dues.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To eat a full meal and see the children do the same.
- **Fear:** Mikk's debt reaching the Amt.
- **Contradiction:** She is the hardest bargainer on the road and gives half her curds away.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has begun to walk home with another man after Mass, innocently, and does not tell Mikk.

## Work and money
She makes three or four öre a month beyond her cost; Amt dues are two pfennigs a month. Debts: none of her own. A bad month is spoilt milk in warm weather.

## Faction and belief
None: she cannot afford to hold a view. A small favour is a cup of whey; a large one she refuses; she does not inform. She pours the first drop of every churn on the ground.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, loud for the road.
- **Sample lines:** "Fresh today, not yesterday's." / "Pay now or go thirsty." / "Ole tubli."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which cow gives the richest milk. She believes the rumour that milk spoils when a rising is near.
