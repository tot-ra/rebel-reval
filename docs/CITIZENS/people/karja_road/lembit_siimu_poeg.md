## Biography
Born in 1306 at the quay, Lembit trained under a porter-master who later trained [Lars Folkesson](../../people/viru_road/lars_folkesson.md). In 1333 a hook took the rim of his ear. He rents a room from [Tõll Hinricku poeg](../../people/karja_road/toll_hinricku_poeg.md) and a lock-up under the eaves for salt and hides. He is respected on the quay and resents being treated as a beast.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be paid by the load, not by the day, and to own the lock-up.
- **Fear:** A merchant who hires another gang.
- **Contradiction:** He despises the merchants and depends on them.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He quietly listens to the Black Cloaks and has said yes to nothing.

## Work and money
About three schillings a week in season; stock-keeping fees extra. He keeps salt and hides for merchants at a pfennig a lispfund a week. A bad month is a late ship and a gang idle.

## Faction and belief
A secret sympathiser of the Black Cloaks because the Danish guard once beat him for a short load; he says yes to nothing and no to nobody. A small favour, moving a sack, he gives; a large one waits; informing he does not do. He prays at St Olaf and touches the nicked ear for luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only.
- **Delivery:** Low and unhurried, no wasted words.
- **Sample lines:** "It will be there when it is paid." / "Ask the clerk." / "Ootame."
- **Verbal tic:** Pauses before every yes.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which warehouse holds what. He would trade it for a fair rate. Rumour he believes: that the Order has a ship waiting at Narva.
