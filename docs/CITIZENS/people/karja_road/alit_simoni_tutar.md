## Biography
Born in 1332, Alit is the eldest of the household. She carries water, minds Kristiina and runs errands to the market, where she listens to the German traders and repeats what she hears. Her squint came with a fever at four.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To work in the Lower Town as a maid and wear a clean cap.
- **Fear:** Being laughed at for the squint.
- **Contradiction:** She wants to speak German and forgets Estonian words.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has seen her mother whisper at a funeral.
