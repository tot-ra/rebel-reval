## Biography
Born in 1333, Veronika took the household's reckoning from her mother and likes numbers the way her father does. The ear was nicked by a dog at the miller's. She sells her mother's thread to the weaver and collects the coins.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the count right.
- **Fear:** The household running out of bread.
- **Contradiction:** She is small and orders adults about.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows the family owes the miller two öre.
