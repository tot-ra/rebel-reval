## Biography
Born in 1300 outside the Cattle Gate, Simon has dug ditches, carried bricks and hauled hay since he was nine. He married [Ursula Mikku tütar](../../people/karja_road/ursula_mikku_tutar.md) in 1327. Of six children four live. Two winters ago the carpenter [Tõll Hinricku poeg](../../people/karja_road/toll_hinricku_poeg.md) built on to the shared wall, and his chimney has smoked into Simon's loft since. Simon learned numerals from a tally-keeper who paid him in beer.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Steady work and a dry loft.
- **Fear:** A bad winter and a sick child.
- **Contradiction:** He distrusts every writer and is proud of reading numbers.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** After Mass he tells Telseke about his troubles, and feels guilty for liking it.

## Work and money
Four pfennigs a day when hired, perhaps three days a week; Ursula's spinning adds a schilling. He owes two öre to the miller. A bad month is a wet spring with no digging.
