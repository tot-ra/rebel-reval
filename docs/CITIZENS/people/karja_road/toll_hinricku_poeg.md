## Biography
Born in 1296 on the Tartu road, Tõll was apprenticed at thirteen to a Danish carpenter on Toompea and rebuilt half the hill's roofs after the wars of 1313 to 1325. His wife Anne died of a lung fever in 1339; their children did not live. He took in a lodger, [Lembit Siimu poeg](../../people/karja_road/lembit_siimu_poeg.md), to ease the rent and the quiet.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be asked to build a house with a proper chimney.
- **Fear:** A bad winter and a bad cough.
- **Contradiction:** A man who builds for everyone and sits in a smoky room.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He cannot afford the bricks for a proper flue.

## Work and money
About two schillings a week in good weather; timber from the Harju sawyers. Owes four öre to the sawyer. A bad month is a wet roof and no payment.

## Faction and belief
None: work is work whoever pays. A small favour he gives, a large one he prices; he would not inform. He crosses himself before the ridge-beam is raised.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; a few German carpentry terms.
- **Delivery:** Clear and carrying.
- **Sample lines:** "Measure twice, and then ask the beam." / "It will draw when the wind turns." / "Ei, ei."
- **Verbal tic:** Taps the wall with a knuckle.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which beams in the Lower Town are rotten. He would trade it for bricks. Rumour he believes: that the Lower Town gates are to be rebuilt in stone.
