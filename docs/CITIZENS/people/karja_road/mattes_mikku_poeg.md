## Biography
Born in 1337 in the spring calving season, Mattes was put on a cow before he could talk. He learns the herd-calls from [Mikk Madise poeg](../../people/karja_road/mikk_madise_poeg.md) and repeats them at the top of his lungs.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To lead the herd on his own.
- **Fear:** The church goat.
- **Contradiction:** He is bold with cows and hides from geese.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has named the lead cow and tells her things.
