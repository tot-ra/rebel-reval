## Biography
Born in 1304 on a gate-farm, Ursula spun for the market from eight and married [Simon Leho poeg](../../people/karja_road/simon_leho_poeg.md) in 1327. She lost a front tooth to a fever. A year ago, in a lean March, a neighbour left a sack of barley at her door and later asked for a favour in return. Since then she goes to funerals she would otherwise skip.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Barley in the bin and the debt of the sack paid off.
- **Fear:** Being found out by the Vogt, losing the children.
- **Contradiction:** She hates being treated as a debtor and cannot refuse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has never told Simon about the cell.

## Work and money
A hank is worth two pfennigs; she makes about a schilling a week. Wool comes on credit from the weaver. She owes the neighbour a sack of barley, about two öre. A bad month is a sick child and an idle wheel.

## Faction and belief
A secret sympathiser of the Black Cloaks; her reason is a grudge against debt and the Vogt's fines. A small favour she gives; a large one she refuses; informing she will not. She crosses herself and sets a bowl of milk for the house-spirit.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only.
- **Delivery:** Monotone, even, rarely loud.
- **Sample lines:** "I heard nothing." / "The thread is paid." / "Ma ei ütle."
- **Verbal tic:** Says "So" before every sentence.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who sits with whom at funerals. She would trade it for the debt cancelled. Rumour she believes: that the Black Cloaks have a smith.
