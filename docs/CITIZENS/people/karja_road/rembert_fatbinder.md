## Biography
Born in 1332 outside the Cattle Gate, Rembert was put to the hoop-hammer at nine. He learned Estonian from Mikk's children and the herdsmen's shouting. He knows which staves are clean and which are suspect, and does not say.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To raise a full barrel without help.
- **Fear:** His father's disappointment.
- **Contradiction:** He loves oak and hates the dust.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows the cheap staves come from the beach.
