## Biography
Born in 1314 outside the Cattle Gate, Mikk inherited the herd-horn from his father at twenty and was confirmed by the council the following Easter. He married [Marga Marteni tütar](../../people/karja_road/marga_marteni_tutar.md) in 1336. Last spring he put his name to a fine of three marks against the shoemaker Sander van Deventer, as surety, because Sander begged. Sander has paid nothing. Mikk has begun to notice who notices.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the post and see his sons given a trade.
- **Fear:** The council learning that he owes.
- **Contradiction:** He is trusted with every burgher's cow and cannot be trusted with his own purse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has sold one cow's milk at night to cover a fee.

## Work and money
The city pays him a few öre a quarter plus a pfennig per cow per month from each owner; in April about a mark in all. He rents the shed from [Reimar Fatbinder](../../people/karja_road/reimar_fatbinder.md) for firewood and tools. He owes nothing in his own name but stands surety for three marks. A bad month is a calf lost to wolves.

## Faction and belief
None: a herdsman is neither burgher nor rebel; the council pays him. A small favour is easy; a large one he cannot afford; informing he would do only if the herd was threatened. He leaves the first curds on a stone for the old spirits.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; shouts to cows and speaks quietly to people.
- **Delivery:** Nasal and measured.
- **Sample lines:** "Forty-one head, forty-one back." / "I do not know whose it is." / "Tule, tule."
- **Verbal tic:** Counts under his breath.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows where every pasture has water and where the wolf crosses. He would trade it for a debt paid. Rumour he believes: that the Harju manors are gathering.
