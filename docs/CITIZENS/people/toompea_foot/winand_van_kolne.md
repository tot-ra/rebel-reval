## Biography
Born in 1286 in Cologne to a ring-maker, apprenticed at twelve, and came to Reval in 1314 as a journeyman armourer. The Rat refused him burgher right because he could not pay the fee, and the Danish crown took him on to mend the castle's mail, on its ground and its terms. In 1337 a fire destroyed his stock and the Crown's clerk advanced thirty marks of iron and charcoal against his labour. He has repaid sixteen. He has not left the castle road since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the debt and buy a stall in Lower Town, free of the hill.
- **Fear:** The bailiff and the castle's bell if a rumour reaches Toompea that he is slack.
- **Contradiction:** A proud craftsman who reports on his own customers.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a list, cut into a stick, of every smith who ordered iron rods of spear-length in the last year.

## Work and money
He mends mail at three pfennigs a shirt and sells new rings by the thousand. His debt stands at fourteen marks. He pays the Crown's clerk each quarter-day with a shirt or a bag of rings, not silver. He buys iron wire from Antti Anttanpoika and sells him rings in return, or did.

## Faction and belief
Danish Crown, coerced. His root is the debt; his branch is the habit of obeying. A small favour is a word at the gate; a large one is naming a name, which he has done twice; he would inform without being asked if the clerk threatened his stock. He prays at St Mary's and trusts his hammer more than his priest.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German with the Crown; broken Estonian with Els and the carters.
- **Delivery:** Clipped, precise, no slack.
- **Sample lines:** "Three pfennigs; I do not haggle with my own hand." "Ei, not tonight." "Count the rings twice."
- **Verbal tic:** Rubs his blackened thumb against his forefinger.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which smiths ordered spear-iron and which carts slipped through the Smiths' Gate. He would trade it for a quarter of his debt. He believes, wrongly, that the Danish viceroy is about to leave Reval.
