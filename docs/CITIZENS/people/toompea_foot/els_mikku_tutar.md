## Biography
Born in 1302 on a Harju manor, where her father Mikk was flogged for hiding a calf. She walked to the Crown's land at Toompea in 1320 and was hired to carry stone when the castle wall was mended. She married a mason's helper who drowned in the harbour in 1334. She has lived since in lodgings and lofts. The King's reeve once paid her wages in full when the manor would have stolen them; she remembers.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A bed that is hers and a roof that no lord can sell.
- **Fear:** The manor's bailiff finding her and returning her to the plough.
- **Contradiction:** Hates all lords, yet serves the Crown's.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She counts the stones she carries, and knows which wall-section is hollow.

## Work and money
She earns four to six pfennigs a day when work lasts, and a quiet month leaves her counting örtug. She pays Winand eight pfennigs a week for the loft corner. She owns a yoke, a kettle and a blanket.

## Faction and belief
Danish Crown, sympathiser. Her root is the manor's flogging; her branch is the King's reeve's fair wage. A small favour is carrying a word; a large one she would do for the reeve; she would inform on a lord's man but not a neighbour. She keeps old grove-offerings of bread at a birch beyond the wall and Mass at St Mary's.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; she answers German with nods.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, a whistle through the gap; slow when afraid.
- **Sample lines:** "The King's peace is peace." "Ei oska öelda, I cannot say." "Stones do not lie."
- **Verbal tic:** Presses her tongue to the gap before speaking.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows which walls are hollow and which doors the Crown forgets to bar. She would trade it for a written wage. She believes, wrongly, that the King will free all bound peasants this summer.
