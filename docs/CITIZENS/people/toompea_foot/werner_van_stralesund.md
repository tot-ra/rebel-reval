## Biography
Born in 1314 in Stralsund to a net-mender; he came to Reval at nineteen on a Hanseatic cog and took service with Winand van Kolne when his first master died. He has pushed aketon-needles and closed mail-rings for ten years. A year ago, in a hard January, Meinhard Snelle of the watch gave him a pair of boots without a word. Since then a man has stopped him after Mass, and he has carried small things to the hill.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be a journeyman armourer under his own name.
- **Fear:** Winand finding out what he carries.
- **Contradiction:** Hates being treated as a debtor and cannot refuse.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows the postern behind the castle road is left unbarred on Thursdays, and has told one man.

## Work and money
He gets board, a mark and a half a year, and a share of the odd shirt. He buys nothing. He owns the boots, a leather palm and a borrowed knife.

## Faction and belief
Danish Crown, sympathiser. His root is a debt of boots; his branch is wanting to be someone's man. A small favour is carrying a word; a large one he hesitates over; he would inform only if his master's debt were cleared. He prays at St Mary's by rote.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows when the postern is open and who passes. He would trade it for a journeyman's paper. He falsely believes the Black Cloaks hold a keg of gunpowder.
