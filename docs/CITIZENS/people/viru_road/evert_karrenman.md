## Biography
Born in 1332, he is the first in the suburb to know exactly why other boys will not hold his hand. At seven a neighbour's child called his father a hangman and Evert broke the child's tooth. His mother beat him and then wept. He prefers dogs and the lime-kiln wall, where he watches the burners. He likes to hear his father's boots in the evening.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be asked to play, once.
- **Fear:** Being made executioner like his father.
- **Contradiction:** Gruff and fierce, yet he cries when a dog dies.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has buried a dead sparrow under the gallows' shadow with a prayer.
