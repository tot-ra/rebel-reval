## At a glance
- Runs a small forge with his son Eskil, making nails, hinges, hoes and the odd spear-head.
- Left-handed, with unusually long fingers, and deaf in the left ear.
- Flushes red when angry or embarrassed, and cannot hide either.
- Holds a long list of petty grudges and recites it in a husky voice when the fire is low.

## Biography
Born in 1286 in Turku to a farrier, Antti was apprenticed at twelve to a smith in Porvoo and came to Reval in 1314 when the wars drew smiths to the Danish levy. He married Katrin, an Estonian spinner, in 1322, a union neither family cheered. The hearing in his left ear went in 1331 when a forge-hammer shattered on the anvil. The forge outside the Viru gate is cheap but exposed. He hammers from dawn and lets his son take the heavier work now that his shoulder aches.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To hand a working forge, free of debt, to Eskil.
- **Fear:** A fire in the shed, or a second hammer breaking.
- **Contradiction:** Proud of his skill, yet feels permanently cheated by his suppliers.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He does not hear well enough to follow a bargain on his left side, and hides it by nodding.

## Work and money
He buys bar iron and steel from Winand van Kolne, charcoal by the load, and timber from Pärtel Toomase poeg. He sells nails by the hundred, hinges by the pair, hoes and spear-heads by order. A good month clears two marks; a bad one is half a mark and a stockout. He rents a shed from Peter Karrenman for four schilling a quarter. He owes Winand a mark and a half.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A smith with a debt and a family needs every customer, and orders come from all sides. A small favour he gives, a large one he refuses, informing he would never do, as it would cost him his orders. He makes the sign of the cross at the quench and hangs an iron nail by the door against ill luck.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish at home and in the forge; broken Low German with customers.
- **Delivery:** Husky and loud, because of the ear, with a habit of turning his right ear toward the speaker.
- **Sample lines:** "Say it again, to the right side." / "Iron remembers every blow." / *Rauta ei valehtele.* (Iron does not lie.)
- **Verbal tic:** Snorts before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who is ordering weapons and who is not paying. He would trade it for credit on iron. He believes the rumour that the Danes will take every smith's output for the garrison.
