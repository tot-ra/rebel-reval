## Biography
Born in 1325 in Reval to Antti and Katrin, Eskil grew up under the forge roof. He swung a hammer at twelve and had his own anvil at sixteen. The winter of 1341 was long: the forge fire went out for nine days in January and the family ate boiled peas and the last of the herring. He helped carry a neighbour's body to the churchyard and learned how short a life could be. His father says little praise; Eskil takes it as a kind of respect. He now runs the forge's fire and most of its heavy work.

## Motivation
- **Want:** An anvil of his own and the right to put his mark on his work.
- **Fear:** Being called to the Danish levy or ending up like his father, deaf and bitter.
- **Contradiction:** A patient trader, but he kicks the anvil when a nail splits.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has made a small knife for his sister in secret, and hidden it under the loom.

## Work and money
He gets his board and one schilling a month from his father. He trades work: hinges for planks, nails for beams. His savings are three schilling in a boot. A bad month is a broken hammer.

## Faction and belief
No faction. He is too young and too tired to join anything, and his father would beat him for it. A small favour he does; a large one he weighs against the forge; informing he thinks beneath him. He touches the anvil before work and goes to mass.
