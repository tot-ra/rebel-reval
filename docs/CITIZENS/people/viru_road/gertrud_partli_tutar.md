## Biography
Born in the summer of 1325, the first daughter of Pärtel and Elsa. At six she was holding flax, at ten she had her own wheel, bought with a gift from her mother's merchant housekeeper. By twelve she was supplying thread to the neighbours. The stoop came early. Her father's trade makes her understand price; her mother's makes her understand silence. She keeps her little earnings in a knotted rag in her shift.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Enough thread silver to pay for a wedding chest of her own choosing, not her father's.
- **Fear:** Marriage to an older man chosen for his timber.
- **Contradiction:** Careful and honest in selling, yet jealous of her sister's quiet attention.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has lent three schilling to a sailor's wife without telling her father.

## Work and money
Pays for flax at two pfennig a handful; sells a skein for three. A good month nets twelve schilling, a bad one four. She owns a wheel, two spindles and a purse of ten schilling. A broken wheel is her worst fear.

## Faction and belief
No faction. She believes politics is for those who can afford to lose silver. A small favour she grants if it pays; a large one she refuses; informing she would do only if forced. She keeps a rowan twig over the bed and goes to mass at St Olaf.

## Knowledge and rumours
She hears gossip from the sailors' wives. She would trade it for a few schilling. She believes the rumour that the Rat will close the Viru gate at dusk.
