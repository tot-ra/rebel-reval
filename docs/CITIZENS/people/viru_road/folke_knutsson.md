## At a glance
- Stocky and brown with weather, with a moustache and chin beard grown to look older.
- Keeps four stalls and a hayloft behind the butcher's yard for carters and travellers.
- Sells spare horsehair, hay-twine and harness scraps on the side.
- Speaks in a flat, tired murmur, like a man twice his age.

## Biography
Born in 1320 at Hanko to a fisherman, Knut, he was sent at fourteen to Reval with a cousin who needed a hand in a stable. He was passed to Gunhild when the cousin drowned. He has slept in the loft for six years and knows every horse that stops on the Viru road. He is ambitious in a small way: he wants his own stalls someday. His knuckles split every winter, and he says little about it.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stable of his own, with his name chalked on the door.
- **Fear:** A horse going lame under his care and a traveller demanding payment.
- **Contradiction:** Gentle with animals, curt with people.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has saved eleven schilling in a rag in the hay and told no one.

## Work and money
He charges two pfennig a night per horse and a pfennig for a feed. A good month clears eight schilling, of which Gunhild takes half. A lame horse is a bad month. He buys hay from gate-farms by the cartload.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A servant who needs customers dare not take sides. A small favour he grants, a large one he refuses, informing he avoids. He crosses himself at the stable door and hangs a horseshoe above it.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish with the household; Low German for travellers.
- **Delivery:** A flat, tired murmur, almost without rise or fall.
- **Sample lines:** "Two pfennig the night, feed extra." / "He is lame; I told you." / *Det ordnar sig.* (It will sort itself out.)
- **Verbal tic:** Says "ja, ja" under his breath.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which travellers come and go and which horses carry heavy loads. He would trade it for a place of his own. He believes the rumour that the Rat will tax every horse that enters by the Viru gate.
