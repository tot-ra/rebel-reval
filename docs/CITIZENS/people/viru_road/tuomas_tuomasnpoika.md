## Biography
Born 1293 on a Finnish coast farm near Turku, he came to Reval at nineteen on a trading boat and learned roofing from an Estonian master. He thatched farmhouses, then tiled the new stone houses when Lower Town began to build. In 1328 he married Anna Juhontytär, a midwife, and has since had two daughters living and one buried.

His house on Viru road is the smallest in the row, 47 square metres, and has the best roof.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To tile a church one day, so that his name is on the rafter.
- **Fear:** A fall; a wet spring ruining his thatch orders.
- **Contradiction:** A cautious man on a dangerous ridge; a quiet man in a quarrelsome partnership.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once pocketed ten tiles from a job and still feels the shame.

## Work and money
He earns about 14 marks in a good year at 3 örtug a day. Tiles come from the Lower Town kiln, thatch from Estonian reed-cutters. He owes 2 marks to the kiln. A bad month is a wet one.

## Faction and belief
None; rain does not care who is Danish. He gives a small favour by lending a ladder. He is baptised and attends St Olaf's, but leaves a few thatch straws crossed at the ridge for his grandfather's sake.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every roof's weakness from above, and so who is rich, who poor, and who hides what in the attic. He would trade it for a good commission. He believes, falsely, that a roof laid in the new moon will leak.
