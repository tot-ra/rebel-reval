## At a glance
- A tall, dark Russian sailor who works the Novgorod-bound cogs and lodges between voyages.
- Left-handed, stooped, heavy-lidded, he speaks in short precise phrases.
- Gives and takes news at the harbour and on Vene street.
- Belongs to a harbour fraternity that he dislikes in part and honours in part.

## Biography
Born in 1307 near Pskov, a ferryman's son, he joined a Novgorod ship at sixteen and has sailed the Gulf for twenty years. He came to Reval first in 1330 and lodged with Sven, Gunhild's husband, because the Swedes asked no questions. After a fever in 1338 he stayed, paying a few schilling a month. In 1341 a Novgorod trader saved him from a debt, and he began to carry word. He keeps no wife and no home on the water.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy a share in a small boat and run goods on his own.
- **Fear:** A watch search of his loft, or a merchant calling in a debt.
- **Contradiction:** A man who values precision, yet lives by half-said things.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He carries lists of Hanseatic cargoes, memorised, to his circle and says them only in Russian.

## Work and money
He earns a few schilling a day when a ship is loading and almost nothing in winter. He pays Gunhild three schilling a month and has about two marks in a belt-pouch. He owes a Novgorod merchant half a mark. A bad month is a late ship.

## Faction and belief
Yakov is an active agent of the Pskov / Novgorod circle. His root is that Hanseatic rules strangled the Novgorod trade; his branch is debt and loyalty to Agafya. A small favour he gives easily, a large one he weighs for a price, and informing on his circle he would never do. He crosses himself in the Orthodox way and keeps a small tin icon of St Nicholas in his chest.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Russian with the circle, Middle Low German with the harbour, a few Swedish words at the table.
- **Delivery:** Clipped and precise, never louder than needed.
- **Sample lines:** "Three ropes, two knots. Done." / "A debt is a rope; you must watch where it goes." / *Spasibo, ne nado.* (Thank you, no need.)
- **Verbal tic:** Taps the table twice before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows ship movements and cargoes. He would trade it for a safe harbour and a berth. He believes the rumour that the Order will close the Neva route this summer.
