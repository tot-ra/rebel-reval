## Biography
Born 1337 on Viru road, in the house with the best roof. She has never been inside a church longer than a quarter-hour without escaping. At three she climbed the thatch to the ridge and was brought down by her father with a rope.

The ear was nicked last summer; she says the ram did it, her father says a nail. The ram has been her enemy ever since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To win against the ram.
- **Fear:** The dark corner behind the stove.
- **Contradiction:** Fearless on a ladder, afraid of her own shadow at night.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a turnip for the ram's defeat.
