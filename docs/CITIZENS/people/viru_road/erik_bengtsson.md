## Biography
Born in 1263 at Porvoo, apprenticed to a cooper at twelve, he came to Reval in 1290 when casks were wanted for herring. He worked in the Sauna street workshop for forty years and carried barrels for the Danish levy in 1313, when the shortage of staves was as dangerous as the shortage of bread. His wife and three children died years ago. His great-nephew Sven took him in, and when Sven died, Gunhild kept him. He no longer carries staves, but he sells what he carves, because a man should pay his keep.

## Motivation

## Work and money
He sells a bung for a pfennig and a spoon for two. A month nets about five schilling. He buys beech and ash from Pärtel Toomase poeg and pays on the day. The funeral coins are in the cellar, and he owes nobody.

## Faction and belief
No faction. At eighty he has seen three rulers and says all of them cut the same cask. A small favour he gives, a large one he leaves to the young; informing he does not do. He believes in St Olaf and makes a mark on every barrel.

## Knowledge and rumours
He remembers the wars and the old streets, and which families came from where. He would trade it for a good dinner. He believes the rumour that the old Danish king will return with a fleet.
