## Biography
Born in 1315 at Åbo, the son of a boatman, he came to Reval at sixteen to carry for a merchant who paid his father's burial costs. That debt shaped him. He trained under a porter who also trained Lembit Siimu poeg. He lodges with the Anttanpoika family because Katrin cooks. A merchant's bailiff asked him last year to keep his ears open on the quay, and he did.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A carrying licence of his own and a share in a warehouse.
- **Fear:** A fall on the gangplank and losing his work.
- **Contradiction:** Jokes constantly, but never says a word he has not planned.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He is the head of a small cell that reports on smuggled goods and loose talk to a merchant's agent.

## Work and money
He earns four pfennig per bale and about two marks a month. He pays Katrin three schilling for board. He holds a purse of one mark. A bad month is a closed harbour.

## Faction and belief
He is an active member of the Hanseatic circle. His root is gratitude to the merchant who paid his father's burial; his branch is ambition. A small favour he does; a large one he does for the right price; informing is his job. He crosses himself on the quay and keeps a lead pilgrim badge on his cap.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Swedish at home; Low German on the quay.
- **Delivery:** Clear and carrying, with a laugh at the end.
- **Sample lines:** "Mind the rope, friend." / "Everything is heavy until you lift it." / *Ta det lugnt.* (Take it easy.)
- **Verbal tic:** Laughs before a bargain.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who unloads what and who pays under the table. He would trade it for a licence. He believes the rumour that the Rat will ban foreign porters.
