## Biography
Born in 1312 in Reval, daughter of a poor cooper, Gyla was trained as a retailer by a widow who also taught Eeva. At twenty-three her family arranged her marriage to Peter because he had a house and a wage and nobody else wanted either. She learned that the neighbours would buy from her but not eat with her. In 1342 a merchant's agent offered her goods on credit through a maid in his household. She took them and has been in his debt since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A real stall in the market and sons who can enter any guild.
- **Fear:** The agent calling in her credit, or a neighbour refusing to open the door.
- **Contradiction:** Loud and cheerful in public, but she cries in the pantry.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She is in the circle that reports gossip to the merchants, and she hates it.

## Work and money
She buys salt, thread and ribbon from the agent on credit and sells for a small margin. A good week clears four schilling; a bad one loses one. She owes the agent a mark and a half. She owns the tray, a scale and a few spare weights.

## Faction and belief
A dependent of the Hanseatic circle: debt binds her, not belief. A small favour she grants; a large one she dreads; informing she does, and loathes. She goes to St Olaf and lights a candle for her husband.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German, with a few Estonian words from the street.
- **Delivery:** Booming, warm, unembarrassed.
- **Sample lines:** "Needles, ribbons, salt! Fine goods!" / "I am a respectable woman, whatever they say." / *Ach, Gott.* (Ah, God.)
- **Verbal tic:** Laughs loudly when nervous.

## Knowledge and rumours
She hears which houses are in debt and which servants are cheating. She would trade it for a cancelled debt. She believes the rumour that the Rat will stop executioners' families from selling in town.
