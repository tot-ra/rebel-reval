## At a glance
- Washes for merchants' wives and the Lower Town's better kitchens, and by the harbour in summer.
- Her voice is hoarse from years of wind on the quay.
- Left-handed, like her husband, and the whole household jokes that the knives are all the wrong way.
- Secretly sympathetic to the Black Cloaks; she has told no one at home.

## Biography
Born in 1286 by the harbour, her father a boatman, Elsa learned to wash in the shallows. Married Pärtel in 1306 and lost a son in 1326. She started washing for the Lower Town after a merchant's housekeeper took pity. In the rainy autumn of 1340 she attended the funeral of a harbour man beaten to death by the watch, and heard the first whisper of the Black Cloaks. She has since passed messages in washing baskets. Now she works from dawn to dusk and no one suspects her.

## Motivation
- **Want:** Her daughters married without the weight of a Danish levy or a German master's whim.
- **Fear:** That Pärtel or Gertrud will find the linen-basket's false bottom.
- **Contradiction:** A cautious wife who keeps the peace at home, while secretly in a cell.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She is part of a cell of two with a third unnamed, and she recruited a young clerk a year ago.

## Work and money
She charges two pfennig a sheet and eight for a gown, about one and a half marks a month. She owes a mark to a lye-seller and is owed a few schilling by a wealthy housekeeper. A bad month is a wet spring when sheets will not dry.

## Faction and belief
Elsa is a secret sympathiser: her son's death and a levy that took her brother's boat are her root grievances. She acts from caution and loyalty, not from anger. A small favour she does at once; a large one she weighs; informing on her cell she would never do. She keeps a bundle of herbs by the door and goes to mass at St Olaf.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only, hushed in the lane.
- **Delivery:** Hoarse from the harbour wind, a murmur that carries only a few paces.
- **Sample lines:** "Wet sheets, wet tongues." / "I wash what I am given and I see what I see." / *Vesi kannab kõike minema.* (Water carries everything away.)
- **Verbal tic:** Clears her throat before every name.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who changes their linen and when, and who is moving silver. She would trade it for her cell's safety. She believes the rumour that the Order's knights will arrive at the next full moon.
