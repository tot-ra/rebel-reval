## Biography
Born in 1335, Marquard has never been called a hangman's son to his face, because he has hardly spoken to anyone but his brother, the dogs and his mother. He was sick through the winter of 1342 with a cough that left his voice husky. He believes the gallows is a big tree. His mother pretends this is true.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To play with Evert and be allowed to hold a dog.
- **Fear:** The dark under the stairs.
- **Contradiction:** Brave with dogs, terrified of geese.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has hidden a pebble in his father's boot for luck.
