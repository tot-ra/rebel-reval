## Biography
Born in 1330, the youngest child to survive in the Anttanpoika house, Ilona grew up beside the forge. At six she held the bellows with both hands; at ten she could tell the heat of iron by its colour. She has never been to the Lower Town market alone. She speaks only Finnish at home and has begun to learn Estonian from her mother. Her brother calls her Spark.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be allowed to strike a nail herself.
- **Fear:** The forge going cold and her father angry.
- **Contradiction:** Quiet and shy, but fierce in defending her nails.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She knows where Eskil's secret knife is, and has promised not to say.
