## Biography
Born in 1288 near Tabasalu, daughter of a miller, Priidik, she came to Reval in 1305 to work for a clothier and learned to spin a thread so even that he paid her extra. She married Antti in 1322 after the family quarrel about a Finn; her brother stopped speaking to her. The nose was broken when a drunk carter shoved her into a cart-wheel in 1330. She has two children left, Eskil and Ilona, and two buried. Now she works the wheel by the forge fire and keeps the family fed on her thread.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see Ilona married to a man who does not hit.
- **Fear:** Eskil being taken for a Danish levy.
- **Contradiction:** Teases her husband in public, yet wakes at night to check his breathing.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She hides a dowry of seven schilling in a loom weight, and Antti thinks it is a spare.

## Work and money
She earns about nine schilling a month, spun to the pfund. She buys flax and wool from the clothier on credit. She owns a wheel, a distaff and a loom weight. A bad month is a fever in the house.

## Faction and belief
No faction. A spinner with a smith husband and two children cannot afford a quarrel. A small favour she grants, a large one she refuses, informing she cannot imagine. She keeps a bit of wool on the hearth for the house spirit and prays to St Olaf.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian at home; a few Finnish words with her husband.
- **Delivery:** Husky and warm, with a laugh that arrives before the joke.
- **Sample lines:** "Mind the nose; it knows weather." / "A thread is a promise." / *Küll sa jõuad.* (You will manage.)
- **Verbal tic:** Laughs at the end of her own warnings.

## Knowledge and rumours
She hears which clothiers cut wages and which wives are with child. She would trade it for a better price on wool. She believes the rumour that Finnish smiths will be sent out of town.
