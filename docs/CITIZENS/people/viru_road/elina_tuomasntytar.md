## Biography
Born 1329 on Viru road, the first child to live past the first winter. She has watched births since nine, held the lamp since eleven and has twice been sent out when a labour went bad. She knows the road's women, and which houses lie.

The tremor began last winter after a long night at a stillbirth. Her mother says it will pass; Elina is not sure.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To work at the harbour, among ships, and not among women's pain.
- **Fear:** The tremor; being her mother.
- **Contradiction:** Competent at what she dislikes.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She counts the herring boats from the quay each dawn, and wants one to take her away.
