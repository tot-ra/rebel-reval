## Biography
Born 1326, the younger daughter. When her father's credit dried in 1339 the one maid was let go, and Metteke took her place at twelve. She fetches water, scrubs, carries messages and goes to market, which means she knows the whole street.

A year ago, Ilsabe van Gripeswold, a maid on Pikk, gave her a green ribbon and a loaf at a hard moment, and later asked her to carry a small word. Metteke has been carrying since, and feels owned.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be paid for what she does, by someone.
- **Fear:** Her father learning; the ribbon being a hook.
- **Contradiction:** A knight's daughter doing a servant's work, and a servant's work for the crown.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has begun to read the circle's messages for what they say, and has not told Ilsabe.

## Faction and belief
Her activity for the crown is pressure and pride: she carries because she was asked kindly and cannot say no. A small favour is a word; a large one she would refuse; informing, she is already doing. She prays to St Catherine for cleverness.

## Knowledge and rumours
