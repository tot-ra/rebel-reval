## Biography
Born 1283 in a Westphalian town, daughter of a minor knight's steward. She came to Reval in 1305 as a lady's maid to a Lübeck wife, learned spinning from her, and married Lambert Sasse in 1321 after two years of unspoken understanding. She bore Gesche at forty and Metteke at forty-three, a thing the street called a miracle and her husband a debt.

She keeps the family accounts in her head: 30 marks to Lübeck, 6 to a baker, 3 to a saddler. She spins at dawn, and she has begun to feel her age.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To see both girls married and the lien paid.
- **Fear:** Dying before the debt does; her daughters drawn into something dangerous.
- **Contradiction:** A knight's wife who has not bought new cloth in six years.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a purse of 4 marks in the wall for the girls' dowries and has told only God.

## Work and money
She earns about 6 marks a year from thread; flax from a Harju woman. She owes the baker 6 marks and the saddler 3. Bad months bring thin pottage.

## Faction and belief
She has none; her husband's debts and her daughters' fates are politics enough. She says the Rosary and, quietly, a charm to keep the girls from fever.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows how every household on the street is in debt. She would trade it for her daughters' safety. She believes, falsely, that her husband's lien will end at Easter.
