## Biography
Born 1322 on Toom-Rüütli, the first of two late daughters. She learned to spin at six and to count at ten. The tremor began at sixteen after a long fever and comes when she is tired. She has had no offer of marriage; her father's debts are known up and down the hill.

She goes to the market for her mother on Saturdays, where Niels Galen the armourer greets her and tells her the iron prices, which she remembers as other girls remember songs.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A husband with a trade and a roof that is not mortgaged.
- **Fear:** Becoming her mother, counting debts in a quiet room.
- **Contradiction:** Calm on the surface, a bundle of nerves in the hand.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has learnt to read numbers on tally sticks and has told no one.
