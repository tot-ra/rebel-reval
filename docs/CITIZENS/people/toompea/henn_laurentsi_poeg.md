## Biography
Born in 1315 in a Harju village near Jüri, the son of Laurents, a serf of a Danish vassal. His father died when he was twelve and Henn walked to Reval at fourteen and has hauled sacks ever since.

Two things made him. In 1337 he lifted a cask of salt herring from a cart that had slipped and tore something inside him; the surgeon gave him a truss and a warning. And in 1340 he was offered work as a guard by a man who said the pay was good; he declined, because he did not want a name. He lives in the Rahukohtu house's loft, and carries for the van Mindens in exchange for a bed.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save four marks and buy a boat share with his cousin at Kalarand.
- **Fear:** A bad lift and no truss; being bound to a manor again.
- **Contradiction:** A giant who fears a sack; a man who has no faction and no enemies yet carries every faction's goods.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows the old German woman hides oat-bags under the stairs and has told no one.

## Work and money
He earns about 8 pfennigs a day when there is work and carries for the van Mindens against his bed. He has a mark and a half saved under a loose board. A bad month is a closed gate and no carts.

## Faction and belief
None: he has nothing to gain from a side and much to lose. For a small favour he would carry; for a large one, say nothing; to inform, he would refuse. He crosses himself and sets a heel of bread on the loft beam for the house luck.
