## Biography
Born in 1301 in Odense, the son of a notary, Torben was trained at the cathedral school and came to Reval in 1328 as clerk to a Danish bailiff. He has lived on Toompea since, in a chamber behind the chancery, and is trusted by none.

Two things made him. In 1334 a senior clerk was dismissed for a false column in the grain account, and Torben, who had noticed it, said nothing until after, and was promoted. In 1339 an unnamed hand paid him a mark for a copy of the viceroy's draft letter; he has been paid for such copies ever since. He now writes the toll rolls and keeps a second, secret roll in his head.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A canonry in Roskilde or a wife with property.
- **Fear:** A fire in the chancery; a day when a letter he copied is read in the wrong place.
- **Contradiction:** He despises gossip yet lives by it; he calls it "record".
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He passes what he copies to a young spinner who comes up the Pikk jalg stair with thread; he does not know whom it reaches after her.

- **Spring 1343 disruption:** More grain levy letters and more silence; he writes copies faster than he can read them. After St George's Night he fears the chancery will burn.

## Work and money
He is paid six marks a year plus board and clothing and a small fee per charter; the secret copies bring perhaps one mark a quarter. He buys parchment and oak-gall ink from clerks in the Lower Town. He suspects a rival clerk of undercutting copy fees with smuggled parchment; it costs him an örtug or two on each charter. A bad month is no copying.

## Faction and belief
The Crown pays him; the informing is private. For a small favour he would delay a copy; for a large one, lose a roll; to inform, he does. He is devout, reciting psalms under his breath at the desk, and distrusts folk charms.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Danish at work; formal Latin on charters; plain Low German with townsfolk.
- **Delivery:** Booming, deliberate, loud even when whispering.
- **Sample lines:** "Write it again, and slower." / "Silence is the best ink." / "Dominus vobiscum, and mind the wet."
- **Verbal tic:** "As written."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the grain levy figures and which Harju manors cannot pay. He would trade it for a letter to Roskilde. He believes falsely that the Hanse is secretly paying the Order.
