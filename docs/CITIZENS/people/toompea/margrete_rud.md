## Biography
Born 1335 on Rutu in her father's house. She has learnt Danish from her mother and Low German from the lane. Her grandfather Torben taught her to count to a hundred on a tally stick, and her father taught her how to read the numbers on a hide-weight.

She holds the knot at the end of her father's thread and carries the wax. Last autumn the clerk's goose bit her, and it has been a feud since.

## Motivation

- **Contradiction:** Quiet and exact indoors, loud outside.
