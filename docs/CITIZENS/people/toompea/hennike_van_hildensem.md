## Biography
Born in 1332 on Kohtu, the eldest, he was sworn apprentice to his father at eight so the household could claim the master's privilege. He learns beds, frames and watering by the bucket. He can read numerals and tally his father's seed-money. He is too tired in the evenings to play and wonders aloud why Tideman, a year younger, is not also apprenticed.

## Motivation
