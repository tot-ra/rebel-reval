- Pale, sallow, thin-whiskered, left-handed, so the hawk sits on his right fist; the lane thinks it unlucky and he shrugs.
- Does the night watch on moulting birds, and likes it best when no one speaks.
- Brought a Lower Town apprentice into the crown's circle a year ago and now regrets the debt he has made.

## Biography
Born 1323 on Rahukohtu, the eldest living child. He was walking behind his father's mews by four and carrying a lure at nine. At fourteen he flew his first sparrowhawk; his father let him keep the first quail. A fever at sixteen cost him a winter and a stone in weight, and he has never put it back.

He joined the circle at eighteen, brought in by an older man's quiet word after mass; a year ago he in turn recruited a boy from Pikk, Eler van Dortmund, with a loaf and a good word at a bad moment. He lives at home, bunks with his brother Winand, and expects to inherit the lamp-oil.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A bird of his own that he could call his; failing that, a place in the viceroy's mews.
- **Fear:** Becoming his father, loud and owed.
- **Contradiction:** Silent at home, a recruiter outside; he hates being owed and yet he made Eler a debtor.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** Has twice carried a folded paper sewn into a hood to a Lower Town house, and does not know what was written.

## Work and money
He earns nothing but his keep and a few schillings at Michaelmas; his father pays him in cloth. He makes hoods from goatskin at a penny a day of leather. His one possession is an iron hood-needle bought with three weeks' savings. A bad month is a missing bird, which his father will deduct from his food.

## Faction and belief
He belongs to the crown's side because his father's livelihood does and because a boy joins what is offered. He would do a small favour without thought; a large one, only if his father were safe; he would not inform on Eler. He prays to St Hubert and says the hawk-charm his grandmother taught him.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Low German; he whispers in the mews and mumbles at the circle.
- **Delivery:** Husky, low and breathy, as if he had just run.
- **Sample lines:** "She's moulting, she'll not fly till June." / "I did him a kindness, that's all it was." / "Father, the lamp is low."
- **Verbal tic:** Says "if you like" at the end of nearly everything.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the hawks' hours and so the hill's: which gate opens when, and who comes back late. He would trade it for a hawk of his own. He believes, falsely, that a left-handed falconer can never be hired by the crown.
