## Biography
Born in 1312 in Ribe, the son of a chandler, Kristoffer came to Reval in 1335 with a knight's household as a candle-boy and was kept when the knight died. In 1339 he was promoted to chamberlain's assistant, in 1341 to the keys.

Two things made him. The first was the night in 1336 when he held a burning candle too close to a bed-curtain and was beaten by the old chamberlain until he learned fire. The second was a debt: in 1342 his mother was sick and a clerk lent him four marks without interest, asking only that he keep his ears open. He has done so, and resents the clerk's gentle tone.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy a small chandler's shop in Ribe and retire from keys.
- **Fear:** Fire, theft, and the day the viceroy asks for a key he cannot find.
- **Contradiction:** Obsessively honest with the viceroy's goods, yet he reports the viceroy's visitors to someone else.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once let a stranger into the viceroy's closet for a quarter hour; nothing was taken, and he has never slept well since.

## Work and money
Paid four marks a year plus board and a tunic; a share of candle-ends sold to the Lower Town bring a few pfennigs. He keeps 12 schillings in a tin. He owes a clerk four marks to be repaid in kind. A bad month is a stolen candle blamed on him.

## Faction and belief
A small, anxious loyalty to the Crown and a smaller, steadier loyalty to the debt. For a small favour he would unlock a door; for a large one, give a description of a visitor; to inform, he does. He lights a candle at St Mary for his mother.
