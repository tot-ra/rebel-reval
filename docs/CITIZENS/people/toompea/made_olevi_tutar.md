## Biography
Born in 1303 in a Harju village, she came to Toompea at ten as a scullion for the castle kitchen and learned fire, salt and temper. She worked under the viceroy's cook until a quarrel with a steward over spoiled venison moved her to the chapter in 1335. She has never married; her brother's family farmed near Rebala until the manor bound them. She lives in a cot by the oven and brings dripping to the poor on feast days.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stone hearth of her own in a house with a window.
- **Fear:** Fire in the kitchen, and the reeve's eyes.
- **Contradiction:** She feeds Church men and passes their words to the crown.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She cannot afford to buy her brother's family free and sells kitchen gossip to try.

## Work and money
Paid six marks a year and board plus kitchen scraps. She sells dripping and bones for about a schilling a week. A bad month is when flour runs out in the week after Lent.

## Faction and belief
Loyal to the crown because the viceroy's kitchen pays in meat and its reeve once stopped a steward beating her. A small favour she does at once; a large one for her brother's family; informing is already what she does. She leaves a bone at the hearth for the old house-spirit.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows who eats what and who sits up late. She would trade it for news of her brother. She believes, wrongly, that the viceroy will buy her kin's freedom.
