## Biography
Born in 1290 at a village by the Pirita river, the daughter of a miller named Jaan; the mill was burned in the wars of 1313 and the family dispersed. Gertrud walked to the castle at twenty-three and has scoured its pots for thirty years.

Two things made her. In 1326 a cook was dismissed on her word that he had stolen meat; she was given a loaf and a silver örtug and learned that listening could be rewarded. In 1339 the winter was so hard that the wash-water froze in the buckets; her knuckles split and scarred and she began to mind the pennies she was paid. She now sells small facts to a man who never gives his name.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A stove of her own in the wash-house and a thick shawl.
- **Fear:** Cold, and being sent back to the village that no longer exists.
- **Contradiction:** She whispers so softly that she is never suspected, yet she is the loudest informer in the castle.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She sometimes tells the household things that are not true, to see who repeats them.

- **Spring 1343 disruption:** The kitchen is full of talk of the levy and Harju; she collects it. After St George's Night she expects the wash-yard to be the first place burned.

## Work and money
She earns three marks a year plus board; the informing brings about four schillings a quarter. She buys lye and wool at the Lower Town market and gives her old shoes to a maid. She owes nothing and is owed two small favours. A bad month is a closed wash-yard in frost.

## Faction and belief
She informs for bread and warmth, not love of the Crown. For a small favour she would tell what she heard; for a large one, repeat it to the viceroy's marshal; to inform, she does. She keeps a charm of rowan twigs in her apron against chilblains.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with the servants; Low German with the household.
- **Delivery:** Soft, almost whispering, with long listening pauses.
- **Sample lines:** "I only heard it; I would not say." / "Come closer, the stones listen." / "Külm on, külm."
- **Verbal tic:** "They say."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who quarrels with whom and which servants steal. She would trade it for a warm shawl. She believes falsely that the castle well is poisoned in April.
