## Biography
Born 1322 on Toom-Rüütli, the elder son. He learned the sword from his father at seven and the horse at ten, and fell from one at twelve, which gave him his uneven shoulders. In 1338 he and Hartwig fought in the yard, and Hartwig's practice blade left the scar on Marquard's jaw, while his own blade left one on Hartwig. The brothers and their father now share the same mark.

At twenty he was taken into the castle garrison on half pay and was approached last Michaelmas by a crown steward who knew of his father's debts and of the Order cousins his father writes to. He has said yes to small things and not yet to large ones.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A fief of his own and a wife who will not be ashamed of the roof.

- **Contradiction:** Loyal to his father and informing, in small ways, on him.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has told the steward of his father's cellar guest-list once, and lied about the number.

## Faction and belief
He serves the crown because the crown holds his debts. A small favour: a report. A large one: he would break. Informing he already does, badly. He keeps St George and prays for a sign.

## Voice
