## Biography
Born in 1300 in a village near Kose, the son of Albert, a ploughman of a Danish vassal. He ploughed until thirty-five when his lord sold the strip and he walked to Reval to look for work; a castle horse-master took him on in 1335 because he did not startle the stallion.

Two things made him. In 1336 a stallion kicked a groom's boy and Tõnis carried the child to the surgeon; he has been trusted with horses since. And in 1339 he declined, flatly, to carry a message for a man from the town, saying he knew horses and nothing else; the man never asked again. He now sleeps above the stalls and rises when the horses do.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be head ostler when the old man retires.
- **Fear:** A lame horse on his watch; a viceroy's mount sick.
- **Contradiction:** He says he does not care for politics, yet he knows every rider's route and could draw it in the straw.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He once let a sick mare run free on the Harju road rather than see her killed, and told the marshal she bolted.

## Work and money
He earns three marks a year plus board and a pair of shoes; he sells horse-hair and manure to Lower Town gardeners for a few pfennigs. He has a mark in a boot. A bad month is a loose horse or a missing saddle.

- **Network:** [Thomas Overdyk](../../people/lower_town/thomas_overdyk.md), stable hand on Lühike jalg: Thomas trained under the master who later trained Tõnis, and Tõnis owes him an old, unspoken courtesy. Tõnis has never found the moment to say so, and speaks of Thomas to the farrier instead.

## Faction and belief
None: he is a horseman, and horses are not ruled by crowns. For a small favour he would lend a halter; for a large one, ride at night; to inform, he would say nothing. He leaves a handful of oats for the spirit of the stable at Michaelmas.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian with horses; Low German in as few words as possible.
- **Delivery:** Slow, long pauses, sometimes silent for a minute.
- **Sample lines:** "He is a good horse." / "I do not know. I will look." / "Rahu, rahu."
- **Verbal tic:** A long "Well..." before every answer.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows every horse's habits and who rides where. He would trade it for good oats. He believes falsely that a black horse in the stable foretells a death.
