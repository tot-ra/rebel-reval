## At a glance
- A pale, even-complexioned boy with a husky voice, blue eyes and dark blond hair cut by his mother with the kitchen shears.
- Carries the lure and the dead pigeon bag; knows which bird bites.
- Is not in the circle, and thinks his older brother's silences are about a girl.
- Wants to be a stonemason like Borchard down the lane, which he tells nobody.

## Biography
Born 1329 in the house on Rahukohtu, old enough in 1336 to remember his sister Gertrud's fever. He was set to the mews at nine, as a falconer's second son is: cleaning perches, carrying pigeons, watching the moult. 

Last summer Borchard Steenmetter let him lay one stone in a gable on Rahukohtu.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To lay another stone; to be allowed to fly the goshawk once on his own.
- **Fear:** His father's voice, and the thought of being a falconer all his life.
- **Contradiction:** Obedient in the mews, daydreaming everywhere else.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He keeps a small flat stone from Borchard's gable under his straw.

## Work and money
He is paid nothing but keep; his father calls it training. He earns a penny from a stonemason for carrying water. Bad months mean smaller porridge and his father's lamp-oil rationed.
