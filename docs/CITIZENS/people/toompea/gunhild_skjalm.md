## Biography
Born in 1327 in the Pikk jalg house, the youngest of the surviving children, Gunhild learned to spin at six on her mother's second-best spindle. She has spun for a living since eleven.

Two things made her. In 1339 a household that bought her thread paid her in a sealed folded cloth to carry to another house, and she carried it without a question; no one noticed, and she did not stop. And in the winter of 1341 she took her brother's place on the stair with a message when he was ill, and learned that a girl with a bag of thread is invisible. She now carries the circle's cloths as part of her round.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A silver comb and to go to Roskilde once.
- **Fear:** Being married off to a creditor's nephew.
- **Contradiction:** She is the lowest in rank in the household and the highest in the circle; she hides both by looking bored.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has opened one of the cloths and found only a bit of knotted thread, and has never told anyone what the knots said.

## Work and money
She spins about four skeins a day and sells them for a pfennig each; a household pays her extra for delivery. She buys wool from a Harju carter and gives her mother half. She has a silver örtug in a stocking. A bad month is a lame sheep.

## Faction and belief
A sympathiser because her family is the Crown's; she likes the secrecy more than the cause. For a small favour she would carry a thread; for a large one, hold a door; to inform, she would, with delight. She prays to St Catherine of the spindle.
