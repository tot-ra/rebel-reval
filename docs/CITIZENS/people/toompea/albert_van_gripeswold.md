## Biography
Born in 1336 on Kiriku põik, the first son. He asked for a nail at four and has not stopped; he picks them up from the street near the smithies and the castle works. His grandfather Ricbod gave him a lead weight to hold. He wants to see the viceroy's horses and has never been allowed near.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To own a hundred nails.
- **Fear:** Being left alone in the dark.
- **Contradiction:** He is bold with strangers and shy of his father.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He hid a nail from a cart in the loft.
