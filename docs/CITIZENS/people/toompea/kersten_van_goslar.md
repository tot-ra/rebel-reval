## Biography
Born 1333 in the house on Toom-Kooli, the son of Gyse Hovesche's first husband, Hinrik van Goslar, a clerk who died of a winter fever in 1339. Kersten remembers his father's pen scratching at night. He was sent to the cathedral school at seven, where he learned his letters, and now conjugates and copies.

The master ties his right hand to the bench to force it, and he has learnt to cheat with the left under the table. He is tired by Vespers.

## Motivation
