## Biography
Born in 1298 in Reval to a Grote saddler, she learned flax and wool and married Hildebrand in 1328. She counts in tally-marks and numerals and keeps the household's coin in a locked chest. She bore five children and buried none; Lucia is the youngest. She has built a small trade in yarn by paying poorer women a half-schilling a bundle.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A weaver's loom for her sons.
- **Fear:** Her husband's quietness hiding debts.
- **Contradiction:** She hates waste and spends freely on her boys' education.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden four schillings from her husband for Hennike.

## Work and money
She earns about twelve schillings a week from yarn. She pays out four. The family owes the miller six schillings. A bad month is when a spinner quits.

## Faction and belief
None; her loyalty is to the household. A small favour she does with yarn; a large one she asks her husband; informing she refuses. She keeps a St Anne medal in her purse.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German only.
- **Delivery:** Nasal, brisk, interrupts with arithmetic.
- **Sample lines:** "Four hanks, a half-schilling." / "Count it twice." / "Dat kost Geld."
- **Verbal tic:** "By the bundle."

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows the price of yarn in three streets. She would trade it for a cheaper loom. She believes, wrongly, that wool prices will fall at Midsummer.
