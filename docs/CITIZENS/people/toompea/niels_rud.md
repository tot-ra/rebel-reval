## Biography
Born 1306 in a Zealand market town, apprenticed at fourteen to a saddler of the king's household, and sent to Reval in 1330 when the crown needed craftsmen on the hill. He took the Rutu workshop by marrying Elisabet Lunge in 1334; his father Torben came over the next year. A stallion broke his shin that winter, and the limp has stayed.

He has two daughters and a son and a father in the house. The winter of 1340 was bad, with a ruined barley crop and prices for leather at double; he got through on the crown's late payment and the goodwill of a lane that shared fire. He lives by the saddle and is trusted.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A second journeyman, so he can sit down at midday.
- **Fear:** The crown's late payment; a lame leg turning to a crippled one.
- **Contradiction:** Danish in a crown household, yet he trusts no crown official; stoic, yet fierce about quality.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He lent a saddle on credit to a man he suspected of being a rebel and has never been repaid.

## Work and money
He earns about 30 marks a year when the crown pays. He buys hides from a Lower Town tanner, thread from a Finn, brass buckles from a smith. The crown owes him 12 marks. A bad month is a late-paid order that leaves him buying hides on credit.

## Faction and belief
He stays out of it: a craftsman who takes a side loses the other side's custom, and he has too many mouths. He says the crown is the crown and a saddle is a saddle. He makes a small sign of the cross over every new hide.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Danish at home; Low German at work.
- **Delivery:** Gravelly, slow, with a short laugh.
- **Sample lines:** "A girth holds or it doesn't." / "Pay me when you can, and I will stitch when I can." / "Ja, ja."
- **Verbal tic:** Taps the bench twice before answering.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every horse in the castle stable and so who rides out at night. He would trade it for an early payment. He believes, falsely, that a mare in foal must never wear a new saddle.
