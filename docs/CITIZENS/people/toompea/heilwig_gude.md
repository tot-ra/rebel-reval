## Biography
Born 1332, after her sister Gertrud's death in 1336 she became the one who sits with her mother's satchel. When the Harju trappers come in autumn, they let her hold a goshawk eyas, and she learned a hundred Estonian words from them: the names of birds, of weather, of salt.

She spends her days between the mews and the lane, a favourite of the stonemason's wife and an annoyance to Winand.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To fly a hawk herself, though girls do not.
- **Fear:** A bird dying; her father's loud voice.
- **Contradiction:** A talker with trappers, silent about her mother's births.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has hidden a crippled sparrowhawk chick in the wash-house.
