## Biography
Born in 1290 in Ribe, a roof-tiler's son, he laid lead on churches in Jutland and came to Reval in 1337 when his workshop failed. His wife died in Ribe in 1335. He lives with his son Ove and the grandchildren. A year ago, when the winter froze the cistern, a crown man-at-arms, Niels Bjelke, found him firewood and a loaf and later a favour to repay.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To repay and be quit of the favour.
- **Fear:** Being a burden, and debt to a stranger.
- **Contradiction:** He cares for the crown that humbled him.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He passes small notes to a watch sergeant after mass.

## Faction and belief
Crown sympathiser by origin and by debt. A small favour he does at once; a large one he refuses to be owed; informing he does, unwillingly. He lights a candle to St Olaf and spits over his left shoulder.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Danish at home, Middle Low German in the lane.
- **Delivery:** Clear and carrying, slow and heavy.
- **Sample lines:** "I owe no man." / "Speak up, I am not deaf." / "Gud hjælpe os."
- **Verbal tic:** "Plain as lead."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which roofs leak on the hill. He would trade a whisper for his debt cancelled. He believes, wrongly, that the viceroy will leave Reval before summer.
