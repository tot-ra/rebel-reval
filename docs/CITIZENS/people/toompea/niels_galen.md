## Biography
Born in 1307 in Odense, an armourer's son, he came to Reval with a garrison in 1333. He joined the crown armoury and married a Reval cooper's daughter who died in 1339. He lets his loft to three lodgers to cover the rent. The crown pays him in silver slowly and in rye faster, and the lodgers pay less. A few mail rings a week leave the armoury by his coat.

## Work and money
The crown pays twelve marks a year. He makes three marks a year from stolen rings. Lodgers pay four schillings a month each, late. A bad month is an inspection.

## Faction and belief
None; the crown pays and that is enough. A small favour he does at once; a large one for coin; informing he refuses. He crosses himself at the forge door.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the garrison's arms by number. He would trade it for rent. He believes, wrongly, that the viceroy will disband the armoury.
