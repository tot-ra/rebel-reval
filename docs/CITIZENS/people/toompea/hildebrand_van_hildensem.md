## Biography
Born in 1297 in Hildesheim, a gardener's son, he came to Reval in 1320 and leased a plot behind Kohtu. He married Margarete Grote in 1328. In 1341 he sold cabbages to an Order merchant's agent from Riga, and the agent's friend, old Evert Rode of the Lower Town, recruited him a year ago with a loan of seed when a frost killed his crop. He sleeps badly and watches the lane from his upper window.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy the strip next to his plot.
- **Fear:** Frost, and a neighbour who knows too much.
- **Contradiction:** He is a quiet neighbour who counts everyone's comings and goings.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has seen two neighbours leave after curfew and has told no one.

## Faction and belief
Order by a loan and by a belief that the Order keeps its word. A small favour he does at once; a large one he demands a price for; informing he does by habit. He buries a St Walpurga charm in the leek bed.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German only.
- **Delivery:** Slow with long pauses; answers a question a breath late.
- **Sample lines:** "The leeks will be late." / "I saw nothing." / "Dat wassen schall."
- **Verbal tic:** "We shall see."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which doors open at night. He would trade a name for his thirty marks. He believes, wrongly, that the Order will send ten banners by May.
