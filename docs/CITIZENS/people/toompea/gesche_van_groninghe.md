## Biography
Born in 1314 in Reval to a Groninghe cartwright, she spun flax from nine and married Ludolf in 1333. She has borne three children and buried one. Her father-in-law Ricbod lives with them and eats a great deal. She spins coarse yarn for the Lower Town weavers and sells the surplus on the hill. She suspects the household's thin purse is more than rents and prices.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be sure of rye through Midsummer.
- **Fear:** Debt that takes the house.
- **Contradiction:** She trusts her husband with her children and not with coins.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** She has counted the missing schillings and kept quiet.

## Work and money
She earns about nine schillings a month from yarn. Flax costs three. She puts away one schilling a month in a sock. A bad month is when a child falls ill.

## Faction and belief
None; a spinner's life leaves no room for sides. A small favour she does with a loaf; a large one she asks the priest about; informing she refuses. She keeps a flax-straw cross at the cradle.

## Knowledge and rumours
She knows what each neighbour pays for flax. She would trade it for a place at the market stall. She believes, wrongly, that the viceroy will raise the bread tax.
