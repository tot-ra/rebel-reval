## Biography
Born in 1310 in a Harju village, he came to Reval at nineteen and married a washerwoman. When she died in 1339 he kept her customers and tools, and the castle steward accepted him because the linen came back white. At Lent this year Lutke van Munster, a chaplain, collapsed in a lane in a fever; believing he was dying with no priest near, he confessed to the first man he saw. He recovered and has avoided Simon since.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To keep the castle's linen contract.
- **Fear:** The chaplain's secret turning dangerous.
- **Contradiction:** He is not a priest and is more trusted than one.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He knows what the chaplain confessed and has told no one.

## Faction and belief
Crown sympathiser, because the steward pays on time and the viceroy's court does not flog washermen. A small favour he does at once; a large one he weighs; informing he does under pressure. He drops a pinch of ash in the cistern for the water spirit.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows whose linen is stained and what that means. He would trade it for the steward's good word. He believes, wrongly, that the castle cistern will dry by June.
