## Biography
Born 1335 in the Toom-Kooli house. His byname arrived when the clerk who wrote his baptismal note put "van Lubeke" after "van", and it stuck. His father is not spoken of; his mother says only that he was a Lübeck man. He has a baritone that makes monks stare.

He is the family's singer: he knows the Alleluia, three drinking songs he must not sing, and the cow's name.
