## Biography
Born in 1286 at Roskilde, the son of a cathedral clerk, Niels was trained at the cathedral school and came to Reval in 1311 to serve a Danish bailiff. He has written the castle's toll copies since, and is the senior of the lower clerks.

Two things made him. In 1325 he lent six marks' worth of grain to a Dunkri merchant after a failed harvest; the interest he never named has become a quiet weight on them both. In 1338 he bought into a herring voyage with two others, a boat share that sank off the Finnish coast with its cargo; he owes both partners and has been paying in instalments ever since. He lives with his wife and children in the Pikk jalg house, whose stair is the only path he walks.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear the debt before he dies and see Mogens installed in a clerkship.
- **Fear:** A day when a creditor goes to the viceroy.
- **Contradiction:** Generous in laughter, tight in coin; he lends to a stranger and cannot refuse a friend, yet is ruined by both.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He sells the fresh copies of toll rolls to a Lower Town merchant for a mark, and uses it to pay the instalments.

## Work and money
He earns four marks a year plus fees, perhaps six marks in all; Bodil's washing brings in another few schillings. He owes Ludolf van Munster 18 marks and Magnus Birgersson 30 marks for the herring voyage and has paid back only three. He is owed grain by Borchard van Groninghe, at an unspoken heavy interest.

## Faction and belief
