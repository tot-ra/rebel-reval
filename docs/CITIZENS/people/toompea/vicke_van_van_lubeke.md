## At a glance
- Only 156 cm and built like a barrel, ruddy, brown-haired, and always holding his cup in the left hand.
- Speaks low and without hurry, a habit that makes sergeants repeat themselves.
- Sleeps in the lower barracks, three bunks from the stair.
- Odd: his name has two "van"s. The muster clerk wrote "van van Lubeke" in 1331 and no one has been able to scrape it out.

## Biography
Born in 1303 in Lübeck in the Hüxstrasse lanes, the son of a barrel-hooper, Vicke went to sea at fourteen as a cook's boy and jumped ship at Reval in 1320 rather than return to a beating. The Danish garrison took him because he could carry.

Two things made him. In 1328 his master at the barracks, a sergeant who taught him to fight left-handed on purpose, died of fever and left him the old spear. In 1331 the muster clerk wrote his name twice and the garrison turned the slip into a joke he has let stand; it is the only mark he has left on any roll. He now guards the lower barracks stair, sleeps lightly, and has joined, without being asked twice, a small circle that watches for talk against the Crown.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be forgotten by every roll and sergeant, and to die in a bed in Lübeck with a stone.
- **Fear:** Being sent to the front with Harju levies.
- **Contradiction:** Hates being noticed yet carries a joke about his own name around the barracks so no one will look further.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** At a funeral in Lent he heard a muttered sentence about the Crown's tolls and understood he was hearing the beginning of a plan; he has told only one person.

## Work and money
Paid about two marks a quarter plus rations. He has saved nine marks in a sock sewn to the bunk straw; he buys nothing, pays no debts and owes none. A bad month is a sergeant who takes a share of his pay for "the barracks fund".

## Faction and belief
A soft loyalty to the Crown that pays him; he knows the town hates the Danes and does not blame it. For a small favour he would lose a message; for a large one, hide a document; to inform, he already does. He prays to St Olaf and keeps a pebble from the Trave.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Middle Low German only, with Lübeck vowels.
- **Delivery:** Low, unhurried, long gaps.
- **Sample lines:** "Say it slower; I'm not in a hurry." / "Aye, I carried it, and I forgot it." / "Dat is een Saak."
- **Verbal tic:** "All right then," at the end of every exchange.

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows who hears what at the barracks stair and which sergeant sells grain. He would trade it for an exemption from the Harju levy. He believes falsely that the Hanse will burn the castle.
