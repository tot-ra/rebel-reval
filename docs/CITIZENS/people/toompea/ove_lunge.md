## Biography
Born in 1312 in Ribe, he was apprenticed to a church glazier and came to Reval in 1334 with a cathedral commission. He was raised partly by an Estonian nurse in the Ribe servants' hall, hence the cadence. He married Margrete Munk in 1331, and his father Henrik followed in 1337 after his own workshop failed. Three daughters live in a house of two rooms. He now repairs windows in the castle and chapter in return for crown wages, and tells a reeve who comes by what the street says.

## Work and money
He earns about eight marks a year from the crown plus piece-work. Lead and glass cost him three. He is owed five marks by a shoemaker. A bad month is when a storm breaks castle panes and the treasurer will not pay.

## Faction and belief
Crown, because it pays and the Dane is his own kind. A small favour he does at once; a large one for security; informing he does already. He crosses himself at the north door and keeps a shard of coloured glass in his shirt against the evil eye.

## Voice

- **Delivery:** Sing-song, easy, drops his voice to name a neighbour.

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows which window shutters stay open at night and who owes whom. He would trade it for his debt paid. He believes, wrongly, that the viceroy will move the garrison to Wesenberg.
