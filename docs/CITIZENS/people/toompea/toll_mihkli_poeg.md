## Biography
Born in 1318 in a Harju village, he walked to Reval at sixteen when a bad harvest sent his family to the manor. He carried at the harbour, then on the hill. He lodges with Niels Galen and pays four schillings a month. His voice went hoarse from calling loads at the quay in sea-wind. The winter of 1341-42 he nearly starved and was kept alive by two neighbours.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To buy his own handcart.
- **Fear:** A sickness that stops him lifting.
- **Contradiction:** He is gentle with animals and rough with his hands.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has promised a village girl he will send for her.

## Work and money
He earns about two pfennigs a day, ten schillings a month; lodging costs four. A bad month is when rain stops the hauling.

## Faction and belief
None; his reason is survival. A small favour he does at once; a large one he weighs against his girl; informing he refuses. He leaves a crust at the grove's oak outside the walls.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Estonian only; he understands some Low German.
- **Delivery:** Hoarse, slow, few words.
- **Sample lines:** "Heavy, but not too heavy." / "Pay me when you can." / "Ma ei tea."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows every load-bearing track. He would trade it for a handcart. He believes, wrongly, that the viceroy will free the bound peasants at Midsummer.
