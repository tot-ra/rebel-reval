## Biography
Born in 1301 near Turku, a sheep-farmer's son, he came to Reval in 1330 after his wife's death in Finland left him with a flock and nothing to feed it. He took up spinning to stay near the wool market. A year ago a crown reeve found him through a barber and paid him to listen at the cathedral door. He has done it faithfully, and tells himself it is only nodding.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To save enough to go home.
- **Fear:** Being found out by Estonians who think him a fool.
- **Contradiction:** He is quiet and kindly and sells talk.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He reports a canon's chatter without understanding the Latin.

## Work and money
He earns about ten schillings a month spinning; lodging costs four. The reeve pays two pfennigs a month. A bad month is a wool shortage.

## Faction and belief
Crown by wages; he has no loyalty to Danes but to the reeve's silver. A small favour he does at once; a large one he fears; informing is what he does. He keeps a Finnish rune-knot in his shirt.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Finnish at home; a little Low German at market.
- **Delivery:** Clipped and precise, short phrases.
- **Sample lines:** "Wool is wool." / "I only walk." / "Ei se mitään."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows who talks at the church door. He would trade it for passage home. He believes, wrongly, that Finnish ships will arrive in May.
