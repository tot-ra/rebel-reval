## Biography
Born in 1323 in the Rahukohtu house, the only child of Ricbod and his first wife Alheid, who died of the birth. Arend was raised by a wet nurse and then by his stepmother Gyse, who feeds him and reproaches him in the same breath.

Two things made him. In 1335 a thrown stone from the falconer's boy took out his front tooth, and he has never smiled with his mouth open since. In 1342 two friends lent him small kindnesses, a loan and a copied letter, and he found himself bound to both; he resents the debts and keeps them.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To become a notary and have his debts forgotten.
- **Fear:** His father's disappointment and the word "creditor".
- **Contradiction:** A man who dislikes being owed favours and cannot refuse one; who admires the Crown's order and wants out of its debts.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He reads his father's private tally when the old man is at mass.

## Work and money
He earns his board and two marks a year; his father holds the money. He owes Reynold van Wismar a small silver loan and Mogens Skjalm a copied letter. He buys ink at the Lower Town stalls. A bad month is a spoiled page.

## Faction and belief
A sympathiser of the Crown by habit and debt. For a small favour he would copy a letter; for a large one, deliver it; to inform, he would, if pressed. He goes to mass and says the prayers he knows.
