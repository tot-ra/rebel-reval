## Biography
Born in 1306 in Greifswald, the son of a salt-factor's clerk, he trained in a town chancery and came to Reval in 1329. He married Gesche van Groninghe in 1333 and fathered three children. His master was the viceroy's chancery master. His father Ricbod joined the household in 1338. He lives by his pen and the household has never been quite solvent since he lost four marks at dice in 1341.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To clear his dice debt before Michaelmas.
- **Fear:** Gesche finding out how much is owed.
- **Contradiction:** He copies the accounts of others to the farthing and cannot keep his own.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He leaves by the back lane after curfew to play knucklebones in the gatehouse, and has lost eleven schillings.

## Work and money
He is paid twelve marks a year in kind and silver. Rent and food take ten. He owes about eleven schillings at dice and two marks to the chancery master. A bad month is when the viceroy's pay is late.

## Faction and belief
None: a faction costs money he lacks and a clerk who takes sides loses his post. A small favour he does at once; a large one he fears; informing he would never do, though his father does. He prays to St Nicholas for dice.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Latin in the chancery, Middle Low German at home, Estonian with market women.
- **Delivery:** Low and unhurried, drawn-out vowels.
- **Sample lines:** "It is written, so it is so." / "Mind the sum." / "Ik bün nich dor."
- **Verbal tic:** "In due course."

## Knowledge and rumours
He knows the levy rolls and the garrison's rations. He would trade a figure for a debt forgiven. He believes, wrongly, that the viceroy will cancel the grain levy at Easter.
