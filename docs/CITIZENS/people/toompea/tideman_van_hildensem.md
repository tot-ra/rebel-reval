## Biography
Born in 1333 on Kohtu, the second son and, to his mother's surprise, the one who can add. She puts him on the stall to guard the coins. He is the only child allowed to sit in the kitchen while the reckonings are done. He envies Hennike's tools and pities his tiredness.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To be a merchant's clerk.
- **Fear:** His father's silences.
- **Contradiction:** He is lazy with the hoe and tireless with the abacus.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has skimmed a pfennig a week from the stall.
