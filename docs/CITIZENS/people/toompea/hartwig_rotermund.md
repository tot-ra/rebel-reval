## Biography
Born 1324 on Toom-Rüütli, the younger son. He was the quieter boy, hiding in the loft while Marquard drilled. In 1338 the brothers fought with practice blades in the yard, and each took a scar on the jaw, the same cut, a hand apart. Their father calls it the family mark.

He has no fief to hope for, and has made a trade of what is at hand: rabbits, hides, iron scraps from the armourers. He thinks of leaving, to Visby or Lübeck, and says it to nobody.

## Work and money
He sells hides at 2 schillings and scraps at a few pfennig. His savings of 4 marks are hidden. A bad month is a strict ditch watch.

## Faction and belief
He has none and says so; the crown gave him nothing, the Order nothing, the town nothing. He would do a small favour for a friend, a large one for none, and inform on nobody. He keeps St Hubert's day for the rabbits.
