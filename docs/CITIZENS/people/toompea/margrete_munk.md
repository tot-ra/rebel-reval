## Biography
Born in 1313 on a Zealand dairy farm, she was sent at ten to an Estonian-speaking farm near Saue as a dairy-girl on a Danish manor, and learned her Danish cadence from the Estonian milkmaids. She married Ove Lunge in 1331 when he came through with a window crew. They have three girls. She keeps two cows on a rented strip below the wall and sells milk, curd and butter on the hill.

## Motivation
- **Want:** A third cow and a byre with a roof.
- **Fear:** Murrain, or the castle taking her pasture for horses.
- **Contradiction:** She hates the hill's gossip and trades in it.

## Work and money
She earns about six schillings a week in milk and curd, minus fodder at two. The family owes the cow's seller four marks. A bad month is when a cow goes dry.

## Faction and belief
No faction; her loyalty is to milk and her family. A small favour she does with a jug; a large one she asks Ove about; informing she would not do. She hangs a rowan twig over the byre door.

She knows who buys milk and who does not pay. She would trade it for hay. She believes, wrongly, that the castle will take the slope pasture.
