## Biography
Born in 1280 in Lemgo in Westphalia, the daughter of a weaver, Gyse came to Livonia in 1299 with her first husband, a carter. He died in 1315 in the Order's war, and she was taken in by the Order's house at Weissenstein, where she spun for two winters and was fed. She came to Reval in 1317.

Two things made her. The winter at Weissenstein showed her that the Order, at least, feeds those who work. And the Danish tax of 1330 took the last of her husband's debt-silver and left her with a spinner's wage. In 1331 she married Ricbod van Minden for his hearth, not his love, and became stepmother to his motherless son Arend.

## Work and money
She spins about a pfund of wool a week and sells the thread for six schillings; she buys flax from a Harju carter. She has two marks in a stocking. A bad month is a closed market.

## Faction and belief
The Order's sympathiser out of gratitude, not politics; she wants it to win because it keeps its promises. For a small favour she would hide a bag; for a large one, carry a message; to inform, she would only to the Order. She says a Pater Noster for the Brothers.

## Knowledge and rumours
