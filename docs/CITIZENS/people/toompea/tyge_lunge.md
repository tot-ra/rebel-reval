## Biography
Born in 1298 at a manor near Næstved on Zealand, Tyge had smallpox at nine, the only one of four children to live. Taken as a squire's boy, he was dismissed at seventeen for striking his master, and found his way to Reval as a mercenary in 1315 during the Order's wars.

Two things made him. In 1325 he stood with a patrol that found a Danish courier dead in a ditch and a note unread in his hand; the note was never found, and Tyge understood that what is not said can kill. In 1340 he began to watch for people who watch, and from that came the Crown's quiet circle, to which he now brings one new name a year.

## Motivation
- **Want:** To leave a record, in the hands of the Danish marshal, that he was one man who saw clearly.
- **Fear:** Missing what matters because he cannot read it.
- **Contradiction:** A man who trusts no word spoken, yet whose only skill is hearing words.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He recruited a household servant by paying a debt for his sick sister; he keeps the receipt in a rag.

## Work and money
About two marks a quarter plus rations; he saves one and puts the other into small kindnesses, buying a loaf or settling a pledge. He has five marks hidden behind a loose stone in the barracks wall. A bad month is a servant who does not meet him.

## Faction and belief
Danish Crown because it is the strongest thing on the hill; he calls it order. For a small favour he would drop a word; for a large one, burn a letter; to inform, he is the circle's ears. He crosses himself twice and carries a St Olaf medal.

## Voice
- **Registers:** Danish with soldiers; slow Low German with townsfolk.
- **Delivery:** Low, unhurried, leaves gaps for the other to fill.
- **Sample lines:** "Go on. I am not in a hurry." / "You stopped speaking there. Why?" / "Det var ikke sagt."
- **Verbal tic:** "Hm."

## Knowledge and rumours
Knows the three servants who talk too much and the two clerks who do not. He would trade it for a promotion to the marshal's guard. He believes falsely that the Estonians have a secret king in the north.
