## At a glance
- Healer, weather-reader, finder of thieves.
- The Church calls them witches.
- Hurls a vial of ash and herb as a ranged curse.
- Ellen Luik is the model.

## Folklore origin
The village cunning-folk: healing, weather-magic, finding thieves, cursing (lendva); not evil by default, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4b. Absorbs the Slavic Striga. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Human, middle-aged to old, stooped, steady hands.
- **Materials and colours:** Layered homespun in grey and dyed ochre, herb pouches, bone tokens, ash on the cuffs.
- **What a model must get right:** Herb pouches and bone tokens; steady eyes; practical hands.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a nõid, an Estonian village cunning-woman, middle-aged, stooped, layered grey homespun and ochre-dyed shawl, herb pouches and bone tokens at the belt, ash on the cuffs, holding a small clay vial, steady direct gaze, 14th century Baltic village yard, grounded realism, muted palette, neutral background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** MPFB human base (ADR 0022), female or male, age 40-65; herb and bone props.

## Motivation
To keep her village alive; she fears the Church's witch-trial. Contradiction: healer and curser. Respect and fair payment keep her friendly.

## Manifestation
Villages and Metsik healer huts.

## Combat profile
Hybrid. Physical: caster archetype with vial throw (area poison), flee at 30 percent. Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: need and fear. Non-violent resolution: respect and fair payment. Guilt (christian / folk / civic): killing 3 / 5 / 3.

## Voice
- "Sit. Show me where it hurts."
- "Pay in bread. I take no silver from the sick."
- (threatened) "You will remember this door."

## Relationships and factions
[Metsik cult](../../CITIZENS/factions/cult_metsik.md) members; hunted by Church. Related: [spirit_caller](./spirit_caller.md), [libahunt](./libahunt.md).
