## At a glance
- A pale thin figure leaving no footprints and frost on every surface it passes.
- Tied to the unbaptised dead and to the Church's burial rules.
- Cold comes before it is seen.
- Odd detail: dogs lie down and refuse to bark at it.

## Folklore origin
Malevolent cold spirit or unquiet dead of those who died unbaptised; walks the winter roads, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4a. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Human height, painfully thin, drifts above snow.
- **Materials and colours:** Grey-white skin, frost-rimed rags, breath of ice crystals, eyes pale as snow.
- **What a model must get right:** Frost on the hem and hair, no footprints, and a hunched, shivering stance.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a kulmking, an Estonian frost wight, a gaunt pale figure in frost-rimed rags standing on a snowy road, hoarfrost on its hair and fingers, pale snow-white eyes, no footprints behind it, 14th century winter Baltic landscape, grounded dark-fantasy realism, cold blue-grey palette, neutral grey background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** Humanoid shared rig, frost particle cloak; spirit-layer only; spirit material: translucent pale-blue.

## Motivation
To be warmed and given a name; it fears fire and the sound of a bell. Contradiction: it wants shelter and freezes whoever gives it. A proper burial name and a candle releases it.

## Manifestation
Winter roads and church gates at dusk; stronger the longer a burial is delayed.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: belonging and burial. Temperament: cold, quiet, repeats the apprentice's words. Non-violent resolution: give it a name and a candle. Destroying it: 2 christian guilt (a soul left unburied).

## Voice
- "Cold. Nobody said my name."
- "A coat. A little warmth."
- (named) "Oh. That is mine."

## Relationships and factions
The Church fears it; a priest may ask the apprentice for help. Related: [kodukaija](./kodukaija.md), [myling](./myling.md).

## Game hooks
Act 1 quest seed: an unbaptised child's grave by a winter road. First meet: the Harju road in winter.
