## At a glance
- Seen only as a heavy dark form on the sleeper's chest.
- Iron or scissors at the door keep it out.
- May be a living person's wandering spirit.
- Natural first night-duel after the prologue.

## Folklore origin
The mare that sits on a sleeper's chest; a restless soul or a person's wandering spirit; blocked by iron or scissors in the doorway, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4a. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Human-sized crouching mass, no clear limbs; the shape of a heavy sleeper pressed flat.
- **Materials and colours:** Shadow cloth, no colour of its own, faint breath-fog in the cold.
- **What a model must get right:** A weight, not a face: a pressure in the frame. Needs a chest-pressing pose over a sleeping figure.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a luupainaja, a nightmare spirit, a dark crouching mass sitting on the chest of a sleeping boy in a cold almshouse bed, no face, shadow-cloth body with faint breath mist, thin moonlight, 14th century Baltic dormitory, grounded dark-fantasy realism, desaturated palette, neutral dark grey background, even low lighting.
- **Model notes:** Bespoke shadow mass over the sleeper; spirit-layer only; no physical mesh in the day layer.

## Motivation
To be seen and carried; it is a soul that has no place. It fears iron. Contradiction: it strangles what it wants to speak to. Naming it by its living name releases it.

## Manifestation
The sleeper's room, almshouse, forge loft, in the first night after the prologue and whenever the apprentice is troubled by guilt.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: unspoken guilt. Temperament: silent, pressing; wins by raising the pressure meter. Non-violent resolution: name the person it is. Destroying it: 1 folk-debt.

## Voice
- (breath, no words)
- "Heavy. Heavy. Hear me."
- (named) "Yes. Yes, that one."

## Relationships and factions
Feared by everyone; scissors over the lintel is a household custom. Related: [kodukaija](./kodukaija.md), [kulmking](./kulmking.md).

## Game hooks
Slice night one: first spirit-duel teacher after the prologue. Forge hook: an iron charm commission to keep it out.
