## At a glance
- Seen in boulder fields and hillfort ridges, huge and still.
- Kin of Kalevipoeg and Tõll.
- Absorbs the Scandinavian Troll as the only giant.
- Three archive sketches show its stone body.

## Folklore origin
Ancient giants; boulder-fields and hillforts are their leavings; kin to Kalevipoeg and Tõll, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4a. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** 6-12 m, human proportions, slow heavy stride that shakes ground.
- **Materials and colours:** Stone-grey skin with moss, boulder shoulders, wild hair of root and grass.
- **What a model must get right:** Boulder-like scale, moss and lichen on the skin, a human face under a heavy brow.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a hiid, an Estonian giant, ten metres tall with a skin of grey stone and moss, a heavy brow and human face, wild hair of roots and grass, standing in a field of enormous boulders at dawn, 14th century Baltic hillfort landscape, grounded dark-fantasy realism, muted greys and greens, neutral background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** Bespoke giant on scaled humanoid rig; spirit-layer only; terrain interaction on step.

## Motivation
To rest and be remembered; it fears no one, resents the forgetting. Contradiction: a patient giant with a famously bad temper. A told story of its own deeds quiets it.

## Manifestation
Boulder fields and hillforts in the Harju hinterland, Act 3, under storm light.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: memory and inheritance. Temperament: slow, powerful, proud. Non-violent resolution: retell its story with the right details. Destroying it: 5 folk-debt.
