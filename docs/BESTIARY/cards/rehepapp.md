## At a glance
- Old, cunning, and always one step ahead of the bailiff.
- Works with a kratt.
- The Estonian 'rob the oppressor' archetype.
- Likely an NPC first.

## Folklore origin
The estate's threshing-barn overseer who steals from the German lord to feed the village, in league with his kratt, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4b. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Human, thin, stooped, quick hands.
- **Materials and colours:** Chaff-dusted homespun, rope belt, straw in the hair.
- **What a model must get right:** Chaff and straw dust on everything; a sly half-smile.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a rehepapp, an Estonian barn trickster, thin stooped old man in chaff-dusted homespun with rope belt and straw in his hair, sly half-smile, standing beside a threshing barn door at night with a lantern, 14th century Baltic estate, grounded folklore realism, warm muted palette, neutral background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** MPFB human base (ADR 0022), male, age 55-70.

## Motivation
To feed the village and outwit the lord. He fears the bailiff's whip and a debtor's exposure.

## Manifestation
Estate barns, usually by night.

## Combat profile
Hybrid. Physical: weak (flees at first blow), skilled at distraction; spirit duel topic: cunning and need. Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Guilt: killing 4 / 5 / 3 (a beloved rebel).

## Voice
- "Grain is only grain until the lord counts it."
- "Ask no questions, take the sack."

## Relationships and factions
Village peasants; the [Metsik cult](../../CITIZENS/factions/cult_metsik.md) aids him. Related: [kratt](./kratt.md).

## Game hooks
Backlog seed: the apprentice carries a sack out for him. Likely an NPC first.
