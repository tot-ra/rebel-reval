## At a glance
- An old small figure seen at the hearth or the doorframe.
- Fed first from each meal.
- Offended, it ruins a house quietly.
- Fits the smithy home loop.

## Folklore origin
Guardian of a farmstead; fed the first of every meal; a wronged one brings ruin. Tõnn is the grain-luck spirit kept in the barn, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4b. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Knee- to waist-high, bent old figure.
- **Materials and colours:** Soot-grey wrappings, hearth ember glow, a rim of ash on the shoulders.
- **What a model must get right:** Soot and ash; an old bent stance; warm ember eyes.

- **Model notes:** Small humanoid on the shared rig scaled 0.5; spirit-layer only.

## Motivation
To be fed and remembered. It fears a house that stops caring. Contradiction: a protector that turns on its own. A first spoonful and an apology restore it.

## Manifestation
The smithy home and farmsteads, strongest after a desecration or a missed offering.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: care and neglect. Non-violent resolution: a meal and an apology. Destroying it: 4 folk-debt (the home loses luck).

## Voice
- "Nobody warmed my bowl."
- "This house forgets."
- (fed) "Good. Good. Stay."

## Relationships and factions
Kalev and Mart leave offerings; the Church tolerates it. Related: [domovoi](./domovoi.md), [maa_alused](./maa_alused.md).

Slice seed: forge home luck drops after the apprentice neglects the offering; sets up a spirit-duel lesson.
