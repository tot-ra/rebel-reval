## At a glance
- Seen as an old bearded man in the surf or as a net-tangled shape.
- Controls catch and drownings.
- Sailors bargain with him.
- Harbour content.

## Folklore origin
Old man of the waters or mermaid; controls the catch and drownings; sailors bargain with him, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4b. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Human to large size, wading half-submerged.
- **Materials and colours:** Seaweed beard, barnacle-crusted skin, net-draped shoulders.
- **What a model must get right:** Beard of weed, barnacles on the skin, nets as clothing.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a vetevana, an Estonian old man of the waters, an aged bearded figure half-submerged in harbour water, seaweed beard, barnacle-crusted skin, draped in fishing nets, 14th century Baltic harbour at dawn, grounded dark-fantasy realism, muted green-grey palette, neutral background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** MPFB human base with water shader; spirit-layer only.

## Motivation
To receive tribute and to keep count of the catch. It fears being cheated. A fair bargain of the first catch keeps it calm.

## Manifestation
Harbour and fishing coast at dawn, before the fleet leaves.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: tribute and trade. Non-violent resolution: pay the right tribute. Destroying it: 3 folk-debt and a poor season.

## Voice
- "The first of the catch is mine."
- "You count badly, boy."

## Relationships and factions
Sailors and fishers; ties to [vitalienbruder](../../CITIZENS/factions/vitalienbruder.md). Related: [nakk](./nakk.md), [vodyanoy](./vodyanoy.md).

## Game hooks
Act 1 seed: a fisherman's pact in the Seamen's Inn. First meet: the harbour shore at dawn.
