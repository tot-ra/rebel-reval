# Puuk

> A small black thief that lives for glitter and chaos, and that a household feeds because feeding it is cheaper than being robbed.

## At a glance
- Cat-sized, glossy black, with short bat-like wings that carry it in hops.
- Steals shiny things and hoards them in hollow trunks and rafters.
- Leaves sparks and scorch marks when frightened.
- Odd detail: it returns each night to rearrange its hoard.

## Folklore origin
Northern and South Estonian and Baltic-German term for the milk- and wealth-stealing familiar; overlaps with kratt and tulihänd, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4a. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** About 0.7-1 m long, wiry, long snout, spade-tipped tail, short leathery wings used for hops and glides; darts rather than walks.
- **Materials and colours:** Oily black scales with faint iridescence, amber eyes; spark trail when it flees.
- **What a model must get right:** Dragon-imp proportions (long snout, small wings, spade tail), amber eyes, oil sheen on the scales. Not a bat, not a dog.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a puuk, a small folklore household imp, one metre long, glossy black scales with oil-slick iridescence, long slender snout, amber eyes, tiny leathery wings, spade-tipped tail, crouched on a barn rafter clutching a silver brooch, 14th century Baltic farm interior, grounded dark-fantasy realism, muted palette, plain neutral grey background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** Bespoke quadruped-biped small creature; spirit material: dark shell with ember edge lines; no spirit-sight glow unless carrying stolen goods.

## Motivation
Greed for shine and the joy of mischief. It fears iron and a household that refuses to feed it. Contradiction: loyal to whoever fed it last. Released by returning what it stole in exchange for something shinier.

## Manifestation
Lower Town lofts and harbour stores at night; also market stalls when a commission has gone missing.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: ownership and fairness. Temperament: playful, evasive, steals the apprentice's turn items. Non-violent resolution: bargain a trade of a shiny forge scrap for the stolen item. Destroying it gives no pressure bonus but costs 1 folk-debt (it was a household member).

## Voice
- "Mine. Mine now. Look how it shines."
- "Chase me, chase me, slow-foot."
- (caught) "Trade! I trade! Give the bright one."

## Relationships and factions
Tolerated by fed households; hated by merchants. The [Metsik cult](../../CITIZENS/factions/cult_metsik.md) is said to keep one for luck. Related: [kratt](./kratt.md).

## Game hooks
Quest seed "The Stolen Trinket": a lost amulet is in a puuk hoard. Forge hook: a customer's missing silver is the puuk's. First meet: the almshouse loft on a night walk in Act 1.
