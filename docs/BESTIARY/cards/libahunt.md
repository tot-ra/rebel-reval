## At a glance
- A wolf with a human gait and a human's eyes.
- Cursed by a wolf-hide belt, a spell or a slight.
- Fought as a real wolf; seen as a person in the spirit layer.
- Three archive sketches show the shift.

## Folklore origin
Person turned wolf by a curse, a belt of wolf-hide or witchcraft; a major Estonian tale-type, often a wronged woman, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4a. The legacy silver-only rule is dropped (not Estonian). `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Large wolf (1 m at shoulder), human posture when it stands.
- **Materials and colours:** Grey-brown fur, human hands under the paws, a belt of hide at the waist.
- **What a model must get right:** Human eyes, a belt that is not part of the fur, a gait that is slightly wrong.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a libahunt, an Estonian werewolf, a large grey wolf with human eyes and a hide belt at the waist, rising on hind legs at the edge of a winter forest, torn peasant skirt hem showing, 14th century Baltic woodland, grounded dark-fantasy realism, muted palette, neutral grey background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** Quadruped wolf with morph to MPFB human; spirit-layer shows the person.

## Motivation
To get home, to be a person again. It fears fire and its own name. Contradiction: a victim that kills. Burning the belt and naming the person ends the curse.

## Manifestation
Forests and Harju wilds at night around a village wronging someone.

## Combat profile
Hybrid. Physical: wolf archetype tuned strong, bite and lunge, flees at 30 percent health. Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: a wrong done. Non-violent resolution: burn the belt, name the wrong. Guilt (christian / folk / civic): killing 4 / 5 / 2; breaking the curse 0 / 0 / 0.
