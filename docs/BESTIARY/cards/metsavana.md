## At a glance
- A towering figure of twisted wood, vines and moss with a hollow trunk-face and amber eyes.
- The forest's own voice in the Metsik cult's rites.
- Rustles even in still air.
- Absorbs the Slavic Leshy (one being, many names).

## Folklore origin
Metsik is a straw harvest-effigy paraded and killed for fertility; Metsaema and Metsavana are the forest's owner-spirits; Leshy is the Slavic import, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4a. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** 3-8 m depending on how deep the forest is; rooted gait, long clawed root hands; can uproot itself.
- **Materials and colours:** Bark skin, moss-green with lichen and fungus patches, branch crown, hanging vine hair, amber eyes in a hollow face.
- **What a model must get right:** A face hidden inside a tree-trunk hollow; antlers or branches as a crown; root claws. It must look like a very old tree, not a monster.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of the Estonian forest owner Metsavana, a towering tree-like being of twisted oak bark covered in moss and lichen, a hollow trunk face with two amber eyes, branches for a crown, hanging vines, root-like clawed hands, standing in a dim Baltic oak grove, 14th century folklore, grounded dark-fantasy realism, muted greens and browns, neutral grey background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** Bespoke tall tree-humanoid; branch cards plus bark mesh; spirit material: warm amber heartwood glow in the hollow.

## Motivation
Balance in the forest. It rewards reverence, punishes felling without leave. Contradiction: a protector that kills woodcutters. A spoken ask-and-leave offering releases it.

## Manifestation
The sacred grove and forest edge, Act 1; strongest when trees are felled near it or when the cult holds a rite.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: permission and obligation. Temperament: calm, slow, heavy. Non-violent resolution: name what was taken and offer a counter-gift. Destroying it: 3 folk-debt and loss of cult trust.

## Voice
- "You walk on my roots. Say who you are."
- "The oak was old before the city."
- (wrath) "No more axes."

## Relationships and factions
Patron of the [Metsik cult](../../CITIZENS/factions/cult_metsik.md); wary of the Vanapagan rite. Enemy of the Order's loggers. Related: [pohja_konn](./pohja_konn.md), [vanapagan](./vanapagan.md), [hiie_hirv](./hiie_hirv.md).

## Game hooks
Quest seeds: "The Whispering Woods" (clear Order soldiers), "Rooted Justice" (illegal felling near the grove). Forge hook: charcoal and tool wood commissions that cost it something.
