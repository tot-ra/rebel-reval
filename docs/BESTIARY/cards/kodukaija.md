## At a glance
- Walks to his own house at night and stands in the doorway.
- Looks like the living man, a little too still.
- Only a ritual finishes him, not force.
- Core teacher of the spirit duel.

## Folklore origin
The dead who will not stay buried and return to their house; laid to rest by ritual, not force, [estonian_folklore.md](../../lore/estonian_folklore.md) section 4a. Absorbs the Slavic Upyr. `folklore`.

## Appearance
- **Body:** Human height, in burial clothes; slow, deliberate walk; no breath.
- **Materials and colours:** Grey pallor, burial linen, soil on the hands, familiar face that is subtly wrong.
- **What a model must get right:** Burial shroud over everyday clothes; unmoving eyes; a path of clean footprints without mud.
- **Concept-art prompt:** Concept art of a kodukaija, a returned dead man, standing at the threshold of a wooden farmhouse at night in burial linen over everyday clothes, grey pallor, soil on the hands, unblinking eyes, 14th century Baltic farmstead, grounded dark-fantasy realism, muted palette, neutral grey background, even lighting.
- **Model notes:** MPFB human base (ADR 0022) with a pallor material; spirit-layer visible, faint in the physical layer.

## Motivation
To finish something: a debt, an unsaid word, a missing rite. It fears the church bell. Contradiction: it loves the family and frightens it. A completed ritual lets it rest.

## Manifestation
The dead person's house, at night, while the family is afraid to name him.

## Combat profile
Spirit duel ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-teen-protagonist-and-spirit-dialogue-combat.md)): turn-based exchange of statements and offerings, resolved by the apprentice's reading of the being; no hit points, a pressure meter instead. Topic: unfinished business. Temperament: sorrowful, repetitive. Non-violent resolution: complete the rite it asks for. Destroying it: 3 christian guilt and 2 folk-debt.

## Voice
- "I left the gate open."

- (rest) "Now I can sleep."

## Relationships and factions
The Church and the family both fear it; a priest may refuse to help. Related: [kulmking](./kulmking.md), [luupainaja](./luupainaja.md), [hinged](./hinged.md) (the household's collective dead, hosted each autumn, not one wrongful return).

## Game hooks
Slice quest seed: a dead tanner at the Lower Town; the apprentice finds his missing rite. Spirit-duel teacher: first lesson on resolving without force.
