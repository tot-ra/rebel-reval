*No 3D model.* District maps keep the shrub as data (it slows movement and counts in composition), but the old blob mesh was removed and nothing is drawn ([legacy shrub mesh removed](../../SYSTEMS/LIVING_VEGETATION.md#legacy-shrub-mesh-removed)).

## Where it grows in game

Spruce and pine forest floor.

## Seasons

Berries July-August.

## Folk use and herbalism (planned)

Berries; leaves and dried berries against stomach upset. No gathering system exists yet; see [herbalism readiness](../README.md#herbalism-readiness).
