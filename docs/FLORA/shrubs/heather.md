## Where it grows in game

Sandy heath and dry pine woods.

## Seasons

Purple flowers August-September.

## Folk use and herbalism (planned)

Tea, bee pasture, besoms. No gathering system exists yet; see [herbalism readiness](../README.md#herbalism-readiness).
