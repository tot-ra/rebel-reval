## Where it grows in game

Dry pine woods and heath.

## Seasons

Berries August-September.

## Folk use and herbalism (planned)

Berries keep through winter; leaves for kidney tea. No gathering system exists yet; see [herbalism readiness](../README.md#herbalism-readiness).
