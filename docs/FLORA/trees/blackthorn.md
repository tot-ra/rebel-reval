## Look

Dense thorny thicket.

## Where it grows in game

Thorn scrub on wall feet and dry slopes.

## Seasons

Sloes September-November. Phenology: `VegetationPhenology` ([seasons](../../SYSTEMS/LIVING_VEGETATION.md#seasons-and-fruit)).

## Folk use and herbalism (planned)

Sloes after frost; bark for dye. No gathering system exists yet; see [herbalism readiness](../README.md#herbalism-readiness).
