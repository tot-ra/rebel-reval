## Look

Dark, narrow crown.

## Where it grows in game

Stream banks and wet ground.

## Seasons

Keeps green leaves late. Phenology: `VegetationPhenology` ([seasons](../../SYSTEMS/LIVING_VEGETATION.md#seasons-and-fruit)).

## Folk use and herbalism (planned)

Bark for dye and tanning. No gathering system exists yet; see [herbalism readiness](../README.md#herbalism-readiness).
