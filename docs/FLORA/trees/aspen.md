## Look

Tall, slim, grey-green bole, trembling leaves.

## Where it grows in game

Wood edges and clearings.

## Seasons

Gold to red autumn. Phenology: `VegetationPhenology` ([seasons](../../SYSTEMS/LIVING_VEGETATION.md#seasons-and-fruit)).

## Folk use and herbalism (planned)

Bark as a fever remedy; aspen in folk belief keeps spirits off. No gathering system exists yet; see [herbalism readiness](../README.md#herbalism-readiness).
