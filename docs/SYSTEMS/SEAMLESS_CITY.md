# Seamless Reval city (1343)

Status: implemented as the game's Reval ([ADR 0031](../adr/0031-continuous-reval-city-plan.md), accepted 2026-10-07; board task to be filed, no board access in the authoring session). Attribution: `CREDITS.md` and `docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Scope: the whole walled Lower Town, Toompea, the shore to the 1343 waterline, the Härjapea and the near suburbs and fields as one continuous scene with real relief, the 1343 fortifications, enterable houses and churches, people, shipping, swimming, chimney smoke and one shared wind; every old Reval district destination now arrives here. Out of scope here: quests, dialogue and saves inside the city scene (the forge interior keeps them; see [Limits](#limits)).

Countryside (fields, pastures, woods): [`FARMLAND.md`](./FARMLAND.md). Distant travel destinations (Paide first) run on this builder and runtime as regional sites: [`REGIONAL_SITES.md`](./REGIONAL_SITES.md). Review plates: [`docs/reports/reval_city_plan_2026-10-07.md`](../reports/reval_city_plan_2026-10-07.md).

## What the player can do

- Main menu → **Start**: the almshouse prologue, then the city **inside** Kalev's smithy, in the forge bay by the lit hearth (spawn `kalev_smithy`, task **R-1643**). The smithy is the bespoke landmark site `site.kalev_smithy` ([Landmark sites](./landmark_sites/kalev_smithy.md)): a 1343 stone smithy with the old forge's limestone walls and GLB kit (hearth with live fire, bellows, anvil, slack tub, stock rack, bench), a living bay behind a partition and a lean-to; walk out through its street or yard door, no scene swap. The `forge` scene id is redirected to this spawn (`CityTravel.REDIRECTS`), so old saves and links land here. The standalone `forge` scene file is unused by the live flow.
- Walk anywhere in the plan without a loading screen: in through the Viru gate, along Pikk and Lai to the Coastal Gate and down to the shore, up Pikk jalg or the Lühike jalg steps onto Toompea, out of the Cattle or Smiths' gates to the fields. Movement is 5× the district-map speed (maintainer request, for crossing the city quickly).
- Click an enterable house, council hall or church door with the left mouse button while within 2.4 city units to swing it inward. Walking past a door no longer opens it. The leaf stays open while Kalev is inside and closes after he leaves. A gamepad door-selection action is not implemented yet.
- Jump and slope slide (`player_jump`, `Space`): in the city the jump is context-aware since ADR 0053 (vault a churchyard wall, pull up onto a ledge, jump down or into the water, or refuse; see [`LEAPING.md`](LEAPING.md)); the fixed hop below remains on maps without ground heights.
- Fixed hop and slope slide (maps without ground heights, and the slide everywhere): slopes steeper than 38 degrees (the klint, gullies between houses) are solid on the `CollisionLayers.STEEP` layer. A hop (`PlayerJumpState`: 0.65 s in the air, about 5 units at a run, 8 stamina) masks that layer out, so Kalev clears a steep strip in the road; walls and houses still block. A landing inside a strip keeps the mask off until the slide carries him out. Ground steeper than 22 degrees pulls him downhill (up to 140 px/s at 38 degrees) while on foot (`MapDefinition.downhill_at_world`, fed by `Player.set_downhill_provider`). Height is presentation only (`Player.jump_height()` lifts the rig in `MapViewRuntimeActors.sync_player`); there is no jump animation clip yet and the slide plays the walk clip. Citizens and arrows treat STEEP as a wall (`CollisionLayers.MASK_WORLD`). Verify: `tests/godot/test_player_jump_state.gd`; real-city probe `tools/godot_render.sh --resolution 1600x900 res://tools/capture_city_jump.tscn`.
- Swim: the sea and the water-filled moat use the game's swimming (wade, swim, dive with `player_dive`, breath).
- Magic: the Spellforge works as on the district maps; a Fireball flies as an ember orb and bursts on impact in the city view.
- Travel map (`M`): the district tab fast-travels inside the city (Kalev is moved in place, no reload: centre → forum, north → Pikk granary, monastery → St Olaf, Toompea → castle, harbour → fish landing, ...); the global tab starts journeys to distant regions. Walking within 28 m of the plan's edge opens the global travel map and sets Kalev back inside, so leaving the city always means choosing a destination. Returning from Padise, Harju, the sacred grove and other regions lands at the matching gate or road.
- People: gate garrisons and watch patrols (census residents on duty, [`GATE_GARRISONS.md`](./GATE_GARRISONS.md)), and the census residents of the houses around Kalev on their own timetables ([`CITIZENS.md`](./CITIZENS.md#in-the-city)); click one to see who they are.
- Birds: the game's bird flight and birdsong layers fly over the city. Flights are spawned across a 160 m window around Kalev, lifted 8 m over the highest ground in it, and the species mix follows where he is (`CityMapDefinition.bird_context_at`): gulls and terns on the shore and fishing beach, castle birds on Toompea, market birds round the forum, town birds in the Lower Town, open-country birds outside the walls.
- Ships: Hanseatic cogs ride at anchor in the roads north of the Coastal Gate, swinging bow-to-wind; fishing boats lie off the fish landing; one cog sails along the shore.
- Minimap (top right): a circular painted map that turns with the camera, a north mark, Kalev's arrow, and below it the district, the street (modern and 1343 name) and the building he is in.
- Camera: hold right (or middle) mouse and drag, or use the gamepad right stick, to orbit; mouse wheel to zoom; `Q`/`E` to turn. The camera pulls in front of walls and houses.
- Command line: `-- --city-spawn=<spawn id>`, for example `gate.viru`, `gate.coastal.outside`, `poi.forum`, `kalev_smithy`.

## How the plan is made

`tools/city/build_reval_city_plan.py` compiles three committed inputs into `content/world/reval_city/` (plan, heightfield, surface splat, cart-road raster and the painted `minimap.png`):

| Input | What it gives | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `tools/city/data/osm_reval_extract.json` | streets, plot footprints, surviving towers, wall fragments, cliffs, coast | ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors |
| `tools/city/data/eudem25m_reval.json` | 25 m surface heights (trend only; rooftop bias removed) | EU-DEM v1.1, Copernicus |
| `tools/city/reval_1343_overlay.json` | everything 1343: wall anchors and states, towers, gates, Toompea edge, shoreline, Härjapea, moat, excluded streets and buildings, extramural roads, suburbs, wells, guard posts, barracks, markets, supply and watch flows | project |

Rebuild after editing the overlay: `python3 tools/city/build_reval_city_plan.py`. `--check` fails when the committed outputs are stale. `--import-osm <overpass.json> --import-dem <dem.json>` refreshes the source extracts (see the script header for the queries). The builder also writes `docs/reports/images/city/reval_city_plan.png`, a top-down review map.

Rules the compiler enforces:

- Coordinates: local metres, origin at the forum (59.43725 N, 24.74535 E), x east, y south; output in world units of 1 m (x east, z south), logic pixels = world units × 32. Kalev's rig is 1.83 units tall, so 1 m per unit keeps houses, walls and trees in proportion to him.
- Gates snap onto their street; a gate faces along its street.
- Streets inside the walls come from OSM, renamed from the 1343 street register where it has a record; excluded names (later streets, Rüütli, Uus, Väike-Karja, Patkuli stairs ...) are dropped; a street that crosses the curtain away from a gate is cut back to the inside (later wall breaches).
- Buildings come from OSM plot footprints inside the circuit or on Toompea; post-1343 landmarks and towers are excluded; back plots are thinned for 1343 density; material and roof follow street rank (limestone and tile on the spines, timber and thatch in the lanes); the ridge follows the plot's own axis.
- Terrain: EU-DEM trend plus open-country relief ([Ground relief, roads and prints](#ground-relief-roads-and-prints)), the walled town lowered by the surface-model rooftop bias, the Toompea table authored from the cliff edge, the hill ways carved as ramps, the beach and seabed from the 1343 shoreline, the Härjapea channel and the S/E ditch cut in.
- Stable IDs: `street.osm.<way>`, `bldg.osm.w<way>` / `bldg.osm.r<relation>`, `bldg.lm.<landmark>`, `bldg.<suburb>.<n>`, `gate.*`, `tower.*`, `curtain.NN`, `toompea_wall.NN`, `poi.*`, `flow.*`, `field.*`, `pasture.*`, `wood.*`, `farmstead.*`. OSM ids keep a building's id across rebuilds.

### Ground relief, roads and prints

Status: implemented. Scope: the open country outside the walls, the cart roads, and footprints in soft ground. Out of scope: deformation of the walkable surface (prints are visual; they change neither the heightfield, collision nor walking speed). Real relief geometry exists only in the trail relief mesh round Kalev (task **R-1399**); farther out ruts and prints are shader relief (see Limits).

The EU-DEM trend is a smooth 25 m plate, so the compiler (`tools/city/terrain_relief.py`, called from `build_reval_city_plan.py` after the cut/fill passes) adds relief where nothing else authored the ground:

- **Swells and hummocks:** domain-warped fractal value noise at 260, 110, 48, 21 and 11 m wavelengths (amplitudes 5.0, 3.2, 1.8, 0.6, 0.18 m).
- **Gullies:** smooth ridged noise (95 m) cut up to 0.85 m, deeper where the broad swell is low.
- **Sand ridges:** 34 m wavelength, up to 0.85 m, 20–340 m from the water.
- **Hollow ways:** each `extramural_road` is sunk 0.24 m below its verge with a 0.13 m spoil berm either side, and the small relief is damped on the road so it stays travelable. Fades out within 40 m of the curtain, where the town plan owns the street.
- **Kept untouched:** everything inside the walls (heights within 120 wu of the forum are byte-identical to before), Toompea and its slope, 40 m of shore and the sea, the Härjapea, and the suburbs. The relief fades in from 16 m to 130 m outside the wall.

Everything is seeded lattice noise: the same inputs give the same `height.json`. Trees, grass and houses follow `ground_height`, so they sit on the new relief.

`content/world/reval_city/roads.png` (same rectangle and resolution as `splat.png`; import with `fix_alpha_border=false` or the road centre is overwritten) holds, per pixel: R road body, G signed lateral offset from the centreline (128 = centre, ±5 m), B traffic wear per road (`terrain_relief.ROAD_TRAFFIC`: Viru and the harbour road heaviest, the beach track lightest), A verge band. The splat no longer paints roads as flat mud; roads are packed earth there and the ground shader does the rest.

`city_ground.gdshader` reads that raster:

- **Wheel ruts:** two ruts a cart gauge apart (0.82 wu each side), a berm outside each and a trodden strip between where weeds grow on lightly used roads. The ruts wander with noise, vary in depth along the road and deepen with traffic. Computed as a height profile and lit through finite-difference normals; faded out with distance (`fwidth`) so far ground does not shimmer.
- **Dry:** pale dust, rough, ruts half-filled; footprints are soft with dusty rims.
- **Wet:** dark clay that gives under the wheel, deeper ruts and prints, shiny slick walls; water has a level: `puddles` raises it through the hollows, ruts and prints, so the deepest basins fill first and pools widen as rain accumulates. Gradation, driest to wettest: damp earth margin (darker, slightly glossy), a thin wet film that still shows the bed, then standing water (murky tint, near-mirror roughness, flat normal, rain rings while it is raining). Seep into soaked ruts is film only. `wetness` and `puddles` come from `SkyWeather3D` as before; review with `tools/godot_render.sh --script tools/capture_city_mud.gd -- --tag=x --wet=0.9 --puddles=0.3|1.0`. In a drought the same basins bake into cracked clay: see [`WEATHER_GROUND.md`](./WEATHER_GROUND.md) (R-1516).
- **Earth plate without repeat (R-2068):** the mud plate (`mud_albedo`/`mud_normal`) feeds earth, mud, field soil, cart roads and the wall foot through one hex-tiled sample (`hex_earth`): every hexagon of a triangle grid shows the plate under its own random rotation and offset, and each pixel blends its three hexagons with continuous weights (so no seams) and a variance-preserving blend that keeps crack contrast. Farther off (pixel footprint above ~0.006 plate repeats) it blends to a 1.8x finer second hex layout, so the crack webs shrink a little and what repeat is left breaks up. Cart roads also vary in tone: ~10 m stretches between ochre clay and grey till, patches of a few metres of damp packed ground (more in hollows) or pale loose dust, and fine mottling. Review plates: `tools/capture_city_mud.gd` (the `far` view shows the repeat); before/after: [far](../reports/images/ground_detile/far_before_after.jpg), [close stones](../reports/images/ground_detile/low_before_after.jpg), [wet road](../reports/images/ground_detile/wet_before_after.jpg).
- **Road stones (R-1606, R-2068):** `pebble_layer` stones have irregular outlines (second and third angular harmonics), sizes skewed small with the odd large stone, a per-stone burial depth and pitted faces. On roads they gather in scoured patches and along the ruts (a few elsewhere), carry a clay coat thickest at the bedding line, and darken under a mud coat when wet. Shingle and wall-foot rubble share the same stone shapes.
- **Crests and hollows:** the heightfield texture gives curvature; crests dry to straw, hollows stay lush; grassy banks too steep for turf show bare sand before turning to rock.

`scripts/city/city_ground_trail.gd` (`CityGroundTrail`, built by `CityWorld3D`, fed by `reval_city.gd` each frame) keeps a 1024 × 1024 R8 relief image (0.04 wu cells, a 41 wu window that recentres on Kalev with its contents kept). Each 0.85 wu of walking presses a boot print (heel, arch and ball lobes, toe-out, left and right alternating) and heaps a rim; depth follows wetness (`0.4 + 1.15 * wetness`): dry ground only takes a faint print, soaked clay a deep one that seeps water and darkens to slick mud in the shader; prints bite where the splat says earth or mud (a faint print on grass, none on paving or in the sea). `stamp_foot`, `stamp_oval` and `stamp_track` are public stamping primitives. The shader turns the image into relief, damp darkening and puddles. On dry soft ground a footfall also kicks up a dull brown-grey puff of dust (`CPUParticles3D`, no texture asset; lit, so it darkens at night instead of glowing ochre); wet ground raises none. **Other walkers** (`scripts/city/city_trail_feed.gd`, `CityTrailFeed`, called by `reval_city.gd` each frame after `trail.update_for`): residents on foot (`CityCitizens.live_actors()`, outdoors and walking, not shown at home) call `track_walker` (boot prints every 0.85 wu, 0.85 of Kalev's weight); animals (`CityFauna.live_animals()`) go through `CityAnimalTracks.track` (`scripts/city/city_animal_tracks.gd`, task **R-1992**), which picks the print by species: hooves for `horse`, `cow`, `pig`, `goat`, `sheep` (table `CityGroundTrail.HOOVES`, left and right hoof per stride); paws for `dog`, `red_fox`, `cat` (`PAWS`: a heel pad and four fanned toes, claw nicks for dog and fox, the fox in one straight line, left and right lines for dog and cat); bounding groups for `hare` and `rat` (`BOUNDERS`: long hind feet side by side ahead, small fore feet one behind the other); three-toed bird feet for `chicken` (with a hind toe) and `goose` (webbed, toed-in) (`BIRD_FEET`). Sizes are floored to half a trail cell so small toes do not flicker between texels. Animal prints are weighted by settled snow as well as wetness (`_weight(CitySnow.patch)`, as Kalev's own footfalls are), so tracks show clearly in January snow; species with no entry (fish) leave nothing. The trail's own `track_hooves` stays for tests and the mud plate (plate, January snow, one lane per species: [`animal_tracks_snow_top.jpg`](../reports/images/city/animal_tracks_snow_top.jpg)); wheeled vehicles call `track_cart`: any node in group `city_wheeled` with `world_xz()` and `trail_vehicle_class()` (a `CartTransportModel` class) gets one groove per wheel or runner from `CartTransportModel.wheel_track_spec` (gauge, groove half width, load factor; a barrow is one wheel). Only walkers within `TRACK_RADIUS` (20 wu) of Kalev and 1 wu inside the window leave marks, so the stamps cost nothing out of sight. Depth follows `trail.wetness` for all of them (feet and hooves `0.4 + 1.15 * wetness` times weight; wheels `0.3 + 0.9 * wetness` times load). A walker that is no longer fed is forgotten after a frame; a jump over 4 wu restarts its mark. Prints are not saved: a teleport (> 10 wu in a frame) or leaving the window starts fresh ground.

**Trail relief mesh** (task **R-1399**): round Kalev the prints and ruts are real geometry, so a low grazing camera sees their silhouette. `CityGroundTrail` owns `TrailRelief`, a flat 12.4 × 12.4 wu grid (`RELIEF_HALF` 6.2; `RELIEF_STEP` = one trail cell, 0.04 wu, inside the ±3.16 wu core `RELIEF_FINE_HALF`, 0.08 wu outside it, the two stitched without T-junctions by `relief_mesh()`; ~43k vertices) that re-snaps to the nearest height-grid vertex as Kalev walks, so its vertices stay fixed in the world. It draws with the shared ground material; vertex colour alpha 0 marks it (an `instance uniform` would take a global-buffer slot for every terrain chunk and overflow it in GL Compatibility). In the vertex stage `city_ground.gdshader` rebuilds the chunk surface exactly: the same grid samples (`texelFetch` of `ground_height`), the same diagonal split as `CityPlan.ground_height` and `CityTerrainBuilder._chunk_mesh`, and the same central-difference normals and tangents interpolated over the triangle. On top it adds the trail relief (3 × 3 tent blur 1.5 texels wide, so stamp walls slope like slumped mud) times the fragment's `give` and wetness, plus the cart-road `road_profile`, all scaled by `trail_mesh_depth` (0.6: the shaded relief is exaggerated for reading from above). Chunk vertices within `trail_mesh_rect.z` of the mesh centre sink by `trail_sink` (0.4 wu) so pressed relief is not buried; the mesh never dips below that sunk surface, fades to flat over the part-sunk band at its edge, and sits 4 mm above the chunk so the two never z-fight. Shading keeps the undisplaced normal (the trail normal map already shades prints), so the mesh is seamless with the chunks round it. Collision, walking and `ground_height` still follow the undisplaced surface. Review: `tools/godot_render.sh --script tools/capture_city_mud.gd -- --tag=<t> [--relief=0]` (`--relief=0` hides the mesh for before/after plates); tests `test_relief_mesh_follows_kalev_on_the_height_grid`, `test_relief_vertices_sit_on_trail_texel_corners`, `test_relief_mesh_has_no_cracks_between_its_densities` in `test_city_ground_trail`. Before/after (low grazing camera, `--relief=0` vs default): [`trail_relief_low_norelief.jpg`](../reports/images/city/trail_relief_low_norelief.jpg), [`trail_relief_low_relief.jpg`](../reports/images/city/trail_relief_low_relief.jpg). Cost: 0.5–1.4 ms per 1080p frame on the review Mac (wall-clock probe, readback-synced, relief on vs off, out of ~36 ms); the vertex stage dominates (a uniform 0.04 wu grid cost ~3 ms), the shadow pass is negligible. The headless `tools/run_performance_report.sh --quick` does not render the city ground.

Review: `tools/godot_render.sh --script tools/capture_terrain_relief.gd` renders the open country, the roads, and walked and wheeled prints, each dry and wet, to `docs/reports/images/city/terrain_*.png`.

### Wall foot

Status: implemented (task **R-1930**). Scope: the ground side of every joint between the city ground and a building, a site building or a site wall (close walls, plank fences). Out of scope: the wall shaders (plinth and wall grime: R-1931 below), the 3D grass blades and forbs (the R-1932 scatter below adds 3D clutter), interiors.

Before, a clean terrain plate met a clean wall in one pixel-sharp straight line. Now a ragged band runs along every wall foot:

- **Packed earth:** within about 1.2 m of the wall the loose dark loam turns into packed, lime-dusted grey-brown ground (an absolute tone, so it reads in lanes that are cart-road raster too). It darkens like the loam when wet. North-strip moss (R-1661) survives in it; only turf is worn away. Paving takes only a light grime of it.
- **Damp patches** where the eaves drip, darker and slightly glossy (they add to `wet`).
- **Rubble:** lime-mortar crumbs and small stones from `pebble_layer()` (0.1 m cells), thickest at the wall, muted with the band's dust; only near the camera, where a stone resolves.
- **Contact shade:** 15-30 % darker at the wall, fading out over 0.3-0.6 m; strength and reach vary along the wall.
- **Snow:** the eaves keep a ragged strip at the foot almost bare (down to 10 % of the snow cover within about 0.55 m).

Every edge is wobbled by world-space value noise (band edge +-0.3 m; the contact shade has its own noise so it does not trace the band), so no edge is a straight line. All deterministic, no per-frame state.

Data: `CityShadeMask` (`scripts/city/city_shade_mask.gd`) bakes a B channel into the existing `shade_mask` texture (now RGB8, 2 m cells, same rect as the splat): the signed distance to the nearest `CityPlan.footprint()` ring, site footprint (`CitySite.placed.footprints`) or site wall segment (`CitySite.walls`, minus half its thickness), 0.5 on the wall line, 0 / 1 at `WALL_RANGE` (3 m) inside / outside. Signed and linear, so bilinear filtering reproduces a straight wall exactly between texels and a centimetre-scale band comes from a coarse mask. `city_ground.gdshader` decodes it from the same `shade_mask` fetch (no extra texture sample; `WALL_RANGE` is mirrored as a shader constant). The unbound default (black) decodes as deep inside a building, where the band is off. Built once at load (about 0.2 s for the whole plan). Nothing is saved.

Verify: `test_shade_mask_blue_is_signed_distance_to_walls` in `tests/godot/test_city_forest_floor.gd` (wall line decodes near 0, 1.5 m out reads outside, 1 m in reads inside). Review capture: `tools/godot_render.sh --resolution 1600x900 res://tools/capture_wall_foot.tscn -- --tag=after` (forge street next to Kalev in the painted-iso camera, plus a close oblique of the nearest sunlit wall). Before/after: [`wall_foot_iso_before.jpg`](../reports/images/city/wall_foot_iso_before.jpg), [`wall_foot_iso_after.jpg`](../reports/images/city/wall_foot_iso_after.jpg), [`wall_foot_close_before.jpg`](../reports/images/city/wall_foot_close_before.jpg), [`wall_foot_close_after.jpg`](../reports/images/city/wall_foot_close_after.jpg).

Limits: the distance is sampled on 2 m texels, so outer corners round off and inner corners between close walls are approximate; the noise hides it at gameplay range. The snow strip has not had a winter review capture yet.

#### Wall side of the foot

Status: implemented (task **R-1931**). Scope: the wall side of the same joint, on every outdoor city wall family: plank and log houses, weathered stone and plaster (`city_weathered_wall.gdshader`, also plinths and the fortification masonry) and the sites' outdoor lime render (`city_limewash.gdshader` with `outdoor`). Out of scope: interiors (they keep the `floor_y` grime band), the site kits' plain `StandardMaterial3D` ashlar and plank surfaces, roofs.

Before, the wall above the plinth (and the plinth itself) was clean right down to a straight bottom edge. Now one shared helper, `wall_foot()` in `scripts/city/city_weathering.gdshaderinc`, lays a band on each wall foot:

- **Measured from the local street:** `above` is the height over the city heightfield (`ground_height` / `ground_rect`, bound by `CityBuildingBuilder.bind_ground`), not one floor height, so the band climbs and drops with a sloped lane.
- **Ragged top:** world-space fbm on `pos.xz` sets the top between 0.15 and 0.7 m (continuous round corners); thin vertical drip runs (5-10 cm wide, up to 0.45 m long, each its own length) reach above it, so no horizontal line remains.
- **Splash mud:** the wall's own albedo desaturated to the warm grey-brown of the ground band's packed earth and darkened 30-60 %, speckled by grit, heaviest at the ground. Relative to the albedo (the plates are dark and lifted by exposure), so it neither vanishes on pale stone nor glows on dark timber.
- **Damp:** a further 18 % darkening and slightly lower roughness (kept mild: a glossy band catches the sky and reads lighter). The band also raises the `wetness` response.
- **Rot:** on plank and log walls (`rot` = 1, set by `CityBuildingBuilder.wall_material`) ragged blackened grey patches from the bottom.
- **Shade:** north faces take a green tinge in the band; moss cushions still come from `shade_growth()` (R-1661).
- **Contact:** the last 6-11 cm are up to 38 % darker.

Strength scales with the wall's `age` (weathered plates more, tarred ones less). Cost: the helper returns early above 1.4 m and on near-horizontal faces, about a dozen value-noise taps below. The older soft rising-damp gradient of the weathered wall stays (at reduced strength); its top is too soft to draw a line. The sites' outdoor render is now `CityBuildingBuilder.outdoor_limewash_material()` (cached with the city materials, so it rebinds with the heightfield) instead of a site-kit copy measured from y = 0.

Verify: `tests/godot/test_city_wall_foot_band.gd` (timber walls rot, stone does not; walls and render are bound to the heightfield; both shaders call the shared helper from local ground). Review capture: `tools/godot_render.sh --resolution 1600x900 res://tools/capture_wall_foot.tscn -- --tag=band_after` now adds `wall_foot_slope_<tag>.jpg`, an oblique of the south-facing wall within 120 m whose ground falls most along it (2.9 m at the forge street). Before/after crops: [`wall_band_close_compare.jpg`](../reports/images/city/wall_band_close_compare.jpg) (forge street plank house and plinth, top before, bottom after), [`wall_band_slope_compare.jpg`](../reports/images/city/wall_band_slope_compare.jpg) (sloped street).

Limits: the band is measured from the street, so a plank wall standing on a tall plinth on a slope gets it only where the plinth is low (physically right: the splash comes from the ground). The site kits' plain ashlar and plank materials have no band yet.

#### 3D wall-foot scatter

Status: implemented (task **R-1932**). Scope: real 3D objects that break the silhouette of the joint along every plan building outside (houses, outbuildings, halls, churches: all `CityPlan.buildings`). Out of scope: site walls and fences, interiors, blueprint maps, collision or interaction (everything is walk-through dressing), new art files (asset freeze P0-040).

The shaders above blend the two surfaces, but grass grew evenly over a yard and stopped short of the walls, so the straight line stayed visible. `CityWallFootScatter` (`scripts/city/city_wall_foot_scatter.gd`, a child of `CityGrass`) lays along each footprint ring:

- **Grass clumps** in a 0-0.6 m strip (7 draws per metre, biased to the wall), unmown and tallest against the plinth (about 1.9x down to 1.15x the clump), with the existing `grass_blade_clump_mesh` and `MapViewMaterials.grass_blades()`.
- **Tall weeds** (3 % of draws, plus up to 32 % within 2 m of a corner and 14 % on north faces): nettle and mugwort (`MapViewPlantMeshes`), burdock rosettes standing in for dock, and flowering or dry burdock by season (`CityForbMeshes`, `CityForbs._kind_for`). They follow the burdock season (`VegetationPhenology.forb_phase`): none from mid-November to mid-April.
- **Fieldstones** (3.5 %), half-buried and tipped; **wood chips** (2 %) in small clusters; one **fallen plank** along a wall on about 22 % of buildings; **mud ridge** pieces pushed against the wall (30 % per 1.3 m, up to 65 % on north faces). These four are code primitives (sphere, box) with white vertex colours and dark sRGB instance tints on one matte `StandardMaterial3D`.

Nothing lands on the door span of the door edge (door half-width + 0.6 m, the edge and `door_t` found as the plinth does) or within 1.3 m of the door point (the steps), on paving or market stone (splat paving > 0.35), on a busy cart road body (`roads.png` body > 0.3 with wear B > 0.45), inside another building, or on a site.

Deterministic per building: the RNG is seeded by the building id and every draw is taken whether the spot survives or not, so the season never moves a stone (`building_buffers(grass, index, day)` is pure data). Nothing is saved. Drawing: one `MultiMesh` per model (ten layers) for every building within 40 m of the player, refilled from cached per-building buffers when the player enters a new 6 m cell; buildings are built nearest first inside the `CityGrass` frame budget, right after the near grass tier (queued behind the forbs it took seconds to appear). Shadows are off. The older `CityWallFoot` weed tufts and climbers stay as they are.

#### Irregular footings

Status: implemented (task **R-1933**). Scope: the footing geometry under every plan building (houses, outbuildings, smoke dwellings, chapels) and the lean-to at Kalev's smithy (`site.kalev_smithy`). Out of scope: other site models (churches, St Michael close, town hall: their kits keep their own plinths), fences and close walls, the 3D map view (`MapViewMeshBuilder`, see **R-1218**), blueprint maps.

Before, the R-1760 plinth was one 0.08 m extrusion of the wall ring with a ruler-straight top and foot, and outbuildings and smoke dwellings had no footing at all. In the forge-street screenshot the "big plank wall going straight into the dirt" was the smithy lean-to: its boarded back wall and pent roof, with the posts and boards pushed straight into the ground. Now `CityFooting.build` (`scripts/city/city_footing.gd`) lays the footing stone by stone along the raw (unfilleted) footprint, by wall family:

| Wall | Footing | Top |
|---|---|---|
| limestone | dressed course, nearly regular blocks (0.5-0.85 m, height jitter +-1.2 cm), quoins above as before | floor + 0.22 m |
| plaster | irregular fieldstone plinth | floor + 0.30 m |
| plank | fieldstone sill under a squared timber sill beam (0.2 m) that laps 0.12 m past outer corners | stones floor + 0.12 m, beam to + 0.32 m |
| log (houses, outbuildings, smoke dwellings) | low fieldstone pads under a round sill log (r 0.12-0.16 m, ten facets, end grain) whose ends cross 0.24 m past outer corners | stones floor + 0.10 m |

Every fieldstone draws its own length (0.28-0.66 m), proud (0.05-0.11 m), belly (up to 3 cm), chamfer and shade; its front corners are nudged by up to 2 cm; its foot is sunk 5-15 cm below the local ground (`plan.ground_height`) with a separate depth at each end, so the terrain cuts the footing along a broken line. Stones under a beam or log only drop below their seat, never rise into it. A dark bed 2 cm proud of the wall shows in the 1.4 cm joints. Where the footing is taller than one course plus 0.2 m (the downhill side of a sloped plot), only the top course (0.22-0.42 m) is laid stone by stone and a coursed masonry face (`MASONRY_PROUD` 0.075 m) runs below it with a top that steps with every stone. Door spans (door width + 0.6 m) stay open for the steps. Stones fully under ground on the uphill side are skipped.

The smithy lean-to: fieldstone pads (`CityFooting.pad`) under the three posts, a fieldstone sill and sill beam on both faces of the boarded back wall (`CityFooting.run`, boards now start on the beam), barge boards under both rakes, a thick board edge at the eave and rafter tails running 0.32 m past it, so from the painted-iso camera the eave is a toothed edge instead of one line.

Walls still sink 0.7 m below the lowest ground under the footprint (`SINK`), so no gap opens on slopes; the footings add their own sunk feet on top of that.

Deterministic: one RNG per building seeded by `hash(id) + 4219` (site pieces by fixed seeds); all draws are taken even for skipped stones. Nothing is saved. Geometry goes into the building `Shell` under the existing `stone` and `timber` keys, so chunk merging, the R-1931 wall-foot band and the cutaway split are unchanged. Canon: timber walls on fieldstone footings or pads with a timber sill are `plausible composite` ([`CANON.md`](../CANON.md), building families).

### How buildings are built

Every house, hall and church is generated at load from its plan record (`plan.json` → `buildings[]`) by `CityBuildingBuilder.build_building`; nothing is hand-modelled. The record carries the footprint, `base_h`/`base_span` (lowest ground under the footprint and the slope across it), `wall_h`, `material`, `roof`, `roof_pitch_deg`, `ridge_angle`, the street `door` (point and outward angle), `kind` (`house`, `church`, `chapel`, `hall`) and `enterable`. Intramural houses also persist `wealth_tier` (`poor`/`craft`/`merchant`/`great`, matching the material band), `age_tier` (`new`/`settled`/`old`) and `upkeep` (`kept`/`worn`), deterministic per building id from a separate seeded RNG (task **R-1412**); nothing renders from them yet.

Chosen by the compiler (`tools/city/build_reval_city_plan.py`):

- Material, roof and height follow the plot's street rank ("wealth"): spine streets (Pikk, Lai, Viru, Vene, the forum) get limestone with tile (7.5–10.5 m eaves); middle ranks limestone or lime-plastered with tile or shingle (5.5–8 m); lanes and suburbs log or plank with thatch or shingle (3.2–4.8 m). Landmarks take their authored roof and nave height.
- The ridge follows one of the footprint's own axes (minimum-area rectangle). Near-square plots turn the gable to the street (Diele house); long plots roof along their length.
- The door sits on the footprint edge nearest the street. Because plot footprints are modern survivals, that edge can face straight into a neighbour; `fix_blocked_doors` then moves the door to the nearest edge with clear ground 1.2 m and 2.5 m outside (69 of 639 doors moved on 2026-10-07), and a house with no clear edge gets no door and is not enterable. Ordinary houses of 22 m² and more with a door, the council hall and the churches and chapels are `enterable`. There is no upper size cap (task **R-1906**): the 60 houses over 520 m² (up to about 1,300 m², the great merchant houses on Pikk and Lai, the granary house `bldg.osm.w200516967` among them) all have a census household and are furnished with stores ([`HOUSEHOLDS.md`](./HOUSEHOLDS.md)). Slivers whose minimum enclosing rectangle is under 2 m wide (three OSM wall-remnant relations, `bldg.osm.r3502076`, `r3502079`, `r3519050`) stay closed. `test_city_plan.gd::test_every_house_with_a_door_is_enterable_from_22_m2_up` checks the rule.
- Kalev's smithy is the landmark site `site.kalev_smithy` (it replaces `bldg.osm.r7239857`); only while no site declares `landmark_id: landmark.kalev_smithy` does the compiler tag the enterable house nearest the overlay's `kalev_smithy.near_m` point.

Built at runtime (`scripts/city/city_building_builder.gd`):

1. **Wall ring.** `wall_ring(b, footprint)` orients the footprint consistently and fillets every corner with a short curve (`soften_ring`, `CORNER_SEGMENTS` = 3). Radius by material (`CORNER_RADIUS`): lime plaster 0.22 m, log 0.16 m, plank 0.14 m, dressed limestone 0.10 m, clamped to 30 % of the shorter adjoining edge. Walls, door leaves (`CityDoors.door_gap`), wall-foot weeds and the logic-plane collision of enterable houses all use this same ring, so the door gap lines up everywhere.
2. **Floor.** `CityPlan.floor_height` = `base_h + base_span + 0.12`: the floor is level with the highest ground under the house. Where the street is lower in front of the door, limestone steps (`_door_steps`) climb to the threshold. Churches, chapels and the council hall stand on a **levelled terrace**: the compiler (`terrace_landmarks`) cuts or fills the ground under the footprint to the street level 2 m outside the door and blends it back into the slope over 8 m, so their doors open at street level (before this, St Mary's door stood 5.2 m above Kiriku plats). Houses whose footprint spans 0.3-3 wu of height get the same cut with a 3 m blend (`HOUSE_TERRACE_*`; all levels are read before any plot is cut, then footprints are re-cut with a 2.2 m apron because the heightfield cell is 2 wu); bigger spans are merged OSM polygons and keep the runtime steps. Median door rise fell from 0.38 m to 0.13 m. Non-enterable houses have no interior floor, so `floor_height` puts their threshold at the street level in front of the door instead of the highest footprint ground.
   **High doors (task R-2197).** Two compiler rules keep doors near street level. The footprint re-cut runs from the highest plot level down, so a later (lower) cut can only lower a corner it shares with a higher neighbour. Before, a row of houses down a slope took the uphill neighbour's level at the shared wall, which left 1.5-2.8 m door steps. `lower_high_doors` then moves the door of an enterable building the plot terrace leaves alone (spans over 3 wu) when it stands more than 1 wu above the ground 1 m out (`DOOR_HIGH_RISE_WU`). The new door goes to the clear edge (same probes as `fix_blocked_doors`) with the least rise, if that saves at least 0.5 wu. A house built into a slope is entered from the upper side, and its low side shows the tall footing as a cellar plinth. The census and `citizens.json` follow the moved doors. Enterable doors more than 1 wu up: 38 before, 3 after (the worst went from 5.7 to 2.0 wu).
   **Door stairs (`CityDoorSteps`, task R-2197).** One deterministic layout per door (`CityPlan.door_steps(index)`) is drawn by the builder, stands actors on its treads in `CityPlan.walk_height` (tread top, never below the ground; before, the stair was mesh only and Kalev sank into it), and adds its rails and parapets to the logic collision (`CityCollisionBuilder`). The kind follows the rise (riser at most 0.18 m, up to 14 treads, treads 0.30-0.36 m by seed) and the house:
   - `slab`: one riser, a single threshold stone (a log step on log and plank houses);
   - `straight`: limestone flight straight out from the door;
   - `splayed`: limestone flight whose lower treads widen by up to 0.1 m a side, on limestone houses and houses of 65 m² or more (two in three by seed), never past the wall's corners;
   - `timber`: plank treads on two stringers for log, plank and smoke-dwelling houses, with a post-and-rail on both sides above 0.8 m;
   - `side`: above 1 m of rise, a 1.15 m landing at the door and a flight down along the wall. Treads step down until the ground meets them, so on a slope the shorter run, towards the rising ground, wins. The open side gets a 0.55 m limestone parapet, or a 0.9 m timber rail with the landing on two posts. It is used only when the wall has room and the landing and flight stand on open ground (`CityPlan._open_for_steps`: no other building or site); otherwise the door gets a straight or timber flight.
   Treads the ground already reaches (a street climbing away from the door) are left out. The footing stays open over the whole stair span (`layout["span"]`). City counts: 352 slab, 100 timber, 11 splayed, 3 straight, 4 side, 227 doors with no step. Test: `tests/godot/test_city_door_steps.gd`. Review plates: `tools/godot_render.sh --resolution 1600x900 res://tools/capture_door_steps.tscn -- --tag=<t>` (writes `docs/reports/images/city/door_steps_<kind>_<t>.jpg`). Limits: rails are straight lines only (no turned balusters), there are no covered porches or cellar doors on the low side, and NPCs stand at their door point without climbing. Plates: St Catherine's house with its moved door on a timber side flight ([`door_steps_catherine_0_after.jpg`](../reports/images/city/door_steps_catherine_0_after.jpg), [`door_steps_catherine_1_after.jpg`](../reports/images/city/door_steps_catherine_1_after.jpg)), stone side flight ([`door_steps_side_stone_after.jpg`](../reports/images/city/door_steps_side_stone_after.jpg)), splayed ([`door_steps_splayed_stone_after.jpg`](../reports/images/city/door_steps_splayed_stone_after.jpg)), timber ([`door_steps_timber_wood_after.jpg`](../reports/images/city/door_steps_timber_wood_after.jpg)).
3. **Footing.** `CityFooting.build` lays an irregular footing under every wall by family: dressed limestone course, fieldstone plinth, fieldstone sill with a timber sill beam (plank), or fieldstone pads under a round sill log (log, outbuildings and smoke dwellings included); see [Irregular footings](#irregular-footings) (**R-1933**). Limestone houses with openings keep their dressed quoins (`HouseDetails.quoins`, R-1760).
4. **Walls.** One strip per ring edge from 0.7 m below ground (`SINK`, hides slope gaps) to the eave, with gable infill up to the roof line where an edge crosses the ridge. The door edge leaves a 1.5 × 2.55 m gap centred on the door (clamped to 20–80 % of the edge). Houses get windows every ~3.4 m on edges of 2.6 m or more; churches, chapels and the hall get tall lancets instead.
5. **Roof.** Tile roofs use the procedural monk-and-nun tile shader (`city_tile_roof.gdshader`); shingle and thatch keep their texture plates. Photographic moss and lichen reuse is implemented (**R-1684**): `city_growth_plates.gdshaderinc` samples the existing wall-growth plates against the unchanged R-1465 north-pitch masks, tile channels/course overlaps and cap-lichen coverage; material moss strengths remain shingle 0.5, tile 0.3, thatch 0.2. Cushion height-alpha controls ragged edges, moss normal relief blends into roof relief, and dry roughness blends toward 0.97 (lichen 0.95). Missing plates leave the roof clean. R-1747 replaced the domed plate on roofs with a feather-moss and *Grimmia* mix and tints it by season ([moss species and season](./LIVING_VEGETATION.md#moss-lichen-and-the-forest-floor-r-1661)). No geometry, inputs or saved state change. [Runtime bindings, capture commands and limits](./LIVING_VEGETATION.md#moss-lichen-and-the-forest-floor-r-1661); roof evidence: [tile](../reports/images/vegetation/r1684_roof_tile.jpg), [shingle](../reports/images/vegetation/r1684_roof_shingle.jpg). A gable split along the ridge (`roof_frame`): pitch 52° thatch, 48° shingle, 50° tile, rise capped at 10.5 m, 0.38 m overhang all round. The cover has a thickness (`ROOF_COVER`: thatch 0.36 m, tile 0.13 m, shingle 0.11 m) that ends in a rounded roll at eaves and verges, and a half-round ridge cap runs the length of the ridge (`RIDGE_RADIUS`: bulky bound ridge on thatch, ridge tiles on tile, a ridge roll on shingle). Roll and cap carry roof UVs so they take the roof texture.
6. **Chimney.** Every house with a door (its hooded hearth needs a flue) and 80 % of doorless houses (`CHIMNEY_SHARE`) get a stone stack, thatched roofs included. It stands on the inner face of the wall that is highest under the roof and not the door's - the gable, where the ridge crosses it - at least 3 m from the door and with room for the hearth (`spot` in `scripts/city/city_chimney_builder.gd`), and rises 0.6–1.1 m above the ridge (`CHIMNEY_SIZE` 0.75 m wide, flush with the wall). In an enterable house a chimney breast of the same stone runs from behind the hearth and from the top of its brick flue up into the stack, with a section cap where the cutaway cuts it; windows behind the breast are dropped. The stack top (over the hearth's flue) is recorded for smoke and for `HouseholdLayout`, which stands the hearth under it ([`HOUSEHOLDS.md`](./HOUSEHOLDS.md)). The flue mouth (`flue_mouth`) is not a flat black square: a rounded, lumpy hole (superellipse wobbled by low harmonics, seeded from its position so the house RNG is untouched) in a stone rim with a slight mortar crown, soot shading from the rim to the lip by vertex colour, and the flue modelled 0.55 m down into the dark; the cutaway section cap uses the same hole without the crown.
7. **Inside or cap.** Enterable buildings get an interior: inner walls inset by the wall thickness (limestone 0.62 m, timber 0.32 m) with a door reveal, a plank floor (boards about 0.2 m wide) and a ceiling at the eave. Inner walls are lime-washed (`city_limewash.gdshader`, darkened for indoors). **Cutaway:** the whole shell is split at `CUT_HEIGHT` (2.2 m above the floor) with `Shell.split_at`, which clips triangles at the plane. Everything above the cut (upper walls, gables, windows above head height) joins the roof node with the ceiling, and the wall stubs get a stone section cap (`wall_top_cap`, open at the door). While Kalev is inside, the roof node leaves the camera's view, so every camera sees a room cut at head height instead of tall walls and gables. It keeps casting its shadow (`CityWorld3D.set_cutaway` switches it to shadows-only), so the sun does not pour in through the lifted roof ([before / after](../reports/images/city/cutaway_roof_shadow_before_after.jpg)). Other buildings get a flat cap under the roof.
8. **Materials.** Walls and roofs use the weathered shaders (`city_weathered_wall.gdshader`, `city_weathered_roof.gdshader`, shared `city_weathering.gdshaderinc`): rising damp read from the ground heightfield, run-off streaks, lichen on walls, moss on roofs (shingle most, tile less, thatch least) and rain wetness from the weather.
9. **Batching.** Walls and the roofs of non-enterable buildings are merged per 96 m chunk and per material; each enterable building's roof (with its ceiling and chimney) is its own node so it can be hidden.

Around the buildings: `CityDoors` hangs [wealth- and entrance-role-specific leaves](./CITY_DOORS.md) (R-1692: physical boards, separate ironwork, rings and structural rear ledges; explicit council/church profiles). Farmstead outbuildings (`kind: outbuilding`: pigsty, sheep shed, byre, store, salt/smoke/cargo shed) get a smaller opening from `CityBuildingBuilder.door_size` (e.g. sty 0.62 x 0.9 m, byre 1.0 x 1.65 m, always 0.2 m under the eave; the wall gap, steps and collision follow it) and a `rough` leaf: bare weathered or tarred boards, two wooden ledges, a wooden latch bar, no ironwork or ring pull (checked by `test_outbuilding_doors_are_small_and_under_the_eave`); `CityWallFoot` scatters weed tufts along the wall foot (denser at corners, none in doorways) and hop/ivy climbers on about 9 % of houses; the splat paints trampled earth round each house and a muddy drip line at the wall foot; `CityChimneySmoke` streams smoke plumes (up to 28 within 180 m) over the lit stacks (about a third, with the shared day/night hearth schedule). Each puff (`scripts/map/view3d/chimney_smoke.gdshader`) fakes a small volume, since Mobile has no fog volumes: a sphere density carved by animated 3D noise that frays with age, lit as a soft sphere with noise relief (sunlit and shaded lobes), wrapped diffuse plus forward scattering, darker thick cores, and a soft-particle depth fade where it meets roofs; curl-noise turbulence rolls the column into eddies after it leaves the flue, and a plume is prewarmed for a whole lifetime so it streams in as a full trail. ### Burgher strip plots (data, not yet rendered)

WB-13c (task **R-1413**, parent **R-985**, contract [`WB-13_burgher_plot_prefab.md`](../tasks/world/WB-13_burgher_plot_prefab.md)): `plan.json` → `plots[]` gives ordinary intramural houses the strip plot behind them (H04: frontage commonly 7-11 m, up to 100 m deep). One record per plot: `{id: "plot.<building id without bldg.>", building, street_id, wealth_tier, frontage_m, depth_m, polygon, yard}`, ordered by id. Nothing renders from them yet; service ranges, yard items and ground follow in WB-13e..n. Walls and gates (WB-13d, task **R-1414**) are described below.

How `burgher_plots` in `tools/city/build_reval_city_plan.py` derives a plot (all geometry in metres, exact polygon clipping, no RNG):

- **Eligible:** a non-landmark `house` with `wealth_tier` (inside the circuit or on Toompea), a door and a `street_id`. Houses are taken in building-id order and each placed plot becomes an obstacle for the next, so the result never depends on input order.
- **Frame:** the strip runs perpendicular to the house's street face (the door edge), away from the street. The front line is the house front, pushed back where the house's own street corridor (street half width + 0.25 m) reaches over it.
- **Width:** the free interval along the street around the door, narrowed by anything beside the house; then the house frontage is widened toward the tier target (poor 7.5, craft 8.5, merchant 9.5, great 10.5 m; class D inside H04) where that interval is free. Row houses share party walls and their simplified OSM rings overlap by slivers, so a house may overhang its plot at a party wall. A house wider than 11 m (often a merged modern footprint) keeps its own frontage.
- **Depth:** stops at the first of another plot, a foreign building, a street corridor, the curtain (1.5 m clear), the klint edge (0.5 m), the Toompea wall, the castle, the forum, a site footprint or reserve, or 100 m. A strip under 3 m wide or 2 m deep is no plot.
- **Yard:** the rear part of the plot behind the house's back line (a convex quad), or `[]` when under 1 m deep. The side strips beside the house stay plot but not yard, so later steps place items and walls on a convex shape without polygon booleans.

Plot walls and gates (WB-13d, task **R-1414**): every plot with a yard also carries `wall: {kind, height_m, runs, gate, access, shared_with}` (`null` without a yard). `plot_walls` in `tools/city/build_reval_city_plan.py` samples the yard boundary every 0.1 m: both side lines, the back line, and the part of the front line beside the house (the house's own back wall encloses behind it). A sample is dropped within 0.3 m of any foreign house or site footprint (their walls and eaves enclose there, and a closer wall would z-fight a roof) and where a lower-id plot already walls the same boundary (one shared wall owned by the lower id; both records list the other in `shared_with`). `runs` are polylines in world units, not closed, and leave the gate opening empty. Kind follows the tier ladder (poor wattle; craft wattle or plank; merchant plank or stone; great stone, picked by a hash of the plot id) with height 1.5 / 1.8 / 2.0 m (great 2.2 m). `gate: {at, angle, width, via, ...}`: `angle` is the outward normal in radians, `width` 1.4 / 1.6 / 2.0 / 2.4 m by tier (poor to great; merchant and great are cart gates). `via: "street"` sits on a wall sample within 0.5 m of a street corridor (`street_dist_m`); `via: "passage"` crosses the side strip beside the house when that strip opens on a street (`mouth` is the street end). Without either, `gate` is `null` and `access: "through_house"`: the yard is entered through the house (Diele drive-through, H04). 140 of 152 yards are `through_house` on 2026-10-17 because most yards lie behind other plots, not on a street; that share is class D and will drop only with the UF-04 back lanes. Verify: `python3 -m unittest tests.python.test_build_reval_city_plan`.

Numbers (2026-10-17): 419 plots for 517 eligible houses; 152 with a rear yard. Frontage median 7.65 m for houses up to 11 m wide (2 m-bin histogram printed by the test); 98 houses get no plot because neighbours or a street close round them. `CityPlan.plots` and `CityPlan.plot_at(world_xz)` (index or -1, bucketed like `building_at`) expose them at runtime. ## Music

Music follows where Kalev is, not a scene path (`reval_city.tscn` has an empty entry in `MusicDirector.SCENE_THEME_ROUTES`). Every 0.25 s the scene asks `CityMusicZones.update(xz, district_id)` and hands the result to `MusicDirector.set_zone_theme_override()` (or clears it). The most specific zone around Kalev wins (smallest radius): the council hall beats the forum, the forum beats the wider quarter. A theme already playing holds on for 1.25 x its radius. Where no zone reaches, the plan district decides (`CityPlan.district_id_at`); fields and far suburbs have no theme and the music fades out. Themes are the existing folders under `music/` (`center`, `raekoda`, `holy_spirit`, `north`, `oleviste`, `monastery`, `south`, `viru`, `forge`, `garden`, `harbor`, `toompea`); each theme keeps its own playlist, so nothing is mixed into one random list.

**R-1576** restored the whole `archive/music/` tree to its original `music/` paths. Numbered alternate takes now join their folder's shuffled playlist, and new zones route the restored location sets: `zone.st_mary` (St Mary's cathedral, `st_mary` theme, day and night folders under `music/cathedral_of_saint_mary/`), `zone.dome_school` (cathedral school, `dome_school`, day and night under `music/dome_school/`; placed just north of the church as a plausible composite, since the plan has no school building), and `zone.vene_tavern` (`tavern`, `music/revel_east/tavern/`, on the Vene malt house POI). The Viru quarter (`zone.viru`, `district.viru`) plays the new `viru` theme, which scans all of `music/revel_east/`; the slice `town` theme keeps its two hard-coded tracks for the P2-014 budget. At night the smithy (`forge`) adds The Smith's Song takes to Fireside Tale. The `apothecary` theme (`music/revel_center/apotheca/`) is registered for map fade volumes only: the council apothecary is first recorded in 1422, so it has no 1343 city zone.

`MusicDirector` crossfades between themes (3 s, a second `OutgoingThemePlayer`), also on district scene changes. The scene's day clock is pushed to `MusicDirector.set_cycle_progress()` so night playlists and the night ducking apply.

Add or move a zone by editing `CityMusicZones.ZONES` (centre in world units, radius, theme id). Tests: `--filter=test_city_music_zones`.

- No quests, dialogue, interaction anchors or saves in the city scene; the Act 1 cycles still run on `lower_town_slice` and the forge. City NPCs do not talk, react or fight. **The forge's own gameplay (Mart's conversation, the anvil spearhead pickup, commission ledger) is not yet in the city smithy (`site.kalev_smithy`); it needs a task to mount those controllers in the city.** The bathkeeper household `hh.lt.osm_r7239857` of the replaced smithy plot is retired from the census (R-1934, [displaced households](../CITIZENS/CENSUS.md#households-displaced-by-landmark-sites)).
- The old district scenes and their `.rrmap` maps are still in the repository and the transition manifest (redirected, not deleted).
- Buildings are procedural shells from plot footprints: no kit GLB models, no upper floors. Lived-in houses near Kalev are furnished and split into rooms by [`HOUSEHOLDS.md`](./HOUSEHOLDS.md); churches and halls stay empty. Church interiors are an empty nave; the castle is a massing model. Bespoke, navigable landmark sites replace the generic shells one site at a time ([Landmark sites](./CITY_LANDMARK_SITES.md), ADR 0032): Raekoja plats is done; Kiriku plats and St Mary's, the parish churches, the castle and the gates are planned.
- Corners are filleted at plan level only: the eave line, gable verges and window reveals are still straight-edged, and walls stay perfectly plumb (no lean or bulge).
- Some plan doors open almost straight onto a neighbour's wall (plots from modern footprints); the door plates skip them.
- Plot footprints are modern survivals; individual houses are a plausible composite.
- Ruts, prints and mud have real geometry only inside the 12.4 wu trail relief mesh round Kalev; beyond it they are shader relief on a 2 wu mesh and a low grazing camera sees them flat. Water in a displaced print or rut follows the pit floor rather than lying level. Prints are lost beyond the 51 wu trail window and are not saved. The mud-slowing mechanic of the district maps is not wired to the city ground. Citizens and animals leave tracks only near Kalev. Animal prints are a walking gait only: no trot, gallop or running bounds, and a dog's hind paw always lands in its fore print. Seen from above, prints in summer mud read much fainter than in snow (the ground shader's relief shading, same as for boot prints). No cart rolls through the city yet (the cart props are static): `track_cart` is wired through the `city_wheeled` group and is exercised by tests and the capture tool only. Dust puffs are Kalev's alone. Open-country relief is generic noise, not the real 1343 terrain: only the DEM trend is sourced.
- Life layers (wells, flows, gutters) are data and review-map markers; rain does not show water running in the gutters.
- Distant buildings beyond 1600 units, trees beyond 260 units, shrubs beyond 120 units, weeds beyond 90 units and grass beyond ~50 units are culled; there is no impostor skyline.
- Ships have no collision and cannot be boarded.
- Music zones are hand-placed circles around plan landmarks, not authored per building; interiors other than the council hall share their quarter's theme. Every theme plays its whole folder shuffled; there are no battle or stinger cues in this scene.
- Doors have no sound and no lock state; non-enterable houses keep theirs shut.

### Tall grass, wading drag and trail

Status: implemented ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-tall-grass-height-and-wading-drag.md)). Grass height follows use: `CityGrass.wildness_at` (distance from roads and house walls) scales clump height from 0.55 beside a road or wall to 2.7 (about waist high) in open meadow. Standing grass of any height slows the player to 85% (`CityGrass.walk_drag_at`, see **Ground speed** below; ADR 0039's half speed in tall grass was retired by R-1907). The grass shader parts and lays blades over in proportion to their own height around the walker and around the last 8 footfalls (`VegetationInteractionBuffer`, 4 s spring-back), so a lane stays open behind. Tests: `test_city_grass_height`, `test_vegetation_interaction_buffer`, `test_grass_interaction`. Review plates: `tools/godot_render.sh --script tools/capture_tall_grass.gd` writes `build/grass/tall_{behind,side}_<tag>.png`. Limits: NPCs and animals neither part the grass nor slow down; the trail is session-only.

### Ground speed

Status: implemented (task **R-1907**). Walking and running speed is scaled by the ground under the player, as a fraction of the speed on paving. One table, `scripts/player/surface_speed.gd` (`SurfaceSpeed`, preloaded), holds every number; tune there.

| Ground | Speed | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Cobbles, flagstones, floors, bridge decks, beach sand | 100% | default |
| Dry packed earth, cart roads, fields, dry mud | 95% | `CitySurfaceSampler.soft_ground_drag` |
| Grass (short turf or meadow) | 85% | `CityGrass.walk_drag_at` |
| Soaked earth and mud | 60% | `SurfaceSpeed.soft_ground(wetness)`, wetness = the ground trail's rain-and-puddle wetness (`CityMapView.soft_ground_drag_at`) |
| Snow, just lying (over 6 cm) to half a metre and deeper | 80% to 30% | `CitySnow.walk_drag(depth)` |
| Water, ankle (0.12) to chest (1.25 units) deep | 80% to 15% of the run | `PlayerSwimState` wading; swimming deeper is 32% |

Earth and grass blend by their painted share. `reval_city.gd` feeds `Player.set_ground_drag_provider` with the slowest of grass, soft ground and snow; it applies on dry land only, while wading and swimming use their own fractions. Encumbrance and slope multiply on top. District `MapDefinition` maps use the same mud range (`MapTerrainMovement.mud_speed_multiplier`) and `grass.tall` zones at 85%; plain grass terrain there stays at 100%. Nothing is saved. Tests: `test_surface_speed`, `test_city_seasons`, `test_city_grass_height`, `test_player_swim_state`, `test_mud_weather`. Limits: NPCs and animals ignore ground speed; puddles shallower than ankle depth do not slow.

### Road surface and verge grass

Meadow turf is matte and carries mid-scale relief (`grass_height` in `city_ground.gdshader`): clumps of ~2 m, ridged tufts of ~0.7 m and blade lumps drive cavity shading (`grass_relief_shade`) and a tussock normal (`grass_relief_normal`, faded with distance like the other micro relief) on pure turf only, and turf gets `grass_specular` 0.04 with roughness pushed to 1, so low sun no longer glints off the plate as a waxy sheet (earth, road, sand and paving keep their gloss). Review plate: `tools/godot_render.sh --script tools/capture_meadow_sheen.gd -- --tag=<t>` writes `build/grass/meadow_{high,low}_<t>.png`. Still shader-only relief: silhouettes come from the blade meshes of `CityGrass`.

Cart roads stay bare (`CityGrass.road_clearance`, from `roads.png`: body times traffic wear): no blade clumps or accent plants stand on the road body, tufts that do survive on a quiet edge are scaled to 30 %, and the ground shader shows only a faint green fuzz of sprouts on lightly used margins. Grass stands on the verge beside the road. Ruts are deeper (`road_profile`) and fill with water in patches rather than as continuous rails. Review plates: `tools/godot_render.sh --script tools/capture_city_mud.gd -- --tag=<t> [--wet=0.9]` writes `build/mud/{eye,game,low,verge,far}_<t>.png`. Prints and ruts have a silhouette only inside the trail relief mesh (see "Ground relief, roads and prints"), and tracks of NPCs, hooves and carts are only drawn within 20 wu of Kalev.

#### Klint cliffs and sand bluffs

The Toompea klint and steep banks used to project the masonry plate (`stone_albedo.png`) triplanar, which read as a brick wall. `city_ground.gdshader` now draws slopes steeper than about 30 degrees (`rock`, onset slope 0.24..0.4) procedurally in world space: `cliff_height()` gives limestone beds of varying hardness (hard beds jut, soft ones recess, undulating via a domain warp) cut by vertical joints and spalled blocks; `bluff_height()` gives sand bluffs with gullies and slumped terraces on low banks (`bluff_share()`: below about 11 wu, soft beds, and the sand splat). Colour varies per bed (cream, ochre, grey), with cavity shade, rain streaks and sparse moss. The relief is a normal-map gradient (finite differences projected on the face tangent plane), faded with distance; the surface is matte. Steep grassy banks (`bank`) take the bluff relief too. Geometry is still the smooth heightfield, so silhouettes are unchanged. Review plates: `tools/godot_render.sh --script tools/capture_reval_city.gd -- --only=city_klint_0,city_klint_1,city_klint_2` (eye outside the Toompea edge looking back at it). Known limits: no real overhangs, no talus apron at the foot, the shore shingle band draws procedural stones up close and the natural `shore_shingle` plate (`shingle_albedo`) only at distance ([CITY_SEA.md](./CITY_SEA.md#shingle-and-shore-stones-r-1606)), and the seabed bedrock is procedural; the masonry `stone_albedo.png` plate is no longer bound to the city ground.

### Renderer parity of plates (task R-1535)

Status: implemented (task **R-1535**). Scope: the city ground (`city_ground.gdshader`, its grass array in `city_grass_ground.gdshaderinc`) and the weathered roofs (`city_weathered_roof.gdshader`) look the same on Mobile and Forward+ (Metal) as on the shipping GL Compatibility renderer. Out of scope: the global lighting/grade difference between renderers (interiors brighter, RD a little darker outdoors; ADR 0043 R4) and other `source_color` materials (walls, StandardMaterial3D plates).

Why: GL Compatibility (Godot 4.7, macOS) samples `source_color` textures without the sRGB decode, and the city's procedural tones (fieldstone paving, joint earth, roof moss and lichen, soil) were tuned against those raw plate values. The RenderingDevice renderers decode, so the plates went about 2.5x darker beside the unchanged constants: pale paving without its earth and moss patches, a pale blue-white cart road on the moat bank, and camouflage moss blotches on the roofs. `splat` and `roads` are data masks and carry no colour hint at all (a `source_color` splat zeroed the paving on RD).

How: `scripts/city/city_plate_hint.gdshaderinc` defines `CITY_PLATE` (expands to `source_color,` on Compatibility, to nothing on RD) for the plate samplers `earth_albedo`, `sand_albedo`, `shingle_albedo`, `cracked_albedo`, `grass_ground_albedo` and the roof's `albedo_tex` (`CITY_ROOF_PLATE` in `city_weathering.gdshaderinc`; walls keep `source_color`), and `city_albedo()`, which returns the finished albedo as is on Compatibility and sRGB-decodes it on RD. Both branch on `CURRENT_RENDERER` at compile time, so the shipping shader is unchanged. A new city shader that mixes a plate with constant colours includes the same file. Verify per renderer:

```bash
tools/godot_render.sh --rendering-method <gl_compatibility|mobile|forward_plus> --rendering-driver <opengl3|metal> \
  --script tools/capture_reval_city.gd -- --out=res://build/rd_fix/<renderer> --only=forum,from_toompea_over_roofs
```

Evidence (left to right Compatibility, Mobile, Forward+): [`sheet_town_forum_day_r1535.jpg`](../reports/images/hdr_output_spike/sheet_town_forum_day_r1535.jpg), [`sheet_town_roofs_day_r1535.jpg`](../reports/images/hdr_output_spike/sheet_town_roofs_day_r1535.jpg); measurement in the [HDR output spike report](../reports/hdr_output_spike_2026-10-09.md). Limits: parity is of tone balance, not pixels: GL lights the raw-space albedo while RD lights its decode, so RD stays a little darker and more saturated; the remaining lighting difference is R4.


## Rain under roofs (task R-1539)

Status: implemented (task **R-1539**). Scope: the seamless city only.

While Kalev stands under a roof (a landmark site room or a building interior,
`CityPlan.is_under_roof`), `reval_city._update_interior` reports
`CityWorld3D.set_enclosed_interior` every frame from the same building and site-room
lookup that drives the cutaway. That gates the shared `SkyWeather3D.rain_suppressed`: the falling-rain
particle emitter and the exterior rain ambience stop, the muffled roof-drum bed
takes over, and `MapViewLighting.apply_cycle_progress` receives `enclosed_interior`
(so there are no sky reflections, morning mist, or rain haze inside). The roof
cutaway is a camera trick and does not change the answer.

Verify: `godot --headless --path . --script tools/run_godot_tests.gd`
(`tests/godot/test_city_interior_weather.gd`). Out of scope: per-location rain
suppression for map-hosted layouts (deferred seam work, R-980).
