# HUD, menus, journal, and maps

Status: implemented. Scope: every player-visible screen outside dialogue and forge commissions: main menu, quick-access bar, HUD elements, journal, Hingepuu reflection, world map and fast travel, interaction prompts, cursor. Controller/presentation ownership rules are in [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](../ARCHITECTURE.md#ui-and-interaction-ownership); bindings are in [`CONTROLS.md`](../CONTROLS.md).

## Main menu

Status: implemented (task **R-1799**, restyle and Continue). `scenes/menu/main_menu.tscn` is the project's main scene: the looping intro video, with the logo, a `REVAL  /  1343` subtitle and one left-aligned column of entries over a left-edge shade (`MenuShade`, a gradient, no new texture). The column uses the Escape-menu skin from `scripts/ui/pause_menu_style.gd`: dark plates, 18 px text, bronze focus bar.

| Entry (node) | Script | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Continue (`Continue label`) | `scenes/menu/continue_label.gd` | First entry and initial focus when a save exists; loads the newest slot from `SessionState.list_saves()` and goes to its recorded scene and spawn. Hidden without saves |
| New game (`Start label`) | `scenes/intro/start_label.gd` | New game into the almshouse prologue; initial focus when there is no save |
| Load (`Load label`) | `scenes/menu/load_label.gd`, `save_list_overlay.gd` | Hidden when no save exists; lists slots with phase, location, and time ([`STATE_AND_SAVES.md`](./STATE_AND_SAVES.md#saves)) |
| Credits (`Credits label`) | `credits_label.gd`, `credits_screen.gd` | Renders `CREDITS.md`, generated by `tools/generate_credits.py` from the audio manifests ([`music/README.md`](../../music/README.md)) |
| Assets library (`Assets library label`) | `assets_library_label.gd`, `assets_library.gd` | Browse every imported GLB under `res://assets`, orbit it, play its clips (`AssetLibraryCatalog`). Authoring aid |
| Exit (`Exit label`) | `exit_label.gd` | Quit |

- **Entries** extend `scenes/menu/main_menu_entry.gd` (a `RichTextLabel`): it applies the plate, focus bar (`UiFocusTheme`, so enhanced focus contrast applies) and text colours, and calls the subclass `activate()` on a left click or `ui_accept`. Entries stay `RichTextLabel`s driven by `gui_input` because the packaged walkthrough, platform smoke and capture hosts press Start by emitting `gui_input` with a click.
- **Input**: hovering an entry moves focus to it, so mouse, keyboard and gamepad share one highlight (brighter plate and text). Up/Down (arrows or D-pad) walk a wrapping focus ring; Enter or the gamepad confirm button activates.
- **Layout and focus ring**: `scenes/menu/main_menu.gd` stacks the visible entries (hidden Continue/Load leave no gap) in a 320 px column at x 120 of the 1920x1080 design space and wires `focus_neighbor_top/bottom` between them. Node names are stable; tests and tools address them as root children.
- **Verify**: `--filter=test_main_menu_load,test_asset_library,test_input_bindings`; capture with `tools/godot_render.sh res://tools/capture_hud_gameplay_host.tscn`.

## In-game quick-access bar

`QuickAccessMenu` (`scripts/ui/quick_access_menu.gd`) owns the handlers for every player-visible action. Its bottom-right bar is hidden for immersion; the buttons stay as the single handler route that the Escape menu and the hotkeys call:

**Inventory [I]**, **Journal [J]**, **Reflect** (Hingepuu), **Map [M]**, **Camera [C]** (third-person → first-person → top-down), **Controls [K]**, **Iron** (forge technique toggle), **Magic [R]** (spell cookbook), **Save game**, **Debug**.

## Escape menu

Status: implemented (task **R-1698**). Esc opens `GameSettingsOverlay` (`scripts/ui/game_settings_overlay.gd`, opened by `GameSettingsController`) as a full-screen two-column menu inspired by the Witcher 3 pause screen, with original art only:

- **Left column** (`SecondaryActions`, about 300 px): game title, then **Resume** (initial focus), Save game, Inventory, Journal, Map, Spellforge, Reflect, Iron technique, Change view, Debug. Each action closes the menu and calls the matching `QuickAccessMenu` handler.
- **Right panel** (`SettingsPanel`): a scrolling settings page (graphics, audio, accessibility, Remap controls) as listed in [`SETTINGS_AND_ACCESSIBILITY.md`](./SETTINGS_AND_ACCESSIBILITY.md). An empty right margin (stretch ratio 0.35) keeps values close to their labels on wide screens.
- **Style**: `scripts/ui/pause_menu_style.gd` builds one charcoal and bronze `Theme` (no new textures or fonts). Focus borders follow `UiFocusTheme`, so the enhanced focus contrast setting still applies; the theme is rebuilt on open and when that setting changes.
- **Input**: keyboard and gamepad move focus up and down each column; Right on Resume jumps to the first setting, Left on it jumps back. Esc or Resume closes the menu.

## World map and travel

`WorldMapController` (on every playable player host) opens `WorldMapOverlay`, which has three views:

- **Local**: the full-screen version of the minimap (`WorldMapLocalView`).
- **Fast travel**: the Reval district graph built from the active transition manifest (`WorldMapGraph`, `WorldMapFastTravelView`). Click or focus a district to travel through `DoorNavigator`.
- **Global**: a full-screen, text-free Estonia basemap (`WorldMapGlobalView`, `GlobalMapCatalog`), kept off the district graph. Every campaign location is a round marker, placed from its real latitude/longitude fitted to the painted coastline, including places not built yet (dim hollow rings that never travel; none right now, all are debug greyboxes). The name and a one-line blurb appear only while a marker is hovered or focused. No static roads are drawn between markers; the catalog's road graph only decides which markers are travelable. Choosing a destination walks a small hooded traveler along a gently bowed path with a gold trail (1.4-4 s) before `DoorNavigator` travel starts; headless runs skip the walk. The X button, `M` or Escape close the map. Capture: [global map](../reports/images/world_map/global_map_geo_positions.png). Verify: `--filter=test_world_map_overlay` and `--filter=test_global_map_catalog`.

Doors and transitions: `DoorNavigator` autoload and `content/transitions/active_destinations.json` ([`MAP_AUTHORING.md`](../MAP_AUTHORING.md)).

**Current approach.** The HUD is minimal and diegetic-leaning: the bronze-rimmed minimap with location lines (top right), the quest-tool pouch (top left), the vitals (bottom left), interaction glyphs in the world, and no permanent action bar. Every command lives in the Escape menu or on a hotkey. All UI is built in code (no `.tscn` per screen) with per-screen colour overrides; the minimap rim and the vitals bar frame are the only painted UI assets.

**What works.** The minimap: painted rim, rotating map, clear location hierarchy. The empty playfield. One handler route per action (`QuickAccessMenu`), shared by hotkeys, menu and tests.

**Problems found.**

1. Visual language is split. Minimap and vitals are bronze and painted; the journal, inventory, spellforge and controls overlays still use default Godot panels with ad-hoc colours. `pause_menu_style.gd` should become the shared theme for all overlays.
2. ~~The main menu is a centred stack of yellow (`#FAD606`) `RichTextLabel`s over the intro video with no hover or focus state beyond the cursor.~~ Done in **R-1799**: left-aligned Escape-menu list, bronze focus bar, hover = focus, Continue (latest save) first.
3. No display typeface. Everything uses Noto Sans; titles would read better in a licensed serif or blackletter display face (needs an asset task and a `SOURCES.csv` row).
4. The Escape menu is one long settings page. Split it into tabs (Game / Graphics / Audio / Accessibility / Controls) once there are more than about 20 rows, and move Debug out of the player list in release builds.
5. ~~The vitals frames are drawn with flat polygons.~~ Done in **R-1798**: painted bronze frame, delayed damage trail and idle fade.
6. Willpower (mana orb) appears only in the spirit world and sits in the same corner; when it gets a world presence it should join the vitals block instead of stacking above it.

## Limits

- `PlayerVitalsHud` (layer 21) stays visible under dialogue (`DialogueUi`, layer 45); cutscenes (95) and the spirit arena (80) cover it. It fades out after 4 s at full health and stamina (R-1798); stamina regeneration therefore keeps it visible until full. The trail is health-only; stamina has none.

- `scenes/elements/gameplay_help_hud.tscn` and `scripts/ui/gameplay_help_hud.gd` are deprecated and mounted nowhere. Removal is tracked in the [code-health audit](../reports/code_health_audit_2026-10-07.md).
- Global-map destinations are developer placeholders until their acts activate. Planned markers are positions only; promote one by removing its `planned` flag and adding neighbors and a gate in `GlobalMapCatalog`.
- **Debug travel greyboxes (every charted place is reachable).** For full-map debugging, all former `planned` markers plus Baltic Klint, Soomaa and the Otepää-Vastseliina frontier (14 nodes from [`docs/LOCATIONS`](../LOCATIONS/README.md)) are travelable `release=false` greyboxes, whatever act they belong to. Each is a 54x30-cell `content/maps/world_<id>.rrmap` plus `scenes/world_travel/world_<id>.tscn`, generated by `python3 tools/generate_travel_greyboxes.py` (archetypes castle, town, wild, marsh, cliff) and registered in `DistantLocationDefinitions`, `MapCatalog`, `MapBlueprintRegistry`, `active_destinations.json`, `map_audit_manifest.json` and the conversion plan. They connect to Reval (arrival spawn `from_reval_<east|west|harbor|south>`, return through the catalog gate) and to each other along the roads in the generator's `NODES`; the older built sites are not linked to them because their plans own the arrival spawns, so reach them via Reval. Later game logic is expected to hide them. Limits: bare terrain and massing, no content; the new nodes have no seamless site plan (see [REGIONAL_SITES](./REGIONAL_SITES.md) for the upgrade path). Verify: `--filter=test_global_map_catalog`, `--filter=test_world_map_overlay` (the Harju/Paide/Padise/Saaremaa door assertions there already failed before this change because site scenes have no rrmap doors), `python3 tools/verify_map_audit.py`.
