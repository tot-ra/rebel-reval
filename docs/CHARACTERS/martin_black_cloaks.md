# Martin of the Cloaks

**Confidence label:** `invented`  
**Role:** Younger urban Black Cloaks smith-contact (not the player smith)  
**Promotion:** P7-009 Wave A (`char.martin_cloaks`) · Art tier **1** · Legacy seed inspiration only

## Naming rule
Player-facing copy must keep him distinct from **Kalev** (master smith / protagonist) and from apprentice **Mart**. Prefer "Martin of the Cloaks" or "Martin" with cell context; content ID stays `char.martin_cloaks`.

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** Heavy linen shirt, dark wool trousers, thick leather apron when working; soot at cuffs.
- **Age:** Early twenties.
- **Hair:** Short practical brown.
- **Physical Build:** Lean, wiry apprentice strength; watchful hazel eyes.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** Prove he can arm the city cell and earn a place beside people he calls heroes.
- **Fear:** Being dismissed as a boy - or watched until the forge door is kicked in.
- **Contradiction:** Idealism that still needs Kalev's or Toomas's craft judgment before steel leaves the shop.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has already stamped a Cloaks mark inside at least one tool head that Henning has not traced.

## History (Biography)
Born in Lower Town, raised in a quiet rebel-sympathiser forge. He joined the Black Cloaks early, bringing energy and filing skill more than command. He is a cell contact and arms mender, never a replacement for Kalev's narrative forge.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** Black Cloaks runners, Old Toomas, Kaja when a message needs a hard hand.
- **Enemies:** Viru Watch, Order informants, merchants who sell names.
- **Initial View of Kalev:** Awe mixed with hunger to be useful; will over-promise until corrected.
- **Mart (apprentice):** Different person; do not collapse the two in dialogue.

## Daily Routines
Day work at a small allied forge or yard anvil; night errands for the cell; sleep in shifting safe rooms when heat rises.

## Voice
Quick, earnest Estonian with patchy Low German. Speaks in deeds and short boasts; goes quiet when Toomas or Kaja enters.

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
- **Night Filing:** Finish a batch of quiet blades before a Watch sweep.
- **Wrong Stamp:** Recover or destroy a marked piece that would burn the cell.
- **Endings:** Trusted cell smith; captured example; or tempered ally who learns to wait for Kalev's call.
