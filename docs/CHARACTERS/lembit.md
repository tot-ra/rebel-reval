# Lembit Helme (Elder King)

**Confidence label:** `plausible composite`  
**Role:** Harju Kings spokesman; rural uprising elder  
**Promotion:** P7-009 Wave B (`char.lembit_helme`) · Art tier **1** · Legacy seed inspiration only

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** Dark patched wool tunic, linen trousers, heavy cloak with a simple bronze pin; earth colours.
- **Age:** Early sixties.
- **Hair:** Long white hair and beard, often bound with a leather strap.
- **Physical Build:** Lean, wiry; sharp grey eyes under deep lines.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** Keep land, language, and sacred memory from being taxed and preached out of existence.
- **Fear:** A slaughter that leaves Harju with kings' names and no living villages.
- **Contradiction:** Preaches unity while knowing the four kings already disagree on pace and allies.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has written to city contacts that the rural rising may move before the gates are ready - and he has not told every king the whole of that risk.

## History (Biography)
Village elder turned elected "king" of the Harju host: less a crown than a burden of speech and counsel. His authority is oratory, memory, and the trust of farmers who will not march for a stranger's banner.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** Other Harju Kings (Jüri Ratnik, Urmas Laar, and the fourth seat as content fills), rural elders, cautious Metsik contacts, city couriers such as Kaja.
- **Enemies:** Livonian Order command (Burchard / Goswin), Danish hill authority, any merchant who sells muster lists.
- **Initial View of Kalev:** Tests whether the smith is a useful city hinge or a story people will die for too early.
- **Martin of the Cloaks / Black Cloaks:** Needs their steel and news; does not romanticise street terror.
- **Ellen / Metsik:** Open to old-ways alliance if it does not burn the host for theatre.

## Daily Routines
Council at the rebel camp; walking the lines; hearing grievances; night map-work scratched in dirt and ash.

## Voice
Measured Estonian, fire-sermon cadence when stirring a crowd, quiet and specific in private. Low German only as a tool, never as a home tongue.

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
- **Fire Sermon:** Escort or shield a gathering that must hear him before a village commits.
- **King's Council:** Break a deadlock among the kings with evidence Kalev can forge or find.
- **Endings:** Surviving counsellor after Paide; captured example; or voice that outlives his body in the songs.
