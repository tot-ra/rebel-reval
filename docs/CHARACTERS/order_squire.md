## Stable naming
The old seed called this role only "Order Squire". The active brief uses the stable player-facing name **Tomas Veld** and content ID `char.order_squire`; "Order Squire" remains the role label. Tomas is not Captain Henning, Brother Hermann, or a new Order commander.

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** Plain dark wool surcoat over a padded linen arming coat, serviceable hose, and scuffed riding boots; no oversized heraldry or late-medieval plate silhouette.
- **Age:** Nineteen.
- **Hair:** Sandy brown, cropped short for a helmet and duty.
- **Physical Build:** Broad-shouldered but still growing into his equipment; awkwardly formal posture that relaxes around stable work.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** Earn a permanent place in Henning's detail by proving that discipline can keep a street calm without cruelty.
- **Fear:** Being exposed as inexperienced when a real night emergency makes orders contradictory.
- **Contradiction:** He repeats the Order's hierarchy while quietly recognising that local workers often understand a street better than the men commanding it.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has altered a minor patrol note to spare a stable hand from a fine, then kept the correction hidden because admitting mercy could cost his standing.

## History (Biography)
Tomas Veld is an invented junior retainer assigned to Captain Henning's detail. He was raised near a small Order holding and entered service through stable and escort work rather than a noble household. He knows horses, gates, and equipment better than law or theology. Henning keeps him close because he is observant and easy to direct; Hermann sees him as a useful witness who can still be shaped.

His Act 1 brief supports street-level Order pressure without adding a second antagonist or a new combat archetype. He can be a named witness, escort, or conflicted obstacle. His later promotion remains bounded by the existing Order and Act 1 systems.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** Captain Henning's detail, stable hands, and the guard who values reliable reports over grand speeches.
- **Enemies:** Black Cloaks organisers who test the patrol, officers who punish mistakes by scapegoating, and his own fear of looking soft.
- **Initial View of the Main Player (Kalev):** A dangerous civilian whose work earns respect from people the Order cannot command directly.
- **Captain Henning:** Loyal and eager for approval, but increasingly uneasy when Henning turns uncertainty into punishment.
- **Brother Hermann:** Intimidated by Hermann's calm authority and unsure whether he is being trained or used.

## Daily Routines
He drills and checks tack at dawn, escorts Henning through watch duties, and spends spare hours repairing straps or helping in the stable. He sleeps in shared Order quarters and keeps a small private notebook of patrol landmarks he has not yet learned to discuss aloud.

## Voice
Careful, formal, and occasionally too quick to repeat an officer's phrasing. He asks for clarification after the senior voice leaves, uses plain words with workers, and becomes direct when horses, gates, or route safety are involved.

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
- **The Missing Patrol Note:** Decide whether to expose an altered report, protect the worker it spared, or let Henning's version stand.
- **A Witness at the Forge:** Choose whether Tomas records a suspicious delivery accurately or follows an order to make it disappear.
- **Quest Objectives:** Escort, observe, compare accounts, and select a report whose consequences remain visible in the watch relationship.
- **Rewards:** A patrol route detail, a witness statement, or a small reduction in watch pressure; no new combat or party-control system.
- **Arc:** Obedient retainer -> conflicted witness -> accountable local officer.
- **Endings:** Keeps his post with a conscience, leaves the detail after refusing a false report, or hardens into the institution he once questioned.
