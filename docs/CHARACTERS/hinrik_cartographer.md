# Hinrik the Cartographer

**Confidence label:** `plausible composite`
**Role:** Harbour mapmaker, route witness, and Blackheads candidate-seat intelligence contact
**Promotion:** P7-013 Wave A (`char.hinrik_cartographer`) · Act gate **A1** · Art tier **1** · Legacy seed inspiration only
**Content ID:** `char.hinrik_cartographer`

## Naming and scope
This brief promotes the cartographer seed, not the unrelated legacy painter named Hinrik in the Workers Quarter. Player-facing content must use **Hinrik the Cartographer** and the stable content ID `char.hinrik_cartographer`. He is a candidate-seat contact, not a new faction leader or a free-roaming overworld guide.

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** Dark green linen tunic over an off-white shirt, brown wool trousers, and worn leather boots; a weathered leather satchel is his only prominent accessory.
- **Age:** Late twenties.
- **Hair:** Dark brown, slightly unruly, cut short enough for travel.
- **Physical Build:** Slender and wiry, with quick hands and an observant, restless gaze.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** Make movement through Reval legible enough that merchants, messengers, and ordinary workers are not trapped by someone else's false map.
- **Fear:** That a map will be treated as permission to seize a street, warehouse, or shoreline rather than as a record of people who already use it.
- **Contradiction:** He distrusts military mapping while eagerly collecting details that could make a siege or patrol more effective.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** One of his harbour charts marks an unofficial wet-margin path from a source who would be endangered if the Order learned the route was known.

## History (Biography)
Hinrik learned practical surveying from ship routes, warehouse plans, and copied town sketches rather than from a formal university. He earns coin correcting delivery paths, measuring storage yards, and comparing sailors' descriptions of the harbour edge. Johann values him because he can turn rumour into a route; workers value him because he notices which paths remain usable in mud, snow, or a watch cordon.

His Act 1 role is bounded to map evidence, harbour intelligence, and candidate-seat events. He must not create a seamless overworld, speculative bathymetry, or a new navigation mechanic. The P0-167/P0-168 shoreline work remains the authoritative map pipeline for confidence metadata.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** Johann von Minden when contracts are honest, harbour workers, cart drivers, and messengers who share route conditions.
- **Enemies:** Patrol officers who erase inconvenient paths, exploitative merchants, and anyone who turns his notes into a target list.
- **Initial View of the Main Player (Kalev):** A fixed point in a changing district: useful to locate, but also a person whose route should not be assumed.
- **Johann von Minden:** Accepts Johann's protection while resisting his habit of converting knowledge into leverage.
- **Kalev's apprentice Mart:** Keeps the two Mart names distinct and avoids carrying messages that could expose the forge.

## Daily Routines
He checks harbour approaches at first light, copies route changes in a rented upper room, and trades small corrections for meals or lamp oil. At dusk he compares notes with carters and watches where patrols stop people, then stores sensitive charts separately from ordinary delivery plans.

## Voice
Fast, concrete, and full of bearings: left bank, second gate, after the mud turns, before the bell. He sketches explanations with his fingers, interrupts grand claims with measurements, and becomes unusually careful when a route could endanger a source.

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
- **The Chart That Still Works:** Verify a harbour route against weather, mud, and watch activity without inventing a deep-water crossing.
- **A Line for the Blackheads:** Decide whether to share a route with Johann, a worker collective, or no one.
- **Quest Objectives:** Compare field notes, mark confidence as attested or reconstructed where the map contract permits, and deliver the safe version to the chosen contact.
- **Rewards:** A reliable delivery route, a map annotation, or candidate-seat evidence tied to `ledger.blackheads.hinrik_harbour_chart`.
- **Arc:** Observer -> route witness -> accountable keeper of dangerous knowledge.
- **Endings:** Trusted civic mapmaker, coerced informant, or keeper who burns the chart to protect its source.
