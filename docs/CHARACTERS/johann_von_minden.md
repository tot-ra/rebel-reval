# Johann von Minden

**Confidence label:** `plausible composite`
**Role:** Brotherhood of Blackheads merchant and candidate-seat organiser
**Promotion:** P7-013 Wave A (`char.johann_von_minden`) · Act gate **A1-A2** · Art tier **1** · Legacy seed inspiration only
**Content ID:** `char.johann_von_minden`

## Naming and faction boundary
Johann is a candidate face for `faction.blackheads`, not a ninth launch faction member. The candidate seat records events and content links only; the launch ledger remains the approved eight-faction table. His Blackheads identity is a design hook and does not authorize toll booths, a new merchant system, or a separate Hanseatic ledger.

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** Well-cut black wool over fine undyed linen, silver-edged cuffs, and clean practical boots; sober merchant status rather than later ceremonial costume.
- **Age:** Late thirties.
- **Hair:** Short dark brown hair, carefully combed; clean-shaven.
- **Physical Build:** Tall and lean, with a composed posture and a small scar at the left cheekbone.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** Make the city's merchant networks answer to civic interests instead of being an extension of a castle or a foreign war chest.
- **Fear:** That a failed bargain will close the harbour to his associates and leave local tradespeople exposed to retaliation.
- **Contradiction:** He speaks of city self-rule while treating people and routes as assets in a ledger.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He has promised different versions of the same harbour arrangement to two rival buyers, hoping the resulting dependency will buy the Blackheads time.

## History (Biography)
Johann is a plausible composite merchant from Minden who built his Reval position through credit, shipping contacts, and careful sponsorship of unmarried traders. He is neither a rebel king nor a civic office-holder. His influence comes from knowing which load can be delayed, which supplier can be protected, and which rumour will make a watch patrol reconsider a route.

He is promoted first for Act 1 density: the player can meet him through ordinary supply work, evidence handling, or a candidate-ledger event. The story should keep his ambition legible without presenting the Blackheads as an already-approved ninth launch faction.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** Blackheads candidate organisers, harbour merchants, and artisans who need credit without Order supervision.
- **Enemies:** Predatory Order requisitioners, merchants who sell names, and any faction that turns the harbour into a closed military corridor.
- **Initial View of the Main Player (Kalev):** A scarce skilled producer whose maker's mark can carry trust, risk, or leverage through the city.
- **Hinrik the Cartographer:** Values Hinrik's route knowledge but dislikes being corrected in front of clients.
- **Mart Weaver:** Sees a practical organiser and cultural advocate, not apprentice Mart.

## Daily Routines
He starts at a counting desk near the merchant court, visits warehouse and harbour contacts during daylight, and attends small Blackheads meetings after the market thins. He keeps no grand public office; the campaign should show his influence through ordinary contracts, guarded conversations, and candidate-seat records.

## Voice
Measured, courteous, and transactional. He uses short proverbs about weight, tide, and credit, rarely raises his voice, and names the cost of a choice before naming its moral argument. His Low German is polished; his Estonian is functional and deliberately respectful.

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
- **A Favour on the Harbour Margin:** Resolve a supply contract while keeping the candidate seat from becoming a public faction commitment.
- **The Price of a Name:** Choose whether Johann's protection of a worker is solidarity, investment, or both.
- **Quest Objectives:** Compare two offers, protect a named supplier, and record the resulting Blackheads candidate event without changing the launch ledger.
- **Rewards:** A safer supply route, merchant introductions, or evidence of who benefits from a delayed shipment.
- **Arc:** Benefactor -> candidate-maker -> accountable civic broker.
- **Endings:** Civic ally, compromised oligarch, or exposed intermediary whose network survives without him.
