# Viceroy Konrad Preen

**Confidence label:** `plausible composite`  
**Role:** Danish Crown face on Toompea; fading legitimate authority  
**Promotion:** P7-009 Wave A (`char.konrad_preen`) · Art tier **1** · Legacy seed inspiration only

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** Dark blue wool tunic, fur-lined crimson tabard, muted noble colours showing wear at cuffs; no open weapon at belt.
- **Age:** Late fifties.
- **Hair:** Thinning silver-grey, combed back.
- **Physical Build:** Tall, once strong, now slightly stooped; weary blue eyes.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** Hold the duchy for the Danish crown without begging the Order to swallow it.
- **Fear:** That Reval's ledgers, gates, and vassals already answer to other masters.
- **Contradiction:** Speaks of legacy and law while bargaining away pieces of both to keep the hill quiet.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He already knows how thin the Danish garrison is for a second night like St George's.

## History (Biography)
A composite of late Danish viceregal authority on Domberg: responsible for taxes, castle etiquette, and the fiction that Copenhagen still rules day to day. The Order, Hanseatic coin, and the Lower Town's temper hem him in from every side.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** Remaining Danish household officers; cautious canons; merchants who still prefer royal seals to Order ones.
- **Enemies:** Harju Kings as open rebels; Order commanders who offer "help"; Black Cloaks who treat his writ as paper.
- **Initial View of Kalev:** A useful Lower Town craftsman whose name and commissions can steady or embarrass the crown.
- **Captain Henning:** Needs the Watch but distrusts how much of it already answers Order pressure.
- **Brother Hermann / Burchard:** Rivals in courtesy; predators in practice.

## Daily Routines
Audience hours in the Small Castle chambers; ledger and petition review; sparse walks on the plateau when weather and pride allow.

## Voice
Careful Low German, formal address, long pauses. Avoids Estonian in public; never shouts - disappointment is the weapon.

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
- **Royal Seal:** Forge or refuse a commission that lends Danish cover to a Watch action.
- **Thin Garrison:** Carry proof of how few spears the hill can still field.
- **Endings:** Preserved figurehead; broken man after Order leverage; or quiet departure before the 1346 sale.
