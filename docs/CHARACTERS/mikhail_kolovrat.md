## Naming and faction boundary
[`docs/CANON.md`](../CANON.md) labels the Pskov/Novgorod events **`attested`** (a Pskov force under Prince Ivan reached Otepää on 26 May 1343) and the playable emissaries **`plausible composite`**. Mikhail is such an emissary: invented. His surname echoes the Rus hero Yevpaty Kolovrat (died 1238), who is not related to him and must not be referenced in-game. The legacy seed was written in Cyrillic (Михаил Коловрат); the English rendering above is the canon form. He is a Pskov voice in the combined `faction.pskov_novgorod` seat and an opposing view to Jana Podajalnaja, the Novgorod voice.

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** Dark leather and rough grey-brown fur, chosen for camouflage and weather, well maintained but heavily used.
- **Age:** Late thirties.
- **Hair:** Medium-length dark brown, often unkempt and pushed back.
- **Physical Build:** Lean and wiry; weathered tanned face with fine lines at sharp, intelligent grey eyes.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** Secure Pskov's independence from Novgorod and the Livonian Order; a Pskov where every citizen has a voice and a stake.
- **Fear:** Boyars selling Pskov to a stronger neighbour, and the common people paying for it.
- **Contradiction:** An advocate of the common people who survives through secrecy and the very patronage networks he condemns.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He tests a new ally's motives long before revealing his real plans.

## History (Biography)
Born to poor trappers, learned the forest early, joined the Pskov militia as a scout, fought bloody skirmishes with the Order, grew disillusioned with the boyars' leadership, and built his own network of informers. Now a semi-independent agent.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** Trappers, hunters and peasants of the Pskov lands; common soldiers and idealistic lesser nobles.
- **Enemies:** Pskov's wealthy boyars, the Livonian Order and the more aggressive Novgorod factions.
- **Initial View of the Main Player (Kalev):** Cautious and suspicious; he tests motives and skills before trust.
- **Jana Podajalnaja:** The Novgorod voice who wants to undermine Pskov's independence; their intrigue is the Novgorod Gambit hook.

## Daily Routines
By day a fur trapper checking lines and trading in market; by night meets spies and plans. Registry anchor: `poi.brewery.vene` (the Rus quarter, a safe-house base).

## Voice
Plain, quiet, watchful; forest metaphors, practical questions, never speeches.

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
**Before the uprising (1342)**
- **The Disappearing Trappers:** Investigate trappers missing on the Livonian border; a tense scene with a local commandery knight.
- **A Voice for the Voiceless:** Gather evidence against a corrupt boyar taxing peasants and bring it to the Pskov Veche.
- **The Novgorod Gambit:** Infiltrate the Novgorod trade delegation in Reval, clashing with Jana.

**During the uprising (1343-1345)**
- **The Padise Monastery Massacre:** Guide a Pskov warband through forests to raid an Order supply convoy.
- **The Four Kings:** Escort an envoy of the four kings to Pskov to plead for aid.
- **The Battle of Kanavere Bog / The Siege of Reval:** Scout for Pskov; make contact with sympathetic merchants inside the besieged city.
- Hooks that depend on events outside the Reval hinterland must respect the out-of-scope rule for distant regions (explicit journey, not a seamless map).
- **Arc:** Suspicious Tester -> Ally -> Accountable Actor.
- **Endings:** Pskov aid arrives, is withheld by the boyars, or turns into a Novgorod bargain.
