# Brother Tidemann

**Confidence label:** `invented` (the Dominican friary of St Catherine is `attested`; its infirmary is `plausible composite`, see [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md))
**Role:** Infirmarian of St Catherine's friary; the apprentice's teacher in the Church's medicine ([ADR 0052](../adr/0052-healer-apprentice-and-family-cure-loop.md))
**Stable ID:** `char.brother_tidemann`
**Pronunciation:** TEE-deh-mahn

## Visual Description
- **Clothes:** The Dominican habit: white wool tunic, white scapular and white hood, under a black cappa with a black hood for going out. Leather belt with a small knife and a linen pouch of herbs (`attested` for the order's dress).
- **Age:** About 58 (`invented`).
- **Hair:** Tonsured, a thin grey fringe; clean-shaven.
- **Physical Build:** Thin and stooped from bending over beds; ink and herb stains on his fingers; a kind, tired face.

## Motivations & Core
- **Want:** To leave someone behind who can do the work when his hands start shaking.
- **Fear:** That his remedies help less than his prayers, and his prayers less than he says.
- **Contradiction:** He preaches that sickness of the body follows sin, yet he spends his nights treating sinners and saints alike, and he quietly uses peasant cures that his prior would call superstition.
- **Secret or withheld fact:** He learned half of what he knows from an Estonian wise-woman outside the walls, and still sends patients to her when Latin fails.

## History (Biography)
*All `invented`.* Born in Lübeck, sent to the Reval house young, and given the infirmary after his eyes grew too weak for copying. He keeps the friary's herb beds in the garth, a shelf of remedy books he can barely read any more, and a list of the Lower Town's sick that he cannot visit himself. The almshouse boy starts running his errands and earns his teaching in return: which herbs, which words, which rites for the dying.

## Ties & Relationships
- **Allies:** The friary community; Matron Gertrud, who sends him the cases she cannot manage.
- **Enemies:** None declared. The prior thinks he spends too much on the poor.
- **Initial View of the Main Player (the Apprentice):** A strange, useful boy who stares too long at the sick, and is right too often.
- **Kalev:** Respects his iron charms more than he admits in front of other friars.

## Daily Routines
*`invented`.* Keeps the hours with the community, tends the infirmary between offices, works the garth herb beds in the morning, and goes into the Lower Town only with the boy at his side.

## Voice
Slow, gentle, precise; Latin names first, then the Low German, then a joke about both. "Salvia. Sage. Your Estonians call it something rude, I'm told." "We treat the body, boy. God treats the rest. And sometimes the family does."

## Possible Quest Lines & Outcomes
- **Quest Title:** The Infirmarian's Errands (planned, [`HOUSEHOLD_CURE.md`](../SYSTEMS/HOUSEHOLD_CURE.md)).
- **Quest Objectives:** Fetch herbs, carry remedies to the Lower Town sick, report what you saw in their homes.
- **Rewards:** Remedies, Latin and German comprehension, the rites for the sick and dying.
- **Arc:** Teacher -> Patient -> the one the boy must decide whether to save.
- **Endings:** Dies peacefully having passed on his craft; is broken by the uprising's wounded; or discovers what his student did on the dark path.
