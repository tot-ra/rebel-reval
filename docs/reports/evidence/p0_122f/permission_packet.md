## Subject

**Commercial permission request for two Estonian bird recordings in a game**

## Message

Dear University of Tartu Natural History Museum and Veljo Runnel,

We are preparing *Rebel-reval*, a commercial historical game set in Reval in 1343. We would like to use one or both of the following Estonian field recordings as species-specific ambient audio:

- **Great cormorant** (*Phalacrocorax carbo*, Estonian `Kormoran`):
  <http://loodusheli.ee/ET/loomaliigid/linnud/taxonid=448&speciesid=482>
  Stable source identifier: `speciesid=482`.
- **White-tailed eagle** (*Haliaeetus albicilla*, Estonian `Merikotkas`):
  <http://loodusheli.ee/ET/loomaliigid/linnud/taxonid=302&speciesid=576>
  Stable source identifier: `speciesid=576`.

Could the copyright holder grant us a written, non-exclusive commercial license for each approved exact recording? The requested license should cover:

- commercial reproduction and storage of the source and processed derivatives;
- technical editing, including trimming, loudness normalisation, noise reduction, looping, format conversion, and mixing with other game audio;
- distribution of the resulting game, updates, downloadable content, trailers, and promotional materials on digital and physical platforms worldwide;
- the applicable term, including whether the grant is perpetual or the exact expiry/renewal conditions;
- sublicensing to the project's distributors, storefronts, platform holders, and contractors where required to deliver the game.

A paid license is acceptable. Please state any fee, reporting, credit, territory, platform, or renewal requirements.

For **each approved record**, please confirm in writing:

1. the exact recording URL and stable recording ID;
2. species and scientific name;
3. recordist, recording date, country, location, and exact duration in seconds;
4. the name and authority of the copyright holder/licensor;
5. the license text or a direct link to the commercial grant;
6. that the grant covers commercial storage, editing, reproduction, and distribution as described above;
7. the approved attribution wording, and where that wording must appear;
8. whether separate consent is required from the recordist, institution, performer, dataset operator, or any other rights holder;
9. whether the grant also covers copies already released and future updates, trailers, and promotional materials.

Please reply in writing with the permission tied to the exact record URL/ID. We will preserve the reply and the original source URL in the asset provenance log. We will not use either recording if the only applicable term is CC BY-NC 3.0 EE, another non-commercial term, or a no-derivatives term.

Kind regards,
