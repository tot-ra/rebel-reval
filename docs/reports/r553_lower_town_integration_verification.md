## Scope and decision boundary

This is a final integration verification of the current Lower Town implementation and its directly connected contracts. The checks were run from the project root against the shared dirty worktree with Godot 4.7.1 at `/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot`. The dirty worktree note is material: results describe the current checkout, not a clean historical baseline.

A `PASS` below means that the named structural or focused contract completed successfully. It does not imply visual approval, historical sign-off, minimum-hardware acceptance, or closure of an upstream task. A `BLOCKED` result is retained when a test cannot load, when an existing fixture or authoring contract has drifted, or when packet integrity does not establish visual acceptance.

## Visual evidence limitations

The dedicated packet at `docs/reports/images/lower_town_p0_101/` is useful and reproducible, but it is evidence of capture capability and packet integrity only. It does not establish:

- gameplay-scale readability of all three ordinary house tiers;
- route-scale repetition limits across merchant and craft frontage;
- log, plank, plaster, limestone, tile, shingle, and thatch readability;
- localized wear and repaired-state readability;
- distinct silhouettes for special buildings, St. Catherine's, inner Viru Gate, foregate, walls, and precinct boundaries;
- interaction visibility, occlusion, or human canon/art approval for every required surface.

Existing reports and the capture matrix retain those rows as pending or blocked. The R-553 result must not convert valid PNGs, source IDs, or packet metadata into visual acceptance.

## Final decision and ownership boundary

**R-553 result: BLOCKED.** The current checkout contains meaningful green structural checks, including parser, service-yard, smithy, transition, population-profile, controller, and capture-packet contracts. They are outweighed by the named parity, authoring, chunk-streaming, runtime parse, shader integration, composition-enforcement boundary, unresolved dependency, and visual-review blockers above.

Do not close R-553. Do not close R-109/P0-100 or claim final Lower Town acceptance. Preserve the existing ownership split: R-550 owns composition enforcement and fixtures, R-551 owns capture evidence, R-552 owns route/runtime/streaming verification, and R-547-R-549 own the implementation gaps. R-553 performs no remediation and creates no duplicate follow-up task.
