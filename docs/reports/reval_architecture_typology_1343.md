## How to read a card

Two vocabularies, mapped the same way as WB-12:

- **CANON** (`docs/CANON.md`): `attested`, `plausible composite`, `folklore`, `invented`.
- **P0-072 classes**: A (attested support), B (bounded authoring range), C (later
  survival for general form only), D (gameplay assignment), U (no useful 1343 answer).

An uncited number is not allowed. If the reviewed register does not give the field,
the card says **unknown**. A figure from Lübeck, Riga, Visby, or a later Tallinn
survival is a labelled comparandum, never an attested Reval 1343 measurement.

Card confidence is the **family as a type**. Individual fields may be weaker.

## Shared authoring rules (all urban families)

1. **Gable to the street** on Lower Town strip plots: ridge perpendicular to the
   lane (H04, H26). Toompea curiae and rural longhouses do not copy this.
2. **Storey clear height** for gameplay is **2.5-3.5 m** (H26, H34). Exact 1343
   Reval ceiling heights are **unknown**.
3. **Roof pitch in degrees by cover** (tile / shingle / thatch / straw) is
   **unknown** for Reval 1343. Author a **steep gable** (~45° vernacular analogy
   from H26/H04, `plausible composite`). Do not invent a four-row pitch table
   here. AR-04 must keep one steep band until a measurement row exists.
4. **Door and window metres** (leaf height/width by storey) are **unknown**.
   Cards give opening *types* and relative size. AR-06 must not treat a kit
   default as attested.
5. **Glazing** on ordinary timber fronts in the 1340s is **unknown** (H04). Stone
   merchant ground openings may be unglazed or lightly glazed; do not fill every
   upper storey with glass.
6. **Pentice carpentry** (depth, posts, shutter counter) is **unknown** as a
   measured Reval type. Presence on craft *boda* is `plausible composite` from
   the booth form (H04). WB-12 left the geometry to this file; this file still
   has no metre.

## 6. Ecclesiastical, Lower Town

**Family confidence:** institutions `attested`; April 1343 mass is often a
construction state, not the tourist church.

### St Nicholas (Niguliste)

Late-13th-c. hall (two aisles, square chancel, sacristy, **low** west tower);
north porch first half of the 14th c.; St Barbara chapel recorded **1342**
(H31). **Exclude** 1405-1420 basilica choir, Hermen Rode altar, large organ.

### St Olaf (Oleviste)

Recorded **1267**; vaults dated **c. 1330**; quarter brought inside the walls in
the first half of the 14th c. (H14). West-tower walls cited ~3.2 m on the
short earlier church are a **Visby-analogy reconstruction** (`plausible
composite`, H14/H31). **Exclude** 1364+ tower completion as finished skyline,
1433-fire basilica, 1450 record spire.

### Holy Spirit (Pühavaimu)

Priest **1316** / church **1319**; two-aisle hall on a tight plot; vaults and
tower **1360** (H07). In April 1343: timber ceiling or active vault work, not
finished star vaults. Dual north-street / south-almshouse doors are C/`plausible
composite`.

### Bay / opening (all three)

Nave/aisle bay metres, buttress spacing, lancet width, and portal leaf size are
**unknown** as 1343 Reval measurements. Author a short hall or two-aisle mass
with a low or unfinished west work, pointed openings, and a churchyard wall.
Do not copy later basilica bay counts.

**Excluded with HISTORICAL_AUDIT item 2:** later monumental St Olaf silhouette;
completed later Holy Spirit church.

## 7. Cistercian conventual range (St Michael in town; Padise in Harju)

**Family confidence:** precincts `attested`; 1343 geometry is a construction or
pre-quadrangle state.

### St Michael (Lower Town nuns)

Founded **1249**; patronage of St Olaf since **1267**; 1340s wall expansion
ties the nunnery curtain into the town (H32). The inherited **~6.5 ha** close
is **unknown** as a 1343 measurement (H32 URL 404; do not treat it as
attested). Nuns' Gate name is **1355** - show masons, not a finished labelled
gate. Claustral range width, dorter/refectory/chapter metres, and vault bay
are **unknown**. Author enclosed ranges around a garden, an oratory, and a
service wing - not a 15th-c. show cloister.

Dominican St Catherine (mendicant, not Cistercian) is the eastern counterpart:
stone church and east wing largely up by **c. 1300** (H15). Peak footprint
67.7 x 18.5 m is the **later** hall - do not ship it as April 1343 (H15).

### Padise (hinterland)

Founded **1310** (H21). Kadakas (H30) and the world-map header: **pre-quadrangle
working estate** - stone hall over an undercroft with external stair, a lower
stone building with arched niches, timber oratory and ranges, barn, mill, cottages.
**Exclude** finished claustral quadrangle, abbey church of later centuries, gate
tower, gun tower, and moat as 1343 baseline (H21, H30, AR-08).

   building-bay measurements. This dossier's metres are the form source; do not

house; Pikk Hermann; stone Long/Short Leg; 1454-55 hill curtain; Padise later
