# Grounded character and fauna studies — 2026-09-10

P0-205 supersedes the soft storybook direction explored in P0-203/P0-204, following the maintainer's request for more realism with Magicka 1/2 and Hades 1/2 as inspirations. The stable `assets/storybook/` paths remain in place. P0-206 subsequently integrates the set into live gameplay at the maintainer’s request.

## Delivered set

- Eight people: Kalev, Mart, Aita, Ellen, watchman, Henning, Jurgen, Kaja. Each preserves the existing 41-bone rig, 76 named clips, attachment slots and wardrobe API.
- Nine mammals: forge cat, sheep, dog, pig, goat, boar, fox, hare, rat. Each has a 15-bone skeleton with separate upper legs, shins and feet; Idle, Walk, Run, LookAround, Graze and Alert.
- Five birds: robin, hooded crow, gull, hen, duck. Each has nine bones including shoulder/wing-tip joints; Idle, Walk, Hop, Peck, TakeOff, Fly, Glide and Land.
- Twenty-four body-fitted wearables: mail, kettle helmet and cape for each person, plus sword, shield and hammer props. All equipment is authored against the new body dimensions.
- Six review cases: civilian, forge work, travel, sword/shield, grazing and alert. Cases restore clothing and remove incompatible previous gear through the existing runtime API.

## Visual revision

Human faces use a sculpted cranial profile with integrated nasal bridge/tip, defined jaw, restrained brows, smaller eyes and layered eyelids. Hair/beard shells follow the same profile. The chin-to-crown dimension is approximately 0.263 times stature scale, versus roughly 0.51 in the first study. Eyes are 0.042 wide versus 0.080 in the earlier study. This changes both head-to-body and eye-to-body proportions. Humans retain individual body builds and costume colors; sleeves and hose are continuous meshes with blended joint weights. Hands include modeled fingers using the existing hand bones, without adding a finger-animation contract.

Mart retains P0-204's shortened arm chain across all clips (about 20.7% shorter than P0-203). Pig, dog and sheep body centres were lowered from 0.62 to 0.34, 0.40 and 0.46 model metres respectively, with shorter, species-specific leg chains. Sheep fleece uses smaller staggered tufts. Boar has a longer muzzle/tusks/bristles, fox a narrow muzzle/bushy tail, goat horns/beard, hare tall ears and rat a low body and bare tail. Duck has a flattened bill and webbed feet; hen has comb/wattles.

Reference interpretation: favor adult anatomy, clear silhouette and distinct cloth/leather/metal regions while retaining readable stylization. Official reference pages: [Magicka 2](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/magicka-2/about), [Hades II](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/hades-ii/). All mesh construction is original project code; no assets or character designs were copied from these games.

## Production limits

These models move toward grounded stylized anatomy; they are not photoreal scans or a claim to match the polish of either reference game. Cloth follows skin weights rather than physics; extreme shared combat poses still need garment-specific contact approval before production. Human fingers are not independently rigged. Optimized LODs, production texture polish and further species-specific behavior remain separate work; the existing live actor and fauna controllers are integrated by P0-206. A hen's flight clip is a preview wing cycle, not an implemented sustained-flight behavior. Assets stay within the existing candidate 60,000-triangle/body and 10 MiB/file caps; fully equipped production budgets need a separate optimization pass.
