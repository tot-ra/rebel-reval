# Cutscene records

Authored cinematic sequences ([ADR 0034](../../docs/adr/0034-cutscene-mode-and-cinematic-prologue.md)),
loaded by `ContentDB` and played by `CutscenePlayer`. Schema:
[`schemas/cutscene.schema.json`](../../schemas/cutscene.schema.json). Feature page:
[`docs/SYSTEMS/CUTSCENES.md`](../../docs/SYSTEMS/CUTSCENES.md).

| Record | Chapter | Shots | Chains to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cutscene.prologue.conquest` | I. The Forging of Chains (1219-1343) | 6 | the almshouse scene |
| `cutscene.prologue.almshouse_dawn` | II. The almshouse of the Holy Spirit | 3 | returns to the host scene (not played by the shortened opening) |
| `cutscene.prologue.taken_in` | III. The forge | 3 | the forge, spawn `smithy_start` |

Each shot carries an `authoring` block with the `direction`, the `image_prompt` that produced
the shipped frame, and the `video_prompt` for the later AI-video tier. Write them under
[`cinematics/PROMPT_GRAMMAR.md`](../../cinematics/PROMPT_GRAMMAR.md); the readable screenplay
is [`cinematics/PROLOGUE.md`](../../cinematics/PROLOGUE.md).

Verify with `python3 tools/validate_content.py content/cutscenes` and
`python3 tools/verify_cutscenes.py`.
