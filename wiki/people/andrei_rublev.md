# Andrei Rublev

**Dates:** c. 1360-1370 - c. 1427/1430

The most celebrated painter of the Moscow school of Russian icon painting, traditionally a monk of the Andronikov Monastery. The Trinity (c. 1411) is his best-known work; he also painted frescoes in the Dormition Cathedral in Vladimir and in Zvenigorod.

Nothing is recorded about his parents or birthplace, and sources differ on whether "Andrei" was his monastic name. The game uses exactly this gap: in 1343 it offers only an invented Novgorod girl's dream of a son named Andrei, son of a board-maker called Rubl (see [Anisia of Novgorod](../../docs/CHARACTERS/anisia_of_novgorod.md) and the archive sheet [`characters/famous/andrei_rublev.md`](../../characters/famous/andrei_rublev.md)). The game never claims a connection; it lets the player suspect one.
