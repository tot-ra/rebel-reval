# Reval council prosopography, 1340-1345

## Brief for Character / Narrative / Quest / Dialogue

This dossier separates dated council evidence from the still-unread annual election rolls. The
public-domain AWB edition identifies several men as `consulatus socius` or `consul` in entries dated
1341, 1343, 1344, and 1345, while the Diplomatarium Danicum identifies the town's council only
collectively in the 1343 crisis documents. It does **not** establish a named burgomaster pair for
every year in 1340-1345.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. Use **Johannes de Breme**, **Hermannus More**, **Wynandus Longus**, **Bertoldus Bickinchusen**,
   **Reynekinus Crowel**, **Detmarus de Unna**, **Hunoldus de Ostinchusen**, **Remboldus de Lenepe**,
   and **Arnoldus** as dated council-member candidates only when the cited entry is named [1].
2. Keep **Dietrich (Didericus) Zierenberg** as the one near-1343 burgomaster identified by the
   existing council dossier, with death on 6 December 1345; this file does not independently upgrade
   the burgomaster claim [4][5].
3. Do not present an AWB `consulatus socius` as a burgomaster. The phrase proves council
   membership or council association in that entry, not which chair the man held [1].
4. The 1333 cohort remains a dated name stock, not proof that all fourteen councillors still sat in
   1343. Mark continued membership as `plausible composite` unless a dated council-book entry is
   supplied [3][6].
5. The exact 1343-1345 burgomaster pair and the December 1343 election outcome remain an archival
   gap pending a folio-level read of Denkelbuch TLA.230.1.Aa2 [2].

## Findings

### Source and method boundary

The accessible AWB edition is Leonid Arbusow's *Das älteste Wittschopbuch der Stadt Reval
(1312-1360)* (Reval, 1888). It is a property and mortgage register, not a complete council
membership list. The entries below are transcribed from the public-domain MDZ OCR and checked against
the corresponding page OCR; the printed page and AWB number are retained so a later manuscript
collation can correct OCR readings [1].

The ILC catalogue identifies the first Denkelbuch volume as **TLA.230.1.Aa2**, 48 pages,
1333-1374, with notes on council elections, punishments, and judgments. The catalogue supplies the
archive reference and period, but its public page does not expose the folio text [2]. This is why the
present dossier reports dated AWB names and a bounded negative result rather than inventing a full
annual roll.

| **Arnoldus** | 1345, AWB 635, printed p. 98 | `nostri consulatus socius`; appears with his brother, a Reval canon | attested council member/associate in a dated 1345 act; forename alone is all the edition supplies here [1] |

The AWB language is specific enough to support dated council membership, but it is not a roster.
Repeated names are therefore useful continuity candidates, not proof of uninterrupted tenure. For
example, **Hermannus More** appears as a council associate in both 1343 and 1344, and **Johannes de
Breme** appears as a council associate in 1341 and in later property acts; continuity across the
interval is **plausible composite** unless the Denkelbuch confirms each year [1][2].

### Civic acts and the council's collective signature

The 1343 diplomatic record supplies dated civic actions but not individual council names:

| Date | Document | What it proves | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **11 May 1343** | *Diplomatarium Danicum* III:1, no. 321 | Bertram von Parembeke and other Danish officers received a grain levy from the **town of Reval** | attested civic act; does not identify councillors [7] |
| **21 May 1343** | *Diplomatarium Danicum* III:1, no. 324 | `staden Reval` joined an armistice with Swedish representatives and Danish royal councillors in Estonia | attested collective representation; no named burgomaster pair [7] |
| **27 October 1343** | *Diplomatarium Danicum* III:1, no. 376 | `staden Revals borgmestre og rådmænd` petitioned the Livonian Order with bishop, chapter, monasteries, and Danish vassals | attested collective mention of burgomasters and councillors; individual names absent from the regest [7] |
| **7 January 1345** | *Diplomatarium Danicum* III:2, nos. 116-119 | The king confirmed Reval's privileges and authorised the town's councillors and burghers to manage watercourses and mills | attested collective authority; individual names absent [8] |

These documents establish that the council acted as a corporate body during the uprising and that
multiple burgomasters existed by October 1343. They do **not** justify assigning Johannes de Breme,
Hermannus More, or any other AWB witness to a named burgomaster chair without the Denkelbuch or a
full charter witness list [1][7].

### Burgomaster and turnover verdict

- **Dietrich (Didericus) Zierenberg** is the project's existing near-attested burgomaster candidate,
  with a recorded death on 6 December 1345. Retain him as a senior civic face, but cite the existing
  council dossier rather than presenting this dossier as a new primary proof [4][5].
- The **1333 Denkelbuch cohort** (four probable burgomasters and fourteen councillors) is securely
  dated to 1333, but survival, re-election, and chair assignment in 1343-1345 are not demonstrated
  by the accessible AWB pages [3][6].
- **No named 1343-1345 burgomaster pair is established in this pass.** The October 1343 DD regest
  gives the collective phrase `borgmestre og rådmænd`; the accessible AWB excerpts identify council
  associates but do not call any of them `burgimagister` or equivalent [1][7]. This is an
  **attested negative result for the reviewed evidence**, not a claim that the pair cannot be found
  in TLA.230.1.Aa2 or another charter series.
- **Turnover:** the dated AWB sequence shows that the council's active network included Hunoldus de
  Ostinchusen and Remboldus de Lenepe in 1341, Hermannus More in 1343-1344, Wynandus Longus and
  Bertoldus Bickinchusen in 1344, and Arnoldus in 1345. This demonstrates named council activity
  across the period, but not the full rate or cause of turnover. Any replacement narrative is
  `plausible composite` until the election notes are read [1][2].

## Production hooks

- **Character:** Use the dated AWB names as a small, source-labelled pool for council scenes. Keep
  Dietrich Zierenberg as senior burgomaster; use generic burgomaster clothing for the second chair
  rather than inventing a named partner. Mark continuity from one dated AWB entry to another as
  `plausible composite`.
- **Narrative / Quest:** A 1343 council scene can truthfully invoke a collective `Rat`, the grain
  levy of 11 May, the armistice of 21 May, or the October appeal to the Order. A named NPC may be
  present as a council associate only with the relevant AWB date in the writer's note.
- **Dialogue:** Preserve documentary variants (`Johannes de Osenburge` / `Osenbryg`) until a
  manuscript collation resolves them. Do not have a character claim that a named AWB associate is
  `Bürgermeister` unless the source note supplies the chair.
- **Art:** Two civic chairs remain historically required by the collective 1343 wording, but only
  one named burgomaster face is near-attested in the current evidence. Render the second as an
  unnamed senior merchant, not as a fabricated historical person.
- **Dev:** If council members are represented in content data, store `evidence_date`, `source_id`,
  and `office_confidence`; treat `consulatus_socius` as `councillor_associate`, not as an automatic
  `burgomaster` enum.
