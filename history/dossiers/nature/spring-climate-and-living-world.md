# Spring climate and living world (April–May 1343)

## Brief for Map and Art

You are dressing **Reval in the last cold weeks before harvest**, not a lush high-summer Baltic postcard. April–May 1343 sits **after Lent/Easter** and **on St George's Night (23 April)** — the countryside ignites while the town still reads **mud, pale green, and late frost risk** [1][2][3].

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Weather band:** **Cool, windy, changeable** — daily highs roughly **8–14 °C**, nights **0–5 °C** with **frost still possible** through late April `plausible composite` from modern Tallinn April climatology applied as a regional band, not a measured 1343 thermometer series [4][5]. Show **rain, sea mist, and mud** more often than warm sun.
2. **Light:** **Long spring days** — sunrise ~**5:30**, sunset ~**20:30** on 23 April at ~59.4°N `plausible composite` [5]. **Golden low-angle sun** at dawn/dusk; **overcast flat grey** is the default midday read for art.
3. **Sea ice:** Gulf of Finland **ice cover breaks late March–May**; **no attested 1343 ice-off date** [6][7]. Harbour scenes may show **open muddy water and last ice pans offshore**, not a frozen port through May [6][7].
4. **St George's Night (23 April):** **Dark after ~20:30**, **chilly**, **wind audible**; rebel **signal fire on a Harju hilltop** is documented in campaign chronicle tradition [1][2]. Town sky may show **distant orange glow** and **smoke** beyond Viru Gate — not midsummer warmth [1][8].
5. **Plants in bloom (visible):** **Willow catkins, coltsfoot, dandelion, wood anemone, early birch leaf**; **apple/pear not yet in full bloom** in late April — buds and first leaf `plausible composite` [5][9]. **Cereals/emerging green** in foreland strips; **no standing harvest** [5][10].
6. **Birds audible/visible:** **Skylark, chaffinch, robin, blackbird, lapwing** on meadows; **gulls, terns, cormorant** at wet margin; **swallows arriving** late April–May `plausible composite` phenology [9][11]. **Nightingale** — treat as **May-margin** song, not dominant on 23 April [9].
7. **Livestock:** **Cattle, sheep, horses** on foreland pasture and penned yards; **pigs and poultry** in service plots; **geese** on wet margins [10][12]. **Calving/lambing season** — young animals **plausible** in pens `plausible composite` [10].
8. **Field work:** **Ploughing, harrowing, first sowing** on dry foreland and manor strips; **garden beds** show **onion, cabbage, turnip starts** not full crops [10][13]. **Hay not cut** — last year's stacks still visible [10].
9. **Do not show:** **Snow cover on streets** (except rare fresh night frost), **deep summer forest**, **autumn leaf colour**, **fireflies**, **ripe grain fields**, **grape vines**, **exotic plants** absent from [`docs/FLORA_FAUNA.md`](../../../docs/FLORA_FAUNA.md).

### Weather, temperature, and light

Baltic spring is **maritime**: frequent **cloud**, **sea fog**, and **wind from the Gulf** `plausible composite` [4][5]. Modern Tallinn April normals (used only as a **production band**, not historical measurement) centre on **~8 °C mean** with **~30 mm precipitation** and **high variability** [4][5].

| Parameter | April production band | May production band | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day high | 8–14 °C | 12–18 °C | plausible composite [4][5] |
| Night low | −2–5 °C (frost risk late April) | 2–8 °C | plausible composite [4][5] |
| Rain / mud | Frequent showers; lane mud | Showers easing; drier lanes | plausible composite [4][5] |
| Sea mist | Common at harbour dawn | Less persistent | plausible composite [6] |
| Day length (23 Apr) | ~15 h daylight | ~16.5 h by late May | plausible composite astronomy [5] |

**Art lighting:** favour **cool blue-grey ambient** with **warm torch/belfry/fire accents** at night; **avoid tropical saturation** on vegetation [5].

### Sea ice and harbour season

Gulf of Finland **freeze–thaw** is annual; medieval accounts and modern hydrography agree ice **clears progressively from south to north** through spring [6][7]. Reval's wet north margin ([`harbour-and-shoreline.md`](../topography/harbour-and-shoreline.md)) should read **open water with muddy foreshore** by late April in most years, while **roadstead cogs** may still stand off beyond shallow grounding zones [6][7][15].

| Element | Late April 1343 | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Ice in harbour basin | **Absent to fringe** — mud and open channel | plausible composite [6][7][15] |
| Ice pans offshore | **Possible** distant white specks | plausible composite [6] |
| First coastal sailings | **Plausible** — cog traffic resuming | plausible composite [7][15] |
| Harbour mud / reed | **Dominant** shore read | attested wet margin [15] |

Exact **1343 ice-off day** is **unknown** — do not script a dated harbour ice festival.

## Production hooks

- **Map:** On `lower_town_slice` and `viru_gate_foreland`, bias terrain vegetation to **early-spring weights**: `grass.spring`, sparse `plant.dandelion`, `plant.coltsfoot` (use `plant.yarrow`/`plant.plantain` as stand-ins if coltsfoot absent), **emerging** `crop.rye`/`crop.barley` strips on foreland only [10][13]. **No autumn stubble** tiles. Harbour maps: **reed fringe**, **mud shore**, gull/tern spawn weights high [15][11].
- **Art:** **Colour palette** — muted ochre mud, grey limestone, **yellow-green** new leaf, **not** deep emerald; **wet surfaces** specular; **wool and felt** clothing still plausible for night watch [5][16]. St George's Night key art: **orange hill fire** low on northern horizon, **Viru Gate torchlight**, **no foliage blocking sightlines** on wall walks [1][8].
- **Character:** Labourers in **layered wool**; gardeners with **mud-caked boots**; fishermen mending nets at cold shore [15][12].
- **Quest / Narrative:** `st_georges_night` climax — **chill wind**, **distant fire**, **bell then alarm** [`watch-duty-and-town-defence.md`](../military/watch-duty-and-town-defence.md); `bread_and_iron` — **pre-harvest grain tension**, not abundance [1][14].
- **Dialogue:** "Last year's hay", "ice gone a week or two", "fields too wet to plough" — **plausible composite** spring small talk; avoid midsummer harvest idioms [10].
- **Dev / systems:** `GameCalendar` spring phase may gate **market-day herring** and **swallow ambient** without inventing a temperature sim — use **flags**, not a full climate model [11].
