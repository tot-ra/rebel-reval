# Order comptoir transition on Toompea (May 1343 – November 1346)

## Brief for Quest / Narrative

You are staging **post-siege authority on the hill**, not a clean regime change. Between **16 May 1343** and **1 November 1346** Reval is a **three-layer city**: the **Order holds the castles** on Toompea by vassal grant; the **Danish crown remains nominal overlord** of the duchy until the sale closes; the **Lower Town council keeps Lübeck law** inside the walls. Act 2–3 scenes must show **banners and seals changing on the hill** while **market proclamations still carry the council seal** below.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **16 May 1343 (DD 322):** The king's **councillors and vassals in Estonia** — not the town council as grantor — elect the **Livonian Order master** as their captain (*høvedsmand*) against the rebels and **surrender Reval and Wesenberg castles** with all appurtenances [1]. Lower Town corporate privileges are **not** in this document.
2. **Who runs Toompea after 16 May:** Military and feudal administration shifts to **Order brothers** in *Castrum Danorum*; the **Komturei Reval** is re-established in the castle on the Domberg [2][3]. Day-to-day castle routine is **Komtur / Vogt of the castle**, not the burgomaster [3]. **plausible composite** that hill gates are controlled by Order garrison with council still regulating Lower Town ascents [4].
3. **Council continuity:** On **21 May 1343** the town is a named party to the Swedish armistice alongside "the Danish king's councillors in Estonia" — corporate diplomacy continues [5]. On **27 October 1343** burgomasters and councillors jointly petition the Order with bishop, monasteries, and vassals [6]. The council **asks for** Order protection; it does **not** dissolve.
4. **Interim Danish captain (1343–1346):** Between castle handover and sale, a **Danish Hauptmann** still represents crown interests — **Stig Andersen** attested as *høvedsmand* in **April 1345** [7]. **Burchard von Dreileben** receives Estonia and Reval **on 1 November 1346** from Danish captain **Stigot Andersson** on behalf of the Hochmeister, then administers the post for several months [8]. Do not show a power vacuum on Toompea in 1344–1345 scenes.
5. **1 November 1346 sale:** Valdemar IV sells the Duchy of Estonia — Harrien, Wierland, **Reval town and castle**, Wesenberg, Narva, bishopric presentation rights — to the Teutonic Order for **19,000 marks** [9][10]. Physical receipt at Reval is **1 November 1346** per contemporary chronicle tradition cited in DD [11].
6. **4 November 1346 privilege confirmations:** Livonian Master **Goswin von Herike** at Weissenstein promises the Hochmeister will **confirm all Danish-granted privileges** for both the **Estonian knighthood** and the **burghers of Reval** [12][13]. Lower Town law, market rights, and council self-government are **explicitly preserved in intent** — the change is **overlord on the hill**, not Hanseatic autonomy abolished [14].
7. **June 1347 administrative settlement:** Hochmeister transfers Estonia and Reval to the **Livonian branch** under **redemption reservation**; Livonian master becomes **de facto Landesherr** while Hochmeister retains **nominal sovereignty** and judgments are sometimes issued in his name [8]. Post-game epilogue territory; flag for time-skip scenes only.
8. **What changes for Lower Town:** **No change** to Vogt/Rat courts, guild regulation, or wall watch inside All-linn **attested** through privilege confirmations [12][13][14]. **Changes:** Danish viceroy audience hall becomes **Order Komtur residence**; crown revenue collection on the hill routes through **Order treasury**; extraordinary war levies (**11 May 1343** grain levy) shift from Preen's agents to Order-Vassal coalition **plausible composite** [15][16].
9. **Bishop and cathedral:** Bishop **Olaf of Roskilde** remains in post; cathedral chapter on Toompea; bishop co-signs October petition [6]. Ecclesiastical jurisdiction **unchanged** by castle handover [17].
10. **Do not show before 16 May 1343:** Order cross as permanent Toompea government banner; Komtur holding court in Lower Town Rathaus; council oath to Order master replacing Lübeck-law oath to town [4][18].

### What the 16 May document does and does not say

**DD 322 (16 May 1343, Reval)** states that the king's councillors and vassals in Estonia choose the Livonian Order master as their captain to defend against the pagan Estonians and **hand over the castles of Reval and Wesenberg with all appurtenances** [1].

**Explicit gaps:**
- **Council assent** to castle surrender is **not recorded** in DD 322 — only vassal party [1]. The **27 October** petition shows council alignment months later [6].
- **Legal status of the handover** under Danish feudal law (pawn vs alienation) is **disputed in historiography** — treat as **military-administrative transfer** pending the **1346 purchase**, not a burgher constitutional amendment [8][9].
- **Preen's personal authority** ends with capture **early May 1343** **attested** in viceroy scholarship [19]; DD 322 is a **vassal-collective** act, not a royal charter.

## Production hooks

- **Quest / Narrative:** **Wrong banner** — player delivers message to "the lord of Reval" and must choose castle (Komtur) vs Rathaus (burgomaster). **16 May aftermath** — vassal ceremony on outer ward [`architecture.toompea-small-castle-interior.md`](../architecture/toompea-small-castle-interior.md) while council closes Long Leg at night. **October petition** — Kalev's smith guild asked to sign armourers' clause in joint letter. **Sale eve (Nov 1346 epilogue)** — same market square, new hill banner, same council seal on stall permit [`power.order-comptoir-transition-1343-1346.04`].
- **Character:** Hill NPCs after May: **Order brother**, **Komtur clerk**, **Danish Hauptmann** (until sale), **vassal knight** — not burgomaster. Lower Town NPCs: **unchanged** council hierarchy [`people/town-council-and-officers.md`](../people/town-council-and-officers.md).
- **Dialogue:** "De Ordensmeister" / "de Komtur" for hill address; "de Rat" / "de Borgemeister" still correct in Lower Town. Oaths: burghers swear on **town privileges**, not Order rule book — until a scene explicitly forces a loyalty test.
- **Map:** Two seal zones after 16 May: `toompea_order_comptoir` (castle, cathedral square, vassal plots) vs `lower_town_council` (All-linn polygon). Hill gates become `order_checkpoint` tiles **plausible composite** [4].
- **Dev / systems:** `overlord_flag`: `danish_1343` → `order_castle_1343` (≥ 1343-05-16) → `order_purchase_1346` (≥ 1346-11-01). Lower Town `jurisdiction_id` stays `luebeck_rat` throughout.
