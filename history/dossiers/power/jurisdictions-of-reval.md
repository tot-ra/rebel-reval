# Jurisdictions of Reval (Spring 1343)

## Brief for Quest / Narrative

You are writing **legal friction**, not a constitutional essay. Spring 1343 Reval is **two cities in one**: a **Danish feudal hill** (Toompea) and a **self-governing Hanseatic Lower Town** (*All-linn*) under **Lübeck law**. The Livonian Order is **extramural** until **16 May 1343** but already the region's army. Estonian rebels hold **no recognised jurisdiction** — only siege leverage.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Default forum by place:** Inside Lower Town walls → **town council courts** (Vogt first instance, Rat appeal). On Toompea hill, in the castle, or on a vassal manor → **Danish / feudal authority** (viceroy, vassal lord, or bishop for spiritual matters). **Never** let the council arrest a knight on Toompea or the viceroy tax-gather inside the market hall without a scene-breaking conflict [1][2][3].
2. **Remote overlord:** King **Valdemar IV** owns the Duchy of Estonia; **Konrad Preen** (*capitaneus*) exercises crown power from **Castrum Danorum** in April. Preen is **not** a burgomaster and does **not** sit on the merchant Rat [4][5].
3. **1332 faction seam:** Since Christopher II's death (**August 1332**), the province splits between **Bishop Olaf of Roskilde** (pro-Danish) and **Captain Marquard Breide** (pro-German vassal party). Quests can exploit **contradictory orders** from hill factions without inventing anarchy — both still claim Danish legitimacy [4][6].
4. **Bishop's double role:** Olaf holds **ecclesiastical jurisdiction** (marriage, morals, tithes, clergy discipline) and is a **political co-signatory** with council and vassals in crisis letters. The king **appoints** Reval bishops — a Danish prerogative disputed by Rome but exercised through 1346 [7][8]. Cathedral chapter on Toompea; suffragan to **Lund** until later reorganisation debates [8][9].
5. **Council autonomy (Lower Town):** Lübeck law since **1248**; codex **1255–1282**; burghers freed from castellan fine-share **1265**; independence from castle judicial power **1288** — long before 1343 [1][2][10][11]. Council controls **walls, gates, market, urban justice, and corporate diplomacy** — not rural Harju manors [2][12].
6. **Rural Harju / Viru:** **German and Danish vassals** hold manors under crown enfeoffment; justice over peasants is **manorial / feudal**, not council. ~**21 attested fiefs in Harria** before 1343 — densest feudal zone in Estonia; this is why the uprising starts there [13][14]. Rebels target **vassals and clergy**, not the council's law code [15].
7. **Order before 16 May:** Livonian Master **Burchard von Dreileben** has **no territorial lordship** over Reval in April, but **military intervention rights** once invited. After crushing rebels he captures Preen (**early May**, revised chronology) and receives castles **16 May 1343** from pro-German vassals — **before** the 1346 purchase [4][16][17]. April–mid-May scenes: Order troops may be **inside Lower Town by invitation**, not as city government [15][18].
8. **May crisis beats (attested seams):** **11 May** — vice-castellan **Bertram von Parembeke** receipts **grain levy** from the **town** for the crown (*DD* III:1, no. 321) [19]. **21 May** — **"Staden Reval"** is party to armistice talks with Swedish envoys (*DD* III:1, no. 324) [19]. **27 October** — burgomasters and councillors jointly petition the Order with bishop, monasteries, and vassals (*DD* III:1, no. 376) [19]. Use these to stage **council vs crown vs Order** obligations in Act 2.
9. **Estonians in town:** No council seat; **plausible composite** that unfree rural status does not automatically transfer inside walls — Lübeck-law towns often grant **freedom after a year** (Gadebusch comparandum in Danish charter tradition) [20]. Estonian labourers remain **socially subordinate** but may be **legally invisible** to manor courts while resident in town [2][21].
10. **Swedish envoys:** Diplomatic and military threat only — rebels offered the city to Sweden; a Swedish fleet appears **18–19 May** but does not land [15][18]. Council fear of Swedish annexation motivates **Order protection** deal [15].

### Legal traditions in play

**Lübeck law (Lower Town):** Merchant self-government, co-opted council, written urban custom. Wounding inside walls judged **"by advice of councillors and the king's men"** per **1248** privilege — later interpreted as full municipal fine jurisdiction by **1265** [1][10]. **1288** separation from castle judicial power is standard in Tallinn historiography [11][24].

**Feudal / Danish law (Toompea and countryside):** Vassal enfeoffment (*dominium utile*) for military service; viceroy as royal plenipotentiary [4]. Bishopric created **1240** with royal **presentation right** unique in Latin Christendom [7][8].

**Canon law (church):** Marriage, moral offences, clergy, tithes — bishop's forum. Implementation in Livonia follows wider 13th-c. Christianisation pattern [9]. **Do not** conflate with town Vogt's secular bench without a forum-shopping scene.

**Order martial law:** Applies to campaign zones and Order territories; intervention in Denmark's duchy in **1343** is **expedient**, not chartered urban government [4][15].

## Production hooks

- **Quest:** **Wrong forum** — arrest a vassal's servant in St Olaf parish; bishop's officer vs town Vogt. **Double tax** — Preen's extraordinary levy vs council relief for burghers. **Hill gate** — message for bishop Olaf must pass council night watch. **After 16 May** — Order banner on Toompea but council seal still on market proclamations [`people.town-council-and-officers.01`]. **Sanctuary** — fugitive Estonian in Dominican precinct (composite).
- **Narrative:** Henning (watch) answers to **council**, not viceroy. Jürgen (merchant) invokes **Hanseatic** solidarity and council protection. Kaja moves between **unrecognised rebel law** and town gates. Bishop Olaf and Marquard Breide give **contradictory safe-conducts** in April.
- **Dialogue:** "Des koninges brief" (crown writ) vs "des Rates urkunde" (council deed) vs "des bisscops gericht" (bishop's court) — three registers of authority.
- **Dev / systems:** Tile flag `jurisdiction: {all_linn, toompea, manor, order_camp, church_precinct}`; `authority_strength` 0–3 per faction per week from timeline table; gate state `burgher_closed` boolean after curfew.
