# Liturgical calendar — Spring 1343 (April–May)

## Brief for Narrative, Quest, and Dev

You are scheduling **Catholic time in Julian-calendar Reval** for the playable **April–May 1343** window. All dates below are **Old Style (Julian)** unless labelled otherwise.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Easter Sunday = 13 April 1343** (Julian). Lent ran **Ash Wednesday 26 February** through **Holy Saturday 12 April**; **meat and dairy abstinence** applied for burghers and clergy under standard Western rules — fish, bread, and vegetables permitted `plausible composite` [1][2][3].
2. **St George's Night = Wednesday 23 April 1343** — **ten days after Easter**, not two. The uprising is **post-Easter**, during the **April field-work season**, not in Lent [4][5].
3. **Holy days that close routine trade** in a Lübeck-law town: **Sundays**, **Easter Monday**, **Ascension (22 May)**, **Pentecost Sunday (31 May)** at minimum; **Good Friday** is strict fast and near-total public quiet `plausible composite` from Hanseatic feast ordinances, not a Reval 1343 council text [6][7].
4. **St George (23 April)** is a **major saint's feast** with parish observance, but **no attested Reval market closure** for that day — rural rebels use the **feast-night calendar anchor**, not a town holy-day shutdown [4][8].
5. **Campaign battles:** **Kanavere 11 May (Sunday)**; **Sõjamäe 14 May (Wednesday)** — Kanavere falls on a **Sunday** (reduced routine trade); Sõjamäe on an ordinary weekday [4][9].
6. **Monastic rhythm:** Dominican and Cistercian houses keep **Office hours**; Dominican **fish-and-beer economy** peaks after Easter when Lent ends [`churches-and-religious-houses.md`](./churches-and-religious-houses.md) [10].
7. ~~**Do not use** the **21 April Easter** date in [`hanseatic-trade-and-season.md`](../economy/hanseatic-trade-and-season.md) — computus error; reconcile to **13 April** [1][11].~~ **RESOLVED:** Easter date corrected to **13 April** in `hanseatic-trade-and-season.md` (P0-166).
8. **Estonian rural layer:** Parish **Easter duties** and **tithes** press manors in April; vernacular **St George's feast** overlaps with mobilisation tradition — syncretic colour only, not attested church calendar text in Estonian [12][13].

### Observance and work rules

**Lent (26 Feb – 12 Apr).** Western practice forbade **meat, lard, eggs, and dairy (lacticinia)** for the faithful; **fish** permitted and **imported herring/salt fish** economically important in Hanseatic Lent [`hanseatic-trade-and-season.md`](../economy/hanseatic-trade-and-season.md) `plausible composite` [2][3][14]. **Burgher elites** could afford **spiced fish and imported luxuries** even in Lent — Livonian merchant-festival evidence is **15th-c.** but directionally valid `plausible composite` [14]. **Smithy charcoal work** continued — craft labour was **not** suspended, only **feasting foods** restricted `plausible composite` [2][15].

**Holy Week.** **Good Friday** and **Easter Sunday** frame the last week before the campaign's main action. **No attested 1343 Reval curfew bell name** for these days [`watch-duty-and-town-defence.md`](../military/watch-duty-and-town-defence.md).

**Sundays and double feasts.** Lübeck and other Hanse towns **fined craftsmen and pedlars for trading on holy days**; **courts did not sit** except urgent travel cases `plausible composite` for Reval under Lübeck law [6][7]. **Sunday markets** were increasingly moved to weekdays in the 14th c. across northern Europe [16] — **Reval's weekly market weekday is not attested for 1343** (gap).

**St George's Night vs church calendar.** Chronicles anchor the uprising to **23 April**, the feast of St George [4]. This is **not** a moveable feast and **does not coincide with Easter** in 1343 (Easter was **13 April**). Rural **beacon fires on feast eves** are `folklore`-adjacent calendar practice in Baltic historiography, not a parish ordinance [4][13].

**Danish / Order / Hanseatic seams.** Town observes **Roman-use liturgy** through parish and mendicant clergy [`churches-and-religious-houses.md`](./churches-and-religious-houses.md). **Bishop Olaf of Roskilde** on Toompea is a **political actor** in the same weeks as Easter and George feasts [17]. **Livonian Order** military calendar ignores town feast closures once campaigning begins (May battles on ordinary weekdays) [4][9].
