# Reval cart tolls and Fuhr rent (1340–1343)

## Brief for Quest / Dev / Dialogue

You are pricing **cart movement through forum throats, gate aprons, and harbour margins** in April–May 1343 — not inventing a modern wheel tax or a municipal Uber tariff.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **No attested wheel tax or per-cart gate toll in 1340–1343 Reval.** A dedicated **AWB / council search** for *Wagenzoll*, *Radsteuer*, *Fuhrpacht*, and *Fuhrgeld* in published Tallinn registers **returned no dated 1340–1343 lines** this tick **(gap)** [1][2][3]. Do **not** script a pfennig-per-wheel toll as attested fact.
2. **What *is* attested nearby:** **1248** royal privilege declares Reval burghers **free from customs duties** (*liberi a teloniis* tradition) — limits **external crown tolls**, not internal market police [4]. **1343** council tax records show **in-kind substitution** for a smith's cash levy (armour pieces instead of a mark) — proves fiscal pressure, **not** a cart tariff [5].
3. ***Fuhr* ≠ cart toll.** In Lübeck-law usage *Fuhr* / *Fuhre* can mean **transport servitude** on a manor plot or **a cart-load measure** (*eine Fuhr Holz*) — not a Hanseatic "wheel tax" [6][7]. A **voorimees** (carter) with a **kitchen garden near the Coastal Gate** is attested in 14th-c. property books — profession exists; **rent amount does not** [8].
4. **Working revenue model for carts (composite):** Council collects on **goods and behaviour**, not axles — **steelyard weighing** at forum/harbour, **market-peace fines** for blocking the throat, **gate/curfew breaches** under **Art. 31** Burspraken logic [`reval-law-codex-arms-and-watch.md`](../power/reval-law-codex-arms-and-watch.md) [9][10]. Harbour **crane and landing dues** are a separate dossier (`R-048`) [11].
5. **Quest-usable fee bands when ordinance is silent:** Carter hire **4–12 schillings** per urban barrel run (`plausible composite` from Hanse haulage comparanda) [12]; forum blockage fine **3 marks** scale under **Art. 91** transmission (`plausible composite`) [9]; **do not** mix with attested **60 schilling** witness-tier fines [9].
6. **April 1343 overlay:** Pre–St George's Night carts queue at **Viru apron** and **Vanaturg throat**; post–23 Apr **inland grain/charcoal carts stall** while **harbour lighter-to-cart** runs may continue under siege tension [`hanseatic-trade-and-season.md`](hanseatic-trade-and-season.md) [13].
7. **Danish / Hanseatic / Order:** **Council Rat** sets internal market orders; **Danish crown** holds distant customs interest; **Livonian Order** does not levy Lower Town cart fees before **16 May 1343** handover [`jurisdictions-of-reval.md`](../power/jurisdictions-of-reval.md) `plausible composite` [14].

## Production hooks

- **Quest:** **Harbour barrel run** — negotiate **6–10 sch.** carter fee (`plausible composite`) or fail curfew; **no** wheel-tax line item. **Forum jam** — Henning cites **Art. 91** after cart blocks Vanaturg throat. **Smuggle osmond** — bribe carter, not pay municipal axle fee (none attested). **Siege week** — cart abandoned at Viru queue; council still tries to fine **dumping**, not collect **Radsteuer** [13][16].
- **Dialogue:** MLG *Fuhrmann*, *Karren*, *Wagen*, *Fuhr* (load), *Waage* (steelyard), *Zoll* (use only for **goods/harbour** context post-1343 tariff knowledge — avoid as 1343 attested cart toll); Estonian *voorimees*; *de rat heft setten* for blockage order.
- **Map:** **No toll booth POI** at Viru or Coastal Gate unless tagged `invented`; `cart_queue_node` at Vanaturg throat; `weigh_station` at forum/harbour margin [`old-market-vanaturg.md`](../topography/old-market-vanaturg.md).
- **Dev / systems:** `cart_toll_pfennig: null` default (attested gap); `carter_hire_schilling: {min:4, max:12, confidence: plausible_composite}`; `cargo_weigh_fee` deferred to `R-048`; `forum_blockage_fine_mark: 3` with `confidence: plausible_composite`; `siege_inland_cart_active: false` after 1343-04-23.
