# Merchant cart and road transport (Spring 1343 Reval)

## Brief for Art / Map / Quest / Dev

You are building **A-004 `supply_cart.glb`** and staging **cart traffic on forum throats, harbour approaches, and Harju roads** — not a Hussite war wagon, not a sprung Victorian farm cart, and not an ox-team prairie schooner.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Default urban supply vehicle = two-wheel horse cart** (*Karren* / *Wagen* in MLG usage overlaps; use *Karren* for the smaller two-wheeler). **Open front**, **lattice or wicker sides**, **hinged rear gate**, **single horse in shafts** — North German comparandum pattern `plausible composite` [1][2].
2. **Four-wheel wagons** appear for **heavy bulk** (timber, stone blocks, multiple cloth bales) on **harbour margins and wall-building yards** — wider track, slower turn; do **not** make them the default alley vehicle `plausible composite` [1][3].
3. **Measured Tallinn wheel track:** Rescue archaeology on exported town midden records a **cart path wheelbase of 1.26–1.40 m** — use **~1.3 m track** for Reval street meshes `attested` find; context is **15th-c. export landfill**, back-projected to 1340s practice `plausible composite` [4].
4. **Draught animal = horse**, not ox, for **burgher supply and Harju carting** in April–May; oxen remain **hinterland plough** animals `plausible composite` [5][6]. Estonian **horses were export-controlled strategic goods** — town carters work hired or owned teams under Rat oversight `attested` [7].
5. **Spring 1343 loads:** **Grain sacks, beer barrels, salt kegs, iron bar bundles, charcoal sacks** on inbound routes; **hemp/flax bales, hides, empty barrels** outbound — align with [`hanseatic-trade-and-season.md`](hanseatic-trade-and-season.md). **Post–St George's Night (23 Apr):** inland **grain and charcoal carts stall**; **harbour lighter traffic** may continue `plausible composite` [8][9].
6. **Sledges** (*Schlitten*) are **winter Novgorod-corridor** kit and **mud-season fallback** on Harju roads — not the default cobbled-street vehicle in late April `plausible composite` [10][11].
7. **Materials:** **Oak or ash** frame, **elm** wheel stock (English reconstruction practice), **iron strakes** on wheel rims, **wrought nails** at joints — weathered **natural grey wood**, dark iron `plausible composite` [2][12].
8. **Do not show:** rubber tyres, metal-paneled bodies, coach springs, covered Conestoga silhouettes, or **municipal dung-cart livery** (no attested 1343 Reval cart contract) [`reval-street-cleaning-ordinances-1340s.md`](../power/reval-street-cleaning-ordinances-1340s.md).

### Regional relevance (Danish / Hanseatic / Order)

- **Danish crown** regulates **horse export** at Narva with stamped certificates — town carters rely on **local Estonian horse stock** and imported teams `attested` [7].
- **Hanseatic burghers** (Great Guild merchants) may **own cart and warehouse** pairs on Pikk; **Estonian carters** appear as **porters and drivers** without guild membership [`estonian-and-german-populations`](../people/estonian-and-german-populations.md) `plausible composite` [8][20].
- **Livonian Order** does not administer Lower Town cart traffic in April 1343; **wall-repair stone carts** on Toompea/St Michael belt are **Danish-council** work [`ecclesiastical-precinct-boundaries-1343`](../religion/ecclesiastical-precinct-boundaries-1343.md) `plausible composite` [21].

### Gap statement

No published **1340–1343 Reval customs tariff** names **cart tolls**, **wheel tax**, or **carter guild** in sources reviewed this tick. Vehicle forms are **composite from Hanseatic art, English manuscript comparanda, and one Tallinn wheel-rut measurement** — not excavated cart hardware from a 1343 house plot **attested gap** [4][22].
