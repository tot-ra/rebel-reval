# Coinage, prices, and measures (Spring 1343 Reval)

## Brief for Dev / Quest / Dialogue

You are pricing **April 1343 Lower Town Reval** under **Danish rule with no local mint since 1332**. Money is **silver weighed and counted**, not a tidy decimal shop till.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **No local coin since 1332.** Danish crown minting in Tallinn stopped with the 1332 succession crisis; Reval does not strike again until the Order resumes minting in **1363** — the **artig does not exist in 1343** [1][2]. Do not put *artig* on shop signs or in dialogue.
2. **What jingles in purses:** Residual **crown bracteates** and older **Reval pfennigs** plus **Hanseatic imports** — Lübeck and Hamburg **Hohlpfennige**, **Visby gut coins**, occasional Swedish örtug — all accepted by weight and count at the market [`economy.coinage-prices-and-measures.05`] [1][3].
3. **Account in marks and schillings.** Large sums use the **silver/Riga mark** (*marca argenti*, ~208 g) or the **Lübeck counting mark** (16 schilling = 192 pfennig). Daily small change is **pfennig**; Tallinn books also use **öre** (8 öre = 1 mark) and **örtug** (24 örtug = 1 mark) [1][4][5].
4. **Bulk trade uses weight marks, not coins.** Grain, iron, furs, and herring are sold by **pfund**, **lispfund**, and **schiffspfund** on certified steelyards at the harbour and forum [`economy.coinage-prices-and-measures.01`] [6].
5. **Price table below is mixed confidence.** Reval lacks a published 1343 commodity tariff; **property and tax lines from Wittschopbuch/Denkelbuch** anchor the town; **food, tools, and wages** use Hanseatic comparanda labelled `plausible composite` [7][8][9].
6. **April siege premium.** Nominal prices for **grain, beer, charcoal, and ferry haulage** run **25–50 % above the table** once St George's Night closes the hinterland — a valid quest beat, not a permanent retune [10].
7. **Dialogue units.** Burghers quote **schilling** and **pfennig** for market purchases; council fines and house sales use **marks**; Estonian labourers may think in **örtug** bundles. Latin charters use *marca argenti* [4][5].

## Findings

### Coinage and accounting (April 1343)

| Unit | Relationship | Physical form in 1343 | Confidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Silver / Riga mark** (*marca argenti*, *marca Rigensis*) | Weight & account unit ≈ **208 g** fine silver | Bar, ingot, or counted coin to weight | attested [1][6] | Basis for property sales and crown levies |
| **Tallinn/Reval mark** (money of account) | **1 mark = 8 öre = 24 örtug = 288 penning** | Mixed coin bags weighed at market | attested (1265 decree; Wittschopbuch usage) [1][4] | **öre** and **örtug** are book divisions, not necessarily separate coins |
| **Lübeck mark** (counting) | **1 Ml. = 16 ßl. = 192 dl.** | Schilling is accounting until 1432 minting | plausible composite [5][9] | Mother-city norm for Hanse merchants |
| **Schilling** (*ßl.*) | **12 pfennig** (Lübeck); fines may cite **60 schilling** | Usually **accounting**, not a physical coin in 1343 | attested fine scale [11]; coin form `plausible composite` [5] | Law-courts dossier perjury fine = 60 ßl. [11] |
| **Pfennig** (*denarius*, *d.*) | Smallest common silver coin | **Hohlpfennig** imports, residual bracteates | attested circulation pattern [1][3] | Verify weight — clipped coins common |
| **Artig** | Post-1363 Livonian coin | **Absent in 1343** | attested later [2] | **Anachronism if used in Spring 1343 scenes** |
| **Visby gut** | Bullion coin by weight | Swedish Gotland import | attested in Livonian hoards from 1330s [3] | Common in Baltic purses after local mint stop |

**Mint stop narrative:** Leimus places the end of Tallinn minting at **1332**, when Danish royal minting collapsed and Hanseatic **Hohlpfennige** and **Visby** coins displaced local crown bracteates in the exchange circuit [1]. April 1343 sits in that **import-coin interregnum** eleven years before the 1346 sale and seventeen years before Order artigs.

**Regional arc:** Danish crown (remote), town council (market regulation, steelyards), Great Guild merchants (credit and foreign coin arbitrage), Livonian Order (no Reval mint right until 1346 sale) [1][12].

Keep a `siege_multiplier` float defaulting to **1.0** until St George's Night, then **1.35**.

## Production hooks

- **Dev:** Implement `Currency` with `{pfennig, schilling, mark}` integers; conversion **12 d. = 1 ßl.**, **16 ßl. = 1 Ml.**; weight units `{pfund, lispfund, schiffspfund}` for harbour/mineral trade only; `siege_price_multiplier` hook; shop tables keyed from price matrix above with `confidence` metadata per row.
- **Quest:** **Clipped pfennig** — money-changer accuses Estonian haulier. **Osmund barrel** payment in marks vs counted pfennigs. **Council fine** after perjury at **60 ßl.** [11]. **Charcoal price spike** blocks forge commission. **False artig** — player spots anachronistic coin offered by ignorant looters (teaching moment).
- **Dialogue:** MLG *Pfennig*, *Schilling*, *Mark*, *Öre*, *Pfund*, *Witten*; Estonian *mark* as loanword; **never** *Artig* in 1343. Price haggling in pfennigs for beer and bread; house talk in marks. Reference [`economy.coinage-prices-and-measures.02`] for counting scene tone.
- **Art:** Market table with **beam steelyard** and **basket of mixed coins**; merchant **pocket tumbrel** [`economy.coinage-prices-and-measures.01`]; **silver bar** stock for wholesale [`economy.coinage-prices-and-measures.04`]; no artig coins in any 1343 prop handout.
