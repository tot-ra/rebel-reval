# Hanseatic trade and season (Spring 1343 Reval)

## Brief for Dev / Quest / Dialogue

You are staging **April–May 1343 trade** at a **Danish Hanseatic entrepôt** on the Novgorod corridor — not the warehousing monopoly of the 15th century and not a frozen winter port.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Reval is a transit hub, not a factory.** Bulk eastern goods (**furs, wax, timber, honey**) and western imports (**cloth, salt, metal, wine, herring**) **consolidate and re-ship** through the merchant landing below the Coastal Gate and the forum steelyards [`topography.harbour-and-shoreline.md`](../topography/harbour-and-shoreline.md) [1][2][3].
2. **No forced local warehousing yet.** Tallinn's **warehousing privilege** (*Lagerecht*) dates to **1346**, three years after the game date — April 1343 merchants may still **trans-ship without mandatory Reval middlemen**, though Great Guild practice already favours local storage on Pikk [`coinage-prices-and-measures.md`](./coinage-prices-and-measures.md) [4]. Do **not** script a 1346 staple law in Spring 1343 scenes.
3. **Novgorod route:** Sea leg **Reval → Neva mouth (Kotlin) → Ladoga → Volkhov → Novgorod Peterhof**; land alternatives **Dorpat–Pskov** or **Narva–Wesenberg** when Swedish conflict or ice blocks the water leg [5][6][7]. Convoys run **twice yearly** — **summer and winter travellers** (*Sommer- und Winterfahrer*) [5][8].
4. **April–May season band:** **Ice break-up** on the Gulf of Finland is **under way**; **first coastal sailings plausible** but **mud, grounding risk, and variable ice pans** persist — exact 1343 ice-off day **unknown** [`spring-climate-and-living-world.md`](../nature/spring-climate-and-living-world.md) [9][10]. **Winter Novgorod caravans** have largely **returned**; **summer convoy** is **forming**, not yet at peak.
5. **Calendar anchors:** **Easter 1343 = 13 April** (Julian) [11]; **St George's Night = 23 April** — ten days after Easter, not two. Post-Easter market activity is **plausible**; Lent fasting has **just ended**.
6. **What keeps moving after 23 April:** **Harbour labour, roadstead lighters, and Hanseatic cogs standing offshore** can continue under **siege tension**; **interior cart traffic** from Harju manors **fractures** [12][13]. Apply **×1.25–1.5 scarcity** on grain, beer, charcoal from [`coinage-prices-and-measures.md`](./coinage-prices-and-measures.md) [14].
7. **What stalls:** **Overland grain and livestock** from rebel-held countryside; **charcoal and forge fuel** cart routes; **Estonian stevedore labour** outside the wall; **land Novgorod corridor** through Harju; **Swedish relief fleet** (arrives **18–19 May**, too late) [12][15][16].
8. **Credit is normal.** Long-distance settlement uses **bills of exchange** (*Wechsel*), **money-changers**, and **sealed partnership letters** on the **Novgorod–Reval–Lübeck–Bruges** chain — not bags of coin on every cog [17][18].
9. **Political seam:** Hanseatic merchants **prioritise open lanes and debt collection** over Danish crown loyalty; after St George's Night they **align with Order restoration** against rural chaos threatening property [19][20].

## Findings

### Reval's place in the Hanseatic network

Reval joined the Hanseatic League **by the late 13th century** (city tradition: **1285**) and functioned as a **major eastern Baltic entrepôt** linking western cloth-and-salt markets to the **Novgorod kontor** (Peterhof) [1][2][21]. By the **14th century** Livonian ports — **Reval, Riga, Dorpat, Pärnu** — concentrated **grain, hemp, flax, timber, and naval stores** exports while importing **finished western goods** [3][22].

| Role | April 1343 verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Novgorod gateway | **Present** — standard sea staging post on Lübeck–Reval–Neva route | attested route pattern [5][6][7] |
| Bulk entrepôt | **Present** — goods consolidated before westbound Sound sailings | plausible composite [3][22] |
| Forced transit monopoly | **Absent** — warehousing right only from **1346** | attested privilege date [4] |
| Rival to Lübeck | **Emerging** — eastern towns seek routes outside Lübeck tutelage by late 14th c. | attested trend [3]; 1343 intensity `plausible composite` |

**Regional arc:** **Danish crown** (remote revenue interest), **town council** (market courts, steelyards, harbour access), **Great Guild merchants** (credit, storage, eastern-route partnerships), **Livonian Order** (extramural military pressure from late April; no harbour administration inside walls) [4][19][20].

### The Novgorod route and convoy rhythm

**Sea route (primary):** Hanseatic cogs sail **Lübeck → Reval → Gulf of Finland → Neva mouth**; cargo transfers to **Russian river boats** (*lodja*) for **Ladoga and the Volkhov** to Novgorod [5][7]. **Russian pilots** are hired for rapids; merchants may dispense with them on the return leg [7].

**Land routes (secondary, conflict-season):** From Reval, **Dorpat–Pskov** or **Wesenberg (Rakvere)–Narva** corridors with **armed caravans and sledges in winter** [5][8]. Land routes grew when **Novgorod–Sweden conflict** threatened the water leg in the **late 13th–early 14th centuries** [6] — **1343 Harju uprising** makes overland Harju segments **unsafe**, not the sea lane itself.

**Convoy calendar:**

| Convoy | Typical window | April 1343 status | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Winter travellers** (*Winterfahrer*) | Depart Livonian ports **late autumn**; sledge/ice legs | **Returned or stranded** — winter season closed | plausible composite [5][8] |
| **Summer travellers** (*Sommerfahrer*) | **Late spring–summer** after ice break | **Preparing** — manifests, cargo assembly on Pikk | plausible composite [5][8][10] |
| Peterhof residence rule | Merchants stay **fixed summer/winter terms** only | Applies to **Novgorod yard**, not Reval storage | attested kontor rule [8] |

**1346 institutional note (post-game):** From **1346**, the Hanseatic president at Novgorod was chosen by **Lübeck and Visby representatives** [8] — a shift toward mother-city control; **April 1343 predates** this formalisation.

### Sailing season, ice, and April–May 1343

Hanseatic practice: **larger ships lay up November–March** on the Baltic; **spring reopening** follows ice break-up [23][9]. Gulf of Finland ice **typically clears late March–May** with **year-to-year variance** [9][10].

| Date / window | Trade read | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Early April 1343** | Last winter stocks; ice fringe possible offshore; harbour mud | plausible composite [9][10][24] |
| **13 April (Easter)** | Lent ended; market feasting resumes; convoy preparation | attested calendar [11]; trade volume `plausible composite` |
| **23 April (St George's Night)** | Countryside attacks begin; **interior supply chains fracture** | attested [12][15] |
| **Late April (siege)** | **Harbour may still operate** under walls; roadstead resupply **plausible**; overland carts **risk ambush** | plausible composite [13][24] |
| **11 May** | Crown **grain levy** still collected from town (*DD* III:1, no. 321) — council resources strained | attested [25] |
| **14 May** | Mainland rebel army defeated at Sõjamäe — **interior routes remain unsafe** for weeks | attested [15] |
| **18–19 May** | Swedish fleet arrives **too late**; negotiated peace | attested [16] |

**Dev hook:** `trade_season_phase` enum: `late_winter` → `ice_break` → `pre_convoy` → `siege_interior_stall` → `post_sõjamäe_uneasy`.

### Staple rules and merchant practice

| Rule / practice | 1343 status | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Lübeck law** (1248) | Town governed under **Lübeck Rights**; market courts follow Hanseatic commercial norms | attested [4][21] |
| **Great Guild trade restrictions** | Who may trade what, warehouse licensing, guild court before magistrate | plausible composite [2] — guild dossier R-012 in review |
| **Warehousing / forced middleman** | **Not yet** — privilege **1346** | attested [4] |
| **Steelyard weighing** | Certified beam balances at **forum and harbour** for schiffspfund lots | attested practice [14][26] |
| **Fur inspection** | At **Novgorod**, German merchants may **chip wax/fur samples**; Russians buy cloth lots **unopened** | attested treaty asymmetry [6] |
| **Bills of exchange** | **Wechsel** settlement on Novgorod–Reval–Lübeck–Bruges chain; money-changers at Bruges interface | attested Hanseatic instrument [17][18] |
| **Partnership seals** | Guild and company seals on **credit instruments** | plausible composite [17][27] |

**Credit scene rule:** A merchant can **owe in Lübeck marks** while holding **no local coin** — payment falls due at **named term** (e.g. Michaelmas) via **drawer–drawee** chain [17]. Clipped **pfennigs** are still verified with **pocket tumbrels** at the forum [`coinage-prices-and-measures.01`] [14].

|---|---|---|---|
| **Manor grain carts** | **Stalled** | Harju rebel control; ambush risk on roads to Viru Gate | attested siege [12][13] |

| **Charcoal / wood fuel** | **Reduced** | Forest routes unsafe; forge premium valid | plausible composite [14] |
| **Estonian labour** | **Split** | Some servants remain in town; exterior workers **absent or suspect** | plausible composite [20] |
| **Sea imports (salt, cloth, metal)** | **Continues** | Cogs in roadstead; lighters to jetty if gates open | plausible composite [13][24] |
| **Novgorod eastbound convoy** | **Delayed, not cancelled** | Merchants **wait for truce clarity** before committing furs | plausible composite [5][19] |
| **Land Novgorod route** | **Stalled** | Harju–Pskov corridor unusable | plausible composite [6][12] |
| **Swedish intervention trade** | **Fails** | Fleet **18–19 May** — too late for siege relief | attested [16] |
| **Crown grain levy** | **Still attempted** | **11 May** collection from town [25] | attested |

**Narrative beat:** The **harbour can look busy** while the **market square ration queue grows** — exterior supply fails before maritime trade does.

### Danish, Hanseatic, and Order context

- **Danish crown:** Extraordinary taxation by viceroy **Konrad Preen (1340–1343)** helped trigger the uprising [20]; merchants are **not the primary rebel target** but **manor destruction** destroys their rural suppliers.
- **Hanseatic Reval:** Great Guild and council **protect property and open markets**; natural **Order alignment** once rebels threaten the walled town [19].
- **Livonian Order:** **Extramural** from April; **no kontor harbour rights** inside Reval walls; military corridor separate from merchant landing [24].
