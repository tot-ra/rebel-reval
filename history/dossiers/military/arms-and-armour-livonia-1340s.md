# Arms and armour in Livonia, 1340s (Reval focus)

## Brief for Dev / Art / Quest

You are equipping **Spring 1343 Reval**: burgher militia, town smiths, invited Order knights, and Estonian rebels outside the walls. **No firearms.** Mail and fine blades are **imported or specialist**; everyday ironwork is **local smith output**.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Kalev's forge makes and repairs:** nails, hinges, straps, horseshoes, spearheads, axe heads, arrow/bolt points, simple crossbow prods and stirrups, sword furniture, tool steel, and watch-kit iron caps — not finished mail shirts or plate harness [1][2][3].
2. **Burgher watch minimum (composite Hanse norm):** padded jack, iron cap, sword, crossbow **or** spear/gleve — see tier table in [`watch-duty-and-town-defence.md`](watch-duty-and-town-defence.md) [4].
3. **Order knight kit (after late-April invitation):** mail hauberk, surcoat with black cross, heater shield, lance, sword — visually distinct from burgher militia [5][6].
4. **Estonian rebel kit:** farm tools (axe, scythe, club), improvised spears, bows; **no uniform armour**; occasional captured mail or cap is loot, not standard issue [7][8].
5. **Materials on the bench:** bloomery/charcoal iron for most work; **imported steel** for better edges; leather for scabbards and crossbow bindings; ash/oak shafts from carpenters; linen/wool padding from tailors — smith supplies metal only [2][3].
6. **Do not show:** handgonnes, full plate harness as burgher default, identical Order/rebel silhouettes, or Kalev forging a complete mail shirt in one commission session.

### Order versus rebel equipment (April–May 1343)

| Side | Body armour | Melee | Missile | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Burgher militia** | Padded jack; iron cap; mail rare on wealthy only | Sword, spear/glaive | Crossbow common on walls | Lübeck-law obligation [4][10] |
| **Livonian Order knight** | Mail hauberk, coif, surcoat | Sword, lance | None primary in street fight | Invited late April; distinct heraldry [5][6] |
| **Danish garrison (Toompea)** | Mail-capable men-at-arms | Sword, spear | Crossbow possible | Castle garrison, not Lower Town watch [11] |
| **Estonian rebel levy** | None or looted cap/mail piecemeal | Axes, clubs, scythes, spears | Bows, hunting crossbows rare | Chronicle "farm tools" trope [7][8] |
| **Rebel leaders / "kings"** | Mixed loot and gifts | Sword if noble captive gear | Bow | Named Four Kings use diverse kit [7] |

**Narrative seam:** Kalev can forge **honest watch iron** for the council, **subtle defects** on requisitioned spearheads, or **secret marks** on bolts — but cannot plausibly arm an entire rebel army from one Lower Town shop without detection [`guild-structure.md`](../crafts/guild-structure.md).

## Production hooks

- **Art:** Burgher watch = **padded textile silhouette + iron cap + spear** [`military.arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.03`]; wall crossbowmen [`military.arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.02`]; upper-tier mail [`military.arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.04`]; forge interior [`military.arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.01`]; rebel levy = axe/scythe + wool tunic, no plate (no licensed rebel-specific plate — use tool silhouettes from forge plate).
- **Map:** `weapon_rack` props carry **spears and glaives** in knights' court; **no musket shapes**; Viru checkpoint watch carry **crossbow or spear** variants.
- **Character:** **Kalev** repairs and forges iron; does not wear mail by default. **Henning** sergeant = **glaive + cap**. **Order knight** (P5-008) = mail silhouette distinct from watchman spear profile.
- **Quest:** Council **arms muster** inspects household kit; **smuggled blade** to rebels is guild offence; **Bitter Brew** / **Bell and Chain** forged metal must stay within spear/bolt/lock scale.
- **Dialogue:** MLG *Schwert*, *Spieß*, *Armbrust*, *Kettenhemd*, *Eisenhut*; Estonian *kirves*, *oda* (spear) for rebels — register split per R-024.
- **Dev / systems:** Tag items `smith_producible: true|false`; `materials: [iron, steel, wood, linen, leather]`; `faction_kit: burgher|order|rebel`; forge commissions whitelist producible tags only.
