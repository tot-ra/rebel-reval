# Lower Town weapon and armour finds, c. 1340–1450

## Brief for Art / Map / Quest / Dev

You need **what Reval archaeology actually shows on the ground** for iron projectiles, blades, and armour — not manuscript battle scenes. The record is **thin for in-situ 1343 house plots** and **richer only in secondary dumps and the Lootsi roadstead**.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **In-situ Lower Town occupation (within walls):** Rescue digs inside the wall circuit have produced **many 13th–early 14th-century artefacts** but **no published weapon assemblage** comparable to the Jahu landfill; individual arrowheads and crossbow bolts appear in house and street layers, yet are rarely illustrated or inventory-published per plot **attested gap** [1][2]. Do **not** litter every cellar with sword blades.
2. **Jahu / Väike-Patarei export landfill (Kalamaja, outside walls):** A **second-half-15th-century** midden of town refuse exported from inside the walls documents burgher discard: **11+ massive crossbow bolts**, **two dagger blades**, **one pike tip**, **one sword pommel**, **chain-mail and plated-mail fragments**, plus **post-1343 firearms** that must **not** appear in Spring 1343 scenes **attested** [3][4].
3. **Lootsi 8 wreck (Härjapea mouth, maritime):** **100+ arrowheads** and **a few springald (springald) heads** in a **14th-century cog** context; dendro felling **c. 1360** — **near-contemporary comparandum**, not a burgher household assemblage **attested** [5][6].
4. **Projectile dominance:** Archaeology supports **iron arrowheads and crossbow bolts** as the commonest **recoverable** weapon iron; **complete swords and mail shirts** are **rare in published Tallinn urban contexts** for this window **attested gap** [1][3][5][7].
5. **Spring 1343 rule:** Use Jahu **firearm barrel fragments and lead bullets** only as evidence that such items existed in **15th-century** town discard — **exclude from April–May 1343** kit tables **attested anachronism** [3].
6. **Map placement:** Weapon props belong at **watch posts, knights' courts, smith scrap piles, and harbour salvage** — not random domestic interiors unless a dossier flags a wealthy arms-owning household [`arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.md`](arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.md).

## Findings

### Method: three context types

| Context | What it proves for 1343 Reval | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **In-situ occupation layer** inside walls | Weapons used or stored on the plot; best for household/watch kit | thin published record [1][2] |
| **Exported urban landfill** (Jahu) | What burghers eventually threw away; strong on bolts and armour scraps | solid for **15th c.** export; **plausible composite** back-projection to 1340s kit mix [3][4] |
| **Maritime / foreshore** (Lootsi) | Bulk ammunition and shipboard arms in the roadstead trade sphere | solid for **mid-14th c.** projectiles; not Lower Town street context [5][6] |

Russow notes that by **c. 1360** burgher regulations already required refuse to be **carried outside the walls** to designated areas, ending the earlier practice of spreading waste horizontally on selected plots within the town (e.g. Town Hall Square leatherworking fill). That shift explains why **late-medieval militaria clusters in suburban dumps** rather than in 15th-century house cellars **attested** [4].

#### C — Lootsi 8 cog wreck (foreshore / roadstead, 14th c.)

| Find | Count | Category | Context | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iron arrowheads | **100+** | Projectile | Stacked in stern collections with tools; **shipboard cargo or arms** | attested [5][6] |
| Springald heads (small spearheads) | **Few** | Artillery projectile | Identified per Mäesalu 2000 typology; **wall-defence class** | attested [5][11] |

Wreck lay in **shallow Härjapea-mouth sediments** c. 1.5 m below modern grade [`harbour-and-shoreline.md`](../topography/harbour-and-shoreline.md) **attested** [5].

### Regional relevance (Danish Estonia / Hanseatic Reval / Order)

- **Danish-Reval burgher militia** kit implied by Jahu **crossbow bolts and polearm tip** aligns with Lübeck-law watch tiers — not Order knightly harness [`reval-law-codex-arms-and-watch.md`](../power/reval-law-codex-arms-and-watch.md) **plausible composite** [3][13].
- **Lootsi ammunition mass** fits **Hanse roadstead trade + town defence resupply** at the harbour margin, not Order castle armoury **plausible composite** [5][14].
- **Russian-type arrowheads** at Pärnu Rd show **eastern projectile forms** in the Tallinn hinterland before or during urban growth — useful for **Estonian rebel bow** comparanda, not German burgher dress **attested typology; context uncertain** [9].

## Production hooks

- **Art:** Burgher missile iron = **short socketed crossbow bolts** ([`military.lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s.03`](../../reference/military/lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s/military.lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s.03.jpg), CC BY-SA comparandum from England) and **compact arrowheads** ([`military.lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s.04`](../../reference/military/lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s/military.lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s.04.jpg), CC BY-SA comparandum from England); **springald head** proportions from Lootsi (`military.lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s.01`, linked scholarly evidence only). The two downloaded plates are type-comparanda, not Reval finds, and do not replace the Lootsi PDF figures. **Mail** = small ring patches on wealthy figures only (`military.lower-town-weapon-finds-1340s.05`, linked plated-mail context). **No handgonnes, lead balls, or stone cannonballs** in Spring 1343.
- **Map:** Place **`iron_scrap_pile`** and **`weapon_rack`** at smith lanes and knights' courts; **do not** seed domestic yards with bolt hoards. Harbour margin may show **salvage crates** referencing Lootsi-scale arrowhead lots (quest prop, not ambient clutter).
- **Quest:** **Bitter Brew / watch resupply** — crossbow bolt batches match Jahu **massive bolt** class; **smuggled dagger blade** matches Jahu **two-blade** scale; **forged spearhead** remains plausible but **not archaeologically illustrated** for a named plot.
- **Dev / systems:** Tag finds `context: in_situ | landfill_export | maritime`; tag `chronology_ok_1343: true|false`; whitelist Spring 1343 loot tables to **bolt, arrowhead, dagger, pike tip, pommel, scabbard**; blacklist `firearm`, `cannonball`, `plate_harness_complete`.
