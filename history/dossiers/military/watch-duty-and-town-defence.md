# Watch duty and town defence (Spring 1343)

## Brief for Quest / Art / Dev

You are staging **burgher militia watch** on the mid-1343 stone circuit, not a professional garrison. The **town council** commands walls, gates, and night order inside Lower Town; the Danish viceroy on Toompea does **not** assign watch rotas. April–May 1343 is **siege posture**: gates stay controlled, wall-walk manned, rebel camp fires visible outside — but the record gives **no named watch roster** for 1343.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Who serves:** Adult male **German burghers** and household masters with citizenship standing. Journeymen and apprentices may stand in for a master on his roster night **plausible composite** [1][2]. **Estonian labourers, servants, and transients are not on the militia roll** unless explicitly flagged `invented` [2][3].
2. **Who commands:** The **council** through its sitting bench (*sitzender Rat*). Tower sections were later assigned to named commanders (**eleven** attested **~1355**; **fifteen** by **1373**) — in April 1343 use **gate-and-tower sections without named commanders** unless a scene needs a generic *Turmhüter* or *Wachtmeister* labelled `plausible composite` [4][5].
3. **Nightly routine (peaceable week, April):** Main gates **open by day**; at the **evening bell** (seasonal — treat as **~8:00–9:00 p.m.** in late April, earlier than midsummer) parish bells signal **curfew**; **eight Lower Town gates** shut, barred, and guarded; **hill gates** (Long/Short Leg) **closed from the Lower Town side** at night **plausible composite** [6][7][8]. Watch parties patrol the **arcaded wall-walk** and gate-tower fighting decks; taverns close at the bell **plausible composite** [9]. **Siege April–May:** routine tightens — no night exit without council pass; **Viru** and **Coastal** gates are the pressure points [10][11].
4. **Arms each watchman brings:** Minimum **iron cap or helmet**, **padded jack or textile armour**, **sword**, and **either crossbow or polearm (spear/gleve)** — Hanseatic urban militia norm transferred to Reval **plausible composite** [1][2][12]. Wealthy merchants may wear **mail or better harness**; a sitting councillor on inspection duty may be fully armoured **plausible composite** [12]. **No firearms** in 1343 [11].
5. **Alarm:** **Church bells** (St Olaf, St Nicholas, Holy Spirit parishes ring the hours — specific 1343 curfew bell names are **not attested**); **watchfires** on tower fighting platforms during siege; **trumpet** for muster is Hanseatic practice **plausible composite** [4][9][13]. Rebels outside answer with **horn shouts and camp fires**, not bell discipline [10].
6. **Day gate staff:** One or two **fluent gatekeepers** per major gate by day, checking persons and goods **plausible composite** from London 1282 watch ordinances transferred to Hanseatic practice [9][14]. They may sleep **inside or beside the gate passage** after curfew **plausible composite** [14].
7. **Fire duty:** Watch doubles as **fire watch** — critical in timber-yard and diele-hearth districts **plausible composite** [9][15].
8. **Order troops in town:** After the council invites the Livonian Order (**late April**), **Order knights** may appear on walls or at gates **by invitation**, not as watch commanders. They do not replace burgher obligation [3][10].
9. **Do not show:** muskets, handgonnes, Fat Margaret, Kiek in de Kök garrison, stone Long Leg gate (1380+), Viru foregate towers (1370s+), or a permanent paid night watch corps as the default — those are later or exceptional [11][16].

## Findings

### Legal basis and council authority

- Reval's **Lübeck-law** burghers inherit the Hanseatic principle that towns with *ius Lubicense* share legislation for **defence of merchants and goods** [17].
- General historiography states that in the **14th century** Tallinn/Reval inhabitants were **required to turn out for guard duty**, wearing armour and demonstrating readiness to face invaders [1][18].
- The **town council** controls walls and gates inside Lower Town; the viceroy on Toompea does not assign nightly watch — see jurisdiction table in [`jurisdictions-of-reval.md`](../power/jurisdictions-of-reval.md) [3].
- **1288** separation of Lower Town justice from castle authority supports council command of hill-gate closure from the burgher side **plausible composite** [3][7].

Confidence: obligation to defend **attested** at town-history level [1][18]; detailed roster mechanics **plausible composite** from Hanseatic urban militia literature [2][9][12].

### Concrete nightly routine (plausible composite for production)

Use this as a **default April weeknight**; tighten every row during the **late-April siege**.

| Time | Action | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunrise – ~1 hr before sunset | Eight gates open for cart and foot traffic | Viru, Coastal, Sand, Karja, Harju, Nuns', Long Leg, Short Leg | Hill gates control Toompea access [11] |
| Day | 1–2 gatekeepers check entrants | Coastal + Viru (trade), Karja (pasture traffic) | Composite from London gate ordinance [14] |
| ~8:00–9:00 p.m. (April) | **Curfew bell** from parish churches | Town-wide | Seasonal; no 1343 Tallinn bell inscription survives [19] |
| Curfew + short interval | Gates **shut, barred, chained**; wickets closed | All eight gates | General medieval practice [9] |
| Curfew | Taverns and market stalls close | Raekoja plats, harbour lanes | Composite [9] |
| Night | **Wall-watch** rotas: 4–8 men per gate/tower section | Arcaded wall-walk, gate-tower upper platforms | Section size composite from ward-watch models [9][14] |
| Night | **Hill gates** locked from Lower Town side | Pikk jalg / Lühike jalg | Mistrust seam between All-linn and Toompea [7][8] |
| Night | Lantern patrols in streets near gates | Müürivahe, Viru apron, Lootsi descent | Fire + order watch composite [9][15] |
| Dawn bell | Gates reopen; watch stands down | — | — |
| **Siege overlay** | No civilian exit without council token; doubled wall-walk; hoardings raised | Full circuit | Event attested; detail composite [10][11] |

### Arms and armour by status tier

| Tier | Who | Required kit for watch/muster | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Sitting councillor / wealthy merchant** | Ratsherr, ship-owner, large-house master | Mail or coat-of-plates over padded jack; iron basinet; sword; crossbow with belt hook; buckler optional | plausible composite [12] |
| **B — Ordinary burgher / guild master** | Smaller merchant, master craftsman | Padded jack (*Waffenrock*); iron cap; sword; crossbow **or** spear/gleve | plausible composite [2][12] |
| **C — Journeyman standing for household** | Guild journeyman on master's roster night | Borrows house kit: cap, jack, polearm; usually **no mail** | plausible composite [2] |
| **D — Apprentice / servant / Estonian townsman** | Not on militia roll | **No arms obligation**; may carry club or knife only as civilian | plausible composite [2][3] |
| **E — Order knight (after invitation)** | Livonian Order | Mail, surcoat, shield, lance/sword; **distinct from burgher kit** | attested type; presence in town attested late April [10] |

**Smith-producible vs imported:** Caps, jacks, spears, sword furniture, crossbow bolts, and simple crossbows are **plausible Reval workshop products**; mail and fine blades often **imported** — see [`arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.md`](arms-and-armour-livonia-1340s.md) [12].

### Gate priorities for April–May 1343 scenes

| Gate | Watch priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Viru** | Highest | Main road to rebel-held Harju; likely unfinished foregate works [11] |
| **Coastal / Sand** | High | Harbour smuggling, Swedish fleet approach (**18–19 May**) [10] |
| **Karja / Harju** | Medium | Pasture and smiths' lanes; moat mill zones [11] |
| **Long / Short Leg** | Political | Toompea messages, vassal movement; council closes from below [7] |
| **Nuns'** | Lower in April | NW section heavily fortified but less siege traffic [11] |

### Siege behaviour (attested frame, composite detail)

- **Late April 1343:** Estonian rebel army (~10,000) camps outside; initial skirmish below walls defeats Order field knights, but **stone walls hold** [10].
- Burghers **appeal to the Livonian Order** for military aid (chronicle tradition) while continuing wall defence **attested** [10].
- **14 May 1343:** Main rebel field army destroyed at Sõjamäe — siege pressure changes but walls still manned until political settlement [10].
- Show **wooden fighting platforms**, **hoardings**, **crossbowmen on arcaded walks**, **watchfires** — not gunports or cannon towers [5][11].

### Regional context

| Polity | Relation to Reval watch |
|---|---|
| **Danish crown / viceroy** | Commands Toompea garrison, not Lower Town watch roster [3] |
| **Town council** | Orders muster, gate hours, siege tokens [3] |
| **Livonian Order** | Extramural until invited; may reinforce walls after late April [3][10] |
| **Hanseatic peers** | Lübeck-law defence duty is shared cultural expectation [17] |
| **Estonian rebels** | Outside jurisdiction; no gate-pass; camp alarms opposite wall bells [10] |

## Production hooks

- **Art:** Night watch silhouette = **padded jack + iron cap + spear or crossbow + lantern** [`military.watch-duty-and-town-defence.02`]; wall-walk patrol on **crenellated limestone curtain** [`military.watch-duty-and-town-defence.01`]; gate passage with **barred timber doors** behind rectangular tower arch; hill-gate closure scene at **wooden Long Leg** [`military.watch-duty-and-town-defence.04`]; siege = **hoardings and fire baskets** on tower tops, not cannon [`military.watch-duty-and-town-defence.05`].
- **Map:** Gate states `{open, closed, siege_token}` per eight gates; `watch_post` markers on Viru, Coastal, Karja, Harju, hill gates; `curfew_active` boolean after evening bell; wall-walk nav along arcaded inner face [11].
- **Character:** **Henning-type watch sergeant** answers to **council**, carries **polearm as badge of office** [`military.watch-duty-and-town-defence.02`]; merchant on roster night reluctantly armoured [`military.watch-duty-and-town-defence.06`]; Estonian labourer **not** in mail; Order knight visually distinct surcoat [3][10].
- **Quest / Narrative:** Smuggle message **before curfew bell**; bribe or forge **gate token** during siege; **wrong-side arrest** when watchman detains vassal's servant (forum conflict with [`jurisdictions-of-reval.md`](../power/jurisdictions-of-reval.md)); **Bitter Brew** night encounter at cistern/checkpoint is council-watch jurisdiction; St George's Night climax at **Viru checkpoint** [10].
- **Dialogue:** MLG watch calls: *“Halt! Wer da?”* / *“Tor zu!”*; gate names *Lehmporte* (Viru), *Strantporte* (Coastal) per walls dossier [11]; avoid post-1380 gate nicknames.
- **Dev / systems:** `watch_duty` obligation per burgher household 1 night / N nights per month (default N=3, tunable); `arms_check` fail → fine or council summons; `curfew_hour` seasonal 20–21; `siege_mode` doubles watch posts and disables gate open; `alarm_state` `{none, bell, fire, assault}`.
