# Hygiene and grooming (Spring 1343 Reval)

## Brief for Character / Art / Dialogue

You are dressing **April–May 1343 Lower Town Reval** — a **Hanseatic port** where **German burghers set the civic hygiene norm**, **Estonian labourers live dirtier and cheaper**, and **public bathhouses are a regulated municipal institution**, not a Roman thermae fantasy. **No 1343 Reval grooming inventory survives**; behaviour below mixes **Tallinn archival mentions**, **Baltic archaeology**, and **labelled Hanseatic comparanda**.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Daily minimum (all tiers):** **Hands and face washed** at a **basin** each morning; **teeth rubbed** with **linen cloth** or **hazel twig** and rinsed — not skipped `plausible composite` [1][2]. **Do not show modern toothbrushes, toothpaste tubes, or daily hot showers.**
2. **Bathing rhythm:** **German burgher / craft master** visits a **city-owned public bath** (*Bad*, *Saun*) **weekly** — often **Saturday** before Sunday church — or uses a **private yard sauna** on wealthy plots `plausible composite` [3][4]. **Estonian labourer** = **basin wash + river/shore dunk** in summer; **public bath fee** is a **discretionary expense** `plausible composite` [5][6].
3. **Public baths exist in 1343:** **City-built baths rented to operators** under **council regulation**; attested near **Nunne** (Stocker sauna **1310**), **Oleviste church (sauna 1329)**, **Rataskaevu**, **Sauna street**, and **south-wall bath zone** that later names **Saunatorn** [`churches-and-religious-houses.md`](../religion/churches-and-religious-houses.md) `attested` names with **1343 operational status `plausible composite`** [3][4][7].
4. **Lice and fleas are normal, not shameful:** Everyone has had them; **clean linen** and **fine combing** are the remedy — **not daily full-body delousing scenes** unless plague-panic narrative `plausible composite` [1][8]. **Forge workers** show **soot in hair and nails** regardless of combing.
5. **Soap:** **Tallow-and-lye soap** for **hands, face, and laundry** in town; **wood-ash lye** wash for **wool and linen** `plausible composite` [1][9]. **Imported Aleppo-style hard soap** = **merchant luxury only**.
6. **Hair and beard:** **Men** visit **barber-surgeon** (*Bader*, *Balbier*) for **weekly shave** and **trim**; **clergy** keep **tonsure** [`clothing-and-status-markers.md`](clothing-and-status-markers.md) `plausible composite` [2][10]. **Women** wash and **comb long hair**; **burgher wives** wear **linen coif** to hide day-old oil `plausible composite` [1].
7. **Waste:** **Privy in rear yard** on every burgess plot; **night soil and dung** go to **cart or pit beyond the gate** — **not into the street gutter** under council pressure `plausible composite` [11][12][13]. **April mud** carries **manure smell** at **Viru Gate** and **forum throat**.
8. **Status markers:** **Clean linen collar**, **combed beard**, **unscorched fingernails** = **burgher respectability**; **persistent lice**, **tar-stained hands**, **wooden-shoe mud to the knee** = **Estonian labour tier** — use for **dialogue friction**, not cartoon filth `plausible composite` [5][14].
9. **Do not show:** **Daily full-body bathing at home**, **antiseptic hand gel**, **toilet paper**, **sewer grates**, **Roman hypocaust public baths**, or **1343 Saunatorn stone tower** (tower is **1371+**) [`ecclesiastical-precinct-boundaries-1343.md`](../religion/ecclesiastical-precinct-boundaries-1343.md) [7].

**German bath culture:** Hanse towns ran **steam baths** (*Schwitzbad*) and **tub washes** (*Warmbad*); **beard trimming** sometimes **inside the bath** `plausible composite` [4]. **Operators** (*Saunapidaja*) answered to the **Rat**; **master certification** travelled through **Lübeck** in later records — **directionally valid for Reval's German craft network** `plausible composite` [4].

**Charity bathing:** **1391** Tallinn record requires **free Thursday entry for the poor** when a bath was ceded to the city — **post-1343** but shows **municipal welfare logic** `plausible composite` for earlier practice [17]. **Do not stage a named Thursday free-bath scene as attested 1343 fact.**

**Spring 1343 note:** **April–May** is **not plague-closure season** (15th-c. syphilis panic closures are **later** [3]). Baths stay **open through siege tension** until council orders otherwise — **no attested 1343 closure** (gap).

### Lice, fleas, and bedding

- **Universal presence:** Medieval medical theory held lice **generated from body heat and dirty folds** (spontaneous-generation model) — people still **fought them** with **combing and laundry** `plausible composite` [1][8].
- **Bedding:** **Straw mattress** on **rope or board**; **herbs** (lavender, mint, wormwood) mixed into straw **where affordable** `plausible composite` [2].
- **Remedies:** **Fine-tooth comb**; **change shift**; **sun and smoke** on hung wool; **vinegar or herbal rinse** on hair — **not insecticide** `plausible composite` [1][8].
- **Social tone:** **Scratching** in public is **common**; **open shame** only when **lice become visibly crawling at council or mass** — valid **insult beat** `plausible composite`.
- **No attested 1343 Reval delousing ordinance** reviewed (gap).

### Haircut, shave, and barber practice

- **Barber-surgeon** (*Bader*, *Balbier*, *Scherer*) = **shave, trim, bleed, pull teeth, minor surgery** in one shop `plausible composite` [10].
- **Male burgher:** **Cheeks and neck shaved weekly**; **hair trimmed** monthly; **no daily razor at home** for most `plausible composite` [2][10].
- **Apprentice (Mart):** **Irregular trim**; **bowl cut** hand-me-down `plausible composite`.
- **Clergy:** **Tonsure** maintained — **Dominican** and **parish** [`clothing-and-status-markers.07`](../../reference/dailylife/clothing-and-status-markers/dailylife.clothing-and-status-markers.07.jpg) `plausible composite` [19].
- **Women:** **Husband or servant** does **not** shave head except **penance**; **market women** keep **hair braided under scarf** `plausible composite` [1].
- **Reval-specific barber street name** — **not attested in reviewed AWB**; barbers **mixed into craft lanes** near market (gap).

### Estonian rural vs German urban seam

- **Urban German norm:** **Regulated baths**, **barber**, **linen market**, **street-sweep fines** `plausible composite` [3][13].
- **Estonian labour in town:** **Participates in basin norm** but **cannot afford weekly bath + fresh linen**; **beer-excise and haulage work** = **outdoor dirt** [`estonian-and-german-populations.md`](../people/estonian-and-german-populations.md) `plausible composite` [5][6].
- **Rural sauna (*saun*):** **Healing and birth** site in folklore layer — **valid for hinterland**, **not Lower Town street default** `folklore` [22].
