# Harju village and manor (Spring 1343)

## Brief for Map / Art / Character / Quest

You are building **rural Harria (Harjumaa)** in **April–May 1343** — the zone where St George's Night begins and where rebel camps ring Reval. This is **not** a baroque estate park, **not** a 19th-c. *mõis* manor house, and **not** a street-type southern Estonian village.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Settlement form:** **Compact cluster villages** in north Harju — farms gathered in a loose knot with **open field strips** beyond, not a single street row [1][2]. Built share on developable land **8–18 %**; meadows and strips dominate [3].
2. **Tax unit:** Villages sit inside prehistoric **vakus** (*wacke*) units (~**30 ploughlands** average in Harju at the 13th-c. census) grouping **3–6 villages** per district [4][5]. LCD lists **ploughland counts**, not street plans — hamlet graph is **`plausible composite`** [6].
3. **Manor presence:** Harria had **~21 attested fiefs before 1343** (densest in Estonia) plus up to **nine more** of uncertain date — **91 % of documented pre-1343 fiefs** sat in Harria/Vironia [7]. Show **timber fortified manor yards** and a **stone church or chapel** at the lord's node; **no** plastered Baltic-German manor palace [7][8].
4. **Labour dues (April 1343):** Peasants owe **tithes**, **crown/vassal rent**, and **corvée** on lord's demesne. Pre-uprising burden was **moderate** (~**2 days/week** corvée per household in early colonisation phase) but **worsening** through the 14th c.; **extraordinary taxes 1340–1343** from Valdemar IV are a **documented trigger** [9][10][11]. Spring scene: **pre-harvest scarcity**, last stored grain, ploughing and lambing — see nature dossier [12].
5. **Who rebels:** **Not** a pure peasant jacquerie. Chroniclers call them peasants; modern scholarship stresses **multi-status coalition** — Estonian freeholders, village elders, discontented vassal clients, and manor labourers united against **Danish extraordinary taxation** and **German vassal extraction** [9][10][13]. **Four elected "kings"** lead ~**10,000** at siege — **chronicle scale**, not census [14].
6. **Signal night (23 Apr):** Fire on a **hilltop in Harria** coordinates attacks [14][15]. **Exact hill unattested** — gameplay "signal hill" is **`invented`** anchor unless a future charter pass names it [16].
7. **Roads to Reval:** **Reval–Iru/Viru gate road** (east), **Pirita valley** approach with **uncertain 1343 bridge** [3][17]; **Tallinn–Haapsalu** highway via **Padise ford** (~**50 km** west of town) [18]. Walking **3–6 hours** from inner Harju hamlets to walls is **`plausible composite`** at **4–5 km/h** on mud tracks [19].
8. **Padise (west Harju):** Cistercian abbey **under construction** April 1343 — **cellar level and partial ground floor** likely standing; rebels **burn it and kill 28 monks** who do not flee [18][20]. Use **ruin archaeology**, not restored 21st-c. museum finish [21].
9. **Do not show:** Serfdom fully "complete" (juridical fix is **15th–17th c.**) [11]; stone Order *Ordensburg* in countryside; Lutheran parish churches; dense stone village rows.

## Findings

### Administrative frame — Harria inside Danish Estonia

Harjumaa (German **Harrien**, Latin **Harria**) is the **core of the Duchy of Estonia's** mainland north: ancient **Rävala** lands with **Reval (Tallinn)** as administrative and ecclesiastical hub [1][22]. By 1343:

| Layer | Holder | Rural function | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crown** | Valdemar IV → viceroy **Konrad Preen** on Toompea | Extraordinary taxes **1340–1343**; ultimate overlord | attested [9] |
| **Vassals** | ~**80 % Westphalian German**, 18 % Danish, 2 % Estonian knights | Manorial courts, castle garrisons, rent collection | attested [23] |
| **Church** | Bishop of Reval (Olaf of Roskilde); Padise Cistercians; parish churches | Tithes, labour on church land, pastoral control | attested framework [18][24] |
| **Peasant communities** | Estonian-speaking *vakus* settlements | Field work, corvée, military levy when armed | attested framework [4][5] |

The **Harju–Viru knighthood** (*ritterschaft*) and **Landtag** traditions (12 representatives by **1282**) give vassals collective voice with burghers — **post-1248** but operative by 1343 [22]. Rural Harju is **not** under Lübeck law; manor lords judge peasants on their own benches unless appeal to crown court **`plausible composite`** [25].

### Village layout — what archaeology and LCD allow

**Northern Estonian type:** farms in **compact clusters**; northwest Harju denser than southern dispersed type [1][2]. A typical **vakus** in Harju averaged **~30 ploughlands** at the **Liber Census Daniae** (c. 1220s registration) with **3–6 villages** and roughly **25–30 % fewer farmsteads than ploughlands** [4][5].

| Element | 1343 depiction | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Farmhouses** | Log *rehielamu* tradition — dwelling + threshing under one long roof; **lower walls, high thatch** | Ethnographic continuity from LCD-era settlement names; standing buildings are **18th–19th c.** | plausible composite [1][2][26] |
| **Threshing barn (*rehi*)** | Separate or attached; **smoke-blackened threshing room**, double gates for draft | Voore (Harju) and museum comparanda **19th–20th c.** | plausible composite [27] |
| **Field system** | **Open strips** — spring ploughing, fallow, meadow hay; kitchen garden at croft | Henry of Livonia on cleared fields; spring phenology dossier | attested framework [1][12] |
| **Common well / cattle pen** | Central well, **timber palisade** enclosure | HISTORICAL_AUDIT rural wrapper targets [3] | plausible composite [3] |
| **Church** | **Wooden or stone** parish chapel at kihelkond centre — **not** every hamlet | Bishopric network **1240+**; many stone churches **later** | plausible composite [24] |
| **Manor core** | **Fortified timber yard**, stone cellar/tower, granary, mill — lord often absent in town or on Toompea | Fief density [7]; Padise as stone exception | plausible composite [7][8] |

**Linnaaluste** (14 ploughlands in LCD near ancient **Keava/Varbola** power zone) shows LCD names **tax units** without physical plans — do not invent measured plot maps from LCD alone [6].

### Manor obligations and grievance stack

| Due type | Content | April 1343 note | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tithes** | Grain/livestock to church | Lent/Easter pressure overlaps uprising week | attested framework [24] |
| **Rent / tax in kind** | Grain, honey, livestock to lord and crown | **Extraordinary levies 1340–1343** documented via Preen administration [9] | attested [9] |
| **Corvée (*corvee*, Estonian *rüüt*)** | Labour on lord's demesne, road repair, harvest boon | Early Danish period **~2 days/week**; intensification **late 14th–15th c.** [11] | plausible composite for 1343 [11] |
| **Monopoly fines** | Mill, tavern, market bans | **`plausible composite`** for Harju manors | composite |
| **Legal degradation** | Restrictions on movement, marriage, testimony | **Serfdom juridically incomplete in 1343** but direction of travel clear [11] | attested trend [11] |

Envoys to the king complaining of oppression **before** the fire are **attested in scholarship** as attempting to reverse extraordinary taxes [9]. Chronicle rhetoric (Wigand of Marburg via later tradition) adds **sexual violence and slave-like treatment** — **hostile-source attested**, not independently verified per case [14][28].

**Why Harria first:** Feudal estate formation **most advanced** here; rebels **destroy manors so thoroughly** that fief count **does not recover to 1343 levels until the 15th c.** [7].

### Regional seams — Danish, Hanseatic, Order

- **Danish crown:** Takes extraordinary taxes; viceroy captured early May; castles handed to Order **16 May 1343** [9][23].
- **Hanseatic Reval:** **Not** rural government; fears siege and Swedish offer; **grain levy 11 May** to crown [25]. Burghers ally with Order against rebels **`attested`** [14].
- **Livonian Order:** **Extramural** until invitation; crushes Harju armies **May 11–14**; Padise massacre response **`attested`** campaign [14][20].

### April–May seasonal dressing

Align with [`spring-climate-and-living-world.md`](../nature/spring-climate-and-living-world.md): **leaf-out**, **mud season**, **lambing**, **last year's grain**, **St George's feast (23 Apr)** as syncretic calendar anchor [12]. Field work **urgent** before summer — rebels interrupt **spring ploughing and manor boon days** [12].

## Production hooks

- **Map:** `world.harju` wrapper — **one packed-earth spine road**, **3–6 farm clusters** max, **split strips** on rolling ground, **well + threshing barn** at centre; **manor node 1–2 km off** with palisade [3]. **Rebel camp** (`world.rebel_kings`) on **muddy meadow** within **sight of walls** across **Pirita/Iru corridor** — not inside siege engine range [`hinterland.harju-village-and-manor.05`]. **Padise** separate node — stone **ruin-under-construction** April week, **fire damage** after 23 Apr [21].
- **Art:** **Log farm** — horizontal logs, **60–70 % thatch roof** height ratio, **earth floor**, smoke-darkened interior [`hinterland.harju-village-and-manor.02`][`hinterland.harju-village-and-manor.03`]. **Peasants** — wool/leinen, bare legs, **wooden tools**, **no knightly kit** [`hinterland.harju-village-and-manor.04`]. **Manor** — timber tower/stone cellar, **no baroque wings**. **Burned manor** — collapsed thatch, blackened log, **dead livestock** props.
- **Character:** Kalev's **country kin** — Estonian-speaking, **armed with spears/axes** (peasant weapon right **`plausible composite`** from crusade era) [14]; **not** guild burghers. **German widow/refugee** at Viru gate **`plausible composite`** post-massacre [14]. **Vassal knight** rare on manor April 23 — many **killed first night** [14].
- **Quest / Narrative:** **Corvée dispute** on boon day; **hidden grain** before levy; **signal fire** choice; **Padise monks** flee vs die branch; **envoy to king** fails → uprising; **wrong-road ambush** on Haapsalu highway. Tie **Four Kings** election to **signal hill** [`docs/lore/four_kings_act2_lore.md`](../../../docs/lore/four_kings_act2_lore.md).
- **Dialogue:** Estonian **village elder** address vs German **Herr** on manor; **vakus** as tax unit (*wacke* in Low German documents); **ploughland** (*adramaa*) counts from LCD tradition [5].
- **Dev / systems:** `manor_oppression` 0–5 track from tax events; `fief_burned` boolean per manor node; travel **cost in hours** by route table; `rebel_army_strength` keyed to chronicle beats not continuous simulation.
