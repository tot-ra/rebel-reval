# Back lanes east of Pikk (Spring 1343)

## Brief for Map

You are routing **service lanes behind Pikk frontages** in the eastern Lower Town ward — the blocks between **Pikk** (merchant spine) and the **curtain / Dominican belt** (Vene, Katariina käik, Müürivahe), not the Lai-side rear network west of Pikk.

**Critical limitation:** No 1343 city plan names every rear lane. The German scholarly term **Tagenstraße** (*rear lane behind burgess plots*) is a **plausible composite** label for plot-access routes; Reval records prefer *Gasse*, *strate*, *platea*, and *vicus* [1][2]. Do **not** stamp "Tagenstraße" on in-game UI as if it were an attested Reval street name.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Block logic:** Deep Pikk plots (8–10 m wide × 60–90 m deep) carry a **yard gate** on the rear or side wall opening onto a **parallel work lane** 3–5 m wide; affluent merchants reach the lane without exiting through the diele [3][4].
2. **Three named lanes you can cite (documentary or archaeological support):**
   - **Müürivahe** — inner **wall lane** along the curtain; eastern margin of the ward; watch patrol route [5][6][7].
   - **Vene** — north–south **feeder** between Pikk-block yards and the Dominican precinct; Russian-merchant quarter identity by 1343 even though the modern street name is later attested [3][8][9].
   - **Katariina käik** — **cloister-side passage** between Vene and Müürivahe along the Dominican west range; monastery operational since 1246 [9][10].
3. **Two supporting lanes (pre-1343 route, post-1343 name):**
   - **Rataskaevu** (*dummestrate*, hoist street, **1328**) — rear/work access north of the market for Pikk Block A yards [2].
   - **Apteegi** (*velud itur ad monachos*, street to the monks, **1363**) — approach to Dominican service buildings from the market side [2][11].
4. **Widths (authoring targets, all plausible composite unless noted):** yard-access lanes **3–4 m**; Katariina käik **~2–3 m** (single-file cart); Müürivahe **4–6 m** at wall base; Vene **5–7 m** where it functions as a through-route [3][6][12].
5. **Surfaces:** earth, pebble, and mud on rear lanes; **less stone than Pikk frontage**; sporadic plank or cobble only at Dominican passage thresholds and Viru gate apron (post-tower completion) [6][7].
6. **Gates:** distinguish **yard gates** (*Hofpforte*, timber or iron-strapped plank) from **city gates**; Dominican precinct has **controlled cloister portals**; no 15th-c. arched overhead links in Katariina käik in April 1343 [4][10].
7. **Absent:** blanket cobble on every back lane; uniform 19th-c. Katariina käik tourist finish; St Catherine's Guild workshops (modern); Nuns' Gate formal name (**1355**) though the opening may exist as construction [5].

## Findings

### Terminology: Tagenstraße vs Reval record forms

Hanseatic urban morphology assumes **front street house + rear yard + parallel service lane** on deep strip plots [3][4]. German building archaeology uses **Tagenstraße** for the lane behind the street row; Tallinn scholarship describes the same pattern without requiring that exact word in medieval Reval sources [3][13].

| Term | Meaning | 1343 Reval verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tagenstraße** | Rear lane behind burgess row | Useful **production label**; **not attested** as a Reval street name in reviewed onomastic samples [1][2] | plausible composite [1][13] |
| **Gasse / strate / platea** | Alley, street, open lane | Attested name types in council/Latin record [2] | attested [2] |
| **Hof** / yard | Enclosed rear work yard | Attested plot pattern [3][4] | attested typology [3] |
| **Hofpforte** | Yard gate to rear lane | Implied by diele-dornse layout with courtyard door [4] | plausible composite [4] |

### Geographic scope — what "east of Pikk" means on the ground

Pikk runs **harbour to Toompea** as the main east–west merchant spine [3][14]. For this dossier, **east of Pikk** means:

1. **Plots on the north (harbour-ward) side of Pikk** whose rears fall toward the wall/lowland; and
2. **The eastern half of the Pikk strip** from the market toward Viru / Coastal Gate, where blocks sit **between Pikk and the Dominican–wall belt** (Vene / Katariina käik / Müürivahe) [3][8][9].

The **Lai-side rear network** (Suur-Kloostri, Väike-Kloostri, Aida / Speichergasse along St Michael's convent) lies **south/west of the Pikk–Lai block** and is **out of scope** here — see [`pikk-lai-frontage-materials-1340s.md`](./pikk-lai-frontage-materials-1340s.md) Lai Block A boundaries [15].

No reviewed source provides a **complete 1343 rear-lane cadastre** with measured widths. Plot-level yard gates are **typological**, not individually documented [3][4].

### Lane-by-lane detail

#### Müürivahe (wall lane)

- Runs along the **inner face of the curtain** on the east/northeast of Lower Town; craft and patrol use attested in project military dossier [6][7]. **attested** function
- AVE 2016 Viru archaeology: **limestone wall segment** (~1.1 m wide) near Viru tower inner side; crossing carries **pebble/cobble default**, not continuous slab paving [7]. **attested** locally
- April 1343: **no foregate barbican** at Viru; lane meets **unfinished or newly raised gate tower** at southeast [5][7]. **attested** gate phase; lane continuity **plausible composite**
- Danish / Hanseatic context: wall lane is **burgher watch route**, not Order jurisdiction inside the circuit [6].

#### Vene (Russian-merchant quarter street)

- Dominican priory site chosen **between Vene and Müürivahe** — fixes the belt east of Pikk merchant blocks [9][10]. **attested**
- [`lower-town-street-plan.md`](./lower-town-street-plan.md) treats Vene as a **major north–south feeder**; written Estonian name is post-medieval but **quarter identity by 1343** is production-safe as labelled composite [3][8]. **plausible composite**
- Rear lanes opening from Pikk plots should **debouch into Vene** or a nameless yard lane parallel to it, not directly through the cloister [9].

#### Katariina käik (St Catherine's passage)

- Connects **Vene and Müürivahe** along the **west range of St Catherine's Dominican monastery**; monastery founded **1246**, stone church largely usable **c. 1300** [9][10]. **attested**
- Surviving passage ~**80 m** long, **narrow** (roughly two pedestrians abreast in modern observation) — use **~2–3 m** cart width for 1343 authoring [10][12]. **plausible composite**
- **Tombstones in north wall** are **14th–16th c.** — a few may be visible by late campaign, not a fully curated display in April 1343 [10]. **partial**
- **Overhead arches** linking south-side workshops are **later reinforcement**; April 1343 shows **open lane + cloister wall**, not arcaded gallery [10][12]. **plausible composite**
- Dominican **brewery and fish trade** imply service doors and cart access on this flank in 1343 [9].

#### Rataskaevu and Apteegi (market-edge rear feeders)

- **Rataskaevu** (*dummestrate*, **1328**) — hoist/street-name logic ties to **merchant gear** on market-adjacent plots; functions as **rear access** for Pikk Block A in [`pikk-lai-frontage-materials-1340s.md`](./pikk-lai-frontage-materials-1340s.md) [2][3][15]. **attested** name; rear-lane role **plausible composite**
- **Apteegi** — Latin **1363** *velud itur ad monachos* ("street leading to the monks") documents a **monkward lane** on the Dominican side of the market quarter [2][11]. **attested** 1363; April 1343 presence **plausible composite**

### Yard gates and access control

| Gate type | What it controls | Material / form | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yard gate** (*Hofpforte*) | Plot rear lane ↔ work yard | Timber plank, iron straps; narrower than diele door [4] | plausible composite [4] |
| **Hoist / warehouse door** | Street or lane ↔ diele/storage | Oversized lintel on affluent Pikk plots [4][15] | attested typology [4] |
| **Cloister portal** | Passage ↔ Dominican service range | Stone arch fragments survive; controlled access [10] | attested monastery; 1343 door schedule **unknown** [9] |
| **City gate** | Wall lane ↔ extramural road | Viru under construction mid-14th c. [5][7] | attested phase |

Labourers and **Estonian servants** more often use **rear lanes and yard gates** than the diele front door [`estonian-and-german-populations.md`](../people/estonian-and-german-populations.md) — social colour only, not a written 1343 rule [16].

### Regional power context

- **Town council** regulates fire risk and lane obstruction on work routes; specific 1343 rear-lane ordinances **not extracted** in this tick [3].
- **Dominican friars** hold **precinct autonomy** on cloister-side passages; merchant carts may use Katariina käik under **custom**, not a mapped public right-of-way [9].
- **Livonian Order** remains **extramural** in April 1343; wall lane watch is **burgher obligation** [`watch-duty-and-town-defence.md`](../military/watch-duty-and-town-defence.md) [6].

## Production hooks

- **Map:** For each Pikk plot east of the market, assign `rear_lane_id` ∈ {`vene_feeder`, `katariina_passage`, `muurivahe`, `unnamed_yard_lane`}. Default **unnamed** 3–4 m earth lane behind 60–90 m deep plots; only **one** named connector per block face. Connect yard gates at rear plot line; never route through Dominican close [3][9]. Use the fetched Commons plates in `history/reference/plates.csv` for `topography.back-lanes-east-of-pikk.01`–`.04`; they are reference evidence, not 1343 survey plans.
- **Art:** Rear lanes = **mud, pebble, yard chips**, timber fences, privy sheds, smoke from Dominican brewery; **no** limestone street skin. Yard gates: dark tarred plank, iron strap hinges — plate [`.04`](../../reference/topography/back-lanes-east-of-pikk/topography.back-lanes-east-of-pikk.04.jpg). Katariina käik: **raw cloister masonry**, not gift-shop signage [10].
- **Character:** Craft servants and Estonian labourers enter via **rear gates**; merchants receive guests at diele front [4][16].
- **Quest / Narrative:** Dominican fish cart on Katariina käik; watch lantern on Müürivahe at curfew [`watch-duty-and-town-defence.md`](../military/watch-duty-and-town-defence.md); rear-lane theft/sabotage routes for night missions.
- **Dialogue:** Use *strate* / *gasse* generically; *dummestrate* only near Rataskaevu anchor; avoid anachronistic "Tagenstraße" in NPC speech [2].
- **Dev / systems:** Lane graph: Pikk frontage nodes connect to rear-lane nodes at `yard_gate` cost 1; Dominican precinct nodes `access=restricted`; Müürivahe nodes `patrol_weight=high` [6][9].

## Open questions

- Whether **1340–1343 AWB** entries name any rear lane beside Pikk explicitly — **edition-local negative for Pikk/Lai spine names** delivered 2026-08-28; AWB **302** still attests *platea dicta dummestrate* pre-1343 (see [`pikk-lai-frontage-materials-1340s.md`](./pikk-lai-frontage-materials-1340s.md)).
- **Measured medieval yard-gate widths** on Pikk plots — no published survey; dendrochronology on surviving gates would help art.
- **Unnamed parallel lanes** between Pikk and Lai in the eastern blocks — separate tick from Lai-side Kloostri network.
- **Katariina käik** cart traffic rights vs pedestrian-only custom in the 1340s — Dominican account books not reviewed here.
