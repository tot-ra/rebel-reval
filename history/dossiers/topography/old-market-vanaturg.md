# Old Market / Vanaturu kael (Spring 1343)

## Brief for Map

You are placing the **east throat of the civic market quarter**, not the tourist “Vana Turg” square. In April 1343 the named *forum inferior* / “Old Market” (**1368+**) **does not exist** as a labelled place [1][2]. What exists is a **narrow road convergence** — *Vanaturu kael* (“market neck”) — feeding the attested *forum* at Raekoja plats from Viru, Vene, and the inland roads [3][4].

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Do not label “Vana Turg” in UI or dialogue.** Use the route only; diegetic speech is *forum* / *markt* for the civic square, or “the road to Viru” / “the clay gate road” — not *Vana turg* [1][2][5].
2. **Vanaturu kael is a lane, not an open square.** Model a **4–6 m paved throat** (~45–55 m long) from the forum’s east edge to the Viru × Vene × inland-road junction. No separate market polygon in 1343 [3][4][6].
3. **Raekoja plats stays the market.** All attested 1343 wholesale/retail civic trading is on the *forum* (1313) south of the Town Hall [1][2][7]. The east throat carries **through-traffic and cart staging**, not a second market reserve.
4. **Junction east of the neck** is a **widening crossroads** (~10–14 m clear width) where Viru (clay-gate road toward Tartu/Narva), Vene (Russian-merchant quarter approach), and routes toward Suur-Karja / Kuninga meet [4][5][8]. Treat the short modern “Vana turg” street segment beyond this junction as **built strip frontage**, not open market ground, in 1343 [4][9].
5. **Built edges, not void.** Both sides of the neck carry **7–9 m burgess strip plots** with gable-to-street houses; rear yards behind. No Peppersack (1420–1460), no Pakkhoone warehouse (1655), no Olde Hansa frontage [9][10].
6. **Foreign-merchant staple warehouse** at the convergence is **not attested in April 1343 fabric** — council-authorised stranger trading and the stone *Pakkhoone* are documented from the **15th c.** onward, with staple-right formalisation from **1346** [9][11]. Show **timber or mixed frontage** with possible ground-floor shop bays; do not place a monumental stone warehouse [9][11].
7. **Use the authoring corridor below** (local axes, metres). Vertices are **plausible composite** ±2 m; boundary with *forum* is the west end of the neck at the civic square’s SE throat [6][7].

## Findings

### Names and documentary identity

| Name / concept | First attestation | April 1343 status | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| *forum* (Raekoja plats) | **1313** [1][2] | **attested** — primary market | attested |
| *forum inferior* / “lower market” | **1368** [1][3] | **absent** — name and distinct market function not yet recorded | attested absence |
| *dat olde market* / Vana turg | **1442** MLG; **1732** Estonian [1][3] | **absent** | attested absence |
| *Vanaturu kael* (market neck) | Modern Estonian; MLG *Markthalsgasse* etc. 19th c. [4] | Route **plausible**; street name **not attested** in 1343 speech | plausible composite [4] |
| Viru (clay street) | **1362** *leymstrate* [5] | Route **plausible**; name post-1343 | plausible composite [5][8] |

Postimees and Tallinn Streets agree: **Raekoja plats is the documented central market in 1313**; Vana Turg emerges as a **differentiated lower market only from 1368** as *forum inferior* [1][3]. Estonian Wikipedia’s claim that Vana turg is “usually considered Tallinn’s oldest market” reflects **later tradition and route antiquity**, not 1343 documentary priority — reconcile by treating the convergence as **old in route terms**, not as the named civic market [3][9].

### Physical geography: neck, junction, and “Vana turg” street

**Vanaturu kael** (modern usage) is a **short connector** beginning at Raekoja plats, crossing Raekoja tänav, and ending at the **Viru × Vene × Vana turg** intersection [4]. Archaeological and heritage consensus places this corridor on the **Tartu-road approach** to the castle foot and market from the east, in use from the high medieval period [4][12].

| Segment | Modern identity | 1343 treatment | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| West throat | Vanaturu kael at Raekoja plats | **Paved cart lane** 4–6 m; forum opens to east | plausible composite [6][7][13] |
| Mid neck | Vanaturu kael house frontages | **Strip burgess plots** both sides; one surviving medieval **projecting shop bay** on a rebuilt facade [4] | attested survival type; 1343 exact plot map **partial** |
| East junction | Viru / Vene / Vana turg cross | **Road widening** with converging spines; gate-ward traffic toward future Viru Gate | plausible composite [5][8][13] |
| Vana turg street | Short link to Suur-Karja / Kuninga | **Built frontage street**, not open market; Peppersack site is **1420–1460** [9][10] | attested later fabric |

**Route convergence (attested function type, not 1343 label):** Medieval and modern sources describe this node as where roads toward the harbour, Narva, Tartu, Viljandi, Pärnu, the islands, Riga, and Novgorod met [3][4]. For 1343 maps: show **signposted road logic** without claiming a measured Roman-style milestone plan.

### Function relative to Raekoja plats (*forum*)

| Activity | Raekoja plats (*forum*) | East throat / convergence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civic market stalls | **Primary** open ground | **Not** a second open market reserve | attested vs absent [1][2][7] |
| Council proclamations / civic gathering | Town Hall doorway on south edge | — | attested [7] |
| Cart throughput to/from Viru Gate | Fed via east throat | **Main eastern approach** to forum | plausible composite [8][13] |
| Foreign merchant warehousing | Later *Pakkhoone* / staple | **Not** attested stone warehouse in 1343; stranger trade rules tighten **from 1346** | attested chronology [9][11] |
| Informal street-edge trading | Possible at forum edge | **Plausible** at junction margins; **not** documented as *forum inferior* | plausible composite [3] |

The **1346 staple right** (goods through Reval harbour must pass burgher hands) post-dates the campaign opening [11]. In April 1343, do not model a dedicated foreign-merchant bonded warehouse as established infrastructure — at most **timber shops and rented diele space** on burgess plots [9][11].

There is **no hard wall** between forum and throat — a **surface and usage transition** from open market ground to paved through-lane [6][13].

### Regional context

- **Danish Estonia / Hanseatic Reval:** Lower Town trade is burgher-governed under Danish sovereignty; the east throat is the **mainland and eastern gate approach** to the civic market, not Order territory [15][16].
- **Livonian Order:** Extramural in April 1343; Viru Gate guards control **egress to rebel country** during the late-April siege — the neck becomes a **tension choke point**, not a festival square [16].
- **Estonian population:** Labourers and carters on through-routes; legal exclusion from burgher frontage ownership shapes who works the lane vs who owns the diele [15].

- **Character:** Carters, gate guards, foreign merchants’ factors (without bonded warehouse), Estonian porters; burgher housekeepers on strip plots [9][15].
- **Quest / Narrative:** `bread_and_iron` grain-sack lane **maps here** as east-throat cart traffic — diverted rye and wax seals move through the convergence, not a named “Vana Turg market” [17]. Siege tension: rebel countryside blocks inland roads; queue at Viru Gate [16].
- **Dialogue:** *forum* / *markt* for the square; “road to the clay gate” / *Lehmporte* only if using Viru name cautiously (attested 1362, not April 1343 common speech) [5]; never *Vana turg* [1][3].
- **Dev / systems:** East exit from `market_civic_quarter` = lane width cap 6 m; stall density **zero** on neck tiles; cart traffic weight higher than forum interior.
