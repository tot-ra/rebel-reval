# Walls, gates, and towers (Spring 1343)

## Brief for Map

You are modelling the **mid-1340s stone circuit** around Lower Town (*All-linn*), not the later 15th-century height, foregates, zwinger, or cannon towers. The perimeter is **irregular**, terrain-shaped, and still being extended in the 1340s; the full ring closes only around **1355** [1][2].

**Decisions you can ship now:**

1. **Circuit shape:** Elongated north–south oval hugging Toompea on the west; southern tip aligned with the castle hill; northern extension incorporating St Olaf and the Rus merchant quarter; wet/low ground on north and southeast with moats and mill ponds [1][3].
2. **Wall mass (default curtain):** Limestone, **~6 m** curtain height and **~1.3–1.6 m** base thickness on 14th-century sections; with mid-century arcaded wall-walk, total thickness **~2.3–2.6 m** and height **~6.5 m** above lowered ground — **not** the 11 m+ 15th-century raise [1]. Margaret-era (1265) core sections may be lower (**~4.5–5 m**, **~1.1–1.3 m** thick) where still standing [1].
3. **Eight gate positions** are the mid-century scheme: Nuns', Coastal (Sea), Sand, Viru, Cattle (Karja), Harju, Long Hill, Short Hill [1]. **No foregates or barbicans** in April 1343 — those are 1440s–1460s [1][4].
4. **Viru Gate** (eastern main road to Tartu/Narva): treat as **present but possibly unfinished** — archaeology places the main gate in the **mid-14th century**; written name *Lehmporte* / clay-gate only from **1362**; the surviving round flanking towers are **1370s+ foregate**, not 1343 fabric [1][5][6].
5. **Karja (Cattle) Gate:** early-14th-century wall/gate reconstruction is **plausible**; name *Kariestrate* is **1365**; watermill over the moat may exist but exact 1343 superstructure is **uncertain** [7][8].
6. **Coastal Gate** (Great Sea Gate): low wall and gate by mid-14th c; probable gate tower **1311–1340**; sits **5–8 m** above historical harbour ground on sandstone cliff; **no Fat Margaret** (1520s) [9].
7. **Hill gates** (Long/Short Leg): **wooden** in 1343; stone Long Leg only after **1380** with Order consent [1]. Castle hill provides the real western defence.
8. **Towers in 1343:** use the **mid-14th-century short list** (Nuns', Golden Leg, Rent, Stolting, Hinko, plus gate towers) — **omit** NW bartizan cluster (Loewenschede, Epping, Plate, etc.), At the Monks, Bremen, Helleman, Maiden, Goat, Stable, Kiek in de Kök, and Fat Margaret; those are **third-quarter 14th c or later** [1][3].
9. **Moat:** southern and eastern irrigated ditch under construction/expansion in the 1340s; Ülemiste water rights granted **1345** — in April 1343 moats may be **partial or dry in places** [1][2].
10. **Siege backdrop:** rebel army (~10,000) camps outside; initial skirmish below walls defeats Order knights, but walled town holds [10]. Show watchfires, hoardings on wooden fighting platforms, not gunports.

## Findings

### Wall circuit and 1343 completion state

Stone fortification begins **1265** under Queen Margaret Sambiria; earlier wood-and-earth works likely from the **1248** Lübeck charter period [1]. Governor **Johannes Cannes** renews work **1310**; the **1340s** push encloses previously open southern, southeastern, and northern suburbs (St Olaf quarter, Rus merchants, Cistercian nunnery incorporated) [1][2].

The scholarly consensus (Zobel reconstruction via Medieval Heritage) is explicit: **"It is not known at what stage the works were in 1343"** when the Estonian uprising broke out, but they **must have been advanced** — two years later (**1345**) Valdemar IV grants Ülemiste water to southern/eastern moats and mill rights; the circuit is **fully closed ~1355** [1][2]. For game purposes: **most of the mid-century perimeter exists**, with **active construction** plausible on Viru, moat earthworks, and southeastern wet sections.

**Typical early-14th-century tower forms:** rectangular bartizans (Nuns' Tower ~8.4 m high, projecting 0.4 m); horseshoe Golden Leg (~9 m wide, 7 m high); semicircular/horseshoe gate and corner towers [1]. **Wooden** upper fighting decks with throwing engines; crossbowmen on arcaded wall-walk by mid-century [1].

### Moats, mills, and water defences

1340s expansion includes **new irrigated moat** on southern and eastern sections with major earthworks (up to **10 m** ground lowering along ditch line) [1]. Mill ponds at **Karja**, **Harju**, and **Viru** gates powered by Ülemiste water — canal system attested in development through mid-14th c.; **1345** royal grant is the firm date for diversion rights [1][5]. Southeastern corner is **weak ground** near mill pond almost reaching walls — later buttressed in 15th c. [1][7].

For April 1343: show **wet ditch** on south/east where completed; **timber sluices and earthen dams** plausible; do not show fully engineered 1440s foregate mill integration [1][5].

### Regional power context

- **Danish crown** holds Toompea castle; Lower Town council maintains walls through burgher obligation and fines; monasteries adjacent to curtains contribute labour [1].
- **Livonian Order** is **extramural** in 1343 (sold northern Estonia **1346**, after game period) but fights rebels at the walls during siege; Master **Burchard von Dreileben** defeats main rebel field army at Sõjamäe **14 May 1343** [10].
- **Hanseatic council** self-governs trade inside walls; wall defence is burgher duty — see military dossier (R-010) for watch details.
- **Estonian rebels** lack siege engines sufficient to breach stone walls; camp in suburbs and low ground; initial success is against **field forces**, not the curtain [10].

## Production hooks

- **Map:** Trace irregular mid-1343 circuit from Zobel mid-14th-c. plan [3]; default curtain 6–6.5 m; eight gate gaps; wooden hill gates on west; moat on S/E with partial ponds; Viru and Karja aprons **open** (15–30% built outside wall per audit) [12]; Toompea separate plateau [12].
- **Art:** Limestone ashlar curtains with crenellated wooden walkways; no machicolations; gate towers rectangular, modest; hoardings and shed roofs on towers in siege scenes; rebel camp fires on low ground east/south; Order cross banners on wall walks, not Fat Margaret mass [1][10].
- **Character:** Burghers on wall-walk watch; gate guards at Viru and Coastal; Estonian labourers on earthworks — legal status outside burgher militia [1].
- **Quest / Narrative:** Smuggle through Sand Gate or unfinished Viru works; overhear council debate on mill rights (foreshadow 1345); sabotage wooden hill-gate; relay sortie from Harju Gate crushes rebel foragers; siege tension without breach [10].
- **Dialogue:** *Lehmporte* / clay gate for Viru (pre-1362 speech); *Strantporte* for Coastal; *Smedeporte* for smiths' gate; avoid "Fat Margaret" or "Kiek in de Kök" [1][5].
- **Dev / systems:** Wall segment health pool ~6 m height class; gate states `{open, closed, portcullis}` only at simple gates; `construction` flag for Viru/moat tiles; siege spawns outside moat, not inside [1][12].
