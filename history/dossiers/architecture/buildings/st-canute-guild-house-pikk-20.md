# St Canute's guild house, Pikk 20

## Brief for Map and Art

The St Canute guild (*Kanutigilde*) held a house on the **Pikk 20** plot from **1326**, when the plot covered **about a third of today's site** [1][2]. The present front with statues of Luther and St Canute is **1863-64** and the inside was rebuilt in the 1930s; only the outer walls and the great hall walls of the old building survive, and the great hall itself stood on a neighbouring house bought in **1406** and built in the **1470s** [1]. So the April 1343 house is **one ordinary merchant-type house on a narrow strip**, used as the guild's meeting house: the guild's identity, not its architecture, makes it a landmark.

Build it as a **simplified bespoke** gable house about one third of the modern frontage wide, with a guild sign (the patron's image) over the door and a feast room on the ground floor. It is Kalev's guild ground: the smiths' *Amt* belongs to St Canute [3]. Board task: **R-1987 / SOC-5** places it on the empty Pikk 20 plot.

## 1343 status

**Standing (attested).** The guild owns a house here 17 years before April 1343 [1][2]. Its form is unrecorded; use a simplified Lower Town merchant house per [burgher-house-plan](../burgher-house-plan.md).

## Function in 1343

The umbrella guild of the "finer", mostly German crafts: bakers, tailors, shoemakers, **smiths**, goldsmiths (by the c. 1500 allocation, used with caution for 1343) [3]. Feasts and drinking evenings, elections of the alderman, mutual aid, burials and masses (the guild altar stands in a parish church, not in the house) [3]. From the mid-14th century the guilds speak for the town community against the council [3]. After the 1343 uprising the **Estonian members of St Canute withdrew and formed a separate guild** (Johansen and von zur Mühlen, cited in [3]): the house is the natural stage for that tension in April.

## Exterior

- Narrow gabled house, stone or mixed stone and timber (the merchant spine Pikk; see the frontage material weights in [Pikk and Lai frontage materials](../../topography/pikk-lai-frontage-materials-1340s.md)).
- A painted board or carved image of St Canute (Knud Lavard) over the door; a banner on feast days.

**Leave out:** the 1863-64 Tudor-Gothic front and its Luther and St Canute statues, the 1470s great hall, the 1802 three-gable building, the 1930s interiors [1].

## Decoration and interior

Long tables and benches, candle crowns, painted beams, the guild banner, wooden drinking vessels and a welcome cup, pewter. Composite after Hanseatic guild houses [3].
