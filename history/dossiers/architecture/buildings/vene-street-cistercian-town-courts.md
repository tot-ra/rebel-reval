# The Cistercian town courts on Vene street ("monks' street")

## Brief for Map and Art

In the 14th century Vene street was the **monks' street** (*platea monachorum* 1364, *monnekestrasse* 1388): besides St Catherine's Dominican friary it held the **town courts of three Cistercian abbeys** that lay far from Reval [1]. They worked as **supply and sales offices for the abbeys' Estonian estates**: warehouses, stables and other farm buildings, a house for the resident agent and servants, and room for visiting brothers [1]. In scale they were **modest, hardly different from ordinary town plots** [1].

| Court | Plot (modern) | Owner abbey | Dates | 1343 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Roma (Gutvalla) court | Vene 20 / Vene 22, east side north of the Dominicans [1][2] | Roma, Gotland; estate centre Kolga (Kolk), Harju, held from at least 1298 [3] | court 1259-1519 per Estonian Wikipedia [2]; sold 1519 as the "Gutwalla or Kolga monastery office" [1] | standing (plausible composite for the year) |
| Dünamünde, later **Padise** court | next plot on Vene 22 [1] | Dünamünde (Daugavgrīva) near Riga; passed to **Padise** when the monks moved there (Padise founded 1305) [1][4] | 14th c. | standing, **Padise's office in Reval** (plausible composite) |
| Kärkna (Falkenau) court | Vene 15 // Pühavaimu 8, corner plot on the west side [1] | Kärkna, near Tartu | two *sara* (barns or sheds) on the plot in **1259**; *curia ... in civitate Revaliensi* taxed for the town wall c. **1280**; later often let to burghers (1429 Hans Brakel) [1] | plot and use attested 1259-c. 1280; 1343 use plausible composite |

Recommendation: model them as **one simplified bespoke group**: three walled yards with gateways, a stone warehouse (*kiviait*, attested on the Kärkna plot later [1]), a timber agent's house and stables, with a painted cross or abbey sign over each gate. Not three bespoke churches: they had no churches.

## Why it matters in April 1343

On St George's Night (23 April 1343) rebels sack **Padise** and kill about 28 monks [4]. Padise's town office on Vene street is where the news, refugees, the abbey's grain and the brothers' fear arrive in Reval. Roma's Kolga estate lies east in Harju, also in the uprising area. This is a ready stage for quests and dialogue without inventing a building type.

## Exterior

Plain limestone and timber like the neighbours [1]; no cloister, no chapel tower. A white Cistercian habit on the agent marks the owner better than architecture.

**Leave out:** the Russian merchants' church at Vene 24 (founded 1405 per the source [1]; uncertain), the 19th-century houses on all three plots, the post office and school uses.
