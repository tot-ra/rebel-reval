# The Olde Hansa plot, Vana turg 1 // Vanaturu kael 12: verdict for 1343

## Verdict

**The Olde Hansa building did not exist in April 1343, and the plot's first stone building is later too.** The restaurant occupies the **packhouse (*Pakkhoone*) built 1655-1656** on the ruins of a building that collapsed on 26 August 1654 [1][2]. The register's earliest record for the plot is a **stone warehouse in the second half of the 14th century**, i.e. after 1350 [1]. Shops on the ground and cellar floors along the Old Market go back to the 14th century as a tradition, without a date [1]. The Old Market itself is not a separate square in 1343 (*forum inferior* first 1368) [3].

So in April 1343 this corner behind the Town Hall is **ordinary built strip frontage of unknown form**: show a generic timber or mixed house with booths, not a bespoke model. Olde Hansa's "medieval" interiors are a modern restaurant design and give no 1343 evidence.

## Identity

| Earliest building record | stone warehouse, second half of the 14th century [1] | attested, later than 1343 |
| Restaurant | Olde Hansa, founded 1998 [4] | modern |

## Leave out

The packhouse, its ridge stones, weather vane and dormer hoists, the relief stones on the Town Hall side, all 19th-20th-century openings, the restaurant decor [2][4]. The staple right that made the packhouse necessary dates from **1346**, after April 1343 [2].
