# Raekoja plats 18: the tower house on the market's east corner

## Brief for Map and Art

The large late-Gothic merchant house at **Raekoja plats 18 // Vanaturu kael 3** (Hopner house) was **probably built around a small, multi-storey 14th-century tower house** [1]. In **1367** the site held a dwelling and **three separate shops** [1]. The corner closes the market square where the lane to the Viru road leaves it (Vanaturu kael), so a distinct tall stone tower here would be one of the few evidence-based silhouettes on the forum edge.

Build it as a **simplified bespoke** stone tower house with low timber shop booths against it toward the square. Everything that makes the present house famous (the 1596 relief frieze, the 1681 baroque fit-out, the lion weather vane) is later and is left out [1].

## 1343 status

**Predecessor, plausible.** The tower house is dated only to the 14th century, and the earliest record is 24 years after April 1343. The corner of the forum is attested in 1313 and the market edge was built up with merchants' houses [2][3], so a small tower house is a fair simplified form under the period rule. Mark it `plausible composite`. The later *forum inferior* (1368) is not a second square in 1343 [3].

## Function in 1343

A wealthy merchant's fire-safe house on the busiest corner of the market: goods and money in the tower, retail in rented booths against it, the family possibly in a timber house behind. The three shops are recorded only in 1367, after the game date; booths on the square side are therefore an optional `plausible composite`, not evidence for April 1343 [1].

## Exterior

- Small square footprint, limestone rubble, small openings, steep roof; higher than its timber neighbours.
- Booths: plank, shingle or board lean-to roofs, shutters that fold down as counters.

**Leave out:** the late-Gothic gable and storage storeys, the 1596 polychrome relief frieze, the 1681 baroque windows and painted ceilings, the diele storage floor, the oriel before the portal and the lion weather vane (both reconstructions) [1].

## Open questions

- Footprint and height of the tower core (building survey not read).
- Whether the corner was already built in stone in April 1343.
