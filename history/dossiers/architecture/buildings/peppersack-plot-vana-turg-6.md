# The Peppersack plot, Vana turg 6 // Viru 2: verdict for 1343

## Verdict

**The Peppersack house did not exist in April 1343; the plausible 1343 state is a timber house.** The four-storey diele-dornse merchant house now holding the Peppersack restaurant was **built 1420-1460** [1][2]. The register states that it was **preceded on the same spot by a 14th-century timber dwelling, parts of whose stone foundation survive inside the present stone house** [1]. The decade is not recorded, so a timber house in April 1343 is `plausible composite`, not attested. The name comes from a 15th-century owner family (the project's street register already lists Peppersack 1420-1460 as a phase exclusion) [3].

So the game shows, at the corner of the forum's east throat and the Viru road, a **timber merchant house on stone footings**, the generic Lower Town timber type, not the famous gable. If the maintainer wants the corner recognisable, it can be a **simplified bespoke timber house** (two storeys, plank or log, steep roof, a shop opening to the corner), but evidence does not support anything more specific.

## 1343 plan (for the generic timber house)

Follow the timber front-house type in [burgher-house-plan](../burgher-house-plan.md): one heated room (*dornse*), a front hall or shop toward the street, a loft for goods, a yard with a separate store; overhang no more than two feet [4].

## Leave out

The Gothic stone gable with hoist hatches and light openings, the baroque gable boards and ridge stone, the weather vane, the 1977-80 café fit-out, and all restaurant interiors (the project already bans Peppersack restaurant interiors as a 1343 prop licence) [2][5].
