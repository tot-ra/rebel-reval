# Toompea Small Castle and Great Castle interiors (c. 1340)

## Brief for Map and Art

You are building **interior and courtyard zones** for **Danish-ruled Toompea in April–May 1343**, before the **16 May 1343** castle handover to the Livonian Order. The parent dossier [`toompea-castle-and-upper-town.md`](toompea-castle-and-upper-town.md) covers the plateau; this file names **rooms you may place** and **layouts you must not import**.

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Three-part hill complex:** **Small Castle** (*castrum minus*, SW) = crown secular seat; **intervening ward** (*cingele*) = walled link to **Great Castle** (*castrum maius*, NE/central) = cathedral + vassal plots; **outer bailey** (*eeslinnus*) wrapping the Small Castle, extended east in the early 14th century [1][2]. **attested** for division; measured 1343 room sizes **unknown**.
2. **Danish Small Castle interior (April 1343):** Cross-wall splits the Order-built kastell into a **southern forecourt** and a **northern walled enclosure** holding the **viceroy's residence** (*asehalduri residents*). Gate + tower sit in the inter-courtyard wall. Western outer wall retains **bricked-up large window openings** — the only fabric confidently tied to the Danish build [1][3]. **Do not** use the four-wing Order commandry plan (chapel north, refectory east, dormitory west, commander south) — that is **1350–1360+** [1][4].
3. **Viceroy's hall zone:** Treat the north enclosure as **audience + private chambers + chapel or oratory** served by castle staff, not a merchant diele. Western façade had a **multi-stage fortified residence** on the outer wall, **dansker** projection, and **drawbridge gate** at second-storey level **plausible composite** from surviving western works and Danish castle-residence type [3][5]. **attested** function; room labels **plausible composite**.
4. **Great Castle interior (April 1343):** **Cathedral square** with **Toomkirik** (Gothic enlargement active — nave under construction) [6]; **bishop's residence** and **vassal curiae** on surrounding plots; cross-wall may still divide the Great Castle enclosure [2]. **No full ring wall** around Great Castle is securely dated before **mid-14th century after the 1346 sale** — in April 1343 rely on **cliff + plot walls + Toom Gate** (*Kellatornik* / *Toomvärav*) on the south, not a complete curtain [2][7]. **plausible composite** for pre-ring state; ring wall timing **uncertain**.
5. **Outer bailey (Small Castle):** Courtyard, stables, workshops, garrison muster; eastern extension includes the **hill gate** chain toward **Lühike jalg** (wooden in 1343) [8]. Southern forecourt open to **Tõnismägi** slope — suburb later [1].
6. **Explicitly absent in April 1343:** Order **convent building** (34 × 39 m, Riigikogu footprint) [1][4]; **Pikk Hermann** (first masonry **1360–1370**) [1]; **Stür den Kerl**, **Landskrone**, **Pilsticker** at current height [1][4]; stone **Long Leg gate tower** (**1380**) [8]; four-wing **cloistered upper ward** plan per Medieval Heritage [4][9].
7. **Quest colour:** Viceroy **Konrad Preen** holds audience here in April; capture at Paide negotiations **early May** shifts command to **Bertram von Parembeke** then Order **Goswin von Herike** after **14 May** [10][11]. Danish banner, not Order cross, on the hill until handover.

### Small Castle — build phases relevant to 1343

**1227–1229:** Sword Brethren erect first stone kastell on SW cliff edge; watch tower at SE corner; moat separates castle from rest of Toompea [1]. **attested**

**1238–1240s (Danish resumption):** Danes continue Order-started work. Cross-wall divides kastell: **south = forecourt** (*eeshoov*); **north = walled viceroy's enclosure** on the site of the modern castle courtyard, tying into the western curtain where Pikk Hermann will later rise [1][3]. Inter-courtyard gate with tower **attested** [1]. Western outer wall: **bricked-up large window openings** survive — strongest physical anchor for Danish-period upper rooms [3]. **attested** fabric; internal partition layout **plausible composite** from cross-wall logic [1].

**Early 14th century:** Extension with **forecourts and curtains eastward**; hill gate linking to **Lühike jalg** route **plausible composite** contemporary with outer-ward closure described in parent dossier [1][8]. On-plateau **limestone quarrying** around the cathedral-school belt supplied castle and church stone; Reppo 2018 revises Zobel’s quarry outline northward and ties quarrying to the **1229 SW castrum minus** corner in scholarly reconstruction, while pre-Danish extent remains **unknown** [`toompea-castle-and-upper-town.md`](toompea-castle-and-upper-town.md) [16]. **plausible composite**

### What the Order rebuild teaches (comparandum only)

Medieval Heritage and Zobel summarise the **post-1346** upper ward: four wings around a cloistered courtyard; main-floor chapel, chapter, refectory, dormitory; ground-floor kitchen and stores [4][9]. Estonian Wikipedia dates the core convent house **1350–1360** [1]. Kuressaare bishop's castle (Saaremaa) offers a Baltic **Konventhaus** parallel — square courtyard ~43 m, cellar stores, main-floor representative rooms, dansker [13] — but it is **episcopal**, **Saaremaa**, and **late 14th-century**; use only to understand what the Order **will** build at Reval, not April 1343 Danish layout [13]. **plausible composite** comparandum; **not** transferable as 1343 evidence.

- **Map:** Mesh three labelled zones: `SC-*` (SW Small Castle + outer bailey), `cingele` corridor, `GC-*` (cathedral square + vassal plots). Keep **no Pikk Hermann mesh** in April scenes. Outer bailey eastern gate aligns to **Lühike jalg** top [8]. Great Castle **omit** complete ring curtain unless flagged `reconstructed` [7]. Use **A2.2/A2.3** courtyard split and **A2.6** three-dansker west curtain as topology anchors; do not scale rooms from Order **34 × 39 m** or **52 × 40 m** comparanda [1][4][17].
- **Art:** Danish hill scenes: limestone ashlar, **bricked window voids** on west curtain [`architecture.toompea-small-castle-interior.03`](../../reference/architecture/toompea-small-castle-interior/architecture.toompea-small-castle-interior.03.jpg); forecourt mud and carts; viceroy hall = **boarded tables, wall hangings, Danish arms** — not Hanseatic diele [`burgher-house-plan.md`](burgher-house-plan.md). Post-16 May patch: swap banners to Order cross; **do not** remodel interior to four-wing cloister until time-skip [4].
- **Character:** Viceroy household (clerks, sergeants, Danish-speaking officials), castle chaplain, garrison spearmen; vassals summoned to `SC-viceroy-audience`; bishop uses `GC-bishop-palace` not Small Castle for diocesan business [6][10].
- **Quest / Narrative:** Audience with Preen before Paide; smuggle message through `SC-west-gate` / hill lanes; **14 May** castle handover scene at `OB-courtyard`; grain levy dispute ties Lower Town to `SC-viceroy-audience` [10].
- **Dialogue:** *Asehalduri residents* / *des capiteins hus*; *Castrum Danorum*; distinguish *Toomkirik* (cathedral) from castle chapel [1][2].
- **Dev / systems:** `interior_era` flag per tile: `danish_1343` | `order_post_1346`; block Order-only room IDs until campaign date ≥ 1346-11-01 sale or ≥ 1343-05-16 handover per scene need.
