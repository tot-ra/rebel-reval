# Toompea castle and Upper Town (Spring 1343)

## Brief for Map and Art

You are modelling **Danish-ruled Toompea (Domberg)** in **April–May 1343**, before the Order takeover (May 1343 handover; sale completed November 1346). This is **not** the baroque Riigikogu facade, **not** Pikk Hermann tower (first masonry **by 1371**), and **not** the stone Long Leg gate tower (**1380**).

**Ship these decisions:**

1. **Plateau:** Limestone tableland ~400 × 250 m, ~20–30 m above Lower Town; cliff on north/east/west [1].
2. **Castle complex:** Two-part **13th-century** layout — **Small Castle** (SW, Order-built 1227–1229, Danish again from 1238) and **Great Castle** (northern residence zone) plus **outer ward** enclosed in the **first half of the 14th century** when the Great Castle south wall and moats were tied together [1][2].
3. **Danish seat:** **Castrum Danorum** — crown viceroy's administrative and military centre; garrison and court, not Hanseatic merchant space [1][3].
4. **Dome church (Toomkirik):** Consecrated **1240** as cathedral of the Bishopric of Reval; **Gothic enlargement from the 1330s** — in Spring 1343 show **active building site**: new three-aisle nave rising, rectangular pillars likely in progress, choir/vestry already rebuilt; **no baroque spire** (1779) [4][5].
5. **Vassal belt:** Dense noble/clergy housing on the Great Castle plateau from the late 13th century — fortified town houses, not Lower Town diele-dornse merchants [1][6].
6. **Boundary with All-linn:** Two controlled ascents — **Pikk jalg** (Long Leg) and **Lühike jalg** (Short Leg); in 1343 both gates are **wooden**; Lower Town may close them at night [1][7]. Stone curtain between hill and Lower Town is **not** the 1454–1455 masonry — use timber palisade/earthen barrier **plausible composite** [7].
7. **Political colour (quest/narrative):** Viceroy **Konrad Preen** holds the castle in April; pro-German captain **Marquard Breide** and pro-Danish Bishop **Olaf of Roskilde** split the province after 1332; rebels besiege **Lower Town**, not Toompea directly [3][8].
8. **Do not show:** Pikk Hermann, Catherine Palace east wing, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Swedish bastions, stone Long Leg gatehouse, Order cross on every tower (Order only receives castle **16 May 1343**).

## Findings

### Plateau topography and dual-city split

Toompea is a limestone klint remnant: roughly **7 ha**, **~48 m** summit elevation, **~20–30 m** above surrounding ground, with a **~25 m** cliff on three sides [1]. The Estonian stronghold (*Lindanise*) predates 1219; Danish crusaders built **Castrum Danorum** immediately after the Battle of Lindanise [1][2].

Lower Town (*All-linn*, Tallinn) received **Lübeck rights by 1248** and was **exempted from castellan rule in 1265**; burghers gained **independence from castle judicial power in 1288** [1]. By 1343 the legal and social split is long established: merchants and crafts below the cliff; crown, bishop, and vassals on the hill [1][6].

| Zone | 1343 ruler / law | Primary occupants | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Toompea castle | Danish crown (viceroy) | Garrison, viceroy's court, chapel staff | attested [1][3] |
| Toompea plots outside castle walls | Danish vassals, clergy | Knights, cathedral chapter, wealthy retainers | attested [1][6] |
| Lower Town | Self-governing council under Lübeck law | German burghers, Estonian labourers, clergy in friaries | attested [1] |
| Livonian Order | Extramural until May 1343 | No permanent castle garrison on Toompea in April | attested [3] |

Formal territorial boundary fixed only in **1348** (220 ha *Dome territory* southwest of hill) — **post-game** for strict dating; in April 1343 treat the cliff + hill gates as the operative line [1].

### Castle fabric — what existed in April 1343

**Small Castle (SW corner):** Stone enclosure built **1227–1229** by the Sword Brethren; returned to Denmark **1238**; shape broadly similar to later castle but smaller [1][2]. **attested** for 13th-c. core; exact April 1343 room layout **unknown**.

**Great Castle:** Northern section with governor's residence and early towers; surrounded by a **perimeter wall along the hill edge** built mainly by vassal plot-owners in the late 13th century [1]. **attested**.

**Outer ward:** Southern forecourt in front of the two castles; southern and southeastern sides walled in the **first half of the 14th century** when the Great Castle south curtain was renovated and moats joined [1]. **plausible composite** that this enclosure exists by 1343 — renovation phase is contemporary with Dome church enlargement [1][4].

**Two exits in the early 14th-century scheme** [1]:
- **Southern gate** of outer ward → road toward **Tõnismägi** (suburb develops later).
- **Eastern gate** → **Pikk jalg / Lühike jalg** routes down to Lower Town.

**Explicitly absent in April 1343:**

| Feature | Earliest attested | Notes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pikk Hermann** (Tall Hermann) | First part **by 1371** | Dominant skyline tower today | attested [1][9] |
| Order **Ordensburg** high curtain + 14 towers | Post-1346 rebuild | Western wall tourists photograph is mostly post-Danish | attested [1][2] |
| Stone **Long Leg gate tower** | **1380** (Order consent) | Wooden gate only in 1343 | attested [7] |
| Stone **Short Leg gate** | Mid-15th c. | Wooden in 1343 | attested [7] |
| Masonry curtain Lower Town ↔ Toompea | **1454–1455** | Use timber/earthen barrier until then | attested [7] |
| Catherine Palace / baroque east wing | 1767–1773 | | attested [1] |

Surviving medieval limestone on the modern castle's **west face** is largely **post-1346 Order work** — use for **material and ashlar texture only**, not as a measured 1343 plan [2][10].

### Dome church (Tallinna Toomkirik)

- Wooden church likely **1219**; Dominicans began stone church **1229**; consecrated **1240** as **matrix ecclesiae** (mother church of Estonia) and cathedral of the Bishopric of Reval [4][5]. **attested**
- **1330s onward:** transformation from single-aisle church to **three-aisle Gothic basilica**; work lasted ~100 years [4][5]. **attested**
- Nave rectangular pillars completed **second half of the 14th century**; longitudinal nave (~29 m) finished **1430s** [4][5]. For **April 1343:** show **open nave construction** — scaffolding, half-walls, masons' yard on Toompea square; choir and vestry from early-14th-c. phase already standing [4][5]. **plausible composite**
- Only building on Toompea to survive the **1684** fire intact in its medieval bones; baroque spire and many fittings are **much later** — do not paste 18th-c. silhouette onto 1343 [4]. **attested**

By the **end of the 13th century** the Great Castle plateau was **densely populated** by vassals who preferred hill safety over rural estates [1][6]. Building type is **not** the Lower Town diele-dornse merchant house — expect **stone or stone-timber noble town houses**, smaller footprints, domestic chapels or oratories, service wings, and yard walls on cliff-edge plots [1][6]. **plausible composite** from hill settlement pattern; no measured 1343 vassal house plan survives this tick.

Named factions on the hill in the 1330s–1343 crisis [3][8]:
- **Konrad Preen** — Danish viceroy; in castle in April; captured at Paide negotiations **early May 1343** (revised chronology) [8].
- **Olaf of Roskilde** — Bishop of Reval; pro-Danish party [3].
- **Marquard Breide** — royal captain; pro-German party [3].
- Leading vassals hand Reval and Wesenberg castles to the Order **16 May 1343** [3].

### Boundary control points — Long Leg and Short Leg

Two lanes connect Lower Town to Toompea [1][7]:
- **Pikk jalg (Long Leg):** Longer, gentler ramp along the retaining wall; main cart route in later centuries [1][7].
- **Lühike jalg (Short Leg):** Shorter, steeper stair lane; gate at top of hill [1][7].

In **1343** both hill gates are **wooden** [7]. Lower Town tradition (documented for later Order/Swedish periods) holds that burghers **locked the gates toward Upper Town** at night — transfer to 1343 **plausible composite** for tension scenes [7]. Stone Long Leg tower (**1380**) and mid-15th-c. Short Leg rebuild are **absent** [7].

The castle's **eastern outer-ward gate** feeds these lanes; treat as the **upper end** of the control chain, with Lower Town wall's **Nuns' Gate** and western curtain at the **lower end** [1][7].

### Regional power context (Spring 1343)

| Actor | Position on Toompea / Reval | April–May 1343 |
|---|---|---|
| **Danish crown** | Owns Castrum Danorum and province | Viceroy in castle; sale to Order not yet completed [3] |
| **Town council** | Lower Town only | Holds walls during siege; appeals to Order for aid [12] |
| **Bishop of Reval** | Cathedral on Toompea | Pro-Danish faction; spiritual authority over Estonian clergy [3][4] |
| **Livonian Order** | Extramural until May | Master **Burchard von Dreileben** crushes rebels **14 May 1343**; receives castle **16 May** [3][12] |

## Production hooks

- **Art:** Limestone cliff face and tableland shelf; Danish banner (Dannebrog legend) on castle, not Order cross, in April scenes; Dome church as **construction site** with Gothic cut stone and wooden scaffolding [`architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.02`](../../reference/architecture/toompea-castle-and-upper-town/architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.02.jpg), [`architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.03`](../../reference/architecture/toompea-castle-and-upper-town/architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.03.jpg); vassal houses = sober stone, high chimney, few street-facing openings; **omit Pikk Hermann** from skyline [`architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.01`](../../reference/architecture/toompea-castle-and-upper-town/architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.01.jpg) shows post-medieval wings — limestone colour only.
- **Map:** Separate Toompea mesh from All-linn; cliff ring ~400×250 m; castle SW; cathedral central; two gate edges on west connecting to Pikk jalg / Lühike jalg; southern outer-ward gate to Tõnismägi slope; no stone hill-gate towers [`architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.05`](../../reference/architecture/toompea-castle-and-upper-town/architecture.toompea-castle-and-upper-town.05.jpg) marks **1380+** control point location only.
- **Character:** Viceroy's household, bishop's canons, Danish vassal knights, cathedral masons, castle garrison; dress = northern noble (fur, mail, Danish/German heraldry) — not Hanseatic burgher kit from [`burgher-house-plan.md`](burgher-house-plan.md).
- **Quest / Narrative:** Hill gates closed at curfew; smuggle message up Lühike jalg; pro-German vassals vs pro-Danish bishop; viceroy capture at Paide (**May**) as off-screen escalation; Order entry **16 May** as campaign beat after St George's night [3][8][12].
- **Dialogue:** *Domberg* / *Toompea* for the hill; *All-linn* for Lower Town; *Castrum Danorum* / *Taani linn* in Latin/Low German official speech [1][2].
- **Dev / systems:** `jurisdiction` flag per tile: `{toompea_danish, all_linn_lubeck}`; hill gate `open|closed` state toggled by Lower Town watch after dark **plausible composite** [7].
